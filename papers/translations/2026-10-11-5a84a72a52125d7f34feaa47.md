**Original:** Abstract

**中文：** 摘要

<span id="S004"></span>
**来源**：p.1 S004

**Original:** Low-altitude communications can promote the integration of aerial and terrestrial wireless resources, expand network coverage, and enhance transmission quality, thereby empowering the development of sixth-generation (6G) mobile communications. As an enabler for low-altitude transmission, 3D channel fingerprints (3D-CF), also referred to as the 3D radio map or 3D channel knowledge map, are expected to enhance the understanding of communication environments and assist in the acquisition of channel state information (CSI), thereby avoiding repeated estimations and reducing computational complexity. In this paper, we propose a modularized multimodal framework to construct 3D-CF. Specifically, we first establish the 3D-CF model as a collection of CSI-tuples based on Rician fading channels, with each tuple comprising the low-altitude vehicle’s (LAV) positions and its corresponding statistical CSI. In consideration of the heterogeneous structures of different prior data, we formulate the 3D-CF construction problem as a multimodal regression task, where the target channel information in the CSI-tuple can be estimated directly from its corresponding LAV positions, together with communication measurements and geographic environment maps. Then, a high-efficiency multimodal framework is proposed accordingly, which includes a correlation-based multimodal fusion (Corr-MMF) module, a multimodal representation (MMR) module, and a CSI regression (CSI-R) module. Numerical results show that our proposed framework can efficiently construct 3D-CF and achieve at least 27.5% higher accuracy than the state-of-the-art algorithms under different communication scenarios, demonstrating its competitive performance and excellent generalization ability. We also analyze the computational complexity and illustrate its superiority in terms of inference time.

**中文：** 低空通信能够促进空中与地面无线资源的融合，扩大网络覆盖范围并提升传输质量，从而推动第六代（6G）移动通信的发展。作为低空传输的赋能技术，三维信道指纹（3D-CF，又称三维无线电地图或三维信道知识图谱）有望帮助理解通信环境并辅助获取信道状态信息（CSI），从而避免重复估计并降低计算复杂度。本文提出一种模块化多模态框架，用于构建3D-CF。具体而言，我们首先基于莱斯衰落信道，将3D-CF建模为一组CSI元组；每个元组由低空飞行器（LAV）的位置及其对应的统计CSI组成。考虑到不同先验数据的结构异质性，我们将3D-CF构建问题表述为多模态回归任务：利用相应的LAV位置，并结合通信测量数据和地理环境地图，直接估计CSI元组中的目标信道信息。为此，我们提出一种高效多模态框架，其中包括基于相关性的多模态融合（Corr-MMF）模块、多模态表征（MMR）模块和CSI回归（CSI-R）模块。数值结果表明，该框架能够高效构建3D-CF，在不同通信场景下的准确率至少比现有先进算法高27.5%，并表现出有竞争力的性能和良好的泛化能力。我们还分析了计算复杂度，并说明该方法在推理时间方面的优势。

**中文：** I. 引言

<span id="S008"></span>
**来源**：p.1 S008

**Original:** As sixth-generation (6G) mobile communications continue to evolve, massive demands for low-altitude applications have arisen across transportation, agriculture, and emergency services, catalyzing the vigorous development of low-altitude communications [2]–[6]. Currently, low-altitude networks strive to provide real-time communication and navigation services for low-altitude vehicles (LAVs), facilitate interoperability and coordination with terrestrial networks, deliver communication support for specific regions, and enhance both network coverage and transmission quality [2], [6]. In the foreseeable future, low-altitude communications will further promote the synergistic integration of aerial and terrestrial resources, empowering a new paradigm for mobile communications.

**中文：** 随着第六代（6G）移动通信持续发展，交通、农业和应急服务等领域对低空应用的需求迅速增长，推动低空通信蓬勃发展[2]–[6]。当前，低空网络致力于为低空飞行器（LAV）提供实时通信与导航服务，促进其与地面网络互操作和协同，为特定区域提供通信支持，并提升网络覆盖和传输质量[2], [6]。展望未来，低空通信将进一步促进空地资源协同融合，推动移动通信形成新的发展范式。

<span id="S009"></span>
**来源**：p.1 S009

**Original:** However, acquiring high-quality channel state information (CSI) in low-altitude communication systems remains an unresolved challenge, which significantly impacts the performance of low-altitude wireless transmission. Due to the spatially non-uniform distribution of scatterers [7], [8], radio environments in low-altitude scenarios become more complicated, and signal propagation characteristics vary significantly across different altitudes, rendering accurate CSI acquisition considerably challenging. On the other hand, LAVs in low-altitude airspace exhibit high mobility, constrained power consumption, limited payload, and restricted computing power [7], [9], making real-time and efficient CSI acquisition more difficult.

**中文：** 然而，在低空通信系统中获取高质量信道状态信息（CSI）仍是一项尚未解决的挑战，并显著影响低空无线传输性能。由于散射体在空间中的分布不均匀[7], [8]，低空场景的无线电环境更加复杂，信号传播特性也随高度显著变化，因此准确获取CSI颇具挑战。另一方面，低空空域中的LAV具有高机动性，同时受到功耗、载荷和计算能力的限制[7], [9]，这使实时、高效地获取CSI更加困难。

<span id="S010"></span>
**来源**：p.1 S010

**Original:** Fortunately, channel fingerprints (CF), also referred to as the channel knowledge map (CKM) [10] or the radio environment map (REM) [11], have emerged as a novel approach to address the aforementioned challenge. By definition, CF is a site-specific database storing user-location-related CSI. In terrestrial communication systems, 2D-CF has demonstrated its effectiveness in assisting channel-information acquisition and supporting resource management [12], beam selection [13], wireless positioning [14], and other applications without repetitive estimations. Similarly, in low-altitude communication systems, 3D-CF will directly provide the corresponding CSI based on the positions or trajectories of LAVs, thereby reducing pilot overhead, conserving wireless resources, and improving transmission efficiency. The introduction of 3D-CF is expected to bring a new perspective to the development of low-altitude communications.

**中文：** 信道指纹（CF，又称信道知识图谱（CKM）[10]或无线电环境地图（REM）[11]）已成为应对上述挑战的一种新方法。CF是存储与用户位置相关CSI的场景专用数据库。在地面通信系统中，二维CF已被证明能够辅助获取信道信息，并支持资源管理[12]、波束选择[13]、无线定位[14]等应用，避免重复估计。类似地，在低空通信系统中，三维CF可依据LAV的位置或轨迹直接提供相应CSI，从而减少导频开销、节约无线资源并提高传输效率。预计三维CF将为低空通信的发展带来新的视角。

<span id="S011"></span>
**来源**：p.1–2 S011

**Original:** Currently, several studies have begun to explore efficient approaches to 3D-CF construction. For example, the authors in [15] detected the number of radiation sources using a path-loss (PL) model and constructed a 3D spectrum map accordingly. Nevertheless, adopting a one-size-fits-all PL model inevitably leads to substantial errors in 3D-CF construction because of its considerable variability across different altitudes. To address the limitations of model-based approaches, data-driven methods have been extensively investigated. For instance, the authors in [16] leveraged LAV-based measurements to evaluate interpolation algorithms, including nearest neighbor (NN), linear interpolation, inverse-distance weighting (IDW), and ordinary Kriging, validating their feasibility for 3D-CF reconstruction. Gaussian process regression (GPR), sparse Bayesian learning, and compressed sensing (CS) were also developed to reduce the required number of samples in [17]–[21]. Additionally, t-singular value decomposition (t-SVD), fiber sampling tensor decomposition (FSTD), block-term tensor decomposition (BTD), and other tensor-based algorithms were adopted to further enhance computational efficiency by leveraging the smoothness prior of measurements in [22]–[25]. However, these purely data-driven methods are entirely environment-blind. In practice, wireless channels are profoundly influenced by the geographic environment through a complex interplay of signal reflection, diffraction, and scattering mechanisms [26], [27], especially in low-altitude communication systems where line-of-sight (LOS) paths are predominant.

**中文：** 目前已有若干研究开始探索高效构建3D-CF的方法。例如，文献[15]基于路径损耗（PL）模型检测辐射源数量，并据此构建三维频谱地图。然而，由于路径损耗随高度变化显著，采用一种通用PL模型构建3D-CF不可避免地会产生较大误差。为克服基于模型方法的局限，研究者广泛探索了数据驱动方法。例如，文献[16]利用基于LAV的测量数据评估了多种插值算法，包括最近邻（NN）、线性插值、反距离加权（IDW）和普通克里金法，并验证了这些方法用于3D-CF重建的可行性。文献[17]–[21]还采用高斯过程回归（GPR）、稀疏贝叶斯学习和压缩感知（CS），以减少所需样本数。此外，文献[22]–[25]采用t-奇异值分解（t-SVD）、纤维采样张量分解（FSTD）、块项张量分解（BTD）及其他基于张量的算法，利用测量数据的平滑先验进一步提高计算效率。然而，这些纯数据驱动方法完全不感知环境。实际上，无线信道会受到地理环境的深刻影响，其间存在信号反射、绕射和散射等复杂作用[26], [27]；在视距（LOS）路径占主导的低空通信系统中，这一点尤为明显。

## 第2页

<span id="S014"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S014

**Original:** To empower geographic environment-assisted 3D-CF construction, researchers have turned to machine learning (ML) for feasible solutions. For instance, the authors in [28] designed two deep neural networks (DNNs) to jointly reconstruct the 3D radio environment map (REM) and its communication environment. Moreover, generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) was adopted for high-quality 3D-CF generation, supporting both radiation-aware and radiation-unaware scenarios with sparse spatial observations using generative adversarial networks (GANs) or diffusion models (DMs) [29]–[31].

**中文：** 为了实现地理环境辅助的3D-CF构建，研究者转向机器学习（ML）并探索可行方案。例如，文献[28]设计了两个深度神经网络（DNN），联合重建三维无线电环境地图（REM）及其通信环境。此外，生成式人工智能（GenAI）被用于生成高质量3D-CF，并借助生成对抗网络（GAN）或扩散模型（DM），在辐射感知与辐射非感知场景下利用稀疏空间观测完成生成[29]–[31]。

<span id="S015"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S015

**Original:** However, these computer-vision-based GenAI algorithms must model 3D-CF as images, requiring discretization of the target region so that LAVs in the same grid (pixel) share identical CSI (pixel values) [32], [33]. This assumption introduces critical limitations. First, uniform discretization reduces the flexibility of 3D-CF. Unlike terrestrial networks, LAV trajectories in low-altitude airspace are highly driven by communication demands [34]. Indiscriminate gridding merely mismatches 3D-CF resolution with communication demands while wasting computational resources. Second, sharing CSI within a grid causes significant errors in 3D-CF applications. In particular, when buildings lie within a grid, path loss and channel shadowing for LAVs on opposite sides may differ substantially and cannot be represented by the same CSI. Third, a grid-based 3D model causes an exponential increase in data volume, resulting in high computational complexity and making practical deployment unsuitable.

**中文：** 然而，这些基于计算机视觉的GenAI算法必须将3D-CF表示为图像，因此需要对目标区域离散化，使同一网格（像素）中的LAV共享相同CSI（像素值）[32], [33]。这一假设带来若干关键局限。第一，均匀离散化降低了3D-CF的灵活性。与地面网络不同，低空空域中的LAV轨迹高度取决于通信需求[34]。不加区分地划分网格会使3D-CF分辨率与通信需求不匹配，同时浪费计算资源。第二，同一网格内共享CSI会在3D-CF应用中造成显著误差。特别是当网格内存在建筑物时，建筑两侧LAV的路径损耗和信道阴影可能相差很大，无法用同一CSI表示。第三，基于网格的三维模型会使数据量呈指数级增长，导致计算复杂度高，不适合实际部署。

<span id="S016"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S016

**Original:** Multimodal learning (MML), an intelligent computational approach with capabilities such as understanding, reasoning, and learning, enables integration of diverse data modalities for predictive tasks and offers a promising way to overcome the image-only processing constraint of GenAI. Studies [35]–[45] have demonstrated the substantial potential of MML-based CSI prediction in wireless communications. In particular, low-altitude 3D-CF construction involves more complex modalities, including geographic environment maps, sparse communication measurements, and LAV coordinates. MML is expected to process and integrate information across these modalities, offering new solutions for 3D-CF construction and improving environmental awareness in low-altitude communication systems.

**中文：** 多模态学习（MML）是一种具备理解、推理和学习等能力的智能计算方法，能够为预测任务整合不同数据模态，有望突破GenAI仅处理图像的限制。研究[35]–[45]已显示，基于MML的CSI预测在无线通信中具有很大应用潜力。尤其是低空3D-CF构建涉及更复杂的数据模态，包括地理环境地图、稀疏通信测量和LAV坐标。MML有望处理并融合这些模态中的信息，为3D-CF构建提供新方案，并增强低空通信系统的环境感知能力。

<span id="S017"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S017

**Original:** Motivated by the above discussion, we investigate MML-based 3D-CF construction for low-altitude communication systems. Specifically, we encapsulate LAV coordinates and their corresponding channel information in a CSI-tuple, bypassing discretization and directly fitting the mapping between tuple elements to minimize 3D-CF errors. The CSI stored in 3D-CF can be flexibly defined according to practical transmission requirements, such as received signal strength (RSS), coverage, line-of-sight probability, or even the channel covariance matrix and power angle spectrum (PAS). We then use prior knowledge to assist 3D-CF construction, including geographic environment maps, sparse communication measurements, and LAV positions. Because these data have heterogeneous structures that cannot be processed by a single-type network, we treat them as multimodal data and formulate 3D-CF construction as a multimodal regression task, thereby developing a modularized multimodal framework. The main contributions are summarized as follows:

**中文：** 基于上述讨论，我们研究面向低空通信系统、基于MML的3D-CF构建。具体而言，我们将LAV坐标及对应的信道信息封装为CSI元组，绕过离散化过程，直接拟合元组各元素之间的映射关系，以减小3D-CF误差。3D-CF中存储的CSI可根据实际传输需求灵活定义，例如接收信号强度（RSS）、覆盖情况、视距概率，甚至信道协方差矩阵和功率角谱（PAS）。随后，我们利用地理环境地图、稀疏通信测量和LAV位置等先验知识辅助构建3D-CF。由于这些数据结构异质，无法由单一类型网络处理，我们将其视为多模态数据，并把3D-CF构建表述为多模态回归任务，进而设计模块化多模态框架。本文的主要贡献如下：

<span id="S018"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S018

**Original:** Based on the ground-to-LAV Rician fading channel model, we propose a 3D-CF model more suitable for low-altitude communication systems. Specifically, 3D-CF is conceptualized as a collection of CSI-tuples, each comprising the LAV position and its corresponding channel information. This model enables CSI-tuples to be adaptively adjusted according to practical communication demands, making flexible 3D-CF construction possible.

**中文：** 基于地面至LAV的莱斯衰落信道模型，我们提出一种更适用于低空通信系统的3D-CF模型。具体而言，3D-CF被视为一组CSI元组，每个元组由LAV位置及其对应的信道信息组成。该模型允许根据实际通信需求自适应调整CSI元组，从而实现灵活的3D-CF构建。

<span id="S019"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S019

**Original:** Given the heterogeneous structures of different prior data, we formulate 3D-CF construction as a multimodal regression task, where the target channel information in a CSI-tuple can be directly estimated from the corresponding LAV location, geographic environment maps, and measurement data.

**中文：** 考虑到不同先验数据的结构异质性，我们将3D-CF构建表述为多模态回归任务，使CSI元组中的目标信道信息能够根据相应LAV位置、地理环境地图和测量数据直接估计。

<span id="S020"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S020

**Original:** Based on the structural characteristics and internal relations of prior data, we propose a highly efficient modularized multimodal framework for 3D-CF construction. During data processing, a correlation-based multimodal fusion (Corr-MMF) module and a multimodal representation (MMR) module are designed based on the relationships between communication environments and measurement data; they extract and learn features of the CSI distribution in the horizontal and vertical directions, respectively. During CSI estimation, we align these different features through embedding operations and design a channel state information regression (CSI-R) module that estimates CSI using LAV positions as conditional inputs, thereby recovering CSI-tuples and accomplishing 3D-CF construction.

**中文：** 基于先验数据的结构特征及其内在关系，我们提出一种高效的模块化多模态框架来构建3D-CF。在数据处理阶段，我们根据通信环境与测量数据之间的关联设计基于相关性的多模态融合（Corr-MMF）模块和多模态表征（MMR）模块，分别提取并学习CSI分布在水平方向和垂直方向上的特征。在CSI估计阶段，我们通过嵌入操作对齐不同特征，并设计信道状态信息回归（CSI-R）模块，以LAV位置作为条件输入估计CSI，从而恢复CSI元组并完成3D-CF构建。

<span id="S021"></span>
**来源**：p.2 S021

**Original:** Numerical results show that the proposed modularized multimodal framework achieves at least 27.5% higher accuracy than state-of-the-art algorithms for 3D-CF construction. Experiments also demonstrate competitive generalization capability and computational complexity.

**中文：** 数值结果表明，所提出的模块化多模态框架在3D-CF构建方面的准确率至少比现有先进算法高27.5%。实验还表明，该框架具有有竞争力的泛化能力和计算复杂度表现。

<span id="S022"></span>
**来源**：p.2–3 S022

**Original:** The rest of this paper is organized as follows. **Original:** In this paper, we proposed a modularized multimodal framework to construct 3D-CF for low-altitude communications. Firstly, we established the 3D-CF model based on the ground-to-LAV Rician fading channels, which were defined as a collection of CSI-tuples with each tuple composed of LAV’s positions and its corresponding channel information. Due to the heterogeneous structures of different prior data such as LAV coordinates, geographic environment maps, and sampling data, we transformed the 3D-CF construction problem into a multimodal regression task and proposed a high-efficiency modularized multimodal framework accordingly, where the Corr-MMF module and the MMR module were designed to extract features of CSI distribution in horizontal and vertical directions, and the CSI-R module was developed to estimate the target CSI and reconstruct the 3D-CF. Numerical results demonstrated the competitive performance and generalization ability of our proposed 3D-CF multimodal framework, which attains an accuracy improvement of at least 27.5% over the benchmarks under different communication scenarios. We also analyzed the computational complexity and illustrated its superiority in terms of the inference time. In the future, it will be interesting to quantify the impact of measurement data resolution on the constructed 3D-CF accuracy and its computational complexity. Furthermore, considering air-to-air channels and dynamic environmental factors also constitutes a highly promising research direction for low-altitude 3D-CF techniques.

**中文：** 本文提出了一种模块化多模态框架，用于低空通信中的3D-CF构建。首先，我们基于地面到LAV的莱斯衰落信道建立3D-CF模型，并将其定义为一组CSI元组，其中每个元组由LAV位置及其对应的信道信息组成。鉴于LAV坐标、地理环境地图和采样数据等不同先验数据具有异构结构，我们将3D-CF构建问题转化为多模态回归任务，并据此提出高效的模块化多模态框架：Corr-MMF模块和MMR模块分别用于提取CSI分布在水平方向和垂直方向上的特征，CSI-R模块则用于估计目标CSI并重构3D-CF。数值结果表明，所提出的3D-CF多模态框架具有较强的性能和泛化能力，在不同通信场景下相较基线方法至少提升27.5%的准确率。我们还分析了计算复杂度，并展示了该框架在推理时间方面的优势。未来值得进一步量化测量数据分辨率对3D-CF构建准确率和计算复杂度的影响。此外，考虑空对空信道和动态环境因素，也是低空3D-CF技术极具前景的研究方向。
