# Translation notes

- Scope is intentionally limited to Abstract and Introduction, as requested; this is not a full-paper reader.
- Source format detected: selectable-text PDF (`pdf-text`). Local text extracts were used after checking the corresponding PDF files.
- PDF extraction introduced line-break hyphenation and occasional symbol spacing issues. These were normalized when the sentence meaning was unambiguous.
- Figures mentioned in the Introduction were not cropped or reproduced because the requested scope was text translation only.
- Reported experimental numbers are translated as claims made by the papers. They have not been independently reproduced on wireless-channel data.
- The document keeps paragraph-level English/Chinese pairs and stable anchors for the selected Introduction blocks.
