9 限制和范围

FCM被作为一个从抽取来的培训目标来研究;其他的培训制度(如适应已经交接过的模型)不在我们的主张范围之内. 培训差异。 256px干预武器是每个细胞的单一训练;50克-样本FID和图像级的靴子间隔,我们报告的是约束评价噪音,而不是训练-运行的差异。 在可控的32×32型型号上,复制费用低廉,我们训练出3个独立的种子对接跑道(基线对FCM,匹配种子和数据顺序,每个50k步). 少数步骤的改进贯穿了种子。 NFE 6的配对QQFID为+6.97±3.26,所有三种种子都支持FCM;而NFE 8的配对为+2.87±2.34(另外三种). 根据NFE 10–16,对等三角洲属于种子噪声,与受控模式得到的受控模式是几步效应一致(Appendix B 6.;相匹配的QQ-sweep,其中32×32的得分也集中在低等的NFE上). 因此,256px的干预结果被配对控制的证据,而不是差异解性能比较. 3个数据集的可控模型复制和一致性使得单跑故障不太可能. 计算。 FCM每一步增加一个前行(测量为1.9×分步时间);我们的三角洲处于相匹配的优化步骤和数据曝光,而不是相匹配的培训计算,以及E4研究测试的持久性,因为基线被训练为4×分步。 我们的"从剪接"模型是紧凑而短的,相对于"领跑板"系统:配对的"三角"是主张,而不是绝对的"最先进"质量. 衡量标准是什么。 我们的目标是每个区域解决者的差异(便宜与接近精确的整合),而不是概念上的重要性。 纠正高氟化物区域会减少像素或潜在出错,这些出错不需要与知觉的突出性相吻合。 FC-error相接是单调而并非相称的,所以我们声称是整齐性的改进,而不是相称性的. 预测地平线的位置因模型而异,我们声称它的存在和衡量它的议定书,而不是 " 早期 " 的普遍性。 " 过分减少集中于困难地区 " 索赔按固定的FC-十进制报告,并须按逐一回归,因此,我们将它视为对收益出现地点的描述,而不是一种独立的影响。 32×32的喷发在256px运行时都包含的轻微处罚-执行改进(共享调节口罩)之前.

B.2 Reference discrepancy and signal estimation

Per-region integration error: per-region MSE between a cheap solve (few-step Euler/DDIM, e.g. NFE 6) and a near-exact reference solve (Heun-200) of the same ODE from the same noise. This target is measured independently of the signal: it compares final states only and knows nothing of the predictor. Regions: 16 × 16 px tokens at 2562 resolution (latent models: token-aligned latent patches). Time convention and windows: t runs 1 →0 from noise to data; the observation cutoff f of §5 is the fraction of sampling elapsed from noise. “First ∼10%” = t ∈[1.0, 0.9]; “first 30%” = t ∈[1.0, 0.7]. Rank statistics: all correlations are within-image Spearman ρ (per-bin W ∗profiles likewise), invariant to the overall ∆t scale of Definition 1. Estimator note: Definition 1 is P∥∆u∥; the implementation uses estimators that are rank-equivalent within a fixed uniform window (mean ∥∆u∥; time-weighted mean of ∥∆u∥/∆t), differing from P∥∆u∥only by positive constants that cancel under within-image ranking. Trajectory used for FC: unless stated otherwise the window W is sampled along the recorded reference trajectory. To test what survives without it, we recomputed FC from the cheap NFE-6 Euler solve itself on SiT with a held-out seed (n = 96, disjoint from every other SiT analysis). Within-image ρ against the final per-region error is +0.26 using all six velocity evaluations and +0.32/+0.30/+0.29/+0.27 using the first 2/3/4/5, versus +0.47 (full-window) and +0.58 (early-window) for reference-trajectory FC on the same images. The correlation falls under coarse discretization but does not collapse. Among the prefixes evaluated on this held-out set the earliest (k = 2) was the highest. That observation was made after seeing the results, so we froze k = 2 and the metrics in advance and ran a second held-out seed as a confirmatory test. On the confirmatory seed the frozen primary metric, top-15% error capture by the two-evaluation cheap prefix, is 0.245 [0.23, 0.26] (image-level bootstrap) against an oracle ceiling of 0.352 and a random baseline of 38/256 = 0.148, with mean within-image ρ = +0.40 [+0.36, +0.44], median +0.42, and 3% of images at ρ ≤0. Comparators behave as on the exploratory seed (reference early-window ρ = +0.62, capture 0.29; cheap full ρ = +0.30, capture 0.21). The two seeds are reported separately, never pooled. A cross-model replicate on our own CelebA-HQ-256 flow model under the same frozen protocol (fresh seed, n = 96, unconditional) gives capture 0.262 [0.25, 0.28] against an oracle of 0.408, with ρ = +0.46 [+0.43, +0.50] and 1% of images at ρ ≤0; the frozen k = 2 prefix was again the strongest among the prefixes, without reselection. Per-image statistics and provenance are committed with the code (results/sit/, results/celebahq/).

B. Error reduction concentrates on the hard regions

Figure 9: The penalty’s benefit lands on the difficult landscape. Regions binned by baseline FC percentile (CelebA, n=32768 regions, λ=0.3 vs. baseline at matched 100k steps). (A) the penalty lowers FC by a near-uniform 3.4–4.8% across every difficulty bin. (B) the resulting absolute integration-error reduction climbs about 7.5× from the easy half to the hardest 5%, concentrating the improvement on the high-FC regions §4 flags as difficult. Average FC is thus the wrong summary of the effect.

lecting the highest-FC regions gives FC 14.35 &lt; random 15.35 ≈attention-entropy 15.41 &lt; edge 15.90 &lt; frequency 18.40 ≈velocity-magnitude 18.52: among the reactive signals only FC beats random, and edge, frequency, and velocity-magnitude actively mislead.

An important counter-result. The FID-optimal allocation is the reverse of the error-optimal one. Refining the lowest-FC (smooth, easy) regions instead reaches FID 12.40, better than refining the highest-FC regions (14.35) and than random (15.35), and the same low-FC choice also wins on perceptual LPIPS-to-reference (0.0286 vs 0.0299). Yet by pixel fidelity to the reference solve (the integration error FC is meant to track), the high-FC choice is best (MSE 0.0351). So FC does identify where integration error concentrates, but reducing that error is not the same as improving perceptual sample quality: smooth regions matter more to FID than their small integration error suggests. This dissociation is exactly why we treat FC as a diagnostic of integration difficulty and do not propose it as a sampling policy (§9). Caveat: FC also selects spatially coherent regions, so part of the margin may be a coherence effect.
