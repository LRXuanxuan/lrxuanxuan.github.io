<span id="S004"></span>
**来源**：p.1 S004

**Original:** Generative models are increasingly used to solve scientific inverse problems, but existing evaluations still focus primarily on whether a method can produce a single plausible reconstruction. This is insufficient for ill-posed problems, where multiple solutions may be consistent with the same sparse or noisy observations. In these settings, a method can achieve strong pointwise accuracy while still failing to capture the true posterior through mode collapse, overconfident uncertainty, or averaging incompatible solutions. We introduce PosteriorBench, a benchmark for evaluating the distributional accuracy of generative inverse solvers. PosteriorBench evaluates four physics-based inverse problems: Darcy flow inversion, Poisson source recovery, carbon capture and storage, and light transport material inference. For each task, we construct high-fidelity reference posteriors using computationally heavy but established procedures such as rejection sampling and Markov chain Monte Carlo, enabling direct assessment of whether solvers recover the full set of solutions rather than the single best sample. We pair these references with a five-metric posterior evaluation suite: posterior-mean error, posterior-standard-deviation error, maximum mean discrepancy, sliced Wasserstein distance, and radially averaged power-spectrum error. Together, these metrics assess pointwise accuracy, marginal uncertainty, distributional alignment, and global frequency fidelity. The benchmark spans sparse sensing, low-resolution observations, nonlinear forward models, varying noise levels, and multimodal priors, with a unified pipeline for distribution matching and uncertainty quantification. Our experiments reveal substantial distribution-matching gaps across current solvers, while showing that neural operators improve resolution robustness and that guidance weights and generation noise are key to posterior-variance calibration. The code is available at https://github.com/neuraloperator/PosteriorBench.

**中文：** 生成模型正越来越多地用于求解科学逆问题，但现有评估仍主要关注一种方法能否给出单个看似合理的重建结果。对于适定性较差的问题，这种评估并不充分，因为同一组稀疏或含噪观测可能对应多个解。在这类情形下，方法即使具有很强的逐点精度，也仍可能因模式坍塌、不确定性估计过度自信，或对不相容解求平均而未能捕获真实后验。我们提出 PosteriorBench，这是一个用于评估生成式逆问题求解器分布精度的基准。PosteriorBench评估四类基于物理的逆问题：Darcy流反演、Poisson源恢复、碳捕集与封存，以及光传输材料推断。对于每项任务，我们使用拒绝采样、马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛等计算开销较大但成熟的方法构造高保真参考后验，从而能够直接评估求解器是否恢复了完整解集合，而非仅给出单个最佳样本。我们将这些参考后验与一套包含五项指标的评估方案配对：后验均值误差、后验标准差误差、最大均值差异、切片Wasserstein距离，以及径向平均功率谱误差。这些指标共同评估逐点精度、边缘不确定性、分布对齐程度和全局频率保真度。该基准涵盖稀疏观测、低分辨率观测、非线性前向模型、不同噪声水平及多模态先验，并提供用于分布匹配和不确定性量化的统一流程。实验揭示了当前求解器在分布匹配方面存在显著差距，同时表明神经算子能够提升分辨率鲁棒性，而引导权重和生成噪声是后验方差校准的关键因素。代码见 https://github.com/neuraloperator/PosteriorBench。

**Original:** Recent generative models, especially diffusion and flow-based models, have made this Bayesian decomposition increasingly tractable. Score-based generative models provide expressive priors for high-dimensional data [5–7], and diffusion-based sampling methods combine such priors with measurement likelihoods at inference time [8–12]. In scientific settings, this area now intersects with neural operators and physics-informed learning [13–16], leading to solvers that use joint coefficient-solution diffusion models, PDE residual guidance, or function-space formulations for physical fields [17–22]. **Original:** Existing scientific inverse-problem benchmarks such as InverseBench [23] evaluate plug-and-play diffusion samplers across physical inverse problems, but still center on a single solution for each case. More broadly, evaluation in this area is often anchored to single held-out ground-truth, which are insufficient for ill-posed problems. A solver can match the observations and still underestimate posterior variance or blur out incompatible modes into an unphysical mean.

**中文：** 现有科学逆问题基准（如 InverseBench [23]）会在多种物理逆问题上评估即插即用扩散采样器，但每个案例仍以单个解为中心。更广泛地看，该领域的评估通常以单个留出的真实值为基准，这不足以评估适定性较差的问题。求解器即使与观测相符，仍可能低估后验方差，或把不相容的模式模糊成一个不符合物理规律的均值。

**Original:** This gap motivates PosteriorBench, a benchmark for evaluating whether generative inverse solvers recover the posterior they claim to sample from. PosteriorBench focuses specifically on posterior matching: each benchmark case is paired with a transparent, computationally expensive reference posterior, so solvers can be evaluated as posterior samplers rather than only by their best reconstruction. Generated ensembles are compared to the reference posteriors using metrics for distributional moments, spatial structure, observation consistency, and computational cost. This framing is especially important for scientific settings, where posterior uncertainty informs decision making and downstream physical interpretation.

**中文：** 这一缺口促使我们提出 PosteriorBench，用于评估生成式逆问题求解器是否恢复了其声称采样的后验分布。PosteriorBench专门关注后验匹配：基准中的每个案例都配有一个透明但计算开销较高的参考后验，因此可以把求解器作为后验采样器来评估，而不只是比较其最佳重建结果。我们使用衡量分布矩、空间结构、观测一致性和计算成本的指标，将生成样本集合与参考后验进行比较。在科学应用中，这一视角尤其重要，因为后验不确定性会影响决策和后续物理解释。

**Original:** The benchmark contains four tasks from different scientific domains: Darcy flow inversion, Poisson source recovery, carbon capture and storage (CCS), and light transport material inference (LTMI). Together they cover binary and smooth priors, sparse point and column observations, nonlinear physical maps, low-resolution measurements, and structured scientific priors. The CCS task is a high-impact subsurface monitoring setting, where permeability fields must be inferred from sparse well observations of CO dynamics; reducing the number of wells can substantially lower field intervention and monitoring cost. Ensemble methods such as ES-MDA remain important domain baselines for this inverse problem [24, 25]. The LTMI task adds a two-layer radiative inverse problem motivated by atmospheric imaging, optical tomography, and nondestructive material inspection, where optical measurements must be translated into plausible internal material structure.

**中文：** 该基准包含来自不同科学领域的四项任务：Darcy流反演、Poisson源恢复、碳捕集与封存（CCS），以及光传输材料推断（LTMI）。这些任务共同覆盖二值先验和平滑先验、稀疏点观测和列观测、非线性物理映射、低分辨率测量以及结构化科学先验。CCS任务属于影响重大的地下监测场景，需要依据稀疏井点的CO动态观测推断渗透率场；减少井的数量可以显著降低现场干预和监测成本。ES-MDA等集合方法仍是该逆问题的重要领域基线[24, 25]。LTMI任务则加入了一个双层辐射逆问题，其动机来自大气成像、光学层析和无损材料检测；在这些应用中，需要将光学测量转换为合理的材料内部结构。

**Original:** From the benchmark, we find that function-space diffusion samplers such as DDIS [22] and FunDPS [20] are strong posterior samplers across several scientific inverse tasks. We also find that posterior-mean error alone can be misleading: a solver may place the ensemble center near the reference mean while still misrepresenting posterior spread. This makes moment consistency a joint requirement, where posterior mean and standard deviation errors should be interpreted together and, when possible, alongside distributional metrics such as MMD and SWD.

**中文：** 通过该基准，我们发现，DDIS [22]和 FunDPS [20]等函数空间扩散采样器在若干科学逆问题任务中具有较强的后验采样能力。我们还发现，单独考察后验均值误差可能产生误导：求解器生成的样本集合中心可能接近参考均值，却仍然错误地表示后验的离散程度。因此，矩的一致性需要联合考察：应同时解读后验均值误差和标准差误差，并在可能时结合MMD、SWD等分布指标。

**Original:** We further compare posterior metrics against a traditional pointwise reconstruction metric. This comparison reveals that aggressively fitting a single reference field can degrade posterior structure: low pointwise error can coincide with poor posterior variance, distributional mismatch, or distorted spatial statistics. Through diagnostics based on the governing PDE constraint and the latent GRF smoothness parameter, we find that posterior metrics better reflect physically meaningful recovery than pointwise error alone.

**中文：** 我们进一步将后验指标与传统逐点重建指标进行比较。结果表明，过度拟合单个参考场会损害后验结构：逐点误差较低时，后验方差仍可能不佳、分布可能不匹配，或空间统计特性可能失真。通过基于控制PDE约束和潜在高斯随机场（GRF）平滑参数的诊断，我们发现，与单独使用逐点误差相比，后验指标更能反映具有物理意义的恢复效果。

**Original:** We also study how solvers respond to different observation noise levels. For a Gaussian observation model, guidance weights should, in theory, scale with the inverse observation-noise variance. Our sweeps show that learned samplers do not resolve posterior calibration by this scaling alone: stronger guidance improves observation consistency but often underestimates posterior variance, while weaker guidance preserves diversity at the cost of a biased or weakly conditioned posterior mean. **中文：** 我们还研究了求解器对不同观测噪声水平的响应。对于高斯观测模型，理论上引导权重应按观测噪声方差的倒数缩放。我们的扫描实验表明，仅靠这种缩放无法解决学习型采样器的后验校准问题：更强的引导可提高观测一致性，但往往会低估后验方差；更弱的引导可以保留多样性，却会造成后验均值偏差或条件约束不足。这种均值—方差权衡说明，需要能够联合校准后验均值和不确定性的条件机制，而不能只依赖单一权重。第4节将详细介绍更多消融研究。

**Mean-only summaries can be misleading.**

<span id="S082"></span>
**来源**：p.8 S082

**Original:** Mean-only summaries can be misleading. A central purpose of PosteriorBench is to prevent posterior evaluation from collapsing back to a single point-summary comparison. The LTMI results provide a concrete example: FNO with MC Dropout attains lower posterior-mean error than FunDPS in Table 2, yet its posterior-std error, MMD, and SWD remain high. Figure 3 shows why this is not a metric inconsistency: For a representative LTMI case, the MC Dropout sample is visibly over-smoothed relative to the reference posterior sample, while FunDPS better preserves the fine spatial texture of the material field. Thus, a low mean error can reflect agreement with a central tendency while still missing the geometry and spread of the posterior distribution. Marginal moment consistency should therefore be read jointly, together with metrics like MMD and SWD.

**中文：** 仅看均值可能产生误导。PosteriorBench的一个核心目的，是避免后验评估再次退化为单点摘要比较。LTMI结果给出了一个具体例子：表2中采用MC Dropout的FNO获得了比FunDPS更低的后验均值误差，但其后验标准差误差、MMD和SWD仍然较高。图3说明这并非指标之间相互矛盾：对于一个代表性LTMI案例，相较参考后验样本，MC Dropout样本明显过度平滑，而FunDPS更好地保留了材料场的细致空间纹理。因此，较低的均值误差可能只反映了与中心趋势的一致性，却仍未捕获后验分布的几何结构和离散程度。故应联合解读边缘矩一致性和MMD、SWD等指标。

**Useful inductive bias in Function-space diffusion samplers.**

<span id="S083"></span>
**来源**：p.8 S083

**Original:** Useful inductive bias in Function-space diffusion samplers. Across the benchmark, FunDPS, Fun-DDPS, and DDIS form a family of guided diffusion posterior samplers built on function-space score priors. At the same time, their performance is task-dependent: classical methods such as ES-MDA can be stronger in settings such as LTMI, and the best-performing function-space diffusion sampler varies across datasets. Their results suggest that function-space score priors are useful for posterior matching under sparse or low-resolution observations. The comparison with DiffusionPDE points to the value of function-space score backbones, whose spectral operator blocks are better aligned with continuous physical fields than the grid-based backbone. The comparison with ECI highlights the role of soft likelihood guidance: ECI enforces observed entries directly through hard replacement, whereas guided diffusion samplers condition the sampling process on the observations while trying to stay on the prior manifold. During inference, these samplers dynamically balance learned prior against data consistency, which is central to recovering ensembles rather than only point reconstructions. The remaining variation across tasks and metrics motivates a closer comparison within this guided diffusion family.

<span id="S084"></span>

### 4.6 Observation Noise and Guidance Calibration

<span id="S093"></span>
**来源**：p.11 S093

**Original:** 4.6 Observation Noise and Guidance Calibration

**中文：** 4.6 观测噪声与引导校准

<span id="S094"></span>
**来源**：p.11 S094

**Original:** This experiment tests whether solver hyper-parameters that are often described as inverse noise scales actually calibrate to the posterior induced by different observation-noise levels. As shown in Equations (4) and (5), the Gaussian likelihood score scales with the likelihood precision σ_y^−2, which motivates scaling data-consistency guidance with the observation-noise level. However, the guidance coefficient used in practice may not follow this rule due to the interaction with discretization, normalization, annealing schedules, etc.

**中文：** 本实验检验常被称为噪声倒数尺度的求解器超参数，能否针对不同观测噪声水平所诱导的后验实现校准。如式(4)和式(5)所示，高斯似然分数随似然精度 $\sigma_y^{-2}$ 缩放，因此有理由让数据一致性引导随观测噪声水平相应缩放。然而，实际使用的引导系数可能因离散化、归一化、退火调度等因素的相互作用而不遵循这一规律。

<span id="S095"></span>
**来源**：p.11 S095

**Original:** For Poisson source recovery, we vary the observation-noise scale σ and report the guidance optimum λ*_m(σ) = arg min_λ m(P_gen, P_ref). **中文：** 对于Poisson源恢复，我们改变观测噪声尺度 $\sigma$，并报告引导最优值 $\lambda_m^*(\sigma)=\arg\min_{\lambda}m(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{gen}},\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ref}})$。图7显示，几乎对所有指标而言，最优引导权重通常随观测噪声方差倒数 $\sigma^{-2}$ 增大。频谱误差的波动较大，但总体仍呈相同的正相关关系。这一行为与高斯似然假设一致。完整的引导权重扫描和案例级空间诊断见D.1节。

**来源**：p.11 S096

### 5 Discussion

<span id="S099"></span>
**来源**：p.12 S099

**Original:** 5 Discussion

**中文：** 5 讨论

<span id="S100"></span>
**来源**：p.12 S100

**Original:** PosteriorBench evaluates generative scientific inverse solvers as posterior samplers rather than as single-reconstruction methods. Across Darcy flow inversion, Poisson source recovery, carbon capture and storage, and light-transport material inference, the benchmark makes the residual ambiguity under partial observations explicit by comparing generated ensembles against reference posterior distributions. The experiments highlight two main findings. First, PosteriorBench exposes concrete regimes in which pointwise and distributional evaluation disagree. Second, function-space diffusion samplers provide strong posterior-matching performance across the benchmark, yet still leave gaps in jointly matching posterior means and standard deviations, recovering latent parameters, and satisfying PDE constraints. These findings highlight that a solver can match observations or point estimates while still misrepresenting posterior uncertainty, spatial structure, or distinct meaningful modes.

**中文：** PosteriorBench将生成式科学逆问题求解器作为后验采样器来评估，而非作为单一重建方法。在Darcy流反演、Poisson源恢复、碳捕集与封存以及光传输材料推断任务中，该基准通过将生成样本集合与参考后验分布比较，明确呈现部分观测下残留的歧义。实验突出了两项主要发现。第一，PosteriorBench揭示了逐点评估与分布评估结果不一致的具体情形。第二，函数空间扩散采样器在该基准上具有较强的后验匹配表现，但在联合匹配后验均值和标准差、恢复潜在参数以及满足PDE约束方面仍存在差距。这些发现表明，求解器即使匹配观测或点估计，也仍可能错误表示后验不确定性、空间结构或不同的有意义模态。

### A Related Work

<span id="page-18"></span>

<span id="S176"></span>
**来源**：p.18 S176

**Original:** A Related Work

**中文：** A 相关工作

**Bayesian scientific inverse problems.**

<span id="S177"></span>
**来源**：p.18 S177

**Original:** Bayesian scientific inverse problems. Inverse problems are classically formulated as the recovery of unknown parameters or fields from indirect observations through a forward physical model [1, 2, 31, 32]. The Bayesian formulation treats the unknown as a random function and combines a prior with the observation likelihood to obtain a posterior distribution [3, 4]. This view is essential when observations are sparse, noisy, or non-identifying, since posterior uncertainty can remain large even when the observations are matched. Classical data-assimilation and ensemble methods, including ensemble Kalman approaches, provide scalable approximations for some high-dimensional systems but can be limited by Gaussian or linearized update assumptions [33]. In high-dimensional scientific settings, asymptotically reliable samplers such as MCMC, sequential Monte Carlo, or rejection sampling are often too expensive to use as practical solvers, which motivates amortized or generative approximations [34, 35]. PosteriorBench uses these slow methods not as deployment algorithms, but as reference procedures for evaluating whether faster learned samplers recover the intended posterior.

**中文：** 贝叶斯科学逆问题。经典逆问题通常表述为：通过前向物理模型，根据间接观测恢复未知参数或场[1, 2, 31, 32]。贝叶斯表述将未知量视为随机函数，并结合先验与观测似然得到后验分布[3, 4]。当观测稀疏、含噪或无法唯一确定未知量时，这一视角至关重要，因为即使观测得到匹配，后验不确定性仍可能很大。包括集合Kalman方法在内的经典数据同化和集合方法，为某些高维系统提供了可扩展的近似，但可能受限于高斯或线性化更新假设[33]。在高维科学问题中，MCMC、序贯蒙特卡洛或拒绝采样等渐近可靠的采样器通常开销过高，难以直接用作实用求解器，因此需要摊销式或生成式近似[34, 35]。PosteriorBench并不将这些慢速方法用作部署算法，而是将其作为参考流程，用来评估更快速的学习型采样器是否恢复了目标后验。

**Neural operators and PDE inverse solvers.**

<span id="S178"></span>
**来源**：p.18 S178

**Original:** Neural operators and PDE inverse solvers. Physics-informed neural networks and neural operators have become standard tools for learning PDE solution maps and solving PDE-constrained inverse problems [13–15]. Operator-learning architectures such as FNO and DeepONet learn mappings between function spaces and can generalize across discretizations more naturally than fixed-grid networks [14, 15, 36]. Subsequent architectures extend this idea with multipole, Laplace, transformer, and geometry-aware operator designs [37–40]. Physics-informed neural operators further add PDE residuals or weak supervision to improve generalization when paired data are limited [16]. For inverse problems, deterministic neural solvers can be accurate when the target is a point estimate, but they do not by themselves represent the full posterior over plausible fields. For example, neural inverse operators directly learn maps from observations to unknown coefficients [41]. PosteriorBench treats these models both as components of generative solvers and as important baselines or surrogates.

**中文：** 神经算子与PDE逆问题求解器。物理信息神经网络和神经算子已成为学习PDE解映射、求解PDE约束逆问题的标准工具[13–15]。FNO和DeepONet等算子学习架构学习函数空间之间的映射，相较固定网格网络，能够更自然地跨离散化方式泛化[14, 15, 36]。后续架构通过多极、Laplace、Transformer和几何感知算子设计扩展了这一思路[37–40]。物理信息神经算子还加入PDE残差或弱监督，以在成对数据有限时提升泛化能力[16]。对于逆问题，当目标是点估计时，确定性神经求解器可能很准确，但它们本身不能表示合理场上的完整后验。例如，神经逆算子直接学习从观测到未知系数的映射[41]。PosteriorBench将这类模型既作为生成式求解器的组成部分，也作为重要基线或代理模型。

**Uncertainty-aware neural samplers.**

<span id="S179"></span>
**来源**：p.18 S179

**Original:** Uncertainty-aware neural samplers. Uncertainty-aware neural predictors provide another route to posterior sampling without training a full generative inverse model. Bayesian neural networks place distributions over network weights and infer a weight posterior, so predictive variation reflects model uncertainty [42]. MC dropout offers a scalable approximation by keeping dropout active at inference time and using repeated stochastic forward passes as approximate Bayesian predictions [43]. In scientific machine learning, Bayesian PDE solvers such as B-PINNs [44] and B-DeepONet [45] combine Bayesian neural networks or Bayesian operator learning with PDE constraints to quantify uncertainty in forward and inverse settings. These approaches are complementary to PosteriorBench, but their inverse examples typically recover single- or low-dimensional quantities, whereas PosteriorBench asks solvers to sample posteriors over 64 × 64 or 128 × 128 fields. This high dimensionality is one reason generative inverse samplers are attractive, and hence why the benchmark focuses on them. B-PINNs also require fitting a separate model for each observation case, which does not scale to the amortized multi-case evaluation used here.
