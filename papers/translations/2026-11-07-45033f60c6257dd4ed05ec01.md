<span id="S001-05"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-05

**Original:**

Abstract—In wideband extremely large-scale MIMO (XL-
MIMO) systems, the transmitter (TX) is often in the near field
of the base station array. The resulting spherical wavefront
makes the line-of-sight channel depend on both the TX angle
and distance. Existing polar-domain estimators sample both
parameters across the entire coverage region, requiring many
candidate positions and pilots. This paper proposes a vision-
aided framework that uses the object carrying the TX, as
seen in a camera image, to restrict the candidate positions to
a small grid on that object. An on-grid vision-aided coarse
localization (V-CL) algorithm obtains a coarse estimate from
the grid points that best fit the received pilots, and an off-grid
vision-aided Nelder-Mead refinement (V-NMR) algorithm refines
it over continuous angle and distance, from which the channel
is reconstructed. Simulations show that the proposed algorithms
achieve higher accuracy than both wideband and narrowband
polar-domain estimators while requiring far fewer pilots and
candidate positions.

**中文:**

## 摘要

在宽带极大规模 MIMO（XL-MIMO）系统中，发射机（TX）常常位于基站阵列的近场之内。由此形成的球面波前使视距信道同时依赖于 TX 的角度与距离。现有的极域（polar-domain）估计器在整个覆盖区域内对这两个参数进行采样，因而需要大量候选位置与导频。本文提出一种视觉辅助框架：利用相机图像中所看到的承载 TX 的物体，把候选位置限制在该物体上的一个小网格内。一种在网格上的视觉辅助粗定位（V-CL）算法，从与接收导频最匹配的网格点中得到粗估计；一种离网格的视觉辅助 Nelder-Mead 精化（V-NMR）算法，在连续的角度与距离上对其精化，并由此重建信道。仿真表明，所提算法在所需导频数与候选位置数都少得多的情况下，取得了比宽带与窄带极域估计器更高的精度。

In the far field, methods based on compressive sensing
can reduce the pilot overhead by exploiting angular-domain
sparsity [4], [5]. Specifically, the orthogonal matching pursuit

在远场，基于压缩感知的方法可以利用角域稀疏性来降低导频开销 [4], [5]。具体而言，正交匹配追踪（OMP）算法已被用于窄带信道 [4]，而同步 OMP（SOMP）技术则被用于宽带信道 [5]。

<span id="S001-10"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-10

**Original:**

In the near field, however, the energy of each signal path
spreads angularly, destroying the sparsity [2]. A polar-domain
SOMP (P-SOMP) that considers both angles and distances is
then more appropriate, along with off-grid refinements such as
the polar-domain simultaneous iterative gridless weighting (P-
SIGW) [2]. For wideband channels, bilinear pattern detection
(BPD) shifts the dictionary index across subcarriers [6], the
near-field beam-split-aware OMP (NBA-OMP) uses a separate
dictionary per subcarrier [7], and the segmented P-SIGW (St-
P-SIGW) applies P-SIGW separately to segments of subcarri-
ers [8]. Learning-based solutions are also emerging [9]. These
polar-domain methods, whether traditional or learning-based,
come at a steep computational cost. The dictionary must span
every combination of angle and distance across the coverage
region, whereby its size scales with the number of antennas
times the number of sampled distances, and the ensuing grid
is typically searched exhaustively.

**中文:**

然而在近场，每条信号路径的能量会在角度上扩散，从而破坏这种稀疏性 [2]。此时更合适的是同时考虑角度与距离的极域 SOMP（P-SOMP），以及离网格精化方法，例如极域同步无网格迭代加权（P-SIGW）[2]。对于宽带信道，双线性模式检测（BPD）在子载波之间平移字典索引 [6]，近场波束分裂感知 OMP（NBA-OMP）为每个子载波使用独立字典 [7]，分段 P-SIGW（St-P-SIGW）则把 P-SIGW 分别应用于各子载波段 [8]。基于学习的方案也在不断出现 [9]。这些极域方法，无论是传统方法还是基于学习的方法，都伴随高昂的计算代价：字典必须覆盖覆盖区域内角度与距离的所有组合，其规模随天线数乘以距离采样数而增大，而由此得到的网格通常需要穷举搜索。

VII. CONCLUSION

**中文:**

## VII. 结论

---

<span id="S006-09"></span>
**Source:** p.6 S006-09

**Original:**

This paper presents a vision-aided framework for near-field
channel estimation in wideband XL-MIMO systems, in which
a camera image confines the candidate TX positions to a
small grid on the object carrying the TX. On this grid, V-
CL provides an on-grid estimate and V-NMR refines it off the
grid. Under both the narrowband and wideband models, the
proposed algorithms outperform polar-domain benchmarks in
NMSE and pilot overhead, with V-NMR attaining the genie-
aided reference, while requiring far fewer grid atoms at a
lower or comparable runtime. Future work may address near-
field TX identification, imperfect 3D detection and camera
calibration, and multipath channels beyond the single LoS path
considered here.

**中文:**

本文提出了一种面向宽带 XL-MIMO 系统近场信道估计的视觉辅助框架：由一幅相机图像把候选 TX 位置限制在承载 TX 的物体上的一个小网格内。在该网格上，V-CL 给出在网格估计，V-NMR 再对其进行离网格精化。在窄带与宽带两种模型下，所提算法在 NMSE 与导频开销方面均优于极域基准方法，V-NMR 达到了 genie 辅助参照的水平，同时所需网格原子数少得多、运行时间更低或与之相当。未来工作可以研究近场 TX 识别、不完美的 3D 检测与相机标定，以及超出本文所考虑的单条 LoS 路径的多径信道。
