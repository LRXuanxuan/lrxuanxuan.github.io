**Original:** ABSTRACT

**中文:** 摘要

<span id="S004"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S004

**Original:** Consistency models (CMs) have become a leading approach for generating high-quality samples in few steps. However, adding steps can improve or degrade sample quality in ways that are highly sensitive to the schedule and that existing theory does not fully explain. To provide accuracy guarantees and guide CM sampler design, we analyze multistep CM sampling as a composition of noising and approximate denoising operators. Under explicit, verifiable stability assumptions, we derive a non-asymptotic error bound that separates contraction of the initialization error from accumulation of approximation error. The bound assigns distinct roles to the schedule: large early noise levels drive contraction, while small late noise levels control the residual bias. As a corollary, we obtain explicit constants for strongly log-concave and semi-log-concave targets. We further establish a complementary guarantee whose assumptions, one-step accuracy and stability, can be estimated for a given trained model. Experiments show that the contraction and approximation profiles entering our bounds can be reliably measured and closely match the predicted functional forms. Together, these results provide a meaningful convergence theory for multi-step CMs and a practical route to sampler design.

**中文:** 一致性模型（consistency models，CM）已成为一种主流方法，可用较少步数生成高质量样本。然而，增加采样步数可能提升也可能降低样本质量；这种变化对调度方案高度敏感，现有理论尚不能充分解释。为给出精度保证并指导CM采样器设计，我们将多步CM采样分析为加噪算子与近似去噪算子的复合。在明确且可验证的稳定性假设下，我们推导出一个非渐近误差界，将初始化误差的收缩与近似误差的累积分开刻画。该误差界揭示了调度方案中不同阶段的作用：早期较大的噪声水平推动误差收缩，后期较小的噪声水平则控制残余偏差。作为推论，我们为强对数凹和半对数凹目标分布给出显式常数。我们还建立了一个补充保证，其假设——单步精度与稳定性——可以针对给定的已训练模型进行估计。实验表明，进入误差界的收缩曲线和近似误差曲线可以可靠测量，且与理论预测的函数形式高度吻合。综上，这些结果为多步CM建立了有意义的收敛理论，并为采样器设计提供了可操作的途径。

**Original:** 1 INTRODUCTION

**中文:** 1 引言

<span id="S006"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S006

**Original:** Diffusion models (DMs) (Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Song & Ermon, 2019; Song et al., 2021; Ho et al., 2020) have revolutionized generative machine learning, achieving remarkable results in image (Ramesh et al., 2022; Rombach et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2023), video (Ho et al., 2022; Blattmann et al., 2023; Wan et al., 2025), 3D (Liu et al., 2023b; Poole et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023), and audio generation (Evans et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2023a). Early DMs required hundreds to thousands of neural function evaluations (NFEs) per sample. However, distillation substantially reduces this cost, such as direct (Luhman & Luhman, 2021; Zheng et al., 2022), adversarial (Wang et al., 2022; Sauer et al., 2024), or variational score distillation (Wang et al., 2023; Yin et al., 2024b;a; Luo et al., 2023b; Salimans et al., 2024). Within this context, consistency models (CMs) (Song et al., 2023; Song & Dhariwal, 2023; Kim et al., 2023) have emerged as particularly effective few-step DMs.

**中文:** 扩散模型（diffusion models，DM）（Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015；Song & Ermon, 2019；Song et al., 2021；Ho et al., 2020）推动了生成式机器学习的发展，并在图像生成（Ramesh et al., 2022；Rombach et al., 2022；Zhang et al., 2023）、视频生成（Ho et al., 2022；Blattmann et al., 2023；Wan et al., 2025）、三维生成（Liu et al., 2023b；Poole et al., 2022；Wang et al., 2023）和音频生成（Evans et al., 2024；Liu et al., 2023a）方面取得了显著成果。早期DM每生成一个样本需要数百至数千次神经网络函数评估（neural function evaluations，NFE）。不过，蒸馏显著降低了这一开销，例如直接蒸馏（Luhman & Luhman, 2021；Zheng et al., 2022）、对抗蒸馏（Wang et al., 2022；Sauer et al., 2024）或变分分数蒸馏（Wang et al., 2023；Yin et al., 2024b；Yin et al., 2024a；Luo et al., 2023b；Salimans et al., 2024）。在这一背景下，一致性模型（CM）（Song et al., 2023；Song & Dhariwal, 2023；Kim et al., 2023）作为尤其有效的少步数DM脱颖而出。

<span id="S007"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S007

**Original:** A CM learns a consistency function that maps noisy points along a probability-flow ODE trajectory directly to clean data. It can be distilled from a pre-trained DM (consistency distillation) or trained from scratch (consistency training).

**中文:** CM学习一个一致性函数，将概率流常微分方程（ODE）轨迹上的带噪点直接映射到干净数据。它既可以由预训练DM蒸馏得到（称为一致性蒸馏），也可以从头训练（称为一致性训练）。

<span id="S008"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S008

**Original:** Training CMs is nonetheless delicate. Discrete-time training introduces a discretization error that degrades sample quality, whereas naive continuous-time training is notoriously unstable (Song et al., 2023; Song & Dhariwal, 2023; Geng et al., 2024). sCM (Lu & Song, 2025) addressed this with TrigFlow, a formulation that greatly improves stability. A closely related line of work (Geng et al., 2025; Sabour et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025) learns flow maps (Boffi et al., 2025a), which transport samples between any two points of the trajectory and include CMs as an important special case.

**中文:** 不过，CM的训练并不简单。离散时间训练会引入离散化误差并降低样本质量，而朴素的连续时间训练则以不稳定著称（Song et al., 2023；Song & Dhariwal, 2023；Geng et al., 2024）。sCM（Lu & Song, 2025）通过TrigFlow解决了这一问题；该建模形式显著提升了稳定性。一个密切相关的研究方向（Geng et al., 2025；Sabour et al., 2025；Zhou et al., 2025）学习流映射（Boffi et al., 2025a），使样本能够在轨迹上任意两个点之间传输，并将CM作为一个重要特例包含在内。

**Original:** In principle, CMs can generate a sample with a single network evaluation, but practical samplers often apply the model several times to improve sample quality. Each iteration re-noises the current sample and maps it back to the target with the learned consistency function. Multistep CMs, however, are brittle: additional steps do not always help, sample quality is highly sensitive to the noise schedule, and stopping early sometimes yields better samples than continuing. Existing theory for multistep samplers is limited and offers little guidance to practitioners designing or deploying CMs.

**中文:** 原则上，CM只需一次网络评估即可生成样本，但实际采样器通常会多次调用模型以提升样本质量。每次迭代都先对当前样本重新加噪，再用学习到的一致性函数将其映射回目标分布。然而，多步CM的表现较为脆弱：增加步数并非总能带来收益，样本质量对噪声调度高度敏感，有时提前停止反而优于继续采样。关于多步采样器的现有理论有限，难以为CM的设计和部署提供充分指导。

<span id="S011"></span>
**Source:** p.2 S011

**Original:** Intuitively, multistep CM sampling trades off two competing effects. On the one hand, each additional step can reduce the discrepancy between the generated and target distributions. On the other hand, each step introduces a new error from model bias. The key question is thus whether the contraction gained at each step outweighs the error it introduces. This paper makes that balance explicit.

**中文:** 直观而言，多步CM采样需要权衡两种相互竞争的效应：一方面，每增加一步都可能缩小生成分布与目标分布之间的差异；另一方面，每一步也会引入由模型偏差导致的新误差。因此，关键问题是每一步带来的收缩是否超过其引入的误差。本文将这一权衡明确化。

**Source:** p.2 S012

Our main contribution is a non-asymptotic convergence theory for multistep CMs that explains how the schedule governs sample quality and provides a principled basis for sampler design. Specifically:

**中文:** 为此，我们研究交替对概率分布施加随机加噪算子与确定性去噪算子的多步采样器。本文的主要贡献是建立多步CM的非渐近收敛理论，说明调度方案如何影响样本质量，并为采样器设计提供有理论依据的指导。具体贡献如下：

**Original:** Relation to existing work.

<span id="S017"></span>

**Original:** Convergence theory for CM sampling is recent. Lyu et al. (2024) analyze one-step and multistep sampling under Ornstein–Uhlenbeck noising. Assuming that the learned map is Lipschitz and that the score and consistency training errors are small, they bound the Wasserstein error of the sampler and show that repeating a suitable noise level reduces this error geometrically, down to a plateau set by the training errors. Chen et al. (2025) derive multistep guarantees for bounded targets via a Kullback–Leibler recursion, and other work addresses statistical accuracy and consistency training (Dou et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025).

**中文:** CM采样的收敛理论是近年来才发展起来的。Lyu et al.（2024）研究了Ornstein–Uhlenbeck加噪下的一步与多步采样。在假设学习映射满足Lipschitz条件，且分数函数误差和一致性训练误差较小的前提下，他们给出采样器的Wasserstein误差界，并证明重复使用合适噪声水平可使该误差按几何速度下降，直至达到由训练误差决定的平台。Chen et al.（2025）通过Kullback–Leibler递推为有界目标推导多步保证；其他工作则研究统计精度和一致性训练（Dou et al., 2024；Li et al., 2025）。

<span id="S018"></span>
**Source:** p.2 S018

**Original:** We build on the error recursion of Lyu et al. (2024) but establish its ingredients under different assumptions. First, as they suggest, we place the stability condition required for contraction on the exact denoiser rather than on the learned network, and show that it holds under natural conditions on the target, such as semi-log-concavity. Second, we allow general Gaussian noising processes and arbitrary schedules. Third, we adopt a more realistic error model in which the score error may diverge as the noise level tends to zero (De Bortoli, 2022, Assumption A3), where noising no longer smooths the target, rather than remaining uniformly small. We make the correspondence between the two sets of results precise after Proposition 3.1, in Section 3.3, and in Appendix C.

**中文:** 本文以Lyu et al.（2024）的误差递推为基础，但在不同假设下建立其所需条件。首先，如该文所建议，我们将收缩所需的稳定性条件施加于精确去噪器，而非学习到的网络，并证明在目标分布满足半对数凹等自然条件时该条件成立。其次，我们允许一般的高斯加噪过程和任意调度。第三，我们采用更现实的误差模型：当噪声水平趋于零、加噪过程不再平滑目标分布时，分数函数误差可能发散（De Bortoli, 2022，假设A3），而非始终保持一致地较小。命题3.1之后、第3.3节和附录C中，我们将两组结果之间的对应关系明确化。

## p.3 <span id="page-3"></span>

<span id="S024"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S024

**Original:** 5 CONCLUSION

**中文:** 5 结论

<span id="S125"></span>
**Source:** p.9 S125

**Original:** This paper derived a finite-horizon W2 recursion for multistep CM sampling that separates contraction of the initialization error from accumulation of approximation error. Placing stability on the exact denoiser makes the contraction factor q_t a property of the target, explicit under strong log-concavity and, above a noise threshold, under semi-log-concavity. Under a balance condition, the recursion yields a sharp geometric bound, and a complementary guarantee extends it to trained CMs under estimable assumptions. The experiments corroborate this analysis, as the estimated profiles closely follow the predicted functional forms. Future work includes extending the analysis to zero-shot samplers with inference-time guidance or rewards (Spagnoletti et al., 2025), and optimizing schedules directly from the bound.

**中文:** 本文为多步CM采样推导了一个有限时域$W_2$递推，将初始化误差的收缩与近似误差的累积区分开来。将稳定性条件施加于精确去噪器后，收缩因子$q_t$成为目标分布自身的性质；在强对数凹条件下该因子具有显式形式，在半对数凹条件下则可在超过某一噪声阈值后获得收缩保证。在平衡条件下，该递推给出紧致的几何误差界；另一个补充保证还可在可估计的假设下推广到已训练CM。实验中估计得到的函数曲线与理论预测形式高度吻合，支持了本文分析。未来工作包括将分析推广到推理时引入引导或奖励的零样本采样器（Spagnoletti et al., 2025），以及直接根据误差界优化噪声调度。
