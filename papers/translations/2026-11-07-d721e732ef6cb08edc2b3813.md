**Original:**

Abstract—Existing generative model-based approaches to linear inverse problems typically use the acquired noisy measurement to condition or guide sampling in the image domain. In this work, we introduce Dual Flow, a novel joint flow model for solving linear inverse problems by jointly estimating the clean image and its corresponding noise-free measurement from the observation. Specifically, Dual Flow formulates image reconstruction and measurement denoising as a coupled estimation problem, using coupled flows in the image and measurement domains. The acquired noisy measurement y 0 is used only to initialize the measurement state; thereafter, the image and measurement states are jointly updated based on their current values. By construction, the final image and measurement estimates are guaranteed to satisfy the underlying forward model. We derive the sampling updates for the joint flow and show that they are governed by the conditional mean of the clean image given the current joint state. We further derive a closed-form expression for a sufficient statistic of the joint state and show that the required conditional mean can be obtained by solving an image-domain Gaussian denoising problem involving operator-dependent, correlated noise. We train a velocity-prediction network using this formulation to estimate the conditional mean from the derived sufficient statistic. Dual Flow requires no hyperparameter tuning beyond selecting the number of inference steps and achieves substantially shorter reconstruction times than competing methods. Extensive experiments across various linear inverse problems and image datasets demonstrate state-of-the-art performance in both distortion metrics and perceptual quality. Additional analyses assess robustness to model mismatch, as well as the ability to generate diverse plausible reconstructions and characterize reconstruction uncertainty. Overall, by jointly addressing image reconstruction and measurement denoising within a physically consistent framework, Dual Flow offers an effective approach to solving linear inverse problems with improved reconstruction quality and computational efficiency. Our code is available at https://github.com/METU-SPACE-Lab/Dual-Flow.

## 摘要

现有基于生成模型的线性反问题方法，通常利用所获取的含噪测量在图像域中对采样过程进行条件化或引导。在本工作中，我们提出 **Dual Flow**，一种用于求解线性反问题的新型联合流（joint flow）模型，它通过从观测中联合估计干净图像及其对应的无噪测量来求解问题。具体而言，Dual Flow 将图像重建与测量去噪表述为一个耦合估计问题，在图像域与测量域中使用耦合流。所获取的含噪测量 $y_0$ 仅用于初始化测量状态；此后，图像状态与测量状态基于它们当前的值进行联合更新。由构造方式可知，最终的图像估计与测量估计被保证满足底层前向模型。我们推导了联合流的采样更新，并表明这些更新由给定当前联合状态时干净图像的条件均值所支配。我们进一步推导了联合状态的一个充分统计量的闭式表达式，并表明所需的条件均值可通过求解一个图像域高斯去噪问题获得，该问题涉及与算子相关的、相关噪声。我们利用这一表述训练一个速度预测网络，以从所推导的充分统计量中估计条件均值。Dual Flow 除选择推理步数之外无需任何超参数调优，并且与竞争方法相比可实现显著更短的重建时间。在多种线性反问题与图像数据集上的大量实验表明，Dual Flow 在失真指标与感知质量两方面均达到当前最佳性能。额外的分析评估了其对模型失配的鲁棒性，以及生成多样化的合理重建并刻画重建不确定性的能力。总体而言，通过在一个物理一致的框架内联合处理图像重建与测量去噪，Dual Flow 为求解线性反问题提供了一条有效途径，兼具更好的重建质量与计算效率。代码见 https://github.com/METU-SPACE-Lab/Dual-Flow。

Generative models provide a powerful framework for learning complex image distributions from data and generating diverse plausible samples. In particular, diffusion models and flow matching have emerged as effective approaches for modeling high-dimensional image distributions [19]–[21]. Diffusion models generate new samples by reversing a gradual noise-corruption process, typically using learned score functions. Flow matching models, on the other hand, learn a time-dependent velocity field that transports samples from a simple source distribution to the target data distribution, often using straight-line interpolations between source and target samples during training [21], [22]. Both approaches can thus provide learned priors for image reconstruction. However, solving inverse problems requires incorporating the observed measurements into the generative process, rather than solely sampling from the learned image prior.

**中文:**

生成模型为从数据中学习复杂图像分布并生成多样化的合理样本提供了强大框架。特别地，扩散模型与流匹配已成为对高维图像分布建模的有效方法 [19]–[21]。扩散模型通过逆转一个渐进的噪声污染过程来生成新样本，通常使用学习到的得分函数；而流匹配模型则学习一个时变速度场，将样本从简单源分布输运到目标数据分布，训练时通常采用源样本与目标样本之间的直线插值 [21], [22]。因此，这两类方法都能为图像重建提供学习得到的先验。然而，求解反问题需要把观测到的测量纳入生成过程，而不是仅从学习到的图像先验中采样。

A major class of approaches for inverse problems addresses this by relying on pretrained unconditional generative models and incorporating measurement information only during inference. While these methods allow pretrained models to be reused across different inverse problems, accurately incorporating measurement information into the sampling process appears to be challenging. Many existing methods address this challenge through approximations of the likelihood score at intermediate stages to achieve posterior sampling [4], [23], [24], data-consistency corrections applied to the sampling process of a pretrained unconditional model [25]–[27], or Bayesian filtering techniques that employ weighted particles to approximate posterior sampling [28]. Despite their differences, these methods face limitations in posterior approximation accuracy and computational efficiency, as approximate measurement-conditioning strategies can lead to deviations from the true posterior [29], while Bayesian filtering methods can improve posterior approximation accuracy at the expense of increased computational cost.

In this work, we introduceDual Flow, a novel joint flow model over the image and measurement domains for solving linear inverse problems, which enables the joint estimation of the unknown image and the corresponding noise-free measurement. Specifically, under the forward model in Eq. (1), we define a joint image–measurement interpolation in which both the image and measurement states are defined through coupled straight-line interpolations. In particular, the image state is interpolated between an initial random sample and the clean imagex1, while the measurement state is interpolated between the acquired noisy measurementy 0 and the noise-free measurementAx1 of the final clean image. Thus, the two coupled flows evolve jointly, with the image-domain flow mapping the initial noise process to a clean image estimate and the measurement-domain flow denoising the acquired measurement. These coupled interpolations provide the basis for learning joint flow dynamics in the image and measurement domains.

**中文:**

在本工作中，我们提出 Dual Flow，一种在图像域与测量域上的新型联合流模型，用于求解线性反问题，它能够联合估计未知图像及其对应的无噪测量。具体而言，在式 (1) 的前向模型下，我们定义一种联合的图像–测量插值，其中图像状态与测量状态均由耦合的直线插值定义。特别地，图像状态在初始随机样本与干净图像 $x_1$ 之间插值，而测量状态在所获取的含噪测量 $y_0$ 与最终干净图像的无噪测量 $A x_1$ 之间插值。因此，这两个耦合流协同演化：图像域流将初始噪声过程映射为干净图像估计，测量域流对所获取的测量进行去噪。这些耦合插值为学习图像域与测量域中的联合流动力学提供了基础。

In our development, we combine the image and measurement states into a single variable, referred to as the joint state, and derive the sampling updates of this joint state for the reverse sampling procedure of Dual Flow. We show that these updates are governed by the conditional mean of the clean imagex 1 given the current joint state. To this end, we derive a closed-form sufficient statistic of the current joint state and show that estimating this conditional mean can be formulated as a denoising problem in which the clean imagex 1 is corrupted by operator-dependent correlated Gaussian noise. The derived sufficient statistic serves as the input to a velocity-prediction network, which is trained to obtain the conditional mean required for the joint-state sampling updates.

Specifically, Dual Flow-T generates a single reconstruction using𝑁 𝑒 steps (Δ =1/𝑁 𝑒), whereas Dual Flow-W averages𝐾independently generated reconstructions, each using𝑁 𝑒/𝐾steps (Δ =𝐾/𝑁 𝑒). Thus, both variants require a total of𝑁 𝑒 network evaluations. For CelebA, we use𝑁 𝑒 =18 and𝐾=6 for all inverse problems. For AFHQ-Cat, we use(𝑁 𝑒, 𝐾)=(40,20)for denoising,(40,10)for Gaussian deblurring,(40,40)for super-resolution, and(20,10)for both inpainting problems.

**中文:**

Dual Flow 的采样流程除推理步数 $N$ 之外没有可调超参数。由于每个反向采样步骤只需一次网络求值，我们考虑总网络求值次数（记为 $N_e$，它是运行时间的主导因素）相同的两个变体。这两个变体分别记为 Dual Flow-T 与 Dual Flow-W，其中 T 与 W 分别代表 Tall（高）与 Wide（宽）。具体地，Dual Flow-T 用 $N_e$ 步生成单次重建（$\Delta = 1/N_e$），而 Dual Flow-W 对 $K$ 次独立生成的重建取平均，每次使用 $N_e/K$ 步（$\Delta = K/N_e$）。因此，两个变体都需要总共 $N_e$ 次网络求值。对 CelebA，我们对所有反问题取 $N_e=18$、$K=6$。对 AFHQ-Cat，去噪取 $(N_e,K)=(40,20)$，高斯去模糊取 $(40,10)$，超分辨取 $(40,40)$，两个修复问题均取 $(20,10)$。

In this paper, we developed Dual Flow, a novel joint flow model for solving linear inverse problems by jointly estimating the clean image and its corresponding noise-free measurement.
