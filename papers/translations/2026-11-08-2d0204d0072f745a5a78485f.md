**Original:**

Abstract—Robust Principal Component Analysis (RPCA) is essential in signal processing, with applications in hyperspectral imaging, face recognition, and video background modeling. Traditional RPCA methods often suffer from insufficient utilization of data priors and struggle to capture complex image structures. Conversely, advanced deep denoisers like DnCNN, FFDNet, and DRUNet excel at leveraging image data priors but lack physical model constraints and demonstrate poor robustness against structured noise in RPCA tasks. This paper proposes a novel Plug-and-Play (PnP) method for multi-channel RPCA, integrating both the physical generative model and cutting-edge deep priors. Extensive experiments on RGB and hyperspectral datasets show that PnP-PDA achieves better quantitative performance than conventional RPCA methods and state-of-the-art methods, including deep denoisers and tensor RPCA approaches, while preserving visual details.

**中文:**

## 摘要

鲁棒主成分分析（robust principal component analysis, RPCA）在信号处理中至关重要，其应用包括高光谱成像、人脸识别与视频背景建模。传统 RPCA 方法往往存在数据先验利用不足的问题，且难以刻画复杂的图像结构。相反，DnCNN、FFDNet 与 DRUNet 等先进深度去噪器善于利用图像数据先验，但缺少物理模型约束，在 RPCA 任务中对结构化噪声的鲁棒性较差。本文提出一种面向多通道 RPCA 的新型即插即用（plug-and-play, PnP）方法，同时融合物理生成模型与前沿深度先验。在 RGB 与高光谱数据集上的大量实验表明，PnP-PDA 在定量性能上优于传统 RPCA 方法以及包括深度去噪器与张量 RPCA 方法在内的当前最佳方法，同时保留了视觉细节。

<span id="S001-06"></span>

**Original:**

ROBUST principal component analysis (RPCA) is a fundamental topic in signal processing, with numerous applications in hyperspectral imaging [1], robust face recognition [2], and video background modeling [3]. In RPCA, an image is typically decoupled into two components: a low-rank matrix representing the background, and a sparse object, which may be a target or structured noise. Based on the physical model, RPCA has been utilized for principal component pursuit (PCP) for matrix low-rank recovery [4], stable PCP (SPCP) for noise-corrupted observations [5], outlier pursuit for column-sparse corruptions [6], and multi-channel RPCA [7], [8]. However, conventional RPCA often makes limited use of data priors, and the single low-rank structure cannot capture the intricate prior details of the images.

**中文:**

鲁棒主成分分析（RPCA）是信号处理中的一个基础课题，在高光谱成像 [1]、鲁棒人脸识别 [2] 与视频背景建模 [3] 中有大量应用。在 RPCA 中，图像通常被分解为两个分量：表示背景的低秩矩阵，以及可能为目标或结构化噪声的稀疏目标。基于该物理模型，RPCA 已被用于面向矩阵低秩恢复的主成分追踪（principal component pursuit, PCP）[4]、面向含噪观测的稳定 PCP（SPCP）[5]、面向列稀疏污染的外点追踪 [6] 以及多通道 RPCA [7], [8]。然而，传统 RPCA 往往对数据先验的利用有限，且单一的低秩结构无法刻画图像中复杂的先验细节。

**Original:**

More recently, the trend of combining physical model methods with emerging deep priors has created a new paradigm for handling complex tasks in computational imaging. In this context, plug-and-play (PnP) and regularization by denoising (RED) have garnered increasing attention. For instance, PnP priors [11], [12] replace model-based proximal operators with pre-trained denoisers to recover fine textures. RED [13] constructs an explicit regularizer from the denoiser output, providing a variational interpretation that complements the implicit PnP formulation. While these approaches have been applied to various computational imaging tasks, including denoising, super-resolution, and deblurring, they have not yet been explored for the more challenging RPCA task.

**中文:**

更近期，将物理模型方法与新兴深度先验相结合的趋势，为处理计算成像中的复杂任务开创了新范式。在此背景下，即插即用（PnP）与去噪正则化（regularization by denoising, RED）受到越来越多的关注。例如，PnP 先验 [11], [12] 用预训练去噪器替换基于模型的近端算子以恢复精细纹理；RED [13] 由去噪器输出构造显式正则项，提供了与隐式 PnP 表述互补的变分解释。尽管这些方法已被应用于去噪、超分辨与去模糊等多种计算成像任务，但尚未被用于更具挑战性的 RPCA 任务。

<span id="S001-10"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-10

**Original:**

This letter focuses on leveraging the PnP framework for multi-channel RPCA, aiming to combine both physical models and deep priors. We propose an RPCA formulation that is implicitly regularized by data priors driven by deep denoisers. We apply the nonconvexℓ 2,p norm for the structured noise removal and Schatten-p norm for promoting the low-rank background for better separation. For the resulting problem, we introduce a PnP method embedded in a dedicated proximal distance algorithm (PDA). PnP-PDA decouples physical-model optimization and prior regularization, and uses Nesterov extrapolation for acceleration. We conducted extensive experiments on thirteen datasets, including RGB images and hyperspectral image (HSI) datasets. The results show that PnP-PDA achieves better recovery performance than conventional RPCA methods and the compared state-of-the-art deep denoisers and tensor RPCA approaches.

**中文:**

本文关注利用 PnP 框架处理多通道 RPCA，旨在同时结合物理模型与深度先验。我们提出一种由深度去噪器驱动的数据先验所隐式正则化的 RPCA 表述。我们采用非凸 $\ell_{2,p}$ 范数进行结构化噪声去除，并用 Schatten-$p$ 范数促进低秩背景以获得更好的分离效果。针对所得问题，我们引入一种嵌入专用近端距离算法（proximal distance algorithm, PDA）的 PnP 方法。PnP-PDA 将物理模型优化与先验正则化解耦，并使用 Nesterov 外推进行加速。我们在包括 RGB 图像与高光谱图像（HSI）数据集的十三个数据集上开展了大量实验。结果表明，PnP-PDA 的恢复性能优于传统 RPCA 方法，以及所比较的当前最佳深度去噪器与张量 RPCA 方法。

This letter presents a PnP method for RPCA that integrates both the physical model and deep priors. Our proposed PnP-PDA algorithm transforms the problem into subproblems for evaluating proximal operators, utilizing closed-form updates and deep denoiser processing.
