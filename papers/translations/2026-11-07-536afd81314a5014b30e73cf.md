ABSTRACT
Aerial networks require radio propagation information at unvis-
ited positions and heights to plan routes, select links, and allocate
wireless resources. A three-dimensional radio map (3D RM) rep-
resents propagation conditions over the service area, enabling these
decisions before flight. However, constructing such a map from
autonomous aerial vehicle (AA V) measurements is challenging,
since radio measurements are available only along the trajectory,
whereas the target covers the 3D service volume. Existing methods
use fixed AA V trajectories that ignore how building distributions
and heights affect electromagnetic propagation, missing the heights
most informative for RM reconstruction. To address these issues,
we propose RadioTraj3D, which uses building-height information
to coordinate measurement selection and layer-wise reconstruction.
Building-height variations reveal propagation-sensitive layers that
guide height-adaptive sampling. We therefore propose G-METS
to select geometry-sensitive heights and allocate an exact budget
across edge, corridor, and open regions before channel acquisition.
A dual-view reconstructor then integrates local three-dimensional
boundaries with full-height context through one bounded coefficient
per layer. Simulated outdoor and indoor maps demonstrate state-
of-the-art reconstruction across environments and vertical grids.
On real-world AA V measurements from the LACN dataset, Radio-
Traj3D improves peak signal-to-noise ratio by 0.84 dB and reduces
normalized mean squared error by 17.5% over Completion AE.

**中文:**

## 摘要

空中网络需要获取未访问位置与高度上的无线电传播信息，以便规划航路、选择链路并分配无线资源。三维无线电地图（three-dimensional radio map, 3D RM）刻画了服务区域内的传播条件，使这些决策能在飞行之前完成。然而，仅凭自主飞行器（autonomous aerial vehicle, AAV）的测量来构建这样的地图颇具挑战：无线电测量只能沿轨迹获得，而目标覆盖的却是三维服务体积。现有方法使用固定的 AAV 轨迹，忽略了建筑分布与高度如何影响电磁传播，从而遗漏了对 RM 重建最具信息量的高度。针对这些问题，我们提出 RadioTraj3D，它利用建筑高度信息来协调测量选择与逐层重建。建筑高度变化揭示了传播敏感层，可引导高度自适应采样。为此我们提出 G-METS，在信道采集之前选择几何敏感高度，并在边缘、走廊与开阔区域之间分配精确预算。随后由双视图重建器通过每层一个有界系数，把局部三维边界与全高度上下文融合起来。在仿真室外与室内地图上，该方法在不同环境和竖直网格下均展示了最先进的重建性能。在来自 LACN 数据集的真实 AAV 测量上，RadioTraj3D 相比 Completion AE 将峰值信噪比提升 0.84 dB，并将归一化均方误差降低 17.5%。

location require radio propagation information at positions and
heights not visited by an autonomous aerial vehicle (AA V). A

<span id="S001-09"></span>

**Original:**

Full 3D reconstruction from trajectory-constrained radio measure-
ments faces the dual challenges of selecting informative locations
before flight and recovering unobserved heights after collection. Be-
fore collection, locations must form a collision-free path and meet
an exact budget without known propagation patterns. Fixed trajecto-
ries and heights ignore building-height variations, so they can miss
propagation-sensitive layers and major blockage changes [3, 5, 6].
Random masks also create layouts infeasible for continuous AA V
paths [4]. The resulting sparse measurements must capture local
blockage and vertical variation across the unobserved volume. Their
locations therefore govern spatial coverage and vertical observabil-
ity. Planar estimators reconstruct fixed-height maps [7, 8], 3D meth-
ods require transmitter information [5,6], and trajectory-aware meth-
ods do not recover the complete 3D RM [2–4]. Consequently, exist-
ing methods cannot jointly select informative locations before flight
and reconstruct unobserved heights without transmitter information.

**中文:**

由轨迹受限的无线电测量完成完整三维重建，面临双重挑战：飞行前选择有信息量的位置，以及采集后恢复未观测的高度。在采集之前，位置必须构成无碰撞路径，并在传播模式未知的条件下满足精确预算。固定的轨迹与高度忽略了建筑高度变化，因此可能遗漏传播敏感层以及主要的遮挡变化 [3, 5, 6]。随机掩码还会产生连续 AAV 路径无法实现的布局 [4]。由此得到的稀疏测量必须刻画未观测体积内的局部遮挡与竖直变化，因此这些测量位置决定了空间覆盖与竖直可观测性。平面估计器重建固定高度的地图 [7, 8]，三维方法需要发射机信息 [5, 6]，而轨迹感知方法无法恢复完整的三维 RM [2–4]。因此，现有方法既不能在飞行前共同选择有信息量的位置，也不能在无发射机信息的情况下重建未观测高度。
