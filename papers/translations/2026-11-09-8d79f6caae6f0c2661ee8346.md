**Original:**

Abstract—Learned schemes that jointly perform tasks such as
synchronization and decoding have achieved promising results
for short-packet communication. However, it remains unclear
how close their performance is to information-theoretic bounds.
To investigate this question, we extend the turbo autoencoder
(TurboAE) proposed in D ¨orner et al. (2023) to a single-input
single-output waveform channel with an unknown delay and
memoryless block fading, for which an achievability bound from
finite-blocklength information theory is available. Our extension
incorporates a pilot-free, model-guided neural synchronizer and
enables joint synchronization, channel estimation, and decoding.
At a blocklength of64complex symbols and a rate of one bit
per channel use, the proposed TurboAE achieves a lower bit
error rate (BER) than all evaluated pilot-assisted 5G baselines,
with gains of1.9 dBat a BER of10 −3 over a baseline involving
a low-density parity-check (LDPC) code and1.7 dBover a
baseline involving a polar code and successive cancellation list
decoding (list size8). In terms of block error rate (BLER), the
TurboAE outperforms the LDPC-coded baseline, but lags behind
the polar-coded baseline by about0.8 dBat a BLER of10 −3.
Moreover, all evaluated schemes remain at least2.1 dBaway
from a pilot-assisted achievability bound, indicating substantial
room for improved designs.

## 摘要

联合执行同步与译码等任务的学习型方案在短包通信中已取得令人期待的结果。然而，其性能与信息论界之间究竟相差多远仍不清楚。为研究这一问题，我们把 Dörner 等（2023）提出的涡轮自编码器（TurboAE）推广到具有未知时延与无记忆块衰落的单输入单输出波形信道；针对该信道，有限块长信息论给出了一个可达界。我们的推广引入了一个无导频、模型引导的神经网络同步器，从而实现联合同步、信道估计与译码。在块长为 64 个复符号、速率为每信道使用 1 比特时，所提 TurboAE 的比特错误率（BER）低于所有评估的导频辅助 5G 基线：在 BER 为 $10^{-3}$ 时，相比包含低密度奇偶校验（LDPC）码的基线有 1.9 dB 增益，相比包含极化码与列表长度 8 的串行抵消列表译码的基线有 1.7 dB 增益。在块错误率（BLER）方面，TurboAE 优于 LDPC 编码基线，但在 BLER 为 $10^{-3}$ 时落后于极化码编码基线约 0.8 dB。此外，所有评估方案与导频辅助可达界之间均至少相差 2.1 dB，表明设计仍有可观的改进空间。

**Original:**

Modern coding schemes, such as low-density parity-check
(LDPC) and polar codes [1], [2], achieve excellent error-
correction performance at long blocklengths. However, their
performance degrades significantly in the short-blocklength
regime, for two main reasons. First, short blocklengths limit
the ability of these codes to fully exploit their structural
properties. Second, when the blocklength is short, the finite
symbol budget must be shared among synchronization, chan-
nel estimation, and data transmission. This introduces a critical
trade-off between pilot symbols and payload in conventional
pilot-assisted communication systems.

**中文:**

现代编码方案，例如低密度奇偶校验（LDPC）码与极化码 [1], [2]，在长块长下具有优异的纠错性能。然而，在短块长情形下其性能显著下降，主要有两个原因。第一，短块长限制了这些码充分发挥其结构特性的能力。第二，当块长较短时，有限的符号预算必须在同步、信道估计与数据传输之间分配，这在传统导频辅助通信系统中引入了导频符号与有效载荷之间的关键折中。

One promising approach to optimize the transmitter and
receiver for short-packet communication is end-to-end learning
using autoencoders [6]. However, the original autoencoder
