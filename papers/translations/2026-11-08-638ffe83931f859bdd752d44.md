Abstract
Bayesian inverse problems require both informative prior models and computationally
feasible posterior inference. Score-based diffusion models provide expressive learned priors,
but their use for inversion introduces a conditioning problem: the score of a noised posterior
is generally unavailable from a prior score alone. This selective survey reviews the principal
approaches to that problem, including likelihood guidance, spectral and projection methods,
sequential Monte Carlo corrections, and amortized conditional training. We organize methods
by how they split cost between offline preparation and online inference, and compare their
assumptions, advantages and limitations, with emphasis on computational speedups. Some
ongoing directions and open questions are gathered at the end.

**中文:**

## 摘要

贝叶斯反问题既需要信息量充足的先验模型，也需要在计算上可行的后验推断。基于分数的扩散模型（score-based diffusion models）能够提供表达力强的学习型先验，但将其用于反演会引入一个条件化问题：加噪后验的分数一般无法仅由先验分数得到。本选择性综述回顾了处理该问题的主要方法，包括似然引导、谱方法与投影方法、序贯蒙特卡洛校正，以及摊销式条件训练。我们按各方法在离线准备与在线推断之间如何拆分计算代价来组织这些方法，并比较它们的假设、优势与局限，重点关注计算加速。文末汇集了一些进行中的方向与开放问题。

1.1 Infinite-dimensional Bayesian inverse problems

**中文:**

### 1.1 无穷维贝叶斯反问题

---

<span id="S001-08"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-08

**Original:**

Inverse problems deal with the task of recovering a state, coefficient or source from indirect
observations. Examples include reconstructing internal body structures from X-ray measurements
in CT imaging and identifying coefficients from observations of the solution of a partial differential
equation (PDE). Due to theill-posednature (nonexistence, nonuniqueness and instability) of
inverse problems, one often requires additional information. Bayesian inversion represents this
information by apriormeasure and conditions it on observations to obtain aposterior[30]. Here
we adopt an infinite-dimensional framework that allows the unknown to live in, say, a Hilbert
space. This permits well-posedness and approximation to be investigated before discretization,
which encourages developments of discretization-invariant algorithms whose convergence can be
studied under mesh refinement.

**中文:**

反问题关注从间接观测中恢复状态、系数或源的任务，例如在 CT 成像中由 X 射线测量重建人体内部结构，或由偏微分方程（PDE）解的观测识别系数。由于反问题具有不适定性（ill-posed，即解可能不存在、不唯一且不稳定），通常需要引入额外信息。贝叶斯反演用先验测度（prior measure）表示这类信息，并以观测为条件得到后验（posterior）[30]。这里采用无穷维框架，允许未知量取值于（例如）Hilbert 空间。这样可以在离散化之前研究适定性与逼近性质，从而推动离散化不变算法的发展，使其收敛性可在网格加密下进行研究。

2.1 Reusing pretrained prior models

**中文:**

### 2.1 复用预训练先验模型

---

<span id="S003-02"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S003-02

**Original:**

These methods reuse a pretrained prior model and incorporate the observation model during
inference. Diffusion posterior sampling (DPS) [5] approximates the intermediate likelihood using
a denoised estimateˆx0(x,t ) = E[X0|Xt =x]. For Gaussian observations, the resulting guidance
is based on−∇xΦ(ˆx0(x,t );y). This accommodates differentiable nonlinear maps and, with other
likelihoods, non-Gaussian noise, without joint training on parameter–observation pairs. However,
guidance requires differentiating through the denoiser and forward model and typically needs
tuning. Evaluating a likelihood at the denoising mean remains an approximation even with an
exact prior score.

**中文:**

这类方法复用预训练的先验模型，并在推断阶段引入观测模型。扩散后验采样（diffusion posterior sampling, DPS）[5] 利用去噪估计 $\hat{x}_0(x,t) = \mathbb{E}[X_0 \mid X_t = x]$ 近似中间似然。对高斯观测，所得引导项基于 $-\nabla_x \Phi(\hat{x}_0(x,t);y)$。该方法可容纳可微的非线性映射，配合其他似然也可处理非高斯噪声，且无需在参数—观测对上联合训练。然而，引导需要对去噪器与前向模型求导，通常还需要调参。即便先验分数精确，在去噪均值处评估似然仍是一种近似。

<span id="S003-03"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S003-03

**Original:**

Tweedie moment projected diffusions (TMPD) [3] retain denoising covariances as well as
means. Moment-based approximations toX0|Xt =x produce tractable intermediate likelihoods
for linear-Gaussian observations, accounting for conditional spread at additional derivative or
covariance cost. For non-Gaussian priors, the resulting moment approximation generally retains
conditioning bias. Feynman–Kac analysis [10] characterizes bias in DPS and related guidance
even with exact prior scores, and analyses strong-guidance discretization instabilities.

**中文:**

Tweedie 矩投影扩散（Tweedie moment projected diffusions, TMPD）[3] 同时保留去噪的协方差与均值。对 $X_0 \mid X_t = x$ 的矩近似为线性—高斯观测给出可处理的中间似然，并以额外的导数或协方差代价刻画条件分布的离散程度。对非高斯先验，所得的矩近似一般仍带有条件化偏差。Feynman–Kac 分析 [10] 刻画了 DPS 及相关引导方法在先验分数精确时的偏差，并分析了强引导下的离散化不稳定性。

<span id="S003-04"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S003-04

**Original:**

Denoising diffusion restoration models (DDRM) [19] exploit linear structure through noise-
aware updates in singular-vector coordinates. They enable efficient restoration with reusable
priors and few denoising steps, but require tractable spectral representations. Denoising diffusion
null-space models (DDNM) [31] use range–null-space decompositions to enforce data consistency
while the prior determines unobserved components. Both exploit operator structure, but
reconstruction quality or noiseless consistency does not establish calibrated posterior sampling.

**中文:**

去噪扩散恢复模型（denoising diffusion restoration models, DDRM）[19] 通过在奇异向量坐标中的噪声感知更新来利用线性结构。它们能以可复用的先验和少量去噪步实现高效恢复，但要求谱表示易于处理。去噪扩散零空间模型（denoising diffusion null-space models, DDNM）[31] 使用值域—零空间分解来强制数据一致性，同时由先验决定未观测分量。两者都利用了算子结构，但重建质量或无噪声一致性并不能确立经过校准的后验采样。

**Original:**

The same pretrained priors can also be conditioned using sequential Monte Carlo (SMC),
which approximates intermediate distributions with weighted particles, resampling and mutation.
MCGDiff [4] constructs intermediate linear problems whose posteriors approach the target as
diffusion noise decreases. Filtering posterior sampling [11] relates conditioning to Bayesian
filtering and obtains asymptotically accurate inference via SMC. Decoupled diffusion SMC

**中文:**

同样的预训练先验也可借助序贯蒙特卡洛（sequential Monte Carlo, SMC）进行条件化：SMC 用加权粒子、重采样与变异（mutation）来近似中间分布。MCGDiff [4] 构造一系列中间线性问题，其后验随扩散噪声减小而逼近目标后验。滤波式后验采样（filtering posterior sampling）[11] 将条件化与贝叶斯滤波联系起来，并通过 SMC 获得渐近准确的推断。解耦扩散 SMC（decoupled diffusion SMC, DDSMC）[14] 允许更大的生成式更新，并对线性—高斯观测是渐近精确的。

### 2.2 学习后验条件化

---

<span id="S003-08"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S003-08

**Original:**

Conditional training moves the incorporation of observations into the learning stage. A network
sθ(x,t,y )trained on joint samples of(X,Y )learns the noised posterior score and can generate
samples without repeated forward or adjoint evaluations. Baldassari et al. [2] justify conditional
denoising estimation for infinite-dimensional linear inverse problems, including its small-time
behaviour. The online savings require representative joint training data. Reuse across different
operators, noise statistics or sensor geometries requires those settings to be covered by training
