Jiabao Gao、Xiaoming Chen、Geoffrey Ye Li · 2024 · Frontiers of Information Technology & Electronic Engineering。正式发表。出版方登记的卷页或文章号 1162-1172。[发表证据](https://doi.org/10.1631/fitee.2300760)
