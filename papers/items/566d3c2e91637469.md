摘要原文摘录：
Ill-posed inverse problems are fundamental in many domains, ranging from astrophysics to medical imaging. Emerging diffusion models provide a powerful prior for solving these problems. Existing maximum-a-posteriori (MAP) or poste- rior sampling approaches, however , rely on different compu- tational approximations, leading to inaccurate or suboptimal samples. To address this issue, we introduce a new approach to solving MAP problems with diffusion model priors us- ing a dual ascent optimization framework. Our framework achieves better image quality as measured by various met- rics for image restoration problems, it is more robust to high levels of measurement noise, it is faster , and it estimates solutions that represent the observations more faithfully than the state of the art.
