摘要原文摘录：
We propose Functional Flow Matching (FFM), a function space generative model that gen- eralizes the recently introduced Flow Match- ing model to operate in infinite-dimensional spaces. Our approach works by first defining a path of probability measures that interpo- lates between a fixed Gaussian measure and the data distribution, followed by learning a vector field on the underlying space of func- tions that generates this path of measures. Our method does not rely on likelihoods or simulations, making it well-suited to the func- tion space setting. We provide both a the- oretical framework for building such models and an empirical evaluation of our techniques. We demonstrate through experiments on sev- eral real-world benchmarks that our proposed FFM method outperforms several recently proposed function space generative models.
