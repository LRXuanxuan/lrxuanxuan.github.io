<section class="stapledon-body" data-story-lang="zh" lang="zh-CN" aria-label="中文译文">
<p>当我们的银河系终于能够通过心灵感应对由无数银河系组成的整个宇宙进行完整探索时，它发现宇宙中的生命状态十分岌岌可危。如今仍处于青年期的银河系已经寥寥无几，绝大多数都早已越过壮年。在整个宇宙中，死去而不发光的恒星数量远远超过仍然活着、仍然明亮的恒星。在许多银河系里，恒星与世界之间的战争甚至比我们自己的银河系更加惨烈；双方往往要一直退化到根本没有恢复希望之后，和平才终于出现。不过，在多数较年轻的银河系中，这种冲突还没有发生；而最觉醒的一些银河精神已经开始行动，希望趁那些无知的恒星社会和行星社会尚未误入战争以前，先让双方认识彼此。</p>
<p>我们银河系的共同精神如今加入了宇宙中最觉醒生命组成的小小群体——那是一支分散各处的先进银河精神队伍。它们的目标是创造一个真正的宇宙共同体，形成一个单一心灵；这个心灵将成为无数而多样的世界以及无数单独智慧生命共同拥有的精神。人们希望，通过这种方式能够获得在单纯银河层面根本不可能达到的洞察能力与创造能力。</p>
<p>带着庄严的喜悦，我们这些宇宙探索者——此时早已被吸收进自己银河系的共同心灵——发现自己如今又与大约二十个其他银河心灵紧密结合。我们，或者更准确地说，“我”，如今体验各个银河系缓慢漂移时，就像一个人感受自己四肢摆动一样自然。从大约二十个视点上，我观察数百万银河系组成的巨大暴风雪：它们流动、旋转，并随着空间那无情的“膨胀”不断彼此远离。可是，虽然空间相对于银河、恒星与世界的尺度正在不断增大，对拥有这个分散复合身体的我而言，整个空间似乎也不过是一座巨大的穹顶大厅。</p>
<p>我的时间经验同样发生了变化。就像此前某一次一样，亿万年的漫长时代对我而言如今只像几分钟。我不再把整个宇宙的生命理解成一段极其漫长、从遥远而模糊的源头悠闲前往辉煌且更加遥远永恒的旅程；相反，它变成了一场短促、疯狂向前而又近乎绝望的赛跑——我们必须和飞奔的时间竞争。</p>
<p>面对数量如此庞大的落后银河系，我觉得自己像一片野蛮人和野兽荒原中唯一孤独的智慧。存在的神秘、徒劳与恐怖，此刻以最残酷的方式压在我身上。因为对我——也就是那小群觉醒银河系共同形成的精神——来说，在宇宙最后的日子里，我们被那些尚未觉醒而且注定毁灭的大群银河系包围，再也看不到任何“别处”还可能出现胜利的希望。因为对我而言，存在似乎已经显露出全部范围。根本不存在“别处”。我精确知道宇宙物质的总量。虽然空间的“膨胀”已经让绝大多数银河系彼此远离得比光跨越鸿沟还快，心灵感应探索仍使我与整个宇宙的全部范围保持联系。甚至我自身许多成员之间，也已经被永不停息的“膨胀”制造出的不可跨越鸿沟从物理上彻底隔开；然而在心灵感应中，它们仍保持统一。</p>
<p>我——大约二十个银河系组成的共同心灵——如今觉得自己就是宇宙本身那个流产了、残缺不全的心灵。支撑我的这个由无数层级共同体组成的共同体，本来理应继续扩展，直到把全部存在纳入其中。在宇宙历史的高潮处，完全觉醒的宇宙心灵本来理应终于达到知识与崇拜的圆满。然而，这件事不会发生。因为即使现在，宇宙已经进入晚期，物理潜能几乎全部耗尽，我也只发展到极其低微的精神层次。我的心智仍不过处于青春期，而我的宇宙身体却已经开始腐烂。我是宇宙之卵里挣扎着发育的胚胎，可蛋黄已经先一步败坏。</p>
<p>沿着亿万年的时间长廊回望时，真正令我震动的，与其说是把我带到当前状态的旅程有多漫长，不如说它竟如此匆忙、混乱，甚至如此短促。我竭力窥向最早的年代——恒星尚未诞生、星云甚至尚未从混沌中形成的年代——仍然无法看见任何清晰的源头，只能面对一种和地球上那些微小居民所面对的谜团同样晦暗的神秘。</p>
<p>同样，当我试图探究自身存在的深处时，所发现的仍然是无法穿透的神秘。我的自我意识已经苏醒到比人类自我意识高出整整三个层次：从单一个体上升为世界心灵，从世界心灵上升为银河心灵，再从银河心灵上升到这个发育失败的宇宙心灵；然而，我自身本性的深处依旧一片晦暗。</p>
<p>虽然我的心灵如今已经把一切时代所有世界的全部智慧汇入自身；虽然我的宇宙身体的生命本身，就是无数彼此差异无限的世界与无数彼此差异无限的单独生命共同构成的生命；虽然我的日常生活纹理充满喜悦而富有创造性的事业——但这一切仍然等于什么也没有。因为周围仍遍布那些未能实现自身可能性的银河系；我自己的肉体也已经因为恒星大量死亡而严重贫乏；而一个又一个时代正以致命速度从身边滑过。很快，我这颗宇宙大脑的结构必然开始瓦解。随后，我也必然从自己如此珍惜、却仍不完善的清醒状态中跌落，经过心灵第二童年的所有阶段，一路下降，直至宇宙性的死亡。</p>
<p>这实在奇异至极：我明明知道空间与时间的全部范围，能像数羊一样清点所有漂泊的恒星，一颗也不会遗漏；我明明是一切生命中觉醒程度最高的存在，是无数生命在所有时代献出性命才建立起来的荣耀，又曾被无数生命崇拜；可如今，当我环顾自身周围时，心中涌起的竟仍然是和人类旅人置身荒漠、仰望群星时完全一样的压倒性敬畏，完全一样的羞惭、失语与崇拜。</p>
</section>
<section class="stapledon-body" data-story-lang="en" lang="en" aria-label="English original" hidden>
<h3>XII. A STUNTED COSMICAL SPIRIT</h3>
<p>WHEN AT LAST our galaxy was able to make a full telepathic exploration of the cosmos of galaxies it discovered that the state of life in the cosmos was precarious. Very few of the galaxies were now in their youth; most were already far past their prime. Throughout the cosmos the dead and lightless stars far outnumbered the living and luminous. In many galaxies the strife of stars and worlds had been even more disastrous than in our own. Peace had been secured only after both sides had degenerated past hope of recovery. In most of the younger galaxies, however, this strife had not yet appeared; and efforts were already being made by the most awakened galactic spirits to enlighten the ignorant stellar and planetary societies about one another before they should blunder into conflict.</p>
<p>The communal spirit of our galaxy now joined the little company of the most awakened beings of the cosmos, the scattered band of advanced galactic spirits, whose aim it was to create a real cosmical community, with a single mind, the communal spirit of its myriad and diverse worlds and individual intelligences. Thus it was hoped to acquire powers of insight and of creativity impossible on the merely galactic plane.</p>
<p>With grave joy we, the cosmical explorers, who were already gathered up into the communal mind of our own galaxy, now found ourselves in intimate union with a score of other galactic minds. We, or rather I, now experienced the slow drift of the galaxies much as a man feels the swing of his own limbs. From my score of view-points I observed the great snow-storm of many million galaxies, streaming and circling, and ever withdrawing farther apart from one another with the relentless ‘expansion’ of space. But though the vastness of space was increasing in relation to the size of galaxies and stars and worlds, to me, with my composite, scattered body, space seemed no bigger than a great vaulted hall.</p>
<p>My experience of time also had changed; for now, as on an earlier occasion, the aeons had become for me as brief as minutes. I conceived the whole life of the cosmos not as an immensely protracted and leisurely passage from a remote and shadowy source to a glorious and a still more remote eternity, but as a brief, a headlong and forlorn, race against galloping time.</p>
<p>Confronted by the many backward galaxies, I seemed to myself to be a lonely intelligence in a wilderness of barbarians and beasts. The mystery, the futility, the horror of existence now bore down upon me most cruelly. For to me, to the spirit of that little band of awakened galaxies, surrounded by unawakened and doomed hordes in the last day of the cosmos, there appeared no hope of any triumph elsewhere. For to me the whole extent, seemingly, of existence was revealed. There could be no ‘elsewhere’. I knew with exactness the sum of cosmical matter. And though the ‘expansion’ of space was already sweeping most of the galaxies apart more swiftly than light could bridge the gulf, telepathic exploration still kept me in touch with the whole extent of the cosmos. Many of my own members were physically divided from one another by the insurmountable gulf created by the ceaseless ‘expansion’; but telepathically they were still united.</p>
<p>I, the communal mind of a score of galaxies, seemed now to myself to be the abortive and crippled mind of the cosmos itself. The myriadfold community that supported me ought surely to have expanded to embrace the whole of existence. In the climax of cosmical history the fully awakened mind of the cosmos ought surely to have won through to the fulness of knowledge and of worship. But this was not to be. For even now, in the late phase of the cosmos, when the physical potency was almost all exhausted, I had reached only to a lowly state of spiritual growth. I was mentally still adolescent, yet my cosmical body was already in decay. I was the struggling embryo in the cosmical egg, and the yolk was already in decay.</p>
<p>Looking back along the vistas of the aeons, I was impressed less by the length of the journey that had led me to my present state than by its haste and confusion, and even its brevity. Peering into the very earliest of the ages, before the stars were born, before the nebulae were formed from chaos, I still failed to see any clear source, but only a mystery as obscure as any that confronts the little inhabitants of the Earth.</p>
<p>Equally, when I tried to probe the depths of my own being, I found impenetrable mystery. Though my self-consciousness was awakened to a degree thrice removed beyond the self-consciousness of human beings, namely from the simple individual to the world-mind, and from the world-mind to the galactic mind, and thence to the abortively cosmical, yet the depth of my nature was obscure.</p>
<p>Although my mind now gathered into itself all the wisdom of all worlds in all ages, and though the life of my cosmical body was itself the life of myriads of infinitely diverse worlds and myriads of infinitely diverse individual creatures, and though the texture of my daily life was one of joyful and creative enterprise, yet all this was as nothing. For around lay the host of the unfulfilled galaxies; and my own flesh was already grievously impoverished by the death of my stars; and the aeons were slipping past with fatal speed. Soon the texture of my cosmical brain must disintegrate. And then inevitably I must fall away from my prized though imperfect state of lucidity, and descend, through all the stages of the mind’s second childhood, down to the cosmical death.</p>
<p>It was very strange that I, who knew the whole extent of space and time, and counted the wandering stars like sheep, overlooking none, that I who was the most awakened of all beings, I, the glory which myriads in all ages had given their lives to establish, and myriads had worshipped, should now look about me with the same overpowering awe, the same abashed and tongue-tied worship as that which human travellers in the desert feel under the stars.</p>
</section>
