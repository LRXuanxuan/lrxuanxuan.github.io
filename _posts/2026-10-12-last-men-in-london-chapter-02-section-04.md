<h2>iv. 悬浮于时间之上</h2>
<p>现在，我必须更准确地让你们了解这样一种奇异经验：当探索者试图在某个过去心灵的某个特定瞬间取得立足点时，究竟会发生什么。每个探索者都有自己的技巧，每次冒险也都有自己的曲折；这里我只讲那一次我接近你们的世界和时代时，自己所采用的方式。那段经历，我前面已经开始叙述。</p>
<p>从那个不可言说的瞬间——我曾在其中参与永恒视角——重新浮现出来时，最初占据意识的是一种模糊的不安，一种极尖锐却没有明确对象的渴望；与此同时，我的全部感官都陷入猛烈而混乱的骚动。虽然这并不是我第一次进入这些领域，我仍被人类那种永远无法满足、盲目摸索的欲望所具有的悲怆感压倒；同样压倒我的，还有他那稀少欢乐所施加的暴政，以及各种痛苦带来的折磨。起初，这种渴望和感官骚动仿佛就是我的全部存在；但我努力使自己苏醒到更明确的状态。我把那团晦暗的东西推到自己面前，冷静观察。于是，它就在我心灵的“眼睛”面前展开，变成一股由具体的原始欲望与激情组成的洪流。那些欲望我既像自己的欲望一样亲身经历，又仿佛从极高而疏离的位置俯视。</p>
<p>此刻，我正在经历整个人类全部经验的总和与混合。它们来到我这里时，像一张叠印了无数人的合成照片那样模糊；但同时，我又能感到其中确实存在着无数个体、无数不可替代的独特性——就像有时听见一大群人的低语，会在那片模糊声浪中忽然感觉到“这里有许许多多个别人”。然而，这些无数经验又以某种方式全都是“我的”，并且合并成一个经验，就像人的两只眼睛最终只给出一个统一的视觉。我恐惧，渴望安全；我饥饿，贪求食物；我对某个模糊敌手暴怒。我尝到杀人的快感，也同样尝到受害者最后的绝望。我孤独，肉体因性能力而燃烧；我被放逐，而心灵渴求陪伴。</p>
<p>在这片巨大而持久的原始欲望背景上，我偶尔也会经历更高层欲望的闪光；它们像暴风上方偶尔传来的声音一样稀少而微弱。我感到与另一个心灵达成精神亲密的渴望与喜悦；感到对真理的渴望与喜悦；感到参与伟大共同体的渴望、喜悦与荣耀；也感到信仰的渴望与喜悦。我还感到真正崇拜所必经的恐惧、绝望、平静，以及最后的狂喜。然而，这些精神的照明，不过是偶尔穿透原始欲望暴风云层的星光。</p>
<p>当我承受这些模糊冲动时，所有感官也同时把汹涌的信息倾泻进来。起初，各种印象混成一团，我只意识到一阵无名而令人痛苦的骚乱。但当我把注意力投向其中，不同感官的材料便开始逐步分离。比如，一开始我觉得全身仿佛都受到某种普遍压力；仔细辨认后，它却分化成无数坚硬与柔软、粗糙与光滑、圆润与棱角。我感到脚下土地的压力；感到自己先躺在坚硬地面上，又忽然躺到一张体贴柔软的床上。通过双手，我触到无数质感：石头、树干、皮毛，以及无可比拟的人类肌肤——既有令人排斥的一面，也有令人诱惑的一面。</p>
<p>我还感到模糊的暖与冷；只要继续关注，它们便会发展成猛烈的炎热与严寒，甚至成为火焰和冰霜的啮咬。无数疼痛侵入我。最初，它们似乎还在与一种身体康健的感觉抗争——一种全身微微发麻的健康感；但疼痛很快取胜，因为它从一开始就具有强迫性的尖锐，而且随着注意力集中，强度令人不安地增长。我的整个体表仿佛都在受刑，内脏也被痛苦紧紧攫住。大脑搏动、燃烧，仿佛从各个方向被利刃反复刺穿。但事实上，这个被折磨的大脑不是我的，肠胃不是我的，皮肤也不是我的。痛苦真正的来源，是我无数前人的无数身体。</p>
<p>我强行把注意力从这种恐怖中扯开，又发现了混成一团的味道、芳香与恶臭。声音也随之而来。起初是一片压低的轰鸣，像大海，也像远处炮击；当我继续倾听，那轰鸣便长成撞击与尖啸，回荡在我的整个身体里。通过一种特殊的注意力动作——仿佛用心灵的耳朵竭力向更深处听——我还能穿透这片喧嚣，捕捉到一种模糊的人声。继续听下去，它逐渐变成无意义的哀号，其中这里那里夹杂着笑声。</p>
<p>在视觉领域，最初我只经验到上方一片模糊的明亮，下方一片昏暗。这是因为在绝大多数时代的绝大多数心灵中，只要不是室内生活压倒室外生活，白昼经验通常都包含明亮的天空和较暗的地面。一旦我开始专注于这片视觉，它便显出原来极其丰富的细节。起初，明区和暗区里都有无法辨认的影子移动，偶尔掠过一些颜色。随后，我开始在上方看见白云飘过蓝天，接着是铺满天空的雨云，又变成雷云，下一瞬间又忽然恢复成辽阔无云的蓝；随后是夜晚——有时漆黑，有时星光密布，有时洒满月色，然后又归于黑暗。</p>
<p>下方的景象同样不断更换：有时是山丘、峭壁、树冠、草地、鲜花和羽毛；有时是岸边碎浪；有时是茅屋、各种样式的大宅或拥挤城镇；有时又变成富丽堂皇、舒适亲切或肮脏破败的室内。这些形态不断出现、消失、重现，又彼此侵入、彼此消灭，像我们半梦半醒之间纠缠不休的幻象。</p>
<p>我还看见各种动物形体，以及所有人类物种的身体：从毛茸茸的猿人，到你们这种穿着荒诞衣服、半人半未成人的类型；再到作为最后地球人的那些高大而高贵的存在、金星上蝙蝠般的飞行人，以及我自己在海王星上的直接祖先。我看见痛苦扭曲的肢体、跳舞的肢体、在劳动和运动中绷紧的肢体、美丽地相互拥抱的肢体，也看见畸形与残疾的肢体。面孔同样出现又消失，有时单独出现，有时成群涌来：热切的脸、幻灭的脸，被痛苦或贪婪扭曲的脸，像卵石一样毫无表情的脸，恐惧、仇恨、卑怯的脸，安宁与狂喜的脸；儿童的脸，成熟而充满生命光辉的脸，灰暗、皱缩、下垂而衰老的脸；还有死者的脸——毫无反应，永远封闭，不再接受任何经验。</p>
<p>与这些人类视觉一同到来的，有时还有微弱的性感觉。只要我把注意力放到它们上面，它们便会展开成一个完整的情爱经验世界：抒情而新鲜的第一次结合，荒凉而机械的重复；强暴，被挫败的高潮，孤独的权宜自慰；长期相爱的恋人之间平静温和的结合；老年中的失败，以及各种性偏离的全部谱系。</p>
<p>与这些纯粹感知性的幻象混杂在一起的，还有各种个人关系经验。我被所有男女的爱与恨、合作与竞争淹没，被一切领导方式、一切忠诚与屈从淹没。我变成一个为儿子辛劳的母亲，而儿子的思想却从不在她身上；又变成一个儿子，被家庭柔软而有力的网紧紧包围。我仿佛同时从外部和内部瞥见所有物种中亿万男孩女孩的心灵：他们惊讶地睁眼面对生命，却既不知道自己究竟应该用生命做什么，甚至连怎样从生命中取得那些自己只模糊设想过的简单快乐也不知道。</p>
<p>我同样瞥见亿万成年的男人和女人，他们的希望大都受挫，心灵绝望地只关心一件事：怎样让身体继续活下去；至多再关心怎样使自己的脑袋高过身边人。我也瞥见老人，他们的一生已经不可擦除地写进世界的宏大故事。透过他们的眼睛，我看着年轻生命从四周以及更远处不断升起，就像鸟群纷纷起飞，把受伤者留在地面。我似乎还进入了亿万年代中无数劳动者的心灵。我感到他们四肢的酸痛与铅一般的沉重，感到眼球病态的胀痛，也感到他们残缺灵魂中的绝望。但我也感到少数人的精神平静——那些完全而欣然被自己的工作占有的人，他们拥有一种用力而安宁的状态。</p>
<p>所有时代、所有男女的一切思想、幻想和理想，也向我倾泻而来。我的心灵在这股洪流中踉跄、旋转，被反复抛掷在由奇想、梦境、教条与理论组成的巨大海洋上。和前人类存在一道，我一步一步在想象中搭建起感知常识的整个世界：那是一张由理论和意象织成的网，既真实又虚假；而对绝大多数人类物种中的绝大多数个体来说，它却显得是不容置疑的“现实”。我仿佛在自己的心灵里亲眼看见，那些构成这个事实与虚构大混合体的原始天才闪光。</p>
<p>我也和猿人一道，把灵魂投射进树木、山峰、风暴与星辰；并和他们一道，经历真正理智最初那阵痛苦的出生。我为了捕捉猎物、诱骗配偶、建造住所而推理，把两件事联系起来，从中得出第三件事。但理性的每一次运动，都被纯粹欲望孕育出的幻想扭曲；而当这种理性与幻想杂交形成的东西令我失败时，我反过来责怪理性本身。</p>
<p>各种艰难推理与精巧耀眼的神话，在我心中一闪而过又消失。眨眼之间，我品尝了一个又一个宇宙论；我看见湿婆、奥丁、宙斯、耶和华以及他那光辉的子嗣耶稣，也看见无数其他神祇——愤怒与正义之神、智慧之神、力量之神、爱之神——它们全都是人类各个种族、各个物种心灵所发出的投射。与此同时，我又试遍了百万种科学与哲学。其中，遥远而微弱地，也已经传来你们那个小小游戏室世界中两种响亮争吵的声音，像两个聪明、自信、彼此斗嘴的孩子：一个叫“唯物主义”，另一个叫“唯灵论”。</p>
<p>理论，理论，亿万又亿万的理论，从我身上滚滚掠过，像狂风卷起的落叶；又像某座巨型造纸厂的全部纸张被暴风抛向高空；还像心灵以飓风般速度前进时扬起的尘云。我在这片巨大、旋转而干燥的风暴中喘不过气来，几乎忘记：其实每一粒尘埃里，都藏着有机真理的少数孢子。它们大多已经干枯死亡；可有时仍然活着，孕育着东西，具有真正意义。</p>
</section>

<h2>iv. HOVERING OVER TIME</h2>
<p>IMUST NOW give you a more precise idea of the strange experiences which befall the explorer when he is trying to get a footing in some particular moment of some particular past mind. Each explorer has his own technique, and each adventure its own vicissitudes; but I shall tell simply of my own approach to your world and epoch on that occasion which I have already begun to describe.</p>
<p>Emerging presently from that ineffable moment in which I participated in the eternal view, I was at the outset conscious of a vague restlessness, a most poignant yet indefinite yearning, and along with this a violent and confused agitation of all my senses. Even though this was not my first excursion into these realms, I was overwhelmed with the pathos of man’s ever-unsatisfied and blindly fumbling desire, and equally by the tyranny of his rare joys and the torture of his distresses. At the outset this yearning and this sensory tumult were, so to speak, all that there was of me; but with an effort I woke to something more precise. I held the obscure thing away from me, and looked at it calmly. As I did so, it developed under my very eyes, the eyes of my mind, into a surge of specific primitive cravings and passions, which, though I lived them as my own, I also regarded, as it were, from a great height of aloofness. I was now experiencing the summed and confused experiences of all mankind. They came to me with the vagueness of a composite photograph, but also with that sense of multitude, of innumerable individual uniquenesses, which one may sometimes catch in listening to the murmur of a great crowd. Yet all these multitudinous experiences were in a manner mine, and combined into one experience, like the single deliverance of a man’s two eyes. I was terrified and longed for safety, hungry and lusted for food. I was enraged against some obscure adversary. I tasted the joy of murder, and equally the final despair of the victim. I was solitary, and my flesh burned with sexual potency; outcast, and my mind hungered for companionship.</p>
<p>Against this vast enduring background of primitive craving I experienced also momentary gleams of loftier desire, rare and faint as voices heard above the storm. I felt the longing and the delight of spiritual intimacy with another mind, the longing and the delight of truth, the longing and the delight and glory of participation in a great community, the longing and the delight of faith. I felt also the terror, the despair, the peace and final ecstasy, which is the way of true worship. But all these illuminations of the spirit were no more than stars piercing for a moment the storm-cloud of the primitive.</p>
<p>While I was undergoing all these obscure impulses, tumultuous messages were also pouring in through all my senses. At first indeed I received such a medley of impressions that I was aware only of a nondescript and distressing commotion. But, as I attended to this, the matter of the various senses began to differentiate itself. Thus at first I seemed to be subjected to a general pressure all over my body; but under scrutiny this developed into a profusion of hardnesses and softnesses, roughnesses and smoothnesses, rotundities and angularities. I felt the pressure of earth under my feet. I felt myself lying on hard ground, and then on a kindly bed. With my hands I felt a multitude of textures, as of stone, tree-trunks, fur, and the incomparable touch of human flesh, in all its repellent and its enticing modes. I felt also a vague warmth and chill, which, by attending to them, I could develop into violent heat and cold, or the bite of fire and frost. Innumerable pains invaded me. At first they seemed to struggle against a sense of physical well-being, a tingling healthiness; but soon pain triumphed; for it had at the outset a compelling poignancy which increased alarmingly under attention. The whole surface of my body, it seemed, was tortured; and my internal organs were all gripped in agony. My brain throbbed and burned, and was stabbed through and through in all directions. But in truth the brain that was thus tormented was not mine, nor the bowels mine, nor the skin. The source of my torture lay in the innumerable bodies of my predecessors. Wrenching my attention from this horror, I noticed a confusion of tastes, fragrances and stenches. And along with these there came sounds. A muffled roar, as of the sea or a distant bombardment, grew as I listened to it into a crashing and shrieking, which reverberated through my whole body. By a special act of attention, a straining of the mind’s ear, I could penetrate beyond this uproar, and hear an obscure vocal sound, which, as I listened, became inarticulate lamentation, flecked here and there with laughter.</p>
<p>In the field of vision I experienced at first nothing but a vague brightness above and obscurity below. This was due to the fact that in most minds of all periods, save those in which indoor life predominates over outdoor life, daytime-experience contains in the main a light sky and a darker ground. As soon as I began to attend to the matter of this vision, it revealed itself as being after all rich with detail. First, both in the light and in the dark field, there moved unrecognizable shadowy forms and occasional colours. Then aloft I began to see white clouds sailing in blue sky, then widespread rain-cloud, then thunder-cloud, and then in a moment the broad unbroken blue; then night, obscure, starry, moonlit, and again obscure. Below, I saw sometimes hills, crags, tree-tops, meadows, bright flowers and plumage, sometimes breakers on the shore, sometimes huts, mansions in many styles, or congested towns, sometimes interiors of splendour or comfortable homeliness or squalor. All these forms kept appearing, vanishing, reappearing, invading one another and exterminating one another, like the visions that haunt us between waking and sleeping. I saw also forms of beasts, and human forms of all the species, from hairy pithecanthropus, and your own fantastically clothed half-human kind, to those large and noble beings who were the last Terrestrials, and the bat-like fliers of Venus, and my own immediate ancestors on Neptune. I saw limbs writhing in agony, limbs dancing, limbs straining in toil and sport, fair limbs embracing, limbs grotesque and crippled. Faces also appeared and vanished, singly or in crowds, faces eager and disillusioned, faces twisted by pain, or by rapacity, faces expressionless as pebbles, faces of terror, hate, meanness, faces of peace and of exultation; child faces, faces of maturity all aglow with life, faces grey, wrinkled, sagging, old; and faces of the dead, impassive, sealed against experience.</p>
<p>Along with these visions of humanity, would sometimes come faint sexual sensations, which, when I attended to them, opened out into a whole world of amorous experience, of lyrical first matings and desolate repetitions, of violations, thwarted crises, lonely makeshifts, of the bland unions of long-tried lovers, of senile failures, and the whole gamut of perversions.</p>
<p>Intermingled with these purely perceptual visions, there came to me all manner of experiences of personal relationships. I was flooded with the lovings and hatings, of all men and women, by their co-operations and rivalries, by all the modes of leadership, by all c fealties and servitudes. I became a mother toiling for a son whose thought was not of her; I became a son held within the soft, strong meshes of his home. I had a glimpse, as it were both from without and from within their minds, of myriads of the boys and girls of all the species, opening their eyes in amazement upon life, knowing neither what they ought to be doing with it nor even how to wrest from it the simple joys they so vaguely conceived. I glimpsed likewise the myriads of grown men and women, with all their hopes thwarted, and their minds desparately concerned only to keep their bodies alive, or at most their heads above their fellows. And the old I glimpsed also, with their lives written indelibly into the great story of worlds. Through their eyes I watched the young things rising up all around them and beyond them, like birds that rise and leave their wounded on the ground. It seemed too that I entered into the minds of the innumerable toilers of all the aeons. I felt the ache and the leaden weight of their limbs, the sick ache of their eye-balls, and the hopelessness of their crippled souls. I felt also the calm of spirit, the strenuous peacefulness, of those few who are wholly and gladly possessed by their work.</p>
<p>There poured in upon me also all the thoughts and fantasies and ideals of all men and women in all ages. My mind reeled and staggered in the tide of them, and was swept to and fro upon the great ocean of whimsies and dreams, doctrines and theories. With the pre-human beings, I fashioned imaginatively, step by step, the whole world of perceptual common sense, that tissue of theories and images, so true and so false, which to most human beings of all species appears to be the unquestionable real. I observed, as in my own mind, the flashes of primeval genius that had gone to the making of that great medley of fact and fiction. With the ape-men, too, I projected souls into trees and mountains, storms and stars. And with them also I felt the first painful birth-throes of true intellect. To trap my prey, to beguile my mate, or build my shelter, I reasoned, I put two and two together. But every motion of this reason I distorted with fantasies born of mere desire, and when this hybrid failed me I blamed reason.</p>
<p>All manner of laborious reasonings and intricate bright myths flashed across my mind and vanished. In the twinkling of an eye I savoured cosmology after cosmology, I saw Shiva, Odin, Zeus, Jaweh and his fair offspring Jesus, and innumerable other god-heads, of wrath and righteousness, of wisdom, of power, of love, emanations from the minds of all the races, all the species of man. And with these I tried out a million sciences and philosophies, amongst which, as yet ever so faint and remote, like the voices of two bright confident quarrelling children, that’ materialism’ and that’ spiritism’ which resound throughout your little play-room world. Theories, theories, myriads upon myriads of them, streamed over me like wind-borne leaves, like the contents of some titanic paper-factory flung aloft by the storm, like dust-clouds in the hurricane advance of the mind. Gasping in this vast whirling aridity, I almost forgot that in every mote of it lay some few spores of the organic truth, most often parched and dead, but sometimes living, pregnant, significant.</p>
