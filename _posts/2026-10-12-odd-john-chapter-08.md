<h3>第八章　丑闻缠身的青春期</h3>
<p>虽然在十四岁那一年相当长的一段时间里，金融构成了约翰的主要事务，但他的注意力从未完全被它占据；经过那一段集中研究和投机之后，他已经能够一面继续积累金钱所带来的力量，一面把最好的精力投入完全不同的事情。青春期带来的新经验越来越令他着迷。与此同时，他也极其认真地研究着智人（Homo sapiens）的潜能与局限，尤其是这些东西怎样表现在当代世界的种种问题中。而随着他对普通物种的评价越来越不客气，他开始把注意力转向寻找其他与自己同一层次的个体。所有这些活动事实上同时进行，不过为了叙述方便，最好分别讲述。</p>
<p>同普通人相比，约翰的青春期开始得极晚，而且持续得异乎寻常地漫长。十四岁时，他的身体大约相当于一个正常的十岁孩子。二十三岁去世时，他外表仍像一个十七岁的少年。不过，尽管肉体发育总是远远落后于实际年龄，他在精神上却常常显得不可思议地超前——不只是在智力方面，在气质和感受力方面也是如此。在我看来，这种精神上的早熟完全来自想象力。普通儿童往往在新的、更成熟的能力已经开始苏醒之后，还会长久依恋旧有的兴趣和态度；约翰却仿佛能抓住自己天性中每一个刚刚萌芽的新东西，用想象力的强度和热量，硬把它“催”到提前开花。</p>
<p>这一点在他的性经验方面尤其明显。需要说明的是，就当时的时代而言，他父母对儿童性问题的态度相当少见。三个孩子长大时，都异常少受常见的羞耻感和强迫观念困扰。医生把性发育直截了当地看作生理问题；帕克斯则以完全坦率而幽默的态度对待孩子们的性好奇和试探。</p>
<p>因此，可以说约翰拥有一个格外良好的起点。然而他利用这种条件的方式，同哥哥姐姐满足于采取的方式截然不同。他们的“异常”仅仅在于被允许自然成长，因此避开了通常的扭曲。别的孩子被郑重其事禁止做的事情，他们大多都做过，而且没人因此谴责他们。我毫不怀疑，他们想到什么“坏事”便试上一次，随后又轻轻松松转向别的兴趣。在家里，他们会毫不害羞地谈论性和受孕；但在外面不会，“因为别人还不明白，这本来没什么大不了的”。再后来，他们显然都有过自己的恋爱。再往后，两人都结了婚，而且看起来相当满足。</p>
<p>约翰的情况却明显不同。和他们一样，他在幼年也经历过一个对自己身体极其感兴趣的阶段；和他们一样，他也从身体的某些部位得到特别的满足。但哥哥姐姐产生性兴趣时，对“人格”这件事的意识还远未清晰；约翰则早在青春期到来之前，对自己和他人的意识都已经鲜明而细致。因此，当那场巨大变化第一次开始影响他、而他的想象力又抓住最初那些心理征兆时，他一下便扑进了某些按常理说远超其年龄的行为之中。</p>
<p>例如，约翰十岁时，生理年龄其实还要小得多。他曾经历过一个性兴趣阶段，大致相当于普通儿童的幼儿性欲，只不过被他早熟的智力和想象力弄得格外复杂。好几个星期里，他以在墙壁和门柱上画“下流画”为乐，也借此把邻居气得要命；画中那些他讨厌的成年人被漫画化，正在从事各种所谓“邪恶”的行为。他还把朋友们也诱进自己的“邪道”，惹得附近家长群情激愤，最后父亲不得不出面干预。在我看来，这个阶段很大程度上源于一种无力感，以及由此而来的自卑：他的身体尚未准备好，他却已经想在性方面成为成年人。一两个星期后，他似乎便把这项兴趣彻底消化过去了，就像此前消化掉对肉搏的兴趣一样。</p>
<p>可是，几个月渐渐变成几年，他对自己身体的欣赏显然越来越强，而这种感受终于改变了他对整个生活的态度。十四岁时，人们通常会把他当成一个古怪的十岁孩子；不过也有一些对面部表情特别敏感的人，会以为他是某种十八岁的“天才”，只是身体停止了发育。他整体比例仍像十岁孩子，可儿童般的骨架上已经覆盖着精瘦、筋结分明的肌肉。父亲常说那副肌肉“不大像人类”，还说最好再长出一条能抓东西的长尾巴，画面才算完整。这样的肌肉究竟有多少来自天生，又有多少来自他有意进行的身体训练，我不知道。</p>
<p>他脸部的基本结构已经开始从幼儿转向少年；可嘴、鼻孔和眉毛那些从不停歇的表情运动，已经给这张脸烙上成年、异类、近乎非人的气质。回想这一阶段的约翰，我会想起一种既像顽童又像贤者、既像小恶魔又像幼年神祇的生物。夏天里，他通常穿彩色衬衫、短裤和帆布鞋，样样都脏得不轻。硕大的脑袋、紧贴头皮的铂白色绒发，以及那双巨大、虹膜发绿、像隼一样的眼睛，总让人觉得这些普普通通的衣服不过是他套上的伪装。</p>
<p>正是以这副模样，他开始发现自己的身体对自己有着强烈吸引力，也发现自己竟有一种惊人的力量，能够引诱别人像他本人一样沉迷地欣赏他。对于“征服”的欲望，或许又因为他清楚知道自己在普通物种眼中既怪异又有些令人反感，而被进一步夸大。我猜，他的自恋之所以加剧并维持得如此长，也同另一个事实有关：在他看来，没有任何人能够与他平等相对，没有谁值得让他以那种自私与奉献混合的方式去注视——而那种混合，本来正是浪漫爱情。</p>
<p>我必须说明，在记录约翰这一时期的行为时，我并不是要替他辩护。其中许多事情在我看来都十分骇人。假如那些事是任何别的人干的，我会毫不犹豫地把它们看成一种以自我为中心、而且严重扭曲的心灵表现。然而，尽管约翰的一生中确实发生过一些最该受谴责的事件，我仍深信：无论在道德感受力上还是智力上，他都远远高于我们其余的人。因此，对于下面这些表面上可耻的行为，我觉得正确的态度不是立刻定罪，而是暂缓判断，尽量理解。我告诉自己：如果约翰确实属于更高等的存在，那么他的许多行为必定会冒犯我们，仅仅因为我们的感受力更加粗糙，根本无法把握那些行为的真正性质。事实上，假如他的行为只不过是普通人类行为的理想化版本，我反而不会那么倾向于认为他在本质上属于一种不同而更高的类型。另一方面也必须记住：尽管能力更高，他仍然年轻，很可能以自己的方式遭受年轻心灵缺乏经验、粗糙幼稚的影响。最后，他所处的环境本身也足以把他扭曲，因为他发现自己孤零零生活在一个由他认为“只算半个人”的生物组成的世界里。</p>
<p>约翰对自己这种新的意识，是在十四岁那年第一次明确出现的；而在十四岁生日后不久，它便表现成一场我只能称作“冷酷魅惑狂欢”的东西。在他周围的人里，我是少数几个从未被他刻意“勾引”过的人之一；我之所以幸免，只因为他不能把我视作合理的猎物。我是他的奴隶、他的猎犬，他对我负有某种责任。另一个幸免的是朱迪；对她，他同样没有必要强行施展魅力，而且同样怀有责任感和感情。</p>
<p>据我所知，他第一次真正严重的情感纠葛，是同那个倒霉的斯蒂芬。此时斯蒂芬已经把自己当作完全长大的年轻人，每天上班。他有一个女朋友，星期六常骑摩托车带她出去。某个星期六，约翰和我开车结束一次商业旅行回来——我们去参观了一家橡胶厂——途中在一家很受欢迎的公路咖啡馆停下来喝茶。我们发现斯蒂芬和女友也在那里，正准备离开。约翰劝他们“再坐一会儿，跟我们聊聊”。女孩明显不大情愿，也许此前已经有理由讨厌约翰对她男友的态度；可斯蒂芬还是推迟了离开。接下来便发生了一幕极其令人不适的场景。约翰对待斯蒂芬的方式，分明是有意要让他身边的年轻女人黯然失色。他叽叽喳喳，妙语不断，幽默的程度恰到好处：足以迷住斯蒂芬，却完全越过那个朴素女孩的理解范围。他始终把谈话维持在她跟不上的高度，偶尔又特意把问题抛给她，几乎像在设圈套，让她显得可笑。他面对斯蒂芬时，时而摆出鹿一般羞怯而高傲的神态，时而又显得诱人。他不断寻找借口在房间里走动，展示那种既像猫又像小马驹的奇异优雅。斯蒂芬显然不由自主地被他迷住了，而且我敢肯定，这绝不是第一次。他对女朋友的殷勤越来越勉强、越来越虚假。可怜的女孩根本不是对手，无法掩饰自己的难过；斯蒂芬却完全没有注意，因为他已经像被催眠。最后，她看了看表，声音发颤地厉声道：“太晚了。请你送我回家。”可就在两人快要走出房间时，约翰又用最后一句话把斯蒂芬勾得回过头来。</p>
<p>那对情侣离开以后，我非常严厉地告诉约翰，我怎么看待他的行为。他用一只猫似的、令人讨厌的洋洋得意神情看着我，然后拖长声音说：“智——人！”他是在说我还是斯蒂芬，并不清楚。不过过了一会儿，他又说：“摸对地方，他就归你了。”</p>
<p>一个星期后，人们已经开始议论斯蒂芬的变化。他们说：“他那样跟一个小男孩纠缠，真该害臊。”还说约翰迟早会被毁掉。我看见两人在一起时，觉得斯蒂芬正英勇地同一种迷恋搏斗。他躲避一切同约翰的身体接触；可一旦接触真的发生——无论是偶然还是约翰故意安排——他都会像触电一样，而且总忍不住借打闹的名义把接触延长。约翰自己似乎也正经受一种享受与厌恶之间的冲突。他显然为自己的征服感到满足，可同时又被对方排斥。很多时候，一场带着亲昵意味的扭打会被他突然粗暴终止；他借某种出其不意的残忍动作发泄厌恶，例如狠狠把拇指戳向斯蒂芬的眼睛，或者猛扯他的耳朵。和更早的一次情况一样，我对这种行为表现出的厌恶和愤怒，似乎使约翰开始反省。他并不羞于向比自己低等的人学习。从此，他对斯蒂芬的态度重新变回“男人对男人”的伙伴关系，只是多了一种近乎谦逊的温柔。斯蒂芬也慢慢从迷恋中醒来，但醒来以后留下了长期的伤痕。</p>
<p>据我所知，接下来几个星期里，约翰没有再做这类事情。不过，他对长辈的举止明显变得更自觉，也更注意自己的身体。他显然在自己身上发现了一种此前从未注意过的趣味。他开始研究怎样把年轻身体那种怪异的吸引力，在低等物种眼中发挥到最大效果。当然，他聪明得多，不会沉迷于那些常使青春期少年显得滑稽可笑的过度打扮。事实上，若不是一个极亲近、又极有耐心的观察者，我怀疑根本没人会猜到他行为中的“艺术设计”竟是有意识的。我之所以确信这一点，是因为他的风格会随观众的标准而变化：有时表现为最粗朴的自我欣赏和毫不掩饰的诱惑；有时却达到一种毫无装饰、冷硬锐利的优雅，而这种风格后来成为成年约翰的典型特征。</p>
<p>在十六岁之前的一年半里，约翰偶尔会同年长男孩和年轻男子发生一些短暂而没有结果的恋爱纠葛。他的性发育仍未完成，可想象力已经抢在身体前面，使他拥有远超实际年龄的情感敏感度。然而在整个阶段，他似乎并不在意这样一个事实：多数女孩在他面前都会表现出程度不一的身体性反感。</p>
<p>但到十六岁——外表仍像一个古怪的十二岁孩子——他把注意力转向了女人。此前几个星期里，同他接触的女孩对他的态度已经变得更加积极，甚至常常带有明显的敌意。这至少说明，她们被迫开始用新的眼光注意他，而他显然也已经着手研究一套针对异性的全新行为技巧。</p>
<p>等这套技巧磨练成熟后，他便带着冷静的蓄意，把它用在本地社交圈公认的一位明星身上。这个高傲的年轻女人有一个颇为出人意料的名字：欧罗巴（Europa）。她是一个富有船东的女儿，金发、高大、健壮。她平常的表情是一种颇带轻蔑的撅嘴，只是眼睛里又混着一点近乎牛犊般的惆怅。她曾订婚两次；不过传言坚称，她对异性的实际经验远比“订婚”这种名义所能解释的更亲密。</p>
<p>某天下午，在游泳场，一次（看起来像是）偶然的事件让欧罗巴注意到了约翰。她正躺在太阳下，周围簇拥着追求者。她没有留意，自己恰好躺得离约翰的毛巾很近，手肘压住了毛巾一角。约翰游完泳需要擦身，从她身后走过来，轻轻扯了扯毛巾，低声说：“劳驾。”她回过头，发现一张古怪的年轻面孔离自己的脸很近，厌恶地猛一哆嗦，赶忙松开毛巾；随后她用一句面对众人的评论恢复镇定：“天哪！真是个小妖怪！”约翰一定听见了。</p>
<p>后来，欧罗巴从最高的跳台做了一次非常漂亮的跳水。约翰显然设法在水下同她纠缠到了一起，因为两人浮上水面时贴得很近。约翰笑着挣开。欧罗巴一时间有些喘不过气，随后她也笑了，重新爬上跳台。约翰像一只滴水嘴兽似的怪物，已经蹲在其中一块跳板上。欧罗巴伸出双臂准备起跳时，带着一种善意的轻蔑说：“这回你可逮不到我了，小猴子。”约翰像石头一样掉下去，只比她晚半秒入水。过了好一会儿，两人才又一起冒出来。大家看见欧罗巴扇了他一巴掌，从他缠住自己的手臂里挣脱，游回岸边。到了岸上，她晒着太阳，整理起自己的仪容。</p>
<p>约翰这时开始故意在她眼前展示自己的水中本领，又跳水又游泳。他自创了一种泳姿，同当时在偏远北方地区几乎还没有对手的“特拉真泳”（trudgeon）很不一样。他俯卧水面，两脚交替拍水，双臂则仍按普通特拉真泳的方式动作，速度竟足以超过许多年纪比他大得多的高手。有些人说，只要他肯学一种像样的泳姿，将来一定能成为真正优秀的游泳者。那个小小的外省郊区没有一个人意识到，约翰这种古怪的泳姿——或一种非常相似、来自波利尼西亚的泳姿——当时已经开始在欧洲、美洲乃至英格兰较先进的游泳圈里取代特拉真泳。</p>
<p>约翰用这套古怪泳姿，在欧罗巴那双不情不愿却又不得不注意的眼睛前展示速度。过了一会儿，他上岸同伙伴们玩球，在奔跑、跳跃和扭转中显出那种很少有人察觉、可一旦察觉就会奇异地被吸引的优雅。欧罗巴一边同追求者说话，一边看着，显然渐渐产生了兴趣。</p>
<p>游戏中，约翰像是不小心似的把球扔偏，正好打掉她手里的香烟。他一下跃到她身边，单膝跪下，捧住那几根被“冒犯”的手指吻了一下，既带着戏谑的骑士风度，又似乎掺着一丝真正的温柔。所有人都笑了。约翰仍握着欧罗巴的手，把那双巨大的眼睛抬起来，带着询问意味看她。高傲的欧罗巴也笑起来，却莫名其妙地红了脸，抽回了手。</p>
<p>这就是开端。那个顽童究竟经过哪些阶段才把“公主”俘获，没有必要一一追踪；只需在这场关系最盛的时候稍作停留。欧罗巴完全不知道等待自己的是什么，竟开始纵容这个少年花花公子：不仅在游泳池里同他一起嬉戏，还开车带他出去。我应该说明，约翰太聪明，而且还有太多别的事要做，不会让自己的陪伴变得廉价。他们见面并不十分频繁，却已经频繁到足以使猎物落网。</p>
<p>也许“猎物”这个比喻并不公平。我不敢声称自己能够充分分析约翰的动机，哪怕只是青春期那些相对简单的动机。我相当确信，他最初向欧罗巴发起进攻，是因为新生出一种欲望：他想得到一个正值盛年的女人的欣赏。但我也完全相信，随着关系发展，他看待欧罗巴的方式变得复杂得多。有时他注视她，神情里疏离感不仅同轻蔑冲突，也同真正的欣赏冲突。他喜欢她的爱抚，无疑部分来自刚刚萌生的性意识。可是，尽管他能够借助想象，从她所属物种的雄性视角判断她、欣赏她，我想他始终清楚意识到：无论在生物学上还是精神上，她都低于自己。征服的快感，以及同一个生命力旺盛、会作出回应的女人发生身体接触的满足，在他那里始终被另一种感觉毒害：他接触的是一头兽类，是某种永远无法满足他更深需要、甚至可能把他拉低的东西。</p>
<p>这段关系也在欧罗巴身上产生了明显影响。她的追求者一个个被冷落，尖刻的讥讽开始四处流传。有人说，她“居然迷上了一个小孩，而且还是个怪胎小孩”。她自己显然也在两种冲动之间撕扯：一方面要维护尊严，另一方面却被约翰唤起一种半是性、半是母性的饥渴。对自己处境的恐惧，以及对那个俘获自己的奇异生物的厌恶，又把事情弄得更糟。她有一次对我说过一句话，直接暴露了她对约翰感情的核心。那是在一次网球聚会上。我们俩有几分钟单独待在一起。她一边检查球拍，一边突然问：“约翰这件事，你会怪我吗？”我还在琢磨怎么回答，她又说：“我想你知道他有多大的力量。他就像——一个假装成猴子的神。只要他真正注意过你，你就再也懒得理会普通人了。”</p>
<p>这段奇怪关系的高潮，大概发生在那件事后不久。几年以后，约翰亲自把经过告诉了我。他曾笑着威胁说，有一天晚上要从窗户闯进欧罗巴的卧室。她觉得这根本不可能，便激他真这么干。第二天凌晨，她被颈侧一个轻柔的触碰惊醒——有人正在亲吻她。她还没来得及尖叫，一个熟悉的少年声音已经让她知道，闯入者就是约翰。惊讶、好笑、挑衅，再加上那种半是性欲、半是母性的渴望，使欧罗巴对少年的亲近只作了相当敷衍的抵抗。我可以想象，当她被那双仍带着儿童气息的手臂抱住时，感受到了一种清白与男性力量混杂在一起的醉人矛盾。短暂抗拒之后，她终于抛开谨慎，热烈地回应。但当她开始紧紧抱住他时，约翰却忽然被厌恶和恐惧攫住。魔力破了。</p>
<p>那些爱抚他的手指，起初似乎为他打开了一个新世界——一个能同与自己同等的心灵建立亲密、感情和信任的世界——可现在却越来越显得低于人类，“就像一条狗在我身边闻来闻去，或者一只猴子”。这种印象越来越强，最后他猛地从床上跳起来，从窗户逃走，连衬衫和短裤都留在了房间里。逃得实在太急，以至于一向动作精准的约翰竟在往下爬时失手，重重摔进花坛，扭伤了脚踝，随后一瘸一拐地摸黑走回家。</p>
<p>此后几个星期里，约翰痛苦地在吸引与排斥之间摇摆，但再也没有爬进欧罗巴的窗户。欧罗巴那一边显然也被自己的行为吓坏了。她故意躲着这个少年情人；如果在公共场合碰见，便装成一个疏远却亲切的成年人。可不久以后，她也意识到约翰对她的态度已经变了：原先的热情似乎冷却下去，取而代之的是一种温柔、却令人不安的保护欲。</p>
<p>约翰后来向我吐露他同欧罗巴的关系时，如果我没记错，他大概说过这样一番话：“那一夜第一次真正把我震醒。以前我一直确信自己能控制自己；可突然之间，我发现自己被一股股潮流冲来冲去，既挡不住，也弄不懂。那天晚上我做了一件事，我内心深处知道自己本来就应该去做，可整件事又莫名其妙地完全错了。接下来几个星期，我一次又一次去找欧罗巴，原本都打算同她亲热；可一见到她，我就什么也做不出来。走到她面前之前，我脑子里全是那一夜的记忆：她那种生命力十足的回应，她所谓的美；可真看见她以后——唉，我就像提泰妮娅醒来，看见波顿原来长着一颗驴脑袋。亲爱的欧罗巴忽然只像一头脾气不错的老母驴。当然，是头很出色的驴；可因为没有灵魂，又显得有点可笑、可怜。我并不怨她，只有善意和责任感。有一次，为了做实验，我故意重新摆出暧昧态度；可怜的她立刻像一条受到鼓励的狗一样高兴。可那不行。我身体里有一种凶猛的东西忽然站起来阻止我，同时还让我产生一种吓人的冲动——想把刀扎进她胸口，把她的脸砸烂。接着，我身体里又有另一种东西醒来，仿佛站在极高的地方俯视整件事，对我们两个生出一种混合着轻蔑和爱怜的激情；但它也把我狠狠训了一顿。”</p>
<p>我记得，说到这里以后沉默了很久。最后，约翰又告诉我一件最好不要写出来的事。事实上，我当时确实认真记录过他生涯中这一桩极其令人不安的事件；而且我承认，那时我深受他人格的影响，竟无法真正觉得他的行为卑劣。当然，我知道它公然违背习俗；可是，我对牵涉其中的两个人都怀有深厚的感情和尊重，因此很愿意按照约翰希望我采用的方式理解这件事。多年以后，我天真地把手稿给本物种的其他人看，他们指出：若把这样的材料公开发表，不仅会冒犯许多敏感读者，还会让我招来“纯粹放荡”的指责。</p>
<p>我是英国中产阶级一个体面的成员，而且希望继续体面下去。因此，我只说这么多：约翰所承认的那件行为，其动机似乎有两层。第一，在同欧罗巴那场灾难之后，他需要得到安慰；于是，他寻求同一个感受力和洞察力并非完全无法与自己相比的存在建立细腻而亲密的接触。那个人还是他所爱的人，也深深爱他，并且愿意为了他走得很远。第二，他需要确认自己在道德上独立于智人，需要摆脱内心深处对养育自己的那个物种之习俗所残留的一切无意识服从。因此，他必须打破那个物种最珍视的禁忌之一。</p>
</section>
<section data-story-lang="en" lang="en" hidden>
<h3><span id="a338"></span>CHAPTER VIII. SCANDALOUS ADOLESCENCE</h3>
<p><span class="t28">A</span><span class="t31">LTHOUGH</span><span class="t30"> </span><span class="t31">DURING</span><span class="t30"> </span><span class="t31">A</span><span class="t30"> </span>large part of his fourteenth year finance constituted John’s main occupation, his attention was never wholly absorbed by it; and after his period of intensive study and speculation he was able to continue the business of gaining monetary power while giving the best of his energies to very different matters. He was increasingly intrigued by the new experiences consequent on adolescence. At the same time he was very seriously engaged on the study of the potentialities and limitations of Homo sapiens as manifested in contemporary world-problems. And as his opinion of the normal species became more and more unflattering, he began to turn his attention to the search for other individuals of his own calibre. Though all these activities were pursued together, it will be convenient to deal with them separately.</p>
<p>The onset of John’s adolescence was very late compared with that of the normal human being, and its duration was extremely prolonged. At fourteen he was physically comparable with a normal child of ten. When he died, at twenty-three, he was still in appearance a lad of seventeen. Yet, though physically he was always far behind his years, mentally, and not merely in intelligence but also in temperament and sensibility, he often seemed to be incredibly advanced beyond his actual age. This mental precocity, I should say, was entirely due to imaginative power. Whereas the normal child clings to the old interests and attitudes long after more developed capacities have actually begun to awaken in him, John seemed to seize upon every budding novelty in his own nature and “force” it into early bloom by the sheer intensity and heat of his imagination.</p>
<p>This was obvious, for instance, in the case of his sexual experience. It should be said that his parents were for their time exceptional in their attitude to sex in their children. All three grew up unusually free from the common shames and obsessions. Doc took a frankly physiological view of sexual development; and Pax treated the sexual curiosity and experimentation of her children in a perfectly open and humorous way.</p>
<p>Thus John may be said to have had an exceptionally good start. But the use which he made of it was very different from that which satisfied his brother and sister. They were exceptional only in being permitted to develop naturally, and in thus escaping the normal distortions. They did all that most children were solemnly forbidden to do, and they were not condemned. I have no doubt that they practised whatever “vices” they happened to think of, and then passed light-heartedly to other interests. In the home circle they would prattle about sex and conception in a shameless manner; but not in public, “because people don’t understand yet that it doesn’t matter.” Later they obviously had their romantic attachments. And later still they both married, and are seemingly well content.</p>
<p>The case of John was strikingly different. Like them, he passed in infancy through a phase of intense interest in his own body. Like them he found peculiar gratification in certain parts of his body. But whereas with them sexual interest began long before consciousness of personality had become at all precise, with John self-consciousness and other-consciousness were already vivid and detailed long before the onset of adolescence. Consequently, when that great change first began to affect him, and his imagination seized upon its earliest mental symptoms, he plunged headlong into kinds of behaviour that might have been deemed far beyond his years.</p>
<p>For instance, when John was ten, but physiologically much younger, he went through a phase of sexual interest more or less equivalent to the infantile sexuality of the normal type, but enlivened by his precocious intelligence and imagination. For some weeks he amused himself and outraged the neighbours by decorating walls and gate-posts with “naughty” drawings in which particular adults whom he disliked were caricatured in the act of committing various “wicked” practices. He also enticed his friends into his evil ways, and caused such a storm among the local parents that his father had to intervene. This phase, I take it, was due largely to a sense of impotence and consequent inferiority. He was trying to be sexually mature before his body was ready. After a week or two he apparently worked through this interest, as he had worked through the interest in personal combat.</p>
<p>But as the months advanced into years, he obviously felt an increasing delight in his own body, and this came in time to change his whole attitude to life. At fourteen he was generally taken for a strange child of ten, though it was not unusual for an observer sensitive to facial expression to suppose him some kind of “genius” of eighteen with arrested physique. His general proportions were those of a ten-year-old; but over a childlike skeleton he bore a lean and knotted musculature about which his father used to say that it was not quite human, and that there ought to be a long prehensile tail to complete the picture. How far this muscular development was due to nature, and how far to his deliberate physical culture, I do not know.</p>
<p>His face was already changing from the infantile to the boyish in underlying structure; but the ceaseless expressive movements of mouth and nostrils and brows were already stamping it with an adult, alien, and almost inhuman character. Thinking of him at this period, I recall a creature which appeared as urchin but also as sage, as imp but also as infant deity. In summer his usual dress was a coloured shirt, shorts and sandshoes, all of them fairly grubby. His large head and close platinum wool, and his immense green-rimmed, falcon eyes, gave one a sense that these commonplace clothes had been assumed as a disguise.</p>
<p>Such was his general appearance when he began to discover a vast attractiveness in his own person, and a startling power of seducing others to delight in him no less luxuriously than he did himself. His will to conquest was probably much exaggerated by the knowledge that from the point of view of the normal species there was something grotesque and repellent about him. His narcissism was also, I imagine, aggravated and prolonged by the fact that, from his own point of view, there was no other to meet him on an equal footing, none fit for him to regard with that blend of selfishness and devotion which is romantic love.</p>
<p>I must make it clear that in reporting John’s behaviour at this time I do not seek to defend it. Much of it seems to me outrageous. Had it been perpetrated by anyone other than John, I should have unhesitatingly condemned it as the expression of a self-centred and shockingly perverted mind. But in spite of the most reprehensible incidents in his career, I am convinced that John was far superior to the rest of us in moral sensibility, as in intelligence. Therefore, even in respect of the seemingly disgraceful conduct which I have now to describe, I feel that the right course is not to condemn but to suspend judgement and try to understand. I tell myself that, if John was indeed a superior being, much of his conduct would certainly outrage us, simply because we, with our grosser sensibility, would never be able to apprehend its true nature. In fact, had his behaviour been simply an idealization of normal human behaviour, I should have been less disposed to regard him as of an essentially different and superior type. On the other hand it should be remembered that, though superior in capacity, he was also juvenile, and may well have suffered in his own way from the inexperience and crudity of the juvenile mind. Finally, his circumstances were such as to warp him, for he found himself alone in a world of beings whom he regarded as only half human.</p>
<p>It was in his fourteenth year that John’s new consciousness of himself first appeared, and shortly after his fourteenth birthday that it expressed itself in what I can only call an orgy of ruthless vamping. I myself was one of the few persons within his circle at whom he never “set his cap,” and I was exempt only because he could not regard me as fair game. I was his slave, his hound, toward whom he felt a certain responsibility. One other who escaped was Judy; for there again he felt no need to enforce his attractiveness, and there again he felt responsibility and affection.</p>
<p>So far as I know, his first serious affair was with the unfortunate Stephen, now a consciously grown-up young man who went to business every day. Stephen had a girl friend whom he took out on Saturdays on his motor-bike. One Saturday, when John and I were returning from a business trip in my car (we had visited a rubber factory), we stopped for tea at a popular roadside café. We found Stephen and his girl inside, almost ready to leave. John persuaded them to “stay and talk to us for a bit”. The girl was obviously reluctant, having perhaps already had reason to dislike John’s behaviour toward her man; but Stephen delayed departure. Then began a most distressing scene. John behaved toward Stephen in a manner calculated to eclipse the young woman at his side. He prattled. He sparkled with just the right standard of wit to fascinate Stephen and pass over the head of his simple companion. He kept the whole conversation well beyond her powers, occasionally appealing to her in a manner likely to trap her into making herself ridiculous. He faced Stephen now with the shy hauteur of a deer, now with seductiveness. He found excuses to move about the room and display his curiously feline, though also coltish, grace. Stephen was obviously captivated against his will, and, I feel sure, not for the first time. Toward the girl his gallantry grew laboured and false. She, poor outclassed little creature, could not conceal her distress, but Stephen never noticed it, for he was hypnotized. At last she looked at her watch and tremulously snapped, “It’s frightfully late. Please take me home.” But even as they were leaving the room John enticed Stephen back for a final sally.</p>
<p>When the couple had gone I told John very emphatically what I thought of his behaviour. He looked at me with the offensive complacency of a cat, then drawled, “Homo sapiens!” Whether he was referring to me or to Stephen was not clear. But presently he said, “Tickle him the right way, and you’ve got him.”</p>
<p>A week later people were talking about the change in Stephen. They said “he ought to be ashamed to carry on that way with the boy,” and that John would be ruined. When I saw the two of them together, I felt that Stephen was struggling heroically against an obsession. He shunned all physical contact with John, but when contact came, either by accident or through John’s contrivance, he was electrified, and could not help prolonging it under a pretence of ragging. John himself appeared to be suffering from a conflict of luxury and disgust. It was clear that he was gratified by his conquest, but at the same time he was repelled. Often he would terminate an amatory brawl with harshness, venting his repulsion by some unexpected piece of brutality, pushing his thumb fiercely into Stephen’s eye, or tearing at his ear. As on an earlier occasion, my disgust and indignation at this kind of behaviour seemed to lead John to self-criticism. He was not above learning from his inferiors. His attitude to Stephen changed back to “man to man” comradeship, tempered by an almost humble gentleness. Stephen, too, slowly woke from his infatuation, but he woke with lasting scars.</p>
<p>For some weeks John refrained, so far as I know, from activities of this kind. But his behaviour toward his elders had become definitely more self-conscious and more body-conscious. He was evidently discovering in his own person an interest which had hitherto escaped him. He studied the art of displaying the bizarre attractiveness of his young body to the best advantage in the eyes of the inferior species. Of course he was far too intelligent to indulge in those excesses of adornment which so often render the adolescent ludicrous. Indeed, I doubt whether any but the most intimate and persevering observer would have guessed that the artistry of his behaviour was at all conscious. That it actually was so I inferred from the fact that it varied according to the standards of his audience, now expressing the crudest sort of self-delight and shameless seductiveness, now attaining to that unadorned and steely grace which was to characterize the later John.</p>
<p>During the eighteen months before he reached the age of sixteen John indulged in occasional and abortive love-affairs with older boys and young men. He was still sexually undeveloped, but imagination forestalled his physique, and made him capable of amatory sensitiveness beyond his years. Throughout this phase, however, he seemed indifferent to the fact that most girls showed some degree of physical repulsion in his presence.</p>
<p>But when he was sixteen, and in appearance a queer sort of twelve-year-old, he turned his attention to woman. For some weeks the girls with whom he came in contact had shown a more positive, often a positively vindictive, attitude to him. This suggests at least that they were being forced to take note of him with new eyes, and that he had already begun to study a new technique of behaviour, directed toward the opposite sex.</p>
<p>Having perfected his technique, he proceeded to use it with cold deliberation upon one of the acknowledged stars of local society. This haughty young woman, who bore the surprising name of Europa, was the daughter of a wealthy shipowner. She was fair, large, athletic. Her normal expression was a rather contemptuous pout, tempered by a certain cow-like wistfulness about the eyes. She had been engaged twice, but rumour affirmed that her experience of the opposite sex had been far more intimate than was justified by mere betrothal.</p>
<p>One afternoon down at the bathing place accident (seemingly) brought John to the notice of Europa. She was lying in the sun, attended by her admirers. Unwitting, she had settled herself close to John’s towel. Her elbow was on the corner of it. John, needing to dry himself after a swim, approached her from behind, mildly tugged at the towel and murmured, “Excuse me.” She turned, found a grotesque young face close to her own, gave a start of repulsion, hastily released the towel, and recovered her composure by remarking to her audience, “Heavens! What an imp!” John must have heard.</p>
<p>Later, when Europa executed one of her admirable dives from the top board, John evidently managed to get entangled with her under water, for they came up together in close contact. John laughed, and broke away. Europa was left gasping for a moment, then she, too, laughed, and returned to the diving platform. John, looking like a gargoyle, was already squatting on one of the boards. As she stretched her arms for the dive, she remarked with kindly contempt, “You won’t catch me this time, little monkey.” John dropped like a stone, and entered the water half a second behind her. After a considerable time they appeared together again. Europa was seen to smack his face, break from his clinging arm, and make for shore. There, she sunned and preened herself.</p>
<p>John now disported himself in her view, diving and swimming. He had invented a stroke of his own, very different from the “trudgeon” which was still at this time almost unchallenged in the remote northern provinces. Lying on his stomach in the water and flicking his feet alternately, while his arms behaved in the ordinary “trudgeon” manner, he was able to outstrip many experts older than himself. Some said that if he would only learn a decent stroke he would develop into a really fine swimmer. No one in the little provincial suburb realized that John’s eccentric stroke, or something very like it, a product of Polynesia, was even then ousting the “trudgeon” from the more advanced swimming circles of Europe and America, and even England.</p>
<p>With this eccentric stroke John displayed his prowess before the reluctantly attentive eyes of Europa. Presently he came out of the water and played ball with his companions, running, leaping, twisting, with that queer grace which few could detect, but by which those few were strangely enthralled. Europa, talking to her swains, watched and was evidently intrigued.</p>
<p>In the course of the game John threw the ball, seemingly by accident, so that it knocked her cigarette from her hand. He leapt to her, sank on one knee, took the outraged fingers and kissed them, with mock gallantry and a suggestion of real tenderness. Every one laughed. Still holding Europa’s hand, he brought his great eyes to bear upon her face, inquiringly. The proud Europa laughed, unaccountably blushed, withdrew her hand.</p>
<p>This was the beginning. There is no need to follow the stages by which the urchin captured the princess. It is enough to dwell for a moment on their relations when the affair was at its height. Little knowing what was in store for her, Europa encouraged the juvenile philanderer, not only at the swimming pool, where they gambolled together, but also by taking him out in her car. John, I should say, was much too wise, and much too occupied with other matters, to make his society cheap. Their meetings were not very frequent; but they were frequent enough to secure his prey.</p>
<p>The metaphor is perhaps unjust. I do not pretend to be able to analyse John’s motives adequately, even the comparatively simple motives of his adolescence. Though I feel fairly sure that the origin of his attack upon Europa was his new craving to be admired by a woman in her prime, I can well believe that, as the relationship developed, he came to regard her in a much more complex manner. He sometimes watched her with an expression in which aloofness struggled not only with contempt but with genuine admiration. His delight in her caresses was doubtless in part due to dawning sexual appreciation. But though he could imaginatively judge her and enjoy her from the point of view of the male of her species, he was, I think, always conscious of her biological and spiritual inferiority to himself. The delight of conquest and the luxury of physical contact with a full-blooded and responsive woman were always for him poisoned by the sense that this contact was with a brute, with something which could never satisfy his deeper needs, and might debase him.</p>
<p>On Europa the relationship had striking effects. The swains found themselves spurned. Bitter taunts were flung. It was said that she had “fallen for a kid, and a freak kid, too.” She herself was obviously torn between the need to preserve her dignity and the half-sexual, half-maternal hunger which John had inspired. Horror at her plight and revulsion from the strangeness of the thing that had enthralled her made matters worse. She once said something to me which revealed the nerve of her feeling for John. It was at a tennis party. She and I were alone for a few minutes. Examining her racquet, she suddenly asked, “Do you blame me, about John?” While I was trying to reply, she added, “I expect you know what a power he has. He’s like — a god pretending to be a monkey. When you’ve been noticed by him, you can’t bother about ordinary people.”</p>
<p>The climax of this strange affair must have happened very shortly after that incident. I heard the story from John himself several years later. He had laughingly threatened to invade Europa’s bedroom one night by way of the window. It seemed to her an impossible feat, and she dared him to do it. In the small hours of the following morning she woke to a soft touch on her neck. She was being kissed. Before she had time to scream a well-known boyish voice made known that the invader was John. What with astonishment, amusement, defiance and sexual-maternal craving, Europa seems to have made but a half-hearted resistance to the boy’s advances. I can imagine that in the grip of his still childish arms she found an intoxicating blend of the innocent and the virile. After some protest and sweet struggle she threw prudence to the winds and responded with passion. But when she began to cling to him, revulsion and horror invaded him. The spell was broken.</p>
<p>The caressing fingers, which at first had seemed to open up for him a new world of mutual intimacy, affection, trust, in relation with a spirit of his own stature, became increasingly subhuman, “as though a dog were smelling round me, or a monkey.” The impression became so strong that he finally sprang from the bed and disappeared through the window, leaving his shirt and shorts behind him. So hasty was his retreat that he actually bungled the descent in a most un-John-like manner, fell heavily into a flower-bed, and limped home in the darkness with a sprained ankle.</p>
<p>For some weeks John was painfully torn between attraction and repulsion, but never again did he climb into Europa’s window. She, for her part, was evidently horrified at her own behaviour, for she deliberately avoided her boy-lover, and when she encountered him in public she acted the part of the remote though kindly adult. Presently, however, she realized that John’s attitude to her had changed, that his ardour had apparently cooled and given place to a gentle and disconcerting protectiveness.</p>
<p>When John took me into his confidence about his relations with Europa, he said, if I remember rightly, something like this. “That one night gave me my first real shaking. Before, I had been sure of myself; suddenly I found myself swept this way and that by currents that I could neither stem nor understand. I had done something that night which I knew deeply I was meant to do, but it was somehow all wrong. Time after time, during the next few weeks, I went to Europa intending to make love to her, but when I found her I just didn’t. Before I reached her I’d be all full of the recollection of that night, and her vital responsiveness, and her so-called beauty; but when I saw her — well, I felt as Titania felt when she woke to see that Bottom was an ass. Dear Europa seemed just a nice old donkey, a fine one of course, but rather ridiculous and pitiable because of her soullessness. I felt no resentment against her, just kindness and responsibility. Once, for the sake of experiment, I started being amorous, and she, poor thing, rejoiced like an encouraged dog. But it wouldn’t do. Something fierce in me rose up and stopped me, and filled me with an alarming desire to get my knife into her breasts and smash up her face. Then something else woke in me that looked down on the whole matter from a great height and felt a sort of passion of contemptuous affection for us both; but gave me a mighty scolding.”</p>
<p>At this point, I remember, there was a long silence. At last John told me something which it is better not to report. I did, indeed, write a careful account of this most disturbing incident in his career; and I confess that at the time I was so deeply under the spell of his personality that I could not feel his behaviour to have been vile. I recognized, of course, that it was flagrantly unconventional. But I had so deep an affection and respect for both the persons concerned that I gladly saw the affair as John wished me to see it. Years later, when I innocently showed my manuscript to others of my species, they pointed out that to publish such matter would be to shock many sensitive readers, and to incur the charge of sheer licentiousness.</p>
<p>I am a respectable member of the English middle class, and wish to remain so. All I will say, then, is that the motive of the behaviour which John confessed seems to have been double. First, he needed soothing after the disastrous incident with Europa, and, therefore, he sought delicate and intimate contact with a being whose sensibility and insight were not wholly incomparable with his own; with a being, moreover, who was beloved, who also loved him deeply, and would gladly go to any lengths for his sake. Second, he needed to assert his moral independence of Homo sapiens, to free himself of all deep unconscious acquiescence in the conventions of the species that had nurtured him. He needed, therefore, to break what was one of the most cherished of all the taboos of that species.</p>
