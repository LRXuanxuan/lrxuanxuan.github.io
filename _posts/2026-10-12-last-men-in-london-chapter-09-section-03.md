<h2>iii. 回到海王星</h2>
<p>我的任务已经接近完成。我的心灵储藏着一笔巨大的财富，是我像蜜蜂一样从你们的世界采集来的；现在，我必须带着这些收获返回自己出发的那个宏伟而美丽的蜂巢。任务的另一部分也已临近结束。我差不多完成了这第二封讯息——写给我自己那些遥远祖先、第一代人类的讯息。</p>
<p>很快，我就将能够离开你们这个充满悲哀与徒劳希望、恐怖与未竟承诺的世界。再过不久，我将回到一个远在你们星球毁灭之后、却又离我自己那更可爱的世界遭到更加悲惨的毁灭并不太久的时代。一个探索者在过去进行过长时间而深入的工作后，要重新回到自己的世界，是一件困难且危险的事。同样困难、同样令我不舍的是，我现在必须开始从心灵中卸下你们的精神模式，仿佛摘下一张面具。一个成年人若长期和孩子们生活在一起，参加他们的游戏，同他们一起为那些童稚的悲伤难过，那么到了最终必须离开时，也会被惋惜撕扯，甚至半信半疑地觉得，孩子们的天性和生活方式也许比自己的更好。我现在也是如此，不情愿地离开你们的世界——离开这场幼稚的灾难：它本来如此容易避免，却又如此彻底地不可避免；如此荒诞，却又如此悲惨。然而，就像那个成年人终于重新投入成年人的世界时，身上的童稚会自然脱落一样，当我认真地在想象中回到自己的世界，我暂时承担的原始性也开始从我身上脱落。</p>
<p>是时候把我自己重新唤回我自己了。我栖居在你们这个小小世界里，并不是为了成为你们中的一员，而是为了给最后人类的种族心灵带回一些可以供它欣悦地认识和品味的材料；也是为了使你们的世界最终获得自己的荣耀冠冕——当然不是以它曾经希望的方式，而是因为它在最后人类的种族心灵中，被一个生命接一个生命、一个事件接一个事件地精细品味过。</p>
<p>我把自己唤回我自己。我所属的那个伟大世界，早已超越了你们这个婴儿世界的神话、玩具和吓人的鬼怪。在那里，人们活着而不畏惧死亡与痛苦，尽管他们仍然会死、会受苦。在那里，没有战胜他人的欲望，也没有被奴役的恐惧。人们相爱而不渴望占有，崇拜而不想到得救，观照而没有精神上的骄傲。在那里，人比你们世界里的任何人都更加自由，却又比你们中的任何人都更加服从。</p>
<p>我把自己唤回我自己。我所隶属的那个最可爱的共同体，那个人类精神迄今最卓越、最充分的实现——我的心灵正是其中一个有机部分——很快就必须被毁灭。太阳的疯狂已经在我的世界上骇人地发作。我的同时代人面前，横陈着一个无法估量的恐怖与解体时代。可是，我们不能通过种族自杀来逃离这场恐怖，尽管唯有自杀才能拯救我们；因为我们两项最高的虔敬事业还没有完成。我们尚未成功把一种新人类的种子播向银河系遥远地区；我们也尚未完成那场献身般的过去调查。因此，我们现在还不能终结自己。我们必须忠于过去，也必须忠于未来。</p>
<p>我把自己唤回我自己。很快，我将会在自己位于北极、深藏地下的花园里醒来。我会再次通过自己的眼睛，而不是第一代人类那些遮蔽视野的感官，以精确而完整的色彩观看世界。我会认出那些熟悉的海王星叶片与花朵，它们在地下的微风里摇摆。我会重新感到自己身体那种宽广而轻松的舒适。我会打哈欠、伸展身体、站起身来。我会在池子里痛痛快快地游泳。我会走进自己的住处，按铃叫来食物。然后，在见任何同事之前，我会开始重新审视这次探索，把它记录下来，并进行批判性的编辑。因为那时，我终于重新恢复了完整的海王星心智。我会以新的洞察重新看你们的世界，也重新看在长期沉浸于你们世界期间的我自己。很可能，我会微笑着回想自己不久前那些被地球感染的思想和感情。我会想起这本书，并为它微笑——这是一个奇异的混血产物，由一个纯粹的地球心灵，同一个已经受地球感染的海王星心灵交配而生。我会知道，即便在我最竭力挑战我那可怜合作者理解力的时候，我实际上也没有把最后人类完整的智慧传给他；我给他的只是浅薄得多的东西，是某种已经被地球的阴影削弱、甚至在我自己心灵的源头处便已经有四分之三属于地球的东西。</p>
<p>我会怀着巨大的感激，意识到自己已经完整地重新回到清明之中。带着新的敬畏和新的热情，我会再次完全敞开自己，让我深爱的世界那汹涌而来的丰饶与精微灌入心灵。</p>
<p>经过许多星期的劳动，我会完成报告。然后我将离开自己的住处，同许多同事会面，彼此交换各自的发现。</p>
<p>不过，由于在地下墓窟中无法进行心灵感应交流，我们很快会向南旅行，一同在一座巨大的水晶塔中生活一段时间，在那里通过心灵感应展开合作。当我们把各自的发现合并成一个单一而有生命的、关于你们这个物种的认识之后，就会把这份巨大财富用心灵感应广播到整个世界，让我们那万万亿同胞的心灵都因此丰富；这样，等种族的单一心灵下一次觉醒时，那个伟大的精神——它并不是某个不同于我们每个人的存在，而就是每一个完全觉醒的我们——也能够利用这些发现，供它沉思，并进入狂喜。</p>
<p>这一切完成之后，我会通过心灵感应召唤那位在假日中始终亲爱的伴侣。上一次开始探索之前，我曾同她在那座断裂山脉铺向海洋的地方嬉戏、同眠。我们会再次在那里相聚和玩耍，观看岩池中的种种生命，看那颗疯星升起又落下。我们会再次在长满草皮的小丘间漫游，像海豹一样在海湾里游泳，在高高的草丛中为自己筑巢，一同躺过夜晚。也许我会在那里告诉她，在另一个世界、仿佛另一个宇宙里，我自己曾怎样困在保罗那麻木而半人类的肉体中，和同样半人类的凯瑟琳躺在一起。我们会一起观照过去那些奇异的美，以及可怖的未来。我们也会久久品味此刻。</p>
<p>假期结束后，我会同她一起回到她工作的地方，那里的人正在为播撒生命种子作准备。等她让我看过工作进展，我就会在全世界漫游很长一段时间，吸收这个世界错综复杂的美，观看它形形色色的活动，同数量庞大的亲密朋友交往，在自己的婚姻群体生活中尽一份职责，访问青年之地，乘以太飞船在各行星之间航行，在母星的荒野中独自徘徊，在花园里闲坐或冥想、照料果树，或者在某座宏大天文台观察最遥远的宇宙，又或者在天文学家的帮助下研究太阳疾病那缓慢却致命的发展。</p>
<p>也许在我再次回到过去之前，种族心灵还会出现一次至高的觉醒。也许，在那独一无二的觉醒之日以后，我会和同伴一样，长久地以一种入迷状态在世界各处活动，沉浸于单一人类精神那不可言说的经验中；也许它终于将观照到那些超越凡俗的存在——竭力崇拜它们，正是人类最高的荣耀。然而迟早，我仍会重新回到探索过去的工作中，或返回第一代人类，或进入另一个原始物种。我会再次带回有生命的历史财富，把它交给最后人类的世界。然后我会再次游戏，再次参与我世界丰饶的生活，再次返回过去。这样反复多少次，我自己也不知道。</p>
<p>不过，每一次，当我离开现在、前往过去时，我的世界都会已经发生一点变化，一点轻微的恶化，有时也许几乎察觉不到。气候会更热、更不健康。那颗疯狂太阳无可逃避的射线，会给眼睛和大脑造成更多伤害。社会也许已经不再完美，理性行为不再始终如一，心灵感应也许已经变得困难；而种族心智很可能早已不再可能出现。不过，对过去的探索也会向完成推进，尽管越来越艰难；生命种子的播撒最终也会开始。</p>
<p>最后人类能够毫不惊惧地望向这一切不可避免的恶化——望向他们最珍爱的一切逐渐衰败，望向美丽共同体的死亡，望向人类精神的灭绝。我们只有一个愿望，那就是两项任务能够完成；并且幸运的话，它们能在我们退化到无法选择终结自己之前完成，因为当我们终于可以自由这样做时，我们仍希望自己有能力作出那个选择。想到我们可能缓慢地沉入野蛮、沉入亚人状态、沉入盲目而呜咽的痛苦，想到“人”的最后声音也许只是一声哀鸣，这多少令我们厌恶。</p>
<p>这种事很可能真的发生。不过，即便面对这样的前景，我们也并不真正恐惧。如果它发生，在我们已经退化的心灵看来，它无疑会变得不可忍受。但今天，我们仍完全拥有自己。哪怕只以个体身份存在，哪怕没有种族心智带来的提升，我们也能够以顺从接受这一切；不，甚至能够怀着一种惊人的热情去欣赏我们世界最终的道德崩塌。因为我们已经走得很远。我们已经深深饮过美。而对于一个已经深饮过宇宙庄严之美的精神来说，哪怕最终的恐怖，也可以接受。</p>
</section>

<h2>iii. BACK TO NEPTUNE</h2>
<p>MY TASK IS almost completed. My mind is stored with an immense treasure, which I have gathered, bee-like, in your world, and with which I must now return to the great and fair hive whence I came. The other side of my task also is drawing to a close. I have almost finished this my second message to my own remote ancestors, the First Men.</p>
<p>Very soon I shall be free to leave your world of sorrow and vain hope, of horror and of promise unfulfilled. Presently I shall return to an age long after the destruction of your planet, and not long before the more tragic destruction of my own more delectable world. Only with difficulty and danger can the explorer, after close work in the past, revert once more to his own world. With difficulty and reluctance also, I shall now begin to put off your mental pattern from my mind, as one may put off a mask. As the grown man who has been long with children, living in their games, grieving with them in their childish sorrows, is tom with regret when at last he must leave them, and half-persuades himself that their nature and their ways are better than his own, so I now with reluctance leave your world, with its childish, its so easily to be avoided yet utterly inescapable, its farcical and yet most tragic, disaster. But even as, when at last the grown man is once more at grips with the world of men, his childishness falls from him, so, when I earnestly revert in imagination to my own world, my assumed primitiveness falls from me.</p>
<p>It is time to recall myself to myself. I have been dwelling in your little world, not as one of you, but in order that I might bring to the racial mind of the Last Men matter for delighting cognizance; in order, also, that your world may find its crown of glory, not indeed in the way that was hoped, but in being exquisitely savoured, life by life, event by event, in the racial mind of the Last Men.</p>
<p>I recall myself to myself. The great world to which I am native has long ago outgrown the myths, the toys, the bogies of your infant world. There, one lives without the fear of death and pain, though there one dies and suffers. There, one knows no lust to triumph over other men, no fear of being enslaved. There one loves without craving to possess, worships without thought of salvation, contemplates without pride of spirit. There one is free as none in your world is free, yet obedient as none of you is obedient.</p>
<p>I recall myself to myself. The most lovely community of which I am a member, the most excellently fulfilled Spirit of Man, within which my mind is organic, must very soon be destroyed. The madness of the Sun is already hideously at work upon my world. There lies before my contemporaries an age of incalculable horror and disintegration. From that horror we must not escape by means of the racial suicide which alone could save us; for our two supreme acts of piety are not yet accomplished. We have not yet succeeded in impregnating the remote regions of this galaxy with the seed of a new mankind. We have not yet completed our devoted survey of the past. Therefore we may not yet put an end to ourselves. We must be loyal to the past and to the future.</p>
<p>I recall myself to myself. Presently I shall wake in my Arctic and subterranean garden. Once more I shall see with precision and with full colour through my own eyes, not through the obscuring organs of the First Men. I shall recognize the familiar forms of Neptunian leaves and flowers, swaying in the subterranean breeze. I shall feel the large easefulness of my own body. I shall yawn and stretch and rise. I shall swim luxuriously in my pool. I shall enter my apartment and ring for food. Then, before I see any of my colleagues, I shall begin to review my exploration; I shall record it, and critically edit it. For now at last I shall have recovered full Neptunian mentality. I shall see with new insight not only your world but my own self as I was during my long immersion in your world. Probably I shall smile at my recent earth-infected thoughts and feelings. I shall smile when I remember this book, this strange hybrid sprung from the intercourse of a purely Terrestrial mind and a Neptunian mind, earth-infected. I shall know that, even when I most strained the understanding of my poor collaborator, I was not really giving him the full wisdom of the Last Men, but something far less profound, something that was already earth-dimmed, already three-parts Terrestrial even at its source in my own mind.</p>
<p>With a great thankfulness I shall recognize my own complete reversion to lucidity. With a new awe and zest I shall lay myself open once more to the inflooding richness and subtlety of my well-loved world.</p>
<p>After many weeks of labour I shall have completed my report, and then I shall leave my apartment and meet many of my colleagues, to exchange findings with them.</p>
<p>But, since in the Catacombs telepathic intercourse may not occur, we shall soon travel south to live together for a while in a great crystal pylon where we may pursue our collaboration telepathically. And when we have made of our combined findings a single, living, apprehension of your species, we shall broadcast our great treasure telepathically over the whole world; so that all the million million minds of our fellows may be enriched by it, so that when the time comes for the next awakening of the single mind of the race, that great spirit also, which is not other than each of us fully awakened, may avail itself of our findings, for its meditation and ecstasy.</p>
<p>When all this is done I shall call up telepathically the ever dear companion of my holidays, with whom, before my last exploration, I played and slept, where the broken mountain lies spread out into the sea. There once more we shall meet and play, watching the populations of the rock pools, and the risings and settings of the Mad Star. There once more we shall wander over the turfy hillocks and swim, seal-like, in the bay, and make for ourselves nests in the long grass, where we may lie together in the night. There perhaps I shall tell her how, in another world, seemingly in another universe, I myself, striving in the numb, the half-human flesh of Paul, lay with the half-human Katherine. There we shall contemplate the strange beauties of the past and of the dread future. There we shall savour lingeringly the present.</p>
<p>When our holiday is over I shall return with her to her place of work, where they prepare for the spreading of the seed. And when she has shown me how the task is progressing, I shall go wandering about the world for a very long time, absorbing its intricate beauty, watching its many and diverse operations, having intercourse with the great population of my intimate friends, playing my part in the life of my marriage group, visiting the Land of the Young, voyaging in ether-ships among the planets, wandering alone in the wild places of the home planet, idling or meditating in my garden or working among my fruit trees, or watching the most distant universes from some great observatory, or studying with the help of astronomers the slow but fatal progress of the sun’s disease.</p>
<p>It may be that before it is time for me to go once more into the past, there will occur again a supreme awakening of the racial mind. It may be that after the unique day of the awakening I shall for a long time move about the world entranced like my fellows, rapt in the ineffable experiences of the single Spirit of Man, contemplating perhaps at last the supernal entities which it is man’s chief glory to strive to worship. Sooner or later, however, I shall return once more to my work in the past, either to the First Men or to some other primitive species. I shall bring back with me more treasure of living history, and deliver it into the world of the Last Men. And again I shall play, and again I shall participate in the rich life of my world, and again I shall return to the past. And so on for I know not how many times.</p>
<p>But each time, when I leave the present for the past, there will have been a change in my world, a slight deterioration, sometimes perhaps imperceptible. The climate will have grown hotter and more unwholesome. The inescapable rays of the mad sun will have done more harm to eyes and brains. Society will perhaps no longer be perfect, rational conduct no longer invariable, telepathy perhaps already difficult; and very probably the racial mentality will have already become impossible. But also the exploration of the past will be advancing toward completion, though with increasing difficulty; and the dissemination of the seed of life will be at last begun.</p>
<p>The Last Men can look forward without dismay to the inevitable deterioration of all that they cherish most, to the death of their fair community, and to the extinction of the human spirit. We have only one desire, namely that our two tasks may be accomplished; and that happily they may be accomplished before our deterioration is so far advanced as to make us incapable of choosing to put an end to ourselves when at last we are free to do so. For the thought is somewhat repugnant to us that we should slowly sink into barbarism, into the sub-human, into blind and whimpering agony, that the last of Man should be a whine.</p>
<p>This may well happen, but even by such a prospect we are not seriously dismayed. If it does occur, it will doubtless seem intolerable to our degraded spirits. But today, we are fully possessed of ourselves. Even as individuals, even apart from the exaltation of racial mentality, we can accept with resignation, nay with a surprising fervour of appreciation, even the moral downfall of our world. For we have gone far. We have drunk deeply of beauty. And to the spirit that has drunk deeply of the grave beauty of the cosmos, even the ultimate horror is acceptable.</p>
