<h2>二、人口衰退</h2>
<p>世界人口的下降其实早已开始。工业化初期曾出现过一段人口迅速增长时期，随后趋势便逆转了。原因一部分在于高效避孕方法的普及，一部分在于人们对经济不安全感的焦虑，还有一部分，则来自一种模糊而广泛的感觉：整个文明似乎既虚假又毫无意义。在北美那个略显疲惫的乌托邦中，人口下降最早成为严重问题；在那里，经济不安全感不可能是主要原因，因为繁荣几乎人人共享。但人们对于这个奇怪而缺乏明确目标的乌托邦深感幻灭，这确实成了美国生活中的重要因素。较早的极权国家一直害怕人口减少，也曾竭尽全力试图阻止它，却始终收效甚微。后来的那些极权国家——俄罗斯帝国、中国帝国，以及世界帝国早期——则以它们惯有的冷酷方式处理这个问题。</p>
<p>增加人口最显而易见的办法，本应是唤醒过去各届政府为了处理失业问题而陆续冷藏在世界各地的数亿人。然而一开始，人们却非常不愿这样做，而原因恰好暴露出这一时期人类令人难以置信的愚蠢与迷信。人口下降不但没有解决失业问题，反而使它更加严重。需求持续萎缩，大规模生产机器越来越难以在有利可图的条件下运转。统治者一方面头脑很清楚，知道社会需要增加人口；另一方面又痛苦地看着失业问题，因而不愿再往那潭停滞的潜在劳动力中增加人口。于是，尽管围绕那些冷藏库进行了无数讨论，却始终没有采取行动。与此同时，人口继续减少。</p>
<p>各国政府于是试图强迫人民繁殖。女性从小就被教育相信，生育是自己唯一的职能。母亲获得的荣誉与她们生下的子女数量成正比。生下十五个或更多孩子的女人，会获得“丰产母亲”的称号；谁若成功把二十个人带到世上，甚至会被奉为神圣。避孕被宣布为非法，并受到道德谴责。可是即便采取了这一切措施，生育率仍继续下降。绝望之下，世界政府进一步收紧了对女性的控制。每个女孩一旦被证明性成熟，就必须立即同一名男子性交。认证一个月后，她必须再次到医疗委员会接受检查，以证明自己已不再是处女。若三个月后仍未怀孕，她会被送进一种兼具妓院与种畜场性质的机构；再过三个月若依旧未能受孕，便要接受医疗和外科治疗，以“治愈”不孕。若这种方法仍然失败，她就会被公开羞辱，接受相应的酷刑，然后被缓慢处死。</p>
<p>在无能为力地旁观人口衰退几十年、甚至可能几百年之后，世界政府终于决定采取那个最明显的办法；更何况，这一办法本来就有经典依据。神圣教规中原本就规定：未来某一天，当社会极度需要劳动力时，那些沉睡者必须被唤醒。现在，统治者宣布时机已经到来。政府在惊慌中、也几乎毫无准备地命令生理学家，把全部冷藏人口一次性解冻。这个过程本来极其精细，而较早、更聪明的一代留下的操作说明，起初却被严重搞砸。数百万人直接死亡，或者只短暂醒来，在痛苦与迷惑中挣扎一阵，很快便死去。又有数百万人虽然活了下来，却只能终身残疾或精神失常。多数人倒确实经受住了粗暴的苏醒过程，虽然身体受到严重损害，总算还能过某种程度的主动生活。然而他们已经睡过了漫长的历史时期。他们的心智由一个早已消失的世界塑造；他们的语言和思维常常古老到现代人根本无法理解。刚醒来时，他们的四肢乃至心智都迟钝得令人痛苦。生殖冲动似乎也已经熄灭。更糟的是，他们渐渐发现，这个新世界甚至比自己从前那个世界更加恶劣。</p>
<p>其中有些人终于完全摆脱长达数世纪睡眠留下的阴霾，并艰难适应新环境之后，表现出的机敏程度反而比新时代的正常邻居稍高一些。而且他们并没有从小接受新时代那些更近、更荒谬的偏见，因此普遍非常批判当代习俗和制度。事实上，他们很快就成了当局眼中的严重麻烦。政府赶紧下令，所有“复醒者”都必须立即安装无线电控制装置。这个显而易见的预防措施此前迟迟没有实行，与其说是因为担心他们尚未从冷藏影响中恢复、难以承受手术，倒不如说是由于一种令人吃惊的愚蠢心理：当局不愿意把他们提升到“公民”的地位。如今，数百万人被迫接受手术，其中一半死在麻醉过程中。还有数百万人进行绝望抵抗，只能被消灭。</p>
<p>在少数“复醒者”高度集中的地区，他们甚至能够夺取权力，建立叛乱国家。人类竟然能够反抗权威，这一景象令世界国家那些机器人般的公民彻底困惑。许多人心中出现一种痛苦冲突：一边是由正统无线电控制制造出来的服从意志，另一边却突然冒出一种骇人的反叛冲动。倘若不是由于整个种族智力衰退，使无线电控制技术也严重退化，这种事情大概根本不会发生。在这场冲突的压力下，许多不幸的亚人类——因为这时的人已不再真正具有人性——精神崩溃或者死亡。另一些人成功抵抗了控制，加入叛乱者。一时间，似乎人类就要迎来一个重新燃起希望的时代。不幸的是，这些“复醒者”无法承受长期压力。只要事业进展顺利，他们一切正常；可每次遭受挫折，都会立刻出现大批自杀者。他们对生命的眷恋已经如此微弱。一个接一个的叛乱中心最终全部崩溃，直到再也没有一个留下。</p>
<p>人口问题依旧没有解决。另有一种方法也曾被尝试过，而且最初取得过一定成效。</p>
<p>在世界帝国中期偏早的年代，当创新尚未完全不可能时，一群生理学家和外科医生发明了一种方法，希望能够一劳永逸地解决问题。这项新技术可以算作真正“体外发生”（ectogenesis，即完全脱离母体孕育）的中间阶段。人们会从一名年轻女性体内取出子宫及其他必要器官，并用人工方法维持这些器官存活。提供这些珍贵器官、已经被残害的女性随后会被杀死；不过她的一部分血液会被接入人工循环系统，继续流经切除下来的器官，并作为向它们供应必要化学物质的介质。之后，子宫便可接受人工受精并孕育婴儿。利用各种技术手段，这一过程可以远比正常生殖快；而且每一次受孕都可以人为获得五胞胎。不幸的是，切除后的器官最多只能存活十年，因此必须持续获得年轻女性作为供体。</p>
<p>于是，政府开始因为最轻微的罪行便判处女性死刑，再把她们用于人工生殖。与此同时，政府还试图以某种方式教育女童，让她们长大以后主动渴望得到最高荣耀：牺牲自己的生命，让自己的子宫以更高的繁殖效率继续存在。这种宣传得到的响应令人失望。由于担心人口出现真正灾难性的崩溃，政府最终颁布法律：除神圣统治阶层的成员之外，每个女人到二十五岁时，都必须“为了孩子而献出自己的生命”。</p>
<p>不幸的是，这种人工繁殖方法需要极其精细的外科技术，而当它真正开始普及时，一流的操作智力已经进入衰退期。因此，被切除的子宫越来越多地无法在手术后存活；即使幸存，也越来越难以产生能够存活的婴儿。渐渐地，这个种族中仅存的少数自由心智能够看出：这种方法不仅没有增加人口，反而正在加速人口下降。但此时它已经成为神圣传统的一部分，不允许废除。因此，几十年间，人们仍继续实行这一制度，而结果越来越灾难性。</p>
<p>终于有一天，就连世界天朝帝国那些迟钝而被奴役的头脑也不得不意识到：如果人口下降不能迅速停止，文明就会解体。正统派与抗议者之间于是爆发了一场激烈斗争，最后双方达成妥协：每个年轻女性到二十五岁时，都必须在腹部接受一次仪式性的切割，整个过程配以适当的礼仪与咒语。人们相信，这样便能够提高她生殖器官的繁殖力，而不必真的把器官切除出来。</p>
<p>尽管做尽了一切，人口还是继续下降。我无法查明这个普遍过程的真正原因。问题根源也许是生理性的：某种化学物质的缺乏可能影响了生殖细胞；或者，人类遗传种质发生了某种微妙突变，使受孕变得更加困难；也可能是整个社会的神经质状态造成了不利于受孕的激素环境。但我倾向于相信，无论它最终通过哪一种物理机制发挥作用，真正根源仍是压在整个种族身上的那种深沉气馁与精神荒芜。</p>
<p>不论原因究竟是什么，世界人口持续萎缩，而且在这一过程中逐渐变成一个以中年人为绝对主体的种群。人数稀少的年轻人虽然受到珍爱与尊崇，却对政策决定完全没有影响。一个由衰弱与保守构成的冰河时代，以越来越强的力量笼罩了世界。</p>
</section>

<h2>ii. DECLINE OF POPULATION</h2>
<p>THE DECLINE OF world-population had started long ago after the period of rapid increase which took place in the early phase of industrialization. It was due partly to the widespread use of efficient contraceptive methods, partly to anxiety about economic insecurity, partly to a vague sense of the futility and falsity of civilization. In the rather tired Utopia of North America, where the decline was first seriously felt, insecurity cannot have been a cause, for prosperity was universal. But disillusionment about a curiously aimless Utopia was a serious factor in American life. The early totalitarian states had always feared decline of population, and had done their utmost to check it, but without much success. The newer totalitarian states, the Russian and Chinese Empires, and the World Empire in its early phase, had attacked the problem with characteristic ruthlessness.</p>
<p>The most obvious way to increase population was waken the hundreds of millions whom past governments had from time to time put into cold storage all over the world in order to solve the unemployment problem. There was at first great reluctance to do this, for a reason which reveals the incredible stupidity and superstition of the human race in this period. Declining population, far from solving the unemployment problem, had increased it. Demand was constantly declining. Mass- productive machinery could less easily be worked at a profit. Though the rulers saw clearly enough with one side of their minds that an increase in population was needed, on the other side they were painfully aware of the unemployment problem, and reluctant to add to the stagnant pool of potential labour. Consequently, though there was much discussion about the cold-storage houses, nothing was done. Meanwhile population continued to decline.</p>
<p>The governments tried to compel the peoples to reproduce. Women were educated to believe that their sole function was reproduction. Mothers were honoured in relation to the number of their offspring. Those produced fifteen or more babies were given the title ‘Prolific Mother’. Any who succeeded in launching twenty human beings were deified. Contraception was made illegal and condemned as immoral. In spite of all these measures the fertility-rate declined. In desperation the World Government tightened its grip on the women. Every girl was compelled to have intercourse with a man as soon as she was certified as mature. A month after certification she appeared before her medical board again and was examined to prove that she was no longer a virgin. If after three months she had not conceived, she was sent to an institution that combined the characters of a brothel and a stud-farm. If after another three months she still failed to conceive, she was subjected to medical and surgical treatment to cure her barrenness. If this also failed, she was publicly disgraced, appropriately tortured, and gradually killed.</p>
<p>After helplessly watching the decline of population for many decades, perhaps centuries, the World Government decided to take the obvious step, which, moreover, was sanctioned by scripture. For it was part of the sacred canon that some day, when there was great need of workers, the sleepers must be wakened. The rulers now declared that the time had come. In panic and without proper preparation it ordered the physiologists to thaw out the whole refrigerated multitude. The process was a delicate one, and the instructions left by an earlier and brighter generation were at first badly bungled. Millions were killed, or woke up to a brief period of misery and bewilderment, speedily followed by death. Millions more survived only for a life of permanent invalidism or insanity. The majority, however, though seriously damaged by their rough awakening, were fit for active life of a sort. But they had slept through much history. Their minds had been formed by a world long vanished. Their speech and thought were often so archaic that modern individuals could not understand them. Their limbs, and their minds too, moved at first with painful sluggishness. Their procreative impulses were apparently quenched. Moreover they gradually discovered that their new world was even less propitious than the old one. Some of them, when they had entirely thrown off the miasma of their age-long sleep and had painfully adjusted themselves to the new environment, proved to be rather more quick-witted than their normal neighbours in the new world. And, as they had not been brought up to accept the recent and more extravagant prejudices of the new world, they were generally very critical of the modern customs and institutions. In fact they soon became a grave nuisance to the authorities. The Government hastened to order that all the ‘reawakened’ should at once be fitted with radio control. This obvious precaution had been delayed less through fear of putting them to too great a strain before they had recovered from the effects of refrigeration, than out of an amazingly stupid reluctance to raise them to the rank of citizens. Millions were now subjected to the operation. Half of these died under the anaesthetic. Millions more put up a desperate resistance and had to be destroyed. Here and there, where there was a large concentration of the ‘reawakened’, they were able to seize power and set up a rebel state. The spectacle of human beings resisting authority was utterly bewildering to the robot citizens of the world-state. In many minds there arose an agonizing conflict between the orthodox radio-generated will and a shocking impulse to rebel. This would probably not have occurred had not the technique of radio-control seriously degenerated, owing to the general decline of intelligence. Many of the unfortunate sub-humans (for men were no longer human) went mad or died under the stress of this conflict. Some succeeded in resisting the control and joined the rebels. It almost appeared that an era of new hope was to begin for the human race. Unfortunately the ‘reawakened’ could not stand the strain. While their cause prospered, all was well with them, but every passing misfortune was accompanied by a great crop of suicides. So little heart had they for life. One by one the rebel centres collapsed, till none was left.</p>
<p>The population problem remained unsolved. One other method of coping with it had been tried, at first with some success.</p>
<p>In the early middle period of the world empire, while innovation was still possible, a group of physiologists and surgeons had devized a method which, it was hoped, would settle the matter for ever. The new technique was a. half-way stage towards true ectogenesis. The womb and other necessary organs were removed from a young woman and kept alive artificially. The mutilated donor of these precious organs was then destroyed, but part of her blood-stream was put into artificial circulation through the excised organs and used as the medium for supplying them with necessary chemicals. The womb could then be inseminated, and would produce an infant. By various technical methods the process could be made far more rapid than normal reproduction. Moreover quintuplets could be procured from every conception. Unfortunately the excised organs could not be kept alive for more than ten years, so it was necessary to have a constant supply of young women. The government therefore imposed the death penalty on women for the most trivial offences, and used them up for artificial reproduction. At the same time it tried to educate female children in such a way that when they reached maturity many would actually desire the supreme glory of sacrificing their lives so that their wombs might live on with enhanced fertility. The response to this propaganda was disappointing. In fear of a really catastrophic decline of population the government passed a law that every woman, except members of the sacred governing class, must ‘give her life for her children’s sake’ at the age of twenty-five.</p>
<p>Unfortunately the method of artificial reproduction involved a very delicate surgical technique, and it did not come into general use until first-class manipulative intelligence was already in decline. Increasingly, therefore, the excised wombs failed to survive the operation, or, if they did survive, failed to produce viable infants. Presently it became clear to the few free intelligences of the race that the method, far from increasing the population, was actually hastening its decline. But already the method had become part of the sacred tradition and could not be abandoned. For decades, therefore, it continued to be practised with increasingly disastrous results. There came a time, however, when even the dull and enslaved wits of the Celestial Empire could not but realize that if the decline of population was not quickly stopped civilization would disintegrate. A great struggle ensued between the orthodox and the protestants, until at last a compromise was agreed upon. At the age of twenty-five every young woman must receive a ceremonial cut on the abdomen, accompanied by suitable ritual and incantations. This, it was believed, would increase the fertility of her reproductive organs without the necessity of excising them.</p>
<p>In spite of everything, population continued to decline. I was not able to discover the cause of this universal process. Perhaps the root of the trouble was physiological. Some chemical deficiency may have affected the germ cells. Or again some subtle mutation of the human stock may have rendered conception less ready. Or perhaps the neurotic condition of the population had produced’ hormones unfavourable to conception. I am inclined to believe that the real cause, through whatever physical mechanism it took effect, was the profound disheartenment and spiritual desolation which oppressed the whole race.</p>
<p>Whatever the cause, the world-population continued to shrink, and in the process it became a predominantly middle-aged population. The small company of the young, though cherished and venerated, counted for nothing in decisions of policy. An ice-age of feebleness and conservatism gripped the world with increasing force.</p>
