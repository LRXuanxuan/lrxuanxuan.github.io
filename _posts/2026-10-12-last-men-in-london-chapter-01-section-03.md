<h2>iii. 生平札记</h2>
<p>说到这里，我似乎应该简单介绍一下自己，好让你们知道：此刻究竟是怎样一种存在在与你们通讯，他又是从怎样的角度观察你们。</p>
<p>按照你们地球上的计年法，我目前两万岁，因此仍处在人生的春季。我的父母是别人为我选择的。他们彼此早已十分亲密，但之所以决定让他们生下一个孩子，是因为人们认识到：社会需要一种只有他们两人结合才能产生的个体。那时，人类进入过去心灵的能力刚刚有了新的改进；同时，由于我们的世界即将毁灭，必须在此之前完成对过去的探索，这项工作也变得更加紧迫，于是社会对过去探索者的需求迅速增加。甚至在我受孕以前，我就已经被指定走上这条道路；而当我仍在母体内时，优生学家还不断对我施加影响，使我获得一些新的能力。</p>
<p>婴儿时期，我一直和母亲生活在一起。我一生中与她保持的亲密程度，比我们世界通常的母子关系更深。她既温柔，又有一种奇特而天真的冷酷；对我来说，她几乎就是“过去”与“原始性”本身的化身。而这两样东西始终深深迷住我。当然，在你们眼里，她绝不会显得原始；但在她全部理性与高度教养之下，我仍能察觉一种野性的气质。也许正因为我自己的性情过度文明、过度精细，我反而尤其喜欢在别人身上发现这种气质。</p>
<p>我那长达一千年的童年，和我的种族中其他孩子一样度过。大多数时候，我住在儿童寄宿俱乐部里。我们管理那里时，气势有余而效率不足；不过，在家务、游戏、争吵和多愁善感的依恋之间，我们还是设法打下了教育的基础。甚至在这么早的阶段，我对原始事物与过去的偏好就已经开始苏醒。别人在造玩具以太船、搭行星系模型时，我却去挖化石、泡博物馆、沉迷古老民间传说，还为虚构的古代世界写历史。在一切娱乐和一切学习之中，这个兴趣总会悄悄渗进来。</p>
<p>按照我们的习俗，我的第二个一千年是在一块受保护的大陆——“青年之地”——度过的。我们的年轻人在那里过着野蛮人和蛮族的生活，以此尽情挥霍青春。这个阶段，他们的性情确实还是少年式的。他们只欣赏那些即使最原始的人类物种也会推崇的蛮族美德。他们能够忠诚，但这种忠诚只能通过一场不安而英雄式的斗争，艰难战胜自我中心之后才能实现。他们的忠诚从来不会轻松自然，因为他们尚未发展出成年人的自我超脱。此外，他们只懂得欣赏凯旋的个体生命之美。那些构成成年男女主要精神关切的、属于宇宙整体的至高之美，对这些年轻生命仍然隐藏着。因此，我们的成年世界在很大程度上超出了他们的理解。</p>
<p>有几百年时间，我完全投入一种“红印第安人”式的生活，练成了弓箭高手，而且成为真正出色的追踪者。我还记得，自己曾爱上一个危险的金发年轻女战士。我们第一次相遇时是敌对关系。她逃走了，却把自己的标枪留在了我的肩膀里，也把自己的存在留在了我的心里。很久以后，等我们都进入成年世界，我又遇见了她。那时她已经开始自己的职业生涯，被选为我们的职业母亲之一。后来，她自愿参加一项极其危险的生育实验，身体因此遭到严重摧毁，最后优生学家不得不结束她的生命。</p>
<p>我进入青年之地还没有几百年，自己最主要的激情便开始显露。我着手搜集材料，打算写一部关于那个青年世界的完整历史。为此，我尝试了大陆各个角落的一切生活方式。我还通过与许多光彩夺目的年轻女子亲密交往来磨砺自己的观察力——至少当时我是这样说服自己的。不过，我的历史还远未写完，这项工作就开始不断被前往成年世界的访问打断。</p>
<p>青年之地里有一个派别，宣称青年世界比成年人的世界更加幸福、更加高贵。他们会在那些已经开始厌倦原始生活的人群中派遣传教者，努力说服这些人加入一个规模很小、颇为可怜的团体，名叫“老青年”。这个团体的成员选择永远留在青年之地。这些发展停滞的可怜人后来成了青年诸国传统与道德的守护者。他们很像村里的傻子：一面遭到所有人轻视，一面又被认为说出的每句话都蕴含神秘真理。由于我一向迷恋过去和原始事物，我自己也曾被说服，发誓加入这个团体。但当别人把我介绍给其中一个精神奕奕的“老青年”时，我立刻沮丧得改了主意。那一刻我才突然明白：我的任务并不是真正把自己变成原始人，而是研究原始人。我也因此领悟，就连过去和原始状态本身，也只有成熟完整的心灵才能比原始者自己理解得更深、爱得更深。</p>
<p>在青年之地最后一个世纪里，我们的男孩女孩通常会迅速超越幼体阶段，虽然这个过程伴随着严重的精神痛苦。因为这段时期，心灵正处在与自身的激烈冲突之中：它一方面依恋原始状态，另一方面又开始对它感到恶心；它向往成熟，却同时恐惧而抗拒。身体与心灵这场深刻的变态发育，即使年轻人进入成年世界以后，仍会持续数百年。原本简单的男性或女性性征，会进一步分化为九十六种亚性别中的某一种。智力会跃升到一个全新的能力层级。大脑中新出现的中枢会把整个神经系统整合得如此完善，以至从此以后，人的行为在任何情况下都能自然而然保持理性——或者用你们的话说，保持“道德”——无需再经历严重的内心搏斗。与此同时，心灵感应能力也会出现；由于持续不断的心灵感应交流不断累积，每个人都会对其他心灵形成极其精确的理解和鲜明的同感。于是，人与人之间的差异不但不会造成隔绝，反而只会增加每一个心灵自身的丰富性。</p>
<p>这种本性的彻底改变，会在数百年内把个体提升到一个全新的经验层次；而对我来说，由于天生渴求原始事物，这个过程格外痛苦。同样的性格还让我很难适应我们物种极其复杂的成年性生活。它以九十六种亚性别组成的婚姻群体为基础。我的婚姻群体形成得异常艰难；不过，一旦真正构成，它的维系倒无需过分用力。我们大约每隔一个世纪才会被冲动驱使着聚到一起，共同生活、共同相爱，而且每次也只持续一年左右。在这些时期，我们从彼此、也从自己身上获得一种精微的快乐。我们仍然保持有自我意识的独立个体，但与此同时，一个单一的“群体自我”也会从我们之中出现。</p>
<p>在共同生活期间，我们会花很多时间沉浸在只有这种性群体心灵才能产生的感知与思想里，尤其反复体会人格与超人格的细微层次，并为那个远比群体意识复杂得多、会周期性重现的意识模式——种族心灵——预先准备土壤。除了这些偶尔出现的群体心灵阶段以外，我们每个人都过着独立生活，对自己的群体成员身份只有模糊意识。然而，正如游泳之后，一种清新感会在一天剩余的时间里若有若无地使身体愉悦；又如晒过太阳以后，皮肤会长久保留一种微微发麻的幸福感；对我们而言，参与群体意识所带来的生命力提升，也可能持续几十年。</p>
<p>等我真正安定在自己的性群体中时，我大约三千岁。身体已经达到我们这里会永久保持的青年成熟状态，心灵也脱掉了青年之地的一切愚蠢。我开始为自己的专业工作做学徒，而那份工作几乎完全适合我的本性。工作要求很高，因为我身上那些新能力当时还没有被充分理解。不过，即便如此，我的“工作时间”在你们最激进的工会主义者看来，大概也短得可笑。每一个世纪中，真正让我的心灵离开当代世界、沉入过去的时间只有几年；我也只会再花十年或二十年的上午，把研究结果整理成文字。诚然，过去和我对过去的研究几乎始终萦绕在脑中，但我的绝大部分时间其实用在其他活动上。</p>
<p>一个又一个十年里，我往往只是观察我们庞大共同体里形形色色的活动：游历各个国家，设法与各种各样的人建立亲密关系；透过显微镜和天文仪器观察；观看发明如何在别人的心灵中诞生；研究优生学家为新世代制订的计划，或以太航行技术的最新改进。我有很多时间是在以太船上度过的，也有很多时间住在天王星殖民地，还有很多时间深入那座被矿井蛀成蜂窝的冥王星内部。有时，我会以客人的身份回到青年之地，花上几年，把我们自己的年轻人与过去的原始民族作比较。偶尔，我会连续十年主要投入某种创造性艺术的欣赏或实践；其中最多的是我们最高级的艺术，而我不得不勉强把它称作“心灵感应诗”。就像听觉诗歌借助声音一样，这门最精微的艺术利用我们对以太振动的直接感受能力，引发感觉意象与观念的节奏和结构。</p>
<p>另一些时候，我又会沉浸于某个特殊的自然科学问题，除了追踪科学工作者的实验以外几乎什么也不做。当然，我也把大量时间花在宇宙论上，同样有大量时间用于形而上学；在我的世界里，这两类问题会强迫每一个男人和女人给予关注。有时我会用一年左右几乎只重建房屋、料理花园；或者完全沉迷于为某个儿童朋友发明、制作一件玩具。又有些时候，我会连续几年把主要注意力放在与某个女人的亲密关系上；她可能来自我的民族，也可能来自别的民族。必须承认，我比大多数同类都更倾向于用这种方式让自己恢复活力。但我并不后悔。在这些肉体与心灵都短暂而抒情的结合中，我仿佛以一种格外直接的方式体验到：究竟是什么把高度发展的存在与原始存在联结在一起。</p>
<p>偶尔，我也像多数同伴一样，用大约十年时间独自漫游，把自己完全交给感官与本能生活。正是在这样一次独行中，我第一次遇见了那个被我称作“黑豹”的透明女子。我爱过许多人；但只有与她——我只在荒野中同她相见——有一种极其特殊的关系。因为我的精神永远转向过去，而她的精神永远转向未来；离开对方，我们任何一个都会变成空洞之物。</p>
<p>这种生活的巨大自由与多样性，以及闲暇远多于工作的状态，是我的世界普遍具有的特征。工程师、化学家，甚至农业工作者与以太航行员，花在自己专业任务上的时间，都少于花在观察他人生活上的时间。事实上，只有依靠这种相互观察的制度，再加上我们持续的心灵感应交流，我们才能维持对彼此的理解；而这种理解正是我们共同体的生命血液。在你们的世界，这种心灵之间的完美协调根本不可能实现。然而，你们彼此之间那些种族气质和后天偏见上的差异，与我们天生性情的巨大多样性相比，简直微不足道；而这种多样性既是我们永恒的危险，也是我们最主要的力量来源。</p>
<p>从心灵完全成熟、并且熟练掌握自己的专业开始，一直到如今两万岁的年纪，我的生活虽然事件不断，却总体平稳。当然，我也有自己的困难与焦虑、悲痛与胜利。我的两个最亲密朋友都死于一次以太船事故。还有另一个比我年轻的朋友，后来以一种极其奇异的方式发展；即使我已经尽最大努力通过心灵感应理解他，也仍不得不觉得他严重偏离常态，迟早不是遭遇毁灭，就是取得某种前所未有的辉煌。除了工作以外，这一直是我最严重的忧虑。</p>
<p>工作至今仍是我生命中最重要的因素。由于遗传，我获得了一些特殊天赋；我和同样具有天赋的同代工作者合作，把过去探索这门技艺提升到一个全新的能力范围。我的见习阶段用于研究海王星早期文明之一。此后，我已经对十八个人类物种中的每一个都取得相当丰富的第一手知识。另外，我还较为细致地专门研究过金星飞人，以及最后的地球人。不过，我绝大部分工作都花在第一人类身上，尤其花在你们此刻正在经历的这场特殊危机上。</p>
<p>除了工作本身的要求之外，还有两件事件给我的一生带来了深刻改变。但它们与其说具有私人意义，不如说具有公共意义，因为整个种族都同样迫切地受到它们影响。第一件，是我们第一次苏醒进入种族心灵；第二件，则是发现狂星，以及发现我们世界的末日。对我们所有人而言，这两场巨大事件把一种原本从容而不断胜利的进取生活，变成了某种更加神秘、更孕育着重大意义、也更加悲剧性的存在。</p>

<h2>iii. BIOGRAPHICAL NOTE</h2>
<p>AT THIS POINT it seems desirable to say a few words about myself, so that you may have some idea of the kind of being who is communicating with you, and the angle from which he is regarding you.</p>
<p>I am at present twenty thousand years old according to your Terrestrial reckoning, and therefore I am still in the spring-time of my life. My parents were chosen for me. They had long been intimate with one another, but the call to have a child arose from the knowledge that there was need of such a being as they together could produce. Recent improvements in man’s powers of entering into past minds, together with the new urgency for completing the exploration of the past before our own world should be destroyed, had increased the call for past-explorers. For such a career I was destined even before I was conceived; and while I was still in the womb, the eugenists were still influencing me so as to give me novel powers.</p>
<p>During infancy I remained with my mother. Throughout my life I have retained a closer intimacy with her than is common in my world; for with her tenderness and strange innocent ruthlessness she embodies for me the very spirit of the past and the primitive. And these have enthralled me always. Not that in your eyes she would have seemed primitive; but underlying all her reasonableness and sophistication I detect that savage temperament which, perhaps partly because of my own extreme sophistication, I so enjoy in others.</p>
<p>I spent my thousand years of childhood in the manner characteristic of my race. Mostly I lived in a children’s residential club. We managed it with more dash than efficiency; but in intervals between domestic duties, games, quarrels and sentimental attachments, we managed to lay the foundations of our education. Even at this early stage my predisposition toward the primitive and the past was beginning to wake. While others were making toy ether-ships and model planetary systems, I was digging for fossils, haunting museums, brooding on ancient folklore, and writing histories of imaginary past worlds. In all my recreations and in all my studies this interest was ever apt to insinuate itself.</p>
<p>My second thousand years was spent, as is customary with us, in the reserved continent called the Land of the Young. There our young people sow their wild oats by living as savages and barbarians. During this phase they are definitely juvenile in disposition. They appreciate only such barbarian virtues as were admired even by the most primitive human species. They are capable of loyalty, but only by an uneasy and heroic victory over self-regard. Their loyalties are never easeful, for they have not yet developed the self-detachment of the adult. Further, they care only for the beauties of triumphant individual life. The supernal beauties of the cosmos, which form the main preoccupation of grown men and women, are hidden from these young things. Consequently our adult world lies very largely beyond their comprehension.</p>
<p>For some centuries I gave myself wholly to a ‘Red Indian’ life, becoming a master of the bow and arrow, and a really brilliant tracker. I remember too that I fell in love with a dangerous young Amazon with golden hair. My first encounter with her was warlike. She escaped, but left her javelin in my shoulder, and her presence in my heart. Long afterwards, when we were both in the adult world, I met her again. She had by now entered upon her career, having been chosen as one of our professional mothers. Later still, she volunteered to submit to a very dangerous maternity experiment, and was so shattered by it, that the eugenists had finally to put her to death.</p>
<p>I had not been many centuries in the Land of the Young before my master passion began to assert itself. I set about collecting material for a complete history of that juvenile world. For this end I sampled every kind of life in every corner of the continent. I also sharpened my perception, or so I persuaded myself, by intimacy with many resplendent young women. But long before I had completed my history, the work began to be interrupted by visits to the adult world.</p>
<p>There is a party in the Land of the Young who preach that the juvenile world is happier and nobler than the world of the adults. They send missionaries among those whom they know to be turning from the primitive, and seek to persuade them to join a small and pathetic band called the Old Young, who elect to remain in the Land of the Young for ever. These poor arrested beings become the guardians of tradition and morality among the nations of the Young. Like the village idiot, they are despised even while at the same time their sayings are supposed to be pregnant with mysterious truth. I myself, with my taste for the past and the primitive, was persuaded to take the vow of this order. But when I was introduced to one of these bright old things, I was so depressed that I recanted. It came as a revelation to me that my task was not actually to become primitive, but to study the primitive. It was borne in on me that even the past and the primitive can be understood and loved better by the full-grown mind than by the primitive itself.</p>
<p>During their last century in the Land of the Young our boys and girls normally develop beyond the larval stage very rapidly, though not without severe mental agony. For during this period the mind is in bitter conflict with itself. It clings to the primitive, but is at the same time nauseated; it yearns toward the mature, but is at the same time fearful and reluctant. This profound metamorphosis of body and mind continues for some centuries even after the young person has entered the adult world. The simple male or female sexual characters specialize themselves into one or other of the ninety-six sub-sexes. Intelligence soars into a new order of proficiency. New centres of the brain integrate the nervous system so perfectly that henceforth behaviour will be, without exception, and without serious struggle, rational; or, as you would say, moral. At the same time the faculty of telepathic communication appears; and, through the cumulative effect of constant telepathic intercourse, the mind comes into such exact understanding and vivid sympathy with other minds, that the diversity of all serves but to enhance the mental richness of each.</p>
<p>This radical change of nature, which raises the individual in a few centuries to a new plane of experience, was for me the more torturing because of my innate hunger for the primitive. This same characteristic made it difficult for me to reconcile myself to the highly complex adult sexual life of my species, which is based on the marriage group of the ninety-six sub-sexes. My group formed itself with unusual difficulty; but, once constituted, it was maintained without undue emphasis. Only once in a century or so are we moved to come together, and live together, and love together; and then only for about a year. During these periods we take delicate joy in one another, and in ourselves. And though we remain self-conscious individuals, a single group-self emerges in us. We spend much of our time rapt in the perceptions and thoughts which are possible only to the minds of the sexual groups; brooding especially on the subtleties of personality and super-personality, and preparing the ground for the recurrent phases of that far more complex mode of consciousness, the Mind of the Race. Apart from these occasional periods of group-mentality, each of us lives an independent life, and is only obscurely conscious of his group-membership. Nevertheless, much as, after swimming, a freshness may vaguely cheer the body for the rest of the day, or after sunbathing a tingling beatitude, so for each of us participation in the group affords an enhanced vitality which may endure for many decades.</p>
<p>When I had settled into my sexual group, I was about three thousand years old. My body had attained that youthful maturity which with us is perennial, and my mind had shed all the follies of the Land of the Young. I was apprenticed to work which was perfectly suited to my nature. It was exacting work, for the novel powers with which I was endowed were as yet imperfectly understood. But my working hours would have seemed ridiculously short even in your most ardent trade-unionists. Only for a few years in every century was my mind actually absent from the contemporary world. Only for a decade or two would I devote my mornings to formulating the results of my research. Though inevitably the past, and my study of the past, would be nearly always in my thoughts, the great bulk of my time was occupied with other activities. For decade after decade I would merely watch the manifold operations of our great community, wandering into all countries, seeking intimacy with all sorts of persons, peering through microscopes and astronomical instruments, watching the birth of inventions in other minds, studying the eugenists’ plans for new generations, or the latest improvements in ethereal navigation. Much of my time was spent in ether-ships, much in the Uranian colonies, much in the interior of the mine-honeycombed Pluto. Sometimes I would go as a guest into the Land of the Young to spend years in comparing our own young things with the primitive races of the past. Occasionally I have been chiefly concerned for a decade at a time with the appreciation or practice of some creative art, most often with our supreme art, which I must reluctantly call ‘telepathic verse’. Somewhat as auditory verse uses sound, this most subtle of all arts uses our direct sensitivity to ethereal vibrations for the evocation of rhythms and patterns of sensory images and ideas. Sometimes, on the other hand, I have been absorbed in some special problem of natural science, and have done little but follow the experiments of scientific workers. Much of my time, of course, has been given to cosmology, and much to metaphysics; for in my world these subjects compel the attention of every man and woman. Sometimes for a year or so I would do little but rebuild my house or cultivate my garden; or I would become wholly absorbed in the invention or construction of some toy for a child friend. Sometimes my main preoccupation for several years has been intimacy with some woman, of my own nation or another. It must be confessed that I am more disposed to this kind of refreshment than most of my fellows. But I do not regret it. In these many brief lyrical matings of body and mind I seem to experience in a very special manner that which unites the developed and the primitive. Now and then, like most of my fellows, I spend a decade or so in lonely wandering, giving myself wholly to the sensuous and instinctive life. It was on one of these occasions that I first met the translucent woman whom I have called Panther. Many have I loved; but with her, whom I meet only in the wilderness, I have a very special relation. For whereas my spirit turns ever to the past, hers turns to the future; and each without the other is an empty thing.</p>
<p>This great freedom and variety of life, and this preponderance of leisure over work, are characteristic of my world. The engineer, the chemist, even the agriculturist and the ethereal navigator, spend less time at their own special tasks than in watching the lives of others. Indeed it is only by means of this system of mutual observation, augmented by our constant telepathic intercourse, that we can preserve that understanding of one another which is the life-blood of our community. In your world this perfect accord of minds is impossible. Yet your differences of racial temperament and of acquired bias are as nothing beside the vast diversity of innate disposition which is at once our constant danger and our chief strength.</p>
<p>From the time when I had reached full maturity of mind, and had become expert in my special calling, up to my present age of twenty thousand Years, my life has been even, though eventful. I have of course had my difficulties and anxieties, my griefs and my triumphs. I lost my two closest friends in an ether-ship disaster. Another friend, younger than myself, has developed in such a strange manner that, though I do my best to understand him telepathically, I cannot but feel that he is seriously abnormal, and will sooner or later either come to grief or achieve some novel splendour. This has been my most serious anxiety apart from my work.</p>
<p>Work has been by far the most important factor in my life. Peculiarly gifted by inheritance, I have co-operated with the equally gifted fellow-workers of my generation to raise the art of past exploration to a new range of power. My novitiate was spent in studying one of the earlier Neptunian civilizations. Since then I have acquired considerable first-hand knowledge of every one of the eighteen human species. In addition I have specialized in some detail on the Venetian Flying Men and the Last Terrestrials. But the great bulk of my work has been done among the First Men, and upon the particular crisis which you can present.</p>
<p>Apart from the exigencies of my work, two events have produced profound changes in my life; but they were events of public rather than private significance, and have affected the whole race with equal urgency. One was the first occasion on which we awakened into the racial mentality. The other was the discovery of the Mad Star, and of the doom of our world. For all of us these two great happenings have changed a life of serenely victorious enterprise into something more mysterious, more pregnant, and more tragic.</p>
