<h2>iii. 对保罗的早期实验</h2>
<p>直到保罗已经上学以后，我才开始有意识地对他施加影响。我的目的，是让他在刚刚成年时便尽可能完整地理解海王星人看待地球事务的方式。保罗年幼时，我极少干预他；但那几次罕见的干预，却都给他留下了深刻影响。它们的设计目的，是把他的意识强度提升到你们物种通常的限度以外。我刺激并精细控制他的注意力，使他偶尔会突然意识到一些第一人类一般无法察觉的材料。要概括我早期在他身上做的工作，可以这样说：我迫使他比常人更鲜明地感受到知觉对象的真实与独特，感受到自己的真实与独特，感受到别人也是“自我”；同时也让他更强烈地体会到自己与他人在深层上的统一，以及自己参与整个宇宙这一事实。</p>
<p>先从最简单的一件事说起。有一次，保罗找到一块奇特的绿色小石子。他捡起来专心端详，高兴地注意它的纹理、圆滑的弧面和坚硬质感。他把石子翻来覆去，用手指摩挲，又敲了敲。就在这时，我忽然向他的意识灌入一阵与之相关的视觉和触觉意象，使他仿佛在这一块小石子里遭遇了视觉的全部辉煌和触觉的全部锋利意味。那东西突然以一种毫无遮掩的真实扑向他。他经验中的变化，强烈得就像从想象一下朋友的脸，突然变成真正面对面看见那张脸；或者像从仅仅构造一个影像，跃迁到真正的知觉。此前那份当时看来已经足够鲜明的经验，事后竟像只是对一块石子的朦胧想象；而后来的经验才像真正接触到了一个客观实在。</p>
<p>这一切只持续了一瞬。保罗扔掉石子，瘫倒在草地上，双手抱住脑袋。有一刻，他以为有人朝自己扔了一块石头，而且那石头竟直接穿过了他的头。他睁开眼睛，一切又恢复正常。接着，他看见那块石子半藏在一片蒲公英叶子下面。它只是一个绿色的、圆滚滚的小东西，可仿佛意味深长地望着他。保罗和石子彼此“凝视”时，他忽然又觉得那块石子朝自己猛冲过来。他立刻捂住眼睛，坐了很久，脑子里除了仿佛要裂开的头痛以外什么也没有。这次经历确实把他伤得不轻，但同时又使他着迷。它有点像他早已遗忘的出生经验：冲击、疼痛、耀眼的新奇、对未知的恐惧和兴奋，全都混在一起。</p>
<p>接下来两天，保罗因为所谓“胆汁性头痛”卧床不起。恢复以后，他特意去找那块石子。他再次拿起它时谨慎得仿佛抓起一颗活炸弹。石子强烈唤起了那次经历的记忆，却没有再引发新的体验。</p>
<p>此后，我隔很长时间才重复一次这个实验，而且每次选的对象都非常不同：比如他珍爱的折刀，一阵钟声，一只飞过天空的鹰，甚至他自己的手。最后这个实验让他足足躺了一星期，因为他不仅像观察那块石子一样从外部知觉了自己的手，还从内部知觉到了它；更重要的是，他第一次被自己对这只手的意志控制迷住了。那是一只略显肮脏的褐色小手，指甲边缘发黑，布满许多划痕。他把手翻来覆去，弯曲、伸直手指，满怀信心地“命令”它做出各种动作。他极其强烈地意识到自己的意志命令，却还没有在更深意义上形成自我意识。</p>
<p>在我的影响下，整个童年时期，保罗不断经历这类事件。渐渐地，他习惯了它们，并以一种恐惧和期待混合的心情等着下一次发生。终于，我认为他已经成熟到可以接受另一类实验了。</p>
<p>当时，保罗非常想得到一个同伴拥有的微型青铜狗。他试着劝对方拿它来“交换”，却没有成功。他越来越执迷于那个小玩意，最后干脆把它偷了。后来，他正在自己家里怀着罪恶感摆弄这只青铜狗时，我对他玩了一个把戏。我让他看到一个极度鲜明而细致的视觉意象：他的朋友正在自己的玩具堆里疯狂翻找那只丢失的狗。这个形象来势太强，竟完全抹去了保罗真实的周围环境；而且它也具有此前那块石子第一次显现时那种增强过的现实感。</p>
<p>保罗的反应很奇怪。有一会儿，他死死盯着这个幻象，心里却涌起越来越强烈的仇恨。等幻象一消失，他立刻冲到街上，把那只小青铜狗从一个下水道格栅扔了进去。从那以后，他对原来那个朋友表现出惊人的恶意，而那恶意里又始终混着恐惧。</p>
<p>直到保罗已经进入少年期相当一段时间以后，我才开始干预他那渐渐萌生的自我意识。有一天，他和最喜欢的同学一起洗完澡。两个男孩赤裸着身体，湿漉漉地，在阳光下发亮，为了一条毛巾友好地争抢起来。他们朝相反方向拉扯，大喊大叫，气喘吁吁。忽然，两个人一起停住，彼此直视对方的眼睛。</p>
<p>就在这一刻，我重新点燃了保罗此前一切模糊意识：关于自己和别人之间有区别，关于“我”和“你”的那些朦胧感受。我仿佛把这堆经验里原本散乱的微光全部聚焦到当前这一瞬。过去，那块石子和他的手曾以压倒性的真实撞进他的意识；现在，他的朋友和他自己的存在，也以同样方式向他袭来。而这次的“真实”不再只是视觉和触觉形态的真实，也包括了“正在体验”这一事实本身。</p>
<p>通过我对他意象的操纵，他似乎在想象中进入了另一个人的心灵，感受到一个不同的存在怎样活在一个不同的身体里。一只苍蝇落在对方脖子上，保罗仿佛也感觉到那阵发痒，以及随即把苍蝇甩开的快速扭动。透过另一个人的眼睛，他似乎看见了自己那张认真用力的脸，以及自己轻轻拉住毛巾的手臂。他开始喃喃自语：“我，保罗；你，迪克；我，迪克；你，保罗。”又仿佛透过迪克的眼睛，看见自己的嘴唇正在动；透过迪克的耳朵，也同时透过自己的耳朵，听见自己的声音。</p>
<p>与此同时，迪克看见保罗的神情突然变得异样专注，不由得害怕起来。保罗看见了迪克的恐惧，而且几乎亲身体验到了那份恐惧。两个人都松开了毛巾。保罗伸出一只手去摸迪克的肩膀，就像从前摸那块石子一样。“真怪！”他说，“我醒过来了。我有时候会醒过来。不过以前从没像这次这么彻底。”迪克开始追问，可保罗还在继续：“就像我突然真的醒成了我自己。然后我又成了你。然后我们两个又同时都是我。你也发生了吗？”迪克困惑地摇摇头。紧接着，头痛袭来。保罗倒下去，双手捂住眼睛，低声呜咽起来。</p>
<p>下一次实验发生在保罗和迪克的一次激烈争吵中。保罗用一个空闲的下午造小船，正借用迪克的凿子把船体挖空。那把凿子已经逾期很久没还，而迪克现在也需要它来挖自己的船，于是过来索要。保罗一心想在喝茶以前把活干完，拒绝交出工具。他们一边争吵，保罗一边继续干活。迪克试着阻止他。</p>
<p>就在这时，我强迫保罗鲜明地感知另一个男孩，感知他想拿回工具的强烈愿望，以及他对保罗的愤怒。保罗停下了手里的工作。又有那么一瞬，他仿佛真的成了迪克，并透过迪克的眼睛看见自己那张丑陋的怒脸。保罗在精神冲突中僵住了一会儿。然后突然，他用凿子猛扎迪克的手。两个男孩同时因为迪克的疼痛尖叫起来。保罗转身冲向门口，边跑边喊：“畜生！从我身体里滚出去，你的味道恶心死了！”还没跑到门边，他就吐了。随后他尖叫着逃走，而且把凿子也带走了。</p>
<p>保罗这种反应非常具有你们物种的典型性。他短暂看见了另一个人的立场，得到的并不是理解与宽容，反而只是怨恨。后来的悔恨同样典型。这次实验之后，他病了一阵，而悔恨一直折磨着他。迪克则彻底被吓坏了。在他看来，保罗显然发疯了。</p>
<p>不过，过了一段时间，两人的友谊恢复了，甚至比原来更深。因为从那以后，保罗对迪克表现出一种远远超过他年龄的温和与理解。此后我偶尔仍会诱导保罗获得那种对朋友和自己的更鲜明感知，但当他们的利益发生强烈冲突时，我会非常小心地避免这样做。</p>
<p>通过这种方式，我使保罗获得了一种在你们物种中非常罕见的自我洞察，以及对他人的洞察。他那些稀有而异常的经验，从此给整段生活染上了颜色，并在他体内播下一颗种子：一场将持续终生的冲突，一边是符合他这个物种天性的行为方式，另一边则是增强后的洞察要求他采取的行为方式。每当他不得不在两者之间选择，他几乎总是选择较卑劣的那一种；事后却又陷入强烈悔恨。不过，也有一两次事情并非如此。我举一个例子。</p>
<p>保罗属于那种胆小的男孩，对恶霸来说简直时时构成诱惑。只要学校里发生欺凌，而保罗自己没有卷进去，他总会特别小心地不去干涉。有时甚至会主动讨好占上风的强者。</p>
<p>一个冬日，保罗和班上另外几名学生本应在做所谓“预习”，班里最大的恶霸却开始折磨一个保罗本人非常讨厌的小犹太男孩。几个较弱的男孩——保罗也在其中——站在旁边看，时不时紧张地咯咯笑两声，表示赞赏。我选择了这一刻进行实验。</p>
<p>保罗惊恐地感觉到，自己那该死的想象力又开始运作了。他拼命低头钻进法语练习里，却没有用。无论怎样抵抗，他还是不由自主地在想象中进入“那个讨厌的小犹太鬼”体内。接着，他不仅成了那个犹太男孩，也开始成为那个恶霸。他想象那个高大的蠢货内心，其实也有一种无能为力的自我厌恶——保罗自己仅有的一次欺负别人时，也曾尝过这种滋味。</p>
<p>我不断强化这份想象，直到保罗对那恶霸心灵的把握，竟远胜于恶霸本人。他鲜明地意识到，在那一大块肉体里，实际上进行着一种迟钝、摸索、模糊痛苦而又害怕的生命活动；甚至连这个人自己，都用一副吹牛逞强的姿态把它遮盖起来。</p>
<p>突然，保罗用一种清楚却仿佛做梦般的声音开口说道：“住手吧，威廉斯，你是在伤害你自己。”四周忽然一片惊愕的沉默。保罗本人也被自己的声音吓坏了，赶紧低头伏在法语书上。恶霸说：“下一个就轮到你。”随后又继续折磨自己的受害者。那孩子尖叫起来。</p>
<p>保罗站起身，穿过房间，把手放到恶霸肩膀上，立刻被一下甩飞，重重撞进壁炉边的煤灰里。他坐在煤渣中停了一会儿，听着周围一片大笑。然后慢慢站起身，又慢慢环视这些咧嘴嘲笑的同类。威廉斯故意把那个犹太男孩的手臂又扭紧一圈，随即又是一声惨叫。</p>
<p>保罗平静地说：“威廉斯，你必须停下。”说完，他竟把自己的左手伸进火里，捡起一块已经完全烧得通红的大煤块。他无视疼痛，也无视自己皮肉烧焦的气味，径直走到威廉斯面前，说：“现在滚开，不然我就给你烙个印。”有人试图抓住保罗的手臂，他立刻让那块煤发挥了效果，对方只好退开。随后，他直接朝威廉斯冲过去。恶霸逃了。</p>
<p>我感觉保罗的眉毛扬起来，嘴唇扭出一个古怪的微笑。他站在那里发了一会儿呆，然后把那块仍隐隐发红的煤重新放回火中。紧接着，他突然看见了自己的手——已经焦黑、冒烟，几乎成了一团烂肉。从一开始，他其实始终感觉得到疼，只是一直平静地把它忽略掉了。可事情如今既然结束，再想忽略已经不可能。那阵奇异的精神亢奋顿时消失。他猛地尖叫一声，一半因为疼，一半因为惊讶，随后昏倒。</p>
<p>几星期后，保罗的手恢复了，当然，从此永远留下伤疤。真正受影响更深的是他的心灵。他开始害怕自己。他觉得刚才仿佛被某种东西附了体，而且非常恐惧，将来还会不会被卷进更加糟糕的乱子里。不过，他同时也多少有点为自己骄傲，甚至有一半希望：要是自己永远都能成为刚才那样的人就好了。</p>

<h2>iii. EARLY EXPERIMENTS ON PAUL</h2>
<p>NOT UNTIL P AUL was already at school did I begin to exercise a deliberate influence over him. My aim was to prepare him to attain in early maturity as complete an insight as possible into the Neptunian view of Terrestrial affairs. In his early years I seldom influenced him, but the rare occasions on which I did so had a deep effect on him. They were designed to intensify his consciousness beyond the normal limitations of his species. By goading and delicately controlling his attention I contrived to make him startlingly aware, now and again, of data which commonly lie beyond the apprehension of the First Men. The scope of my early work upon him might be described by saying that I forced him to a more vivid sense of the reality and uniqueness of perceptual objects, of himself, of others as selves, and also of his own underlying unity with others, and of his own participation in the cosmos.</p>
<p>For instance, to deal with the simplest matter first, Paul once found a curious green pebble. He picked it up and examined it intently, noting with delight its veining, its smooth curvature, its rigidity. He turned it about, fingered it, tapped it. Then suddenly I flooded him with a spate of relevant imagery, visual and tactual, so that he seemed to encounter in this one pebble the whole splendour of sight, the whole poignancy of touch. The thing suddenly leapt at him with its unveiled reality. The change in his experience was as impressive as the change from imagery to actual perception, from picturing a friend’s face to actually seeing it. It was as though the earlier experience, which at the time seemed normally vivid, had been after all but a shadowy imagination of a pebble, compared with the later experience, which was an actual apprehension of an objective reality. It was over in a moment. Paul dropped the pebble and collapsed on the grass, holding his head in his hands. For a moment he thought someone had thrown a pebble at him and that it had gone right through his head. He opened his eyes. Everything seemed ordinary again. Then he caught sight of the pebble, half-hidden under a dandelion leaf. It was just a little round green thing, but it seemed to look at him meaningly. As they regarded one another, Paul and the pebble, Paul felt the pebble rushing at him again. He covered his eyes and sat for a long while thinking of nothing but his splitting head. He had been rather badly hurt, but also he was intrigued. The effect was something like his forgotten experience of birth, a confusion of shock, pain, brilliant novelty, fear of the unknown, and exhilaration.</p>
<p>Paul spent the next two days in bed with a ‘bilious headache’. When he was recovered, he went in search of his pebble. He picked it up almost as though it were a live bomb, but though it raised a vivid memory of the adventure, it gave him no fresh experience.</p>
<p>I repeated this experiment on Paul at rare intervals and in relation to very diverse objects, such as his precious knife, the tolling of a bell, a flying hawk, and his own hand. The last entailed a whole week in bed, for not only had he perceived his hand externally as he had perceived the pebble, he had also perceived it from within; and moreover he had been fascinated by his volitional control of it. It was a little grubby brown hand with black-rimmed nails and many scratches. He turned it about, bending and straightening the fingers, confidently willing it to perform various antics, intensely aware of his own fiat, yet not at all self-conscious in any deeper sense.</p>
<p>Through my influence Paul continued to suffer these experiences throughout his childhood. He became accustomed to them, and to look forward to them with mingled dread and zest. At length I judged him ripe for a different kind of experiment. He had been coveting a minute bronze dog which belonged to one of his companions. He tried to persuade the owner to ‘swop’ it, but in vain. Paul became more and more obsessed, and finally stole the dog. While he was guiltily playing with it in his own house I played a trick on him. I gave him an intense and detailed visual image of his friend rummaging frantically among his toys for the lost dog. So aggressive did this image become that it blotted out Paul’s actual environment. It had, moreover, the intensified reality that he had first encountered in the pebble. Paul’s reaction was strange. For a moment he stared at the hallucination, while hatred welled within him. Then, as soon as the vision had faded, he rushed into the street and dropped the little bronze figure down a grid. Henceforth he treated his former friend with shocking spite, mingled with terror.</p>
<p>I did not attempt to influence Paul’s slowly dawning awareness of self till he was already well advanced in boyhood. One day, after he had been bathing with his favourite schoolfellow, the two boys, naked, wet, gleaming in the sunlight, had a friendly wrangle over a towel. They tugged in opposite directions, shouting and panting. For a moment they stood still, staring into one another’s eyes. Suddenly I re-kindled in Paul all his past groping realizations of the difference between himself and the other, all his past obscure awareness of ‘me’ and ‘you’. I focused, so to speak, the whole vague illumination of this mass of experience upon the present moment. As formerly the pebble and his hand, so now his friend and his own being, impinged upon him with overwhelming reality. It was a reality not alone of visual and tactual form but also of experiencing. Through my manipulation of his imagery he seemed to enter imaginatively into the mind of the other, to have the feel of a different being, in a different body. A fly settled on the other’s neck. Paul felt it tickle, and felt the quick contortion that sent it away. Through the other’s eyes he seemed to see his own intent face, and his own arms lightly pulling at the towel. He began murmuring to himself, ‘I, Paul; you, Dick; I, Dick; you, Paul.’ And through Dick’s eyes he seemed to see his own lips moving. Through Dick’s ears and through his own ears also he heard his own voice. Dick, meanwhile, had seen the sudden intensity of Paul’s expression, and was alarmed. Paul saw, and vividly experienced, Dick’s alarm. They dropped the towel. Paul reached out a hand and felt Dick’s shoulder, as formerly he had felt the pebble. ‘Odd!’ he said, ‘I’ve waked up. I do sometimes. But never quite this way before.’ Dick began to question, but Paul went on, ‘I sort-of woke to be really me. Then I was you. And both of us at once. Did it happen to you too?’ Dick shook his head, very perplexed. Then came the headache. Paul dropped, and lay whimpering, with his hands over his eyes.</p>
<p>My next experiment was performed during a violent quarrel between Paul and Dick. Paul had spent a free afternoon making a boat. He was hollowing it with a gouge borrowed from Dick. Dick arrived to demand the gouge, which was long overdue, and was needed for hollowing his own boat. Paul, intent on his work and eager to finish it before tea, refused to surrender the tool. They wrangled, while Paul carried on his work. Dick tried to stop him. At this moment I forced on Paul a vivid apprehension of the other boy, and of his craving to have his tool, and of his rage against Paul. Paul stopped working. Once more he seemed for a moment to be Dick, and to see his own ugly scowl through Dick’s eyes. For a moment Paul hung paralysed in a mental conflict. Then suddenly he jabbed Dick’s hand with the gouge. Both boys screamed with Dick’s pain. Paul rushed toward the door, crying, ‘Beast, get out of me, you taste horrid.’ Before he reached the door he vomited. Then he fled, yelling. He carried off the gouge.</p>
<p>This reaction of Paul’s was very typical of his species. His brief insight into the other’s point of view merely filled him with resentment. Typical also was his subsequent remorse, which tortured him during his period of illness after this experiment. Dick had been thoroughly frightened. It seemed to him that Paul had gone mad.</p>
<p>In time, however, the friendship was restored, and even deepened; for Paul now treated Dick with a gentleness and understanding beyond his years. Occasionally I induced in him the more vivid apprehension of his friend and himself, but I carefully refrained from doing so when their interests were violently opposed.</p>
<p>In this way I produced in Paul a self-insight and insight into others which is very unusual in your species. His rare abnormal experiences now coloured his whole life, and sowed in him the seed of a lifelong conflict between the behaviour natural to his kind and the behaviour which his enhanced insight demanded. When he found himself forced to choose between the two, he almost always acted in the baser way, though afterwards he was bitterly remorseful. Once or twice, however, things turned out differently. I will give one example.</p>
<p>Paul was one of those timid boys who is a constant temptation to the bully. When any bullying was afoot in which Paul himself was not involved, he always took care not to interfere. Sometimes he would actually try to ingratiate himself with the stronger party. One winter day at school, when Paul and a few others of his form were supposed to be doing their’ preparation’, the arch bully of the form began tormenting a little Jew whom Paul intensely disliked. The smaller boys, with Paul among them, looked on, applauding now and then with anxious giggles. I chose this moment for an experiment. With dismay Paul felt his cursed imagination beginning to work. He tried to bury himself in his French exercise, but vainly. Do what he would, he could not help entering imaginatively into ‘that beastly little Jew’. Then he started being not only the Jew but the bully also. He imagined the big lout feeling the helpless self-disgust which he himself had felt in his one experiment in the art of bullying. I worked up this imagination till he really apprehended the mind of the bully far better than the bully himself. He realized vividly the drowsy, fumbling, vaguely distressed and frightened life that was going on in that hulk of flesh, and was disguised even from itself by the pose of braggartism. Suddenly Paul said aloud in a clear but dreamy voice, ‘Chuck it, Williams, you’re hurting yourself.’ There was a sudden silence of amazement. Paul, terrified at his own voice, bowed over his French book. The bully remarked, ‘Your turn next’, and then applied himself once more to his victim, who squealed. Paul rose, crossed the floor, laid a hand on the bully’s shoulder, and was sent crashing into the hearth. He sat for a moment among the cinders listening to the general laugh. Then slowly he rose, and slowly surveyed his grinning fellow-mortals. Williams gave an extra twist to the Jew’s arm, and there followed a yell, Paul, saying quietly, ‘Williams, you’ve got to stop’, put his own left hand into the fire and picked out a large completely redhot coal. Ignoring the pain and the smell of his burning flesh, he went over to Williams and said, ‘Now, clear out or I’ll brand you,’ Someone tried to seize Paul’s arm, He used his coal with effect, so that the other retired, Then he rushed at Williams, The bully fled. I felt Paul’s eyebrows rise and his lips twist in a whimsical smile, He stood dreaming for a moment, then restored the still faintly glowing coal to its place in the fire, Suddenly he caught sight of the charred and smoking mess that was his hand. All along he had been aware of the pain, but he had calmly ignored it. But now that the incident was completed, ignoring was impossible. His strange exaltation vanished. He gave one sharp scream, half for pain, half for surprise, and then fainted. Paul’s hand recovered within a few weeks, though of course it remained scarred for ever. Paul’s mind was more deeply affected. He was terrified about himself. He felt that he had been possessed, and he dreaded being let in for even worse scrapes in the future. All the same, he was rather proud of himself, and half-wished he could be like that always.</p>
