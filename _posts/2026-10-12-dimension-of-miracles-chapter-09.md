<h2>第九章</h2>
<p>“宇宙中最根本的事实，”梅利克隆说，“就是一个物种吃另一个物种。它也许不怎么好看，但事实就是如此。进食是最基本的活动，获取食物又是一切其他现象的基础。这个概念涉及‘捕食法则’，可以表述如下：任何给定物种，无论高等还是低等，都会以一个或多个物种为食，同时也会被一个或多个物种捕食。</p>
<p>“这描述的是一种普遍情形，而各种环境因素可以使它恶化，也可以使它缓和。举例来说，一个物种待在自己的原生栖息地时，通常能够维持一种‘平衡’，从而即便不断遭受捕食者侵害，也能活完正常寿命。这种平衡通常用‘胜者—败者方程’，也就是 VV 来表示。当一个物种或某个物种成员迁移到陌生的异域栖息地时，VV 的数值必然改变。有时候，该物种的‘吃—被吃状态’会暂时改善，即 Vv=Ee+1。更常见的情况则是恶化，即 Vv=Ee−1。</p>
<p>“卡莫迪，你身上发生的就是这种事。你离开了自己的正常栖息地，也就等于离开了自己的正常捕食者。在这里，没有汽车会尾随猎杀你，没有病毒会偷偷钻进你的血液，也没有警察会误把你一枪打死。你与地球上的危险隔绝，同时又对银河其他物种的危险具有免疫力。</p>
<p>“遗憾的是，这种改善——Vv=Ee+1——只是暂时的。那条铁一般不可动摇的平衡规则已经开始重新发挥作用。你无法拒绝狩猎，也无法逃脱被狩猎。捕食本身就是必然性。</p>
<p>“你离开地球之后，成了一个独一无二的生物；因此，你的捕食者也必然独一无二。</p>
<p>“你的捕食者，是宇宙法则经过人格化与实体化之后诞生的。这个捕食者只以你为食，而且只能以你为食。它的形态被塑造成与你的特征相互响应、彼此补足的样子。即使不亲眼看到它，我们也能知道：它的颌部专门适合咬卡莫迪；它的肢体关节专门适合抓住并攫取卡莫迪；它的胃拥有一种特殊而独有的能力，可以消化卡莫迪；它的性格则被设计成能够利用‘卡莫迪式人格’的弱点。</p>
<p>“你的处境使你变得独一无二，卡莫迪；所以你的捕食者也独一无二。追赶你的，就是属于你的死亡，卡莫迪；而它追赶你的绝望程度，与你逃命时的绝望完全相等。你和它被绑在一起。如果它抓到你，你就死；如果你成功逃回自己世界那些正常的威胁之中，那么你的捕食者就会因为得不到‘卡莫迪式营养’而饿死。</p>
<p>“关于怎样躲开它，我已经没有更多有用的话可说。我无法预测它会使用什么诡计和伪装，就像我无法预测你会使用什么诡计一样。我只能警告你：概率始终偏向猎手，尽管猎物成功逃脱也并非前所未闻。</p>
<p>“情况就是这样，卡莫迪。你听明白了吗？”</p>
<p>卡莫迪猛地一震，像一个从沉睡中被叫醒的人。</p>
<p>“明白，”他说。“你说的每句话我并不全懂，但重要的部分我懂了。”</p>
<p>“很好，”梅利克隆说。“因为我们已经没有时间。你必须立刻离开这颗行星。即使我身处自己的行星，也无法阻止宇宙性的‘捕食法则’。”</p>
<p>“你能把我送回地球吗？”卡莫迪问。</p>
<p>“如果有足够时间，我大概能做到，”梅利克隆说。“当然，如果给我足够时间，我什么都能做到。这很困难，卡莫迪。首先，三个 W 变量必须彼此联立求解。我得精确确定你的行星此刻位于时空中的‘何处’；然后还得查明，在那些不同概率分支的地球中，‘哪一个’才是你的地球；接着还要找出你出生所属的时间序列，从而确定‘何时’。除此之外，还有斯科里什效应和倍增因子，两者都必须计入。把这些全做完以后，只要运气稍微好一点，我就可以把你塞回属于你自己的那一份‘特定性’里——这其实是个出乎意料地精细的操作——同时又不至于把整个体系弄垮。”</p>
<p>“你能替我做这些吗？”卡莫迪问。</p>
<p>“不能。已经没有时间了。不过我可以把你送去找莫兹利。他算是我的一个朋友，应该能帮你。”</p>
<p>“你的朋友？”</p>
<p>“嗯，也许不能准确地说是‘朋友’，”梅利克隆说。“其实更接近熟人。甚至连这么说，可能都夸大了我们的关系。你看，很久以前有一次，我差一点离开自己的行星出去观光；如果我真去了，就会碰见莫兹利。不过后来由于各种原因，我没有出发，所以事实上从来没见过他。但我们双方都知道，如果我当时去旅行，我们就会相遇，会交换彼此的看法与世界观，会吵上一两架，讲几个笑话，最后对彼此产生一点温和的好感。”</p>
<p>“靠这么薄弱的一层关系去麻烦人家，好像有点勉强。”卡莫迪说。“你不能把我送给别的什么人吗？”</p>
<p>“恐怕不能，”梅利克隆说。“莫兹利是我唯一的朋友。你要知道，概率与现实一样可以界定亲缘和好感。我相信莫兹利会好好照顾你。”</p>
<p>“可是——”卡莫迪刚要说。就在这时，他注意到自己左肩后方有某种巨大、黑暗而充满威胁的东西正在逐渐成形。他立刻明白，自己的时间彻底用完了。</p>
<p>“我去！”他说。“还有，谢谢你做的一切！”</p>
<p>“不必谢我，”梅利克隆说。“我在宇宙中的职责，就是为陌生人服务。祝你好运，卡莫迪！”</p>
<p>那个巨大而危险的形体正在逐渐凝固；不过在它彻底成形以前，卡莫迪已经消失了。</p>
</section>
<section class="stapledon-body" data-story-lang="en" lang="en" aria-label="English original" hidden>
<p><b>CHAPTER 9</b></p>
<p>‘The most fundamental fact in the Universe,’ said Melichrone, ‘is that species eat other species. It may not be pretty, but there it is. Eating is basic, and the acquisition of foodstuffs underlies all other phenomena. This concept involves the Law of Predation, which can be stated as follows: any given species, no matter how high or how low, feeds upon one or more species and is fed upon by one or more species.</p>
<p>‘That sets forth a universal situation, which can be aggravated or ameliorated by a variety of circumstances. For example, a species resident in its own habitat can usually maintain itself in a state of Equilibrium, and thus live out its normal lifetime despite the depredations of predators. This Equilibrium is usually stated as the Victor-Vanquished equation, or VV. When a species or a species member moves to an alien and exotic habitat, the VV values necessarily change. Occasionally, there is temporary improvement in the species’s Eat-Eaten Situation (Vv=Ee plus 1). More typically, there is a deterioration (Vv=Ee minus 1).</p>
<p>‘That is what has happened to you, Carmody. You have left your normal habitat, which also means that you have left your normal predators. No automobiles can stalk you here, no virus can creep into your bloodstream, no policeman can shoot you down by mistake. You are separated from the dangers of Earth, and immune to the dangers of other galactic species.</p>
<p>‘But the amelioration (Vv=Ee plus 1) is sadly temporary. The ironclad rule of Equilibrium has already begun to assert itself. You cannot refuse to hunt, and you cannot escape being hunted. Predation is Necessity itself.</p>
<p>‘Having left Earth, you are a unique creature; therefore your predator is unique.</p>
<p>‘Your predator was born out of a personification and solidification of universal law. This predator can feed exclusively and solely on you. The creature is shaped as a respondent and complement to your characteristics. Even without seeing it, we can know that its jaws are shaped to bite Carmodys, its limbs are articulated to seize and grasp Carmodys, its stomach has the peculiar and unique ability to digest Carmodys, and its personality is designed to take advantage of the Carmodic personality.</p>
<p>‘Your situation has rendered you unique, Carmody; therefore your predator is unique. It is your death that pursues you, Carmody, and it does so with a desperation equal to your own. You and it are bound together. If it seizes you, you die; if you escape to the normal menaces of your own world, your predator dies for lack of Carmodic sustenance.</p>
<p>‘There is no more I can say that will help you to evade it. I cannot predict the tricks and disguises it will attempt, no more than I can predict yours. I can only warn you that the probabilities always favour the Hunter, though escapes are not entirely unheard-of.</p>
<p>‘That is the situation, Carmody. Have you understood me?’</p>
<p>Carmody started like a man awakened from a deep sleep.</p>
<p>‘Yes,’ he said. ‘I don’t understand everything you said. But I do understand the important parts.’</p>
<p>‘Good,’ Melichrone said. ‘For we have no time left. You must leave this planet at once. Not even I on my own planet can arrest the universal Law of Predation.’</p>
<p>‘Can you get me back to Earth?’ Carmody asked.</p>
<p>‘Given sufficient time, I probably could,’ Melichrone said. ‘But of course, given sufficient time, I could do anything at all. It is difficult, Carmody. To begin with, the three W variables must be solved each in terms of the other. I would have to determine exactly Where in space- time your planet is at the present moment; then I would have to discover Which of the alternate-probability Earths is yours. Then I would have to find the temporal sequence you were born into in order to determine When. Then there is the skorish effect and the doubling factor, both of which must be allowed for. With all of that done, I could, with a little luck, slip you back into your own Particularity (a surprisingly delicate operation) without wrecking the whole works.’</p>
<p>‘Can you do this for me?’ Carmody asked.</p>
<p>‘No. There is no time left. But I can send you to Maudsley, a friend of mine, who should be able to help you.’</p>
<p>‘A friend of yours?’</p>
<p>‘Well, perhaps not exactly a<i> friend</i>,’ Melichrone said. ‘More of an acquaintance, really. Though even that may be overstating the relationship. You see, once, quite some time ago, I almost left my planet for a sightseeing trip; and had I done so, I would have met Maudsley. But I didn’t leave for various reasons, and therefore never actually met Maudsley. Still, we both know that if I had gone on my trip, we <i>would</i> have met, and would have exchanged views and outlooks, had an argument or two, cracked a few jokes, and ended with a mild fondness for each other.’</p>
<p>‘It seems a kind of feeble relationship to presume upon,’ Carmody said. ‘Isn’t there anyone else you could send me to?’</p>
<p>‘I’m afraid not,’ Melichrone said. ‘Maudsley is my only friend. Probabilities define affinities just as well as actualities, you know. I’m sure Maudsley will take good care of you.’</p>
<p>‘Well –’ Carmody began to say. But then he noticed that something large and dark and menacing was beginning to take shape just behind his left shoulder, and he knew that he had used up all of his time.</p>
<p>‘I’ll go!’ he said. ‘And thanks for everything!’</p>
<p>‘No need to thank me,’ Melichrone said. ‘My duty in the Universe is to serve strangers. Good luck, Carmody!’</p>
<p>The large menacing form was beginning to solidify; but before it could finish, Carmody had disappeared.</p>
</section>
