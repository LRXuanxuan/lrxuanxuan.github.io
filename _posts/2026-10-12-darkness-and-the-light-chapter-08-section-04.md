<h2>四、人口问题</h2>
<p>世界政府面对的所有问题中，最困难的是人口问题。在俄罗斯帝国、中国帝国时期，以及随后世界帝国统治之下，大多数国家的人口都严重下降，平均年龄也不断上升。法国人已经减少到一小撮垂头丧气的老人，淹没在成群德国和俄罗斯移民之中；然而德国与俄罗斯自身的人口同样发生惊人下降。帝国统治下的中国人口锐减。日本人所受苦难比任何其他民族都严重，几乎遭到灭绝。印度人在摆脱英国统治后人口一度增长，却又在俄罗斯和中国帝国统治下大幅减少。英国人在暴政时期只剩下一小群半野蛮人，生活于遍布废弃工厂的土地上；后来在西藏传教者影响下，即使仍被暴政压在脚下，他们也重新形成一种民族目标，并极其努力地繁衍人口，终于止住下降。几个岛国民族由此恢复足够活力，得以一次又一次发动叛乱。世界联邦建立时，大不列颠约有八百万人口。</p>
<p>两个帝国都曾试图通过禁止节育、迫害无子女者来遏止世界人口下降。这些措施效果很小，因为帝国统治下的生活根本不值得过。</p>
<p>人口数量下降的同时，平均智力水平也在下降。在一个敌对的世界里，较聪明的人比迟钝者更不愿用孩子给自己增加负担；或者，他们凭自己的能力爬到较富裕舒适的位置后，由于没有任何社会理想或宗教理想激励自己承担养育责任，也会主动回避生育。</p>
<p>世界联邦政府最早采取的行动之一，就是设立‘亲职部’。它不仅负责制止总人口下降，还要确保聪明的遗传谱系不会减少、迟钝的谱系却不断增加。第一项任务，是让平均智力和较高智力的人觉得成为父母具有吸引力。办法之一是给予高额补贴。每一个聪明的孩子不仅不再是父母的负担，反而成为经济上的资产。社会投入巨大努力，减轻分娩痛苦与危险，并确保养育儿童不会让母亲在生命中最好的那些年里沦为家庭奴隶。公共膳食、公共托儿所和家中的省力设备解放了母亲，同时家庭仍被保存为社会生活的基本单位。所有女孩都接受育儿训练。亲职部还开展谨慎宣传，劝说所有年轻人相信，成为父母既是他们至高的特权，也是首要义务。说它是至高特权，是因为只有通过婚姻和养育家庭，人才能了解最亲密形式的共同体；说它是首要义务，是因为在人类当前处境下，最紧迫的需要就是制止人口衰退，并大量培养强健聪明的青年。这一时代，他们宣称，才是人类历史真正的日出时代。从物种起源到世界暴政被推翻的漫长时期，不过是拖得极长的黎明；而随着人类联邦建立，明亮的光终于突然出现在地平线上，整个前景第一次清晰而金黄。人口不仅必须停止下降；新世界的需要如此巨大，以至于人类数量必须增加到现有水平的一百倍。世界资源完全足够，而人类潜能要得到实现，就需要一个拥有几十个乃至更多庞大而多样民族的世界。不过，比数量更重要的是质量。每一代人的任务，都必须是确保下一代比此前任何一代更健康、更聪明、更慷慨、更健全、更富创造力。每一对年轻夫妇当然都应希望自己的孩子如此，也都应渴望获得父母所能得到的最稀有荣耀：把某位杰出天才带到世界上来，无论他最终投身政治行动、科学、艺术还是精神领导。</p>
<p>为了培养下一代的智力与正直，社会采取了许多措施。大规模研究终于产生了非常可靠的心理测验。严重缺陷者以及某些容易犯罪的类型被绝育。智力迟钝者受到强烈劝阻，不要生育。具有良好平均智力的父母当然会得到帮助，以养育大家庭；智力格外优秀者则获得丰厚补贴。杰出儿童被视为全世界最宝贵的财富，受到极其细致而娴熟的培养，以便他们能够充分发挥自己的能力。</p>
</section>

<h2>iv. THE POPULATION PROBLEM</h2>
<p>OF ALL THE problems that confronted the World Government the most difficult was that of population. During the period of the Russian and Chinese Empires and subsequently under the World Empire, population in most countries had very seriously declined, and the average age had increased. The French had dwindled to a sprinkling of disheartened old people in a swarm of German and Russian invaders. Yet Germany and Russia themselves had suffered a startling decline’ of population. China under the Empire was badly depleted. The Japanese, whose sufferings had been worse than those of any other people, were almost exterminated. The Indians had multiplied after gaining their independence from Britain, but had declined heavily under the Russian and Chinese Empires. The British, reduced during the tyranny to a handful of semi-barbarians in a land of ruined factories, had later, under the influence of Tibetan missionaries, conceived a new national purpose even under the heel of the tyranny, and had concentrated on reproduction so effectively that the decline was stayed and these island peoples became sufficiently vigorous to undertake rebellion after rebellion. At the founding of the World Federation, Great Britain was inhabited by some eight million human beings.</p>
<p>The two Empires had tried to stem the downfall of world population by forbidding birth-control and persecuting the childless. These methods had little effect, for under the empires life was not worth living.</p>
<p>While numbers were declining, the average level of intelligence was declining also. The more intelligent were more reluctant than the dullards to burden themselves with children in a hostile world; or else, climbing into wealth and-comfort without any social or religious ideals to stimulate them into assuming the burden, they avoided it.</p>
<p>One of the first acts of the World Federal Government was to set up a Ministry of Parenthood, charged not only with stemming the general decline of population but also with securing that intelligent stocks should not dwindle while dull stocks increased. The first task was to make parenthood attractive to people of average and superior intelligence. This was done partly by heavy subsidies. Every intelligent child, far from being a burden to its parents, became a financial asset. Great efforts were made to free childbirth of its distress and danger, and to ensure that the upbringing of children should not demand the enslavement of the mother during the best years of her life. With the aid of communal meals, communal nurseries and labour-saving devices within the home the mothers were freed and yet the home was preserved as the fundamental unit of social life. All girls were trained in mothercraft. The Ministry also undertook careful propaganda to persuade all young people that parenthood was at once their supreme privilege and their first obligation; the supreme privilege, because only through marriage and the rearing of a family could they know community in its most intimate form; the supreme obligation because in the present condition of the species the ‘most urgent need was that the decline of population should be checked, and that there should be a lavish supply of vigorous and intelligent young. For this age of mankind’s history, they said, was the true age of sunrise. The period from the origin of the species to the overthrow of the world tyranny had been merely the long-drawn-out dawn. But with the founding of the Federation of Mankind bright light had suddenly appeared over the horizon. At last the whole prospect was clear and golden. Not only must population cease to decline; the needs of the new world were such that the number of human beings must be increased to a hundred times their present number. The world-resources were ample, and for the fulfilment of man’s potentiality it was necessary to have a world of many scores of great diversified peoples. But more important even than numbers was quality. It must be the task of each generation to secure that its successor should be more healthy, more intelligent, more generous, more sane, and more creative than earlier generations. Every young couple must surely desire this for its own children, and must covet the rarest of parental glories, namely to bring into the world some outstanding genius, whether in political action, science, art, or spiritual leadership.</p>
<p>Much was done in order to foster intelligence and integrity in the rising generation. Lavish research produced at last very reliable mental tests. Defectives and certain types prone to criminality were sterilized. Dullards were severely discouraged from having children. Parents of good average intelligence were of course helped to have large families. Those of exceptionally high intelligence were handsomely subsidized. Outstanding children were treated as the world’s most precious possession, and trained with the utmost care and skill to enable them to make full use of their powers.</p>
