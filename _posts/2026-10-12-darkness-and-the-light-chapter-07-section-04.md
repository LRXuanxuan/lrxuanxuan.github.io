<h2>四、战争再起，以及新秩序</h2>
<p>漫长的武装和平终于结束了。光明阵营的宣传正在整个帝国内迅速扩大影响，帝国主义者于是决定，无论付出什么代价，都必须立刻摧毁敌人。他们秘密准备了数年，同时还竭力让自由民族相信，帝国正变得越来越友善。</p>
<p>然后，帝国突然投入全部军力，从北向南发动一场双线夹击，企图在阿富汗与克什米尔之间切断联邦。成群飞机遮蔽天空，大批适于山地作战的坦克向群山推进。进攻部队后方还跟着所谓“支援部队”；这些部队真正的任务，是一旦前线士兵表现出任何动摇，就立刻从后方炮击自己人。</p>
<p>联邦进行了绝望防御，可敌人的钳形攻势最终仍成功把联邦领土切成两半。没过多久，资源较丰富、但也更容易受到攻击的西半部便崩溃了。西藏同克什米尔、川边一道，再一次孤独面对整个世界；而这次他们的敌人，比此前曾经对抗过的那个世界组织得更加有效。</p>
<p>不仅如此，失去西部领土还严重破坏了东部经济，因为联邦西部与东部原本早已高度整合。西藏在很多方面已经依赖工业化程度较高的西部地区。</p>
<p>然而，西藏人再一次进入那种奇异、非理性、甚至近乎欢快的绝对自信状态。在山地地形与微生物防线帮助下，他们守住了边境。空袭再次摧毁住宅、工厂和水库，可西藏人的生活仍继续下去。牦牛依旧在山坡上吃草；只要没有因为缺水而彻底毁掉，庄稼也仍有人照料。食物受到严格配给，没有任何人真正吃得饱，但也没有谁真的饿死。</p>
<p>西藏、克什米尔和川边全部人口团结在共同的抵抗意志之中。他们说：“只要我们撑得够久，潮水总会转向。”他们说对了。</p>
<p>很快，一个传言传遍世界：即使整个世界帝国的全部力量，也无法征服这些山地民族。他们的榜样鼓舞了所有国家的光明仆从，使他们组织起一系列彼此协调的起义，其中最重要的一场就发生在中国本土。</p>
<p>几乎出人意料地迅速，帝国在整个亚洲与欧洲的权力轰然崩溃，取而代之的是大量不稳定的地方独立国家。有些真正属于光明阵营，有些只是表面如此，还有一些则公开奉行民族主义，依旧盲目狭隘。帝国主义者一度仍控制着中国、美洲和南非，但最终这些地区也相继脱离他们的掌握。</p>
<p>世界陷入混乱。中国和欧洲已经开始爆发小规模战争；各地小型政治领袖也纷纷试图在通向权力的梯子上占据一个位置。山地联邦立即重新建立，联邦政府向全世界各民族发出呼吁，要求建立一个全球性的联邦。每个国家的光明力量都强烈支持这一新秩序。</p>
<p>随后出现了一段短暂的内战与国家间战争时期。然而，可以说就在这些斗争的背后，一个新的世界秩序却在稳步伸展自己的枝杈。负责交通、公共卫生、邮政服务、工业争议调解等等事务的全球委员会逐渐建立起来，并联结成一个庞大的跨国组织网络。</p>
<p>即使彼此正在交战的国家，通常也会尊重这个正在形成的超国家体系。敌对双方在卫生、工业组织以及农业等领域进行合作，反而成了常见现象。</p>
<p>然而，仅靠委员会并不能阻止战争。那些可能彼此敌对的国家不愿把飞机和坦克的控制权交给任何单纯的委员会。更何况，在许多新成立的国家里，新统治阶层虽然口头上忠于光明，实际上仍是一群追逐权力的寡头；他们既掠夺其他国家，也掠夺本国民众。于是，经济竞争仍不断结出战争这枚苦涩果实。</p>
<p>起初看起来，人类摧毁世界帝国之后，似乎只不过是把一种邪恶状态换成另一种。但混乱时期并没有持续很久。世界各民族一个接一个加入新的“光明联邦”。不到二十年，整个行星都被纳入新的秩序之中；此后，这个组织被庄严地重新命名为“人类联邦”。</p>
<p>新世界组织宪法的序言后来成为人类最珍爱的经典之一。它以世界帝国覆灭以后西藏人发出的那份呼吁为基础；随后几年里，各国最优秀的头脑又一点一点共同修改、扩充。因此到了最终形式，它确实是一份集体创造、没有单一作者的文本。</p>
<p>如今我还能记起其中一些已经被记忆弄得不太准确的片段，愿在这里引用：</p>
<p>“我们，来自所有国度的居民，地球这个行星上的智慧生命，在推翻一个遍及世界的暴政、废除一种遍及世界的精神黑暗之后，现在通过我们自由选择的代表，向光明立誓。</p>
<p>“我们承认，一切人类生命的崇高目标，是使自己以及彼此醒来，进入爱、智慧与创造力量之中，并以此服务于精神。</p>
<p>“对于宇宙，我们所知极少；然而在内心深处，我们确切知道：对于一切达到人类这种精神层次的存在，这就是应当选择的生活道路。</p>
<p>“因此，为了服务于精神，我们这个行星上的人类居民现在联合成一种新秩序。在这个秩序中，每一个人，无论天赋多么卑微，都必须作为精神的一个容器而受到尊重；从婴儿期开始，他都应得到一切可能的帮助，使他能够发挥自己所拥有的身体与心智能力，既从中获得自身的喜悦，也服务于共同生活。</p>
<p>“我们决意，从今以后，不再让任何人因为不健康的生活条件而身体残缺，或心智遭到扭曲。</p>
<p>“为实现这一目标，我们宣布：今后，任何强大的个人、阶级或民族，都不得再拥有足以为了私人利益而支配他人生活的经济或军事手段。”</p>
</section>

<h2>iv. WAR AGAIN, AND A NEW ORDER</h2>
<p>AT LAST THE long period of armed peace came to an end. Propaganda for the light was rapidly gaining ground throughout the Empire. The imperialists decided that at all costs they must destroy their enemy at once. For some years they prepared in secret, while trying to persuade the Free Peoples of their increasing friendliness. Then suddenly they flung their whole armament into a double attack, from north to south, to cut the Federation in two between Afghanistan and Kashmir. With clouds of planes and swarms of mountain tanks the imperial armament pressed up among the hills. Behind the attacking forces came ‘supporting’ forces whose office it was to bombard the attackers should they show any signs of wavering. The Federation defended itself desperately, but the pincer movement of the enemy succeeded in cutting the Federal territory in two. Not long afterwards the richer and more vulnerable western half of the Federation collapsed. Tibet, with Kashmir and Chwanben, was once more alone against the world, a world more effectively organized than that which they had formerly opposed. Moreover their own economy was gravely mutilated by the loss of the western lands, which had been well integrated with the eastern districts. Tibet had become largely dependent on the more industrial West. But once more the Tibetans rose to strange irrational and almost hilarious confidence. Aided by their mountains and their microbes, they held the frontiers intact. Air bombardment once more blasted their homes and factories and reservoirs. Yet Tibetan life continued. Still the yak browsed, the crops were tended, save where lack of water had ruined them. Food was strictly rationed. No one had enough, but none actually starved. The whole population of Tibet, Kashmir, and Chwanben was united in the will to resist. ‘If we hang on long enough,’ they said, ‘the tide will surely turn.’ They were right.</p>
<p>Throughout the world the rumour spread that the whole strength of the World Empire could not subdue these mountain peoples. Their example encouraged the servants of the light in every land to organize a crop of well-correlated rebellions, of which the most important was in China itself. With surprising suddenness the imperial power throughout Asia and Europe collapsed, giving place to a medley of unstable independent local states, some genuinely of the light, some merely ostensibly so, some frankly nationalist and blind. For a while the imperialists retained their hold on China, America, and South Africa, but in time these also were lost to them.</p>
<p>The world was in chaos. Already minor wars were breaking out in China and Europe. Already little leaders were seeking a foothold on the ladder to power. The Mountain Federation was at once re-formed, and the Federal Government issued an appeal to the peoples of the world, urging a world-wide federation. The forces of the light in every country worked strongly for the new order. There was a short period of civil wars and interstate wars. But behind the backs of these struggles, so to speak, the new world order was steadily ramifying. World-wide commissions for transport, health, postal services, the regulation of industrial disputes, and so on, were gradually forming into a vast network of cosmopolitan organization. Even states at war generally respected this incipient supranational organization, and it was common for enemies to co-operate with one another in the spheres of health, industrial, and agricultural organization. But mere commissions could not prevent wars from occurring. Potentially hostile states would not surrender to any mere committee their control of aeroplanes and tanks. And because they would not do this, and because in many of the new states the new ruling class, though ostensibly loyal to the light, was in fact a power-seeking oligarchy, predatory towards other states and its own subject population, economic rivalry often produced the bitter fruit of war.</p>
<p>But though it seemed at first that in breaking the World Empire mankind had merely exchanged one evil condition for another, the period of chaos was brief. One by one the peoples of the world joined the new ‘Federation of the Light’. Within a couple of decades the whole planet was brought within the new order, which then was solemnly renamed the Federation of Mankind.</p>
<p>The preamble to the constitution of the new world organization became one of the most cherished scriptures of the human race. It was based on the appeal which the Tibetans had issued after the downfall of the world empire, and it had been developed little by little in subsequent years by the best minds of all countries; so that in its final form it was truly co-operative and anonymous. I now remember and will quote some garbled fragments of it.</p>
<p>‘We, inhabitants of every land, intelligences of the planet Earth, having overthrown a world-wide tyranny, having abolished a world-wide darkness of the spirit, now, through our chosen representatives, pledge ourselves to the light. We acknowledge that the high goal of all the lives of men is to awaken themselves and one another to love and wisdom and creative power, in service of the spirit. Of the universe we know very little; but in our hearts we know certainly that for all beings of human stature this is the way of life. In service of the spirit, therefore, we the human inhabitants of this planet, unite in a new order, in which every human being, no matter how lowly his nature, shall be treated with respect as a vessel of the spirit, shall be given every possible aid from infancy onwards to express whatever power is in him for bodily and mental prowess, for his own delight and for service of the common life. We resolve that in future none shall be crippled in body or perverted in mind by unwholesome conditions. ‘For this end we declare that in future no powerful individual or class or nation shall have the means, economic or military, to control the lives of men for private gain.’</p>
