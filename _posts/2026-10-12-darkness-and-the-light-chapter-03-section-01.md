<h2>一、德意志帝国的兴亡</h2>
<p>关于这个反复动荡时代的具体历史事件，我已无法恢复多少。就我此刻写作时正在发生的这场战争而言，我几乎什么也记不得。零星的记忆似乎暗示它最终以某种意义上的英国胜利告终，但我绝不敢信赖这个猜测。倘若它是真的，那么这场胜利所提供的巨大机会——通过宽大的和平安排和欧洲联邦秩序开创新局面的机会——一定被错过了；因为此后敌对的帝国主义仍继续存在，真正的和平也没有建立。再往后的战争和剧变，在我记忆中反而稍微清楚一些。例如，我似乎记得民主诸国曾经遭到失败；它们起初由英国领导，后来则由北美领导，对手是一个极权主义的欧洲。有一段时间，战争几乎成了英国单独对抗整个欧洲，而欧洲又一次由德国统率。直到英国残存的兵力被赶进苏格兰，拼命守住一条大致相当于古罗马长城的位置，美国的力量才开始显现——可也只显现了短短一阵。因为在美国和别处一样，旧秩序正在失败，领导者既缺乏想象力，也没有勇气让自己适应新的世界条件。等到终于轮到他们迎战时，他们根本无力把自己那套杂乱无章的资本主义组织成一部战争机器。美国人民逐渐意识到自己成了无能与背叛的牺牲品，大西洋沿岸的人口开始要求新政权。在这种局面下，继续抵抗已经不可能。英国被放弃，北美退回一种岌岌可危的孤立主义，同时明知斗争很快又会重新开始。</p>
<p>尽管存在明显相似之处，这场欧美战争显然并不是我写作本书时正在进行的战争。那时的德国人已经从早年那种曾令全世界反感的夸张流氓气质中恢复过来；某种意义上说，他们已经从纳粹主义退回到更“体面”的普鲁士主义。还有别的事实证明这不是我们眼下的战争。印度和南非都已脱离大英帝国，成为稳固的独立国家。武器也已经远比现在致命，欧洲机群经常大规模轰炸美国海岸。在这场战争中，苏格兰显然已经成为英国经济的重心。低地地区实现了彻底工业化，西部海峡挤满巨型潮汐发电设施。潮汐电力已经成为英国能源的基础。但在德国进攻开始以前，受衰朽金融寡头支配的英国并未有效开发这一新资源。</p>
<p>民主国家战败之后，似乎自由的事业已经永远失败。俄国人最初的革命激情早已被持续不断的外敌威胁腐蚀，并由此重新滑向民族主义；此时，他们为了拼命抵御德国统治阶级支配全球的企图，牺牲掉此前艰难取得的一切社会成果。中国战胜日本后，又在阶级斗争的礁石上分裂。共产主义的北方与资本主义的南方之间毫无和谐可言。北美则裂解成一群所谓“独立”国家，德国控制它们几乎和控制拉丁美洲一样容易。印度虽已摆脱英国统治，却只能在德国威胁面前勉强保持统一。</p>
<p>然而，一个极权主义的世界终究没有形成。德国权力以一种意料之外的方式终结，其原因是心理因素和经济因素的奇特混合。最主要的原因，也许是德国平均智力的下降。自工业革命以来，欧洲和美洲人口的平均智力一直在缓慢降低。避孕不仅造成总人口减少，也造成一种趋势：人口中较聪明的血统繁殖得比迟钝者和低能者更少。因为在争夺舒适与奢侈生活条件的竞争中，较聪明者从长远看更容易进入生活优裕的阶级；在那里，他们比贫困阶级更容易使用避孕手段。又因为他们比迟钝者更会为个人舒适与安全作长远打算，也更不愿意让孩子成为负担。结果就是，总人口虽然都趋于减少，但较聪明的血统减少得更快；于是欧洲和美洲各民族，后来连亚洲民族也一样，在政治、工业、科学与一般文化领域开始严重缺乏有能力的领导者。</p>
<p>在德国，这一过程又被更早的希特勒第三帝国、以及后来的第四帝国对自由知识分子的迫害进一步加剧。第四帝国之所以战胜美国，靠的并不是更高的智力，而是更强的生命力，以及一个囊括全欧洲和非洲大部的帝国资源。</p>
<p>第四帝国在所有被征服地区都迫害、摧毁自由的头脑，只有一个例外：挪威。对那里，它不得不允许相当大的自治。</p>
<p>许多世纪以前，挪威人曾令欧洲沿岸民族闻风丧胆；而在近代，他们却以和平、务实而通情达理著称。与其他几个昔日的小型民主国家一样，他们的社会发展水平其实高于那些强大的邻国，尤其重视智力的培养。被第四帝国征服后，挪威社会中数量可观的优秀头脑发挥了巨大作用。他们成功迫使征服者给予自己某种“自治领地位”，从而既能延续相当一部分原有社会生活，又能完成征服者要求的职责。然而两股力量逐渐结合起来，把他们的温顺变成了行动力，甚至变成狂暴的斗志。第一，是长期亲身经历德国统治所积累的影响；与外国主人的接触使他们充满蔑视与愤怒。第二，是他们清楚知道，在德国人的开发之下，自己的国家已经成为世界最大的潮汐发电基地，而这些能源却被用于帝国目的，而非全人类的目的。</p>
<p>德国独裁政权对挪威人的待遇确实十分特殊。其他被征服民族不是直接沦为奴隶，就是干脆遭到灭绝。英国人例如被降为德国地主贵族之下的农奴。波兰人、捷克人和大多数法国人遭到迫害，被禁止交配繁殖，最后甚至集体绝育，直到他们的族群几乎彻底消失。但斯堪的纳维亚人是另一回事。“北欧人种”的神话在德国大众中影响极深；他们无法假装挪威人不是北欧人，更无法假装挪威人不比血统其实极其混杂的德国人“更北欧”。此外，德国统治者又需要挪威人的航海能力，许多挪威水手被安排到重要岗位，负责训练德国人，甚至操纵德国船只。最后，峡湾潮汐能的大规模开发还造就了一个人数众多、技术高度专业化的挪威工程师阶层。于是，这个小小的挪威民族逐步在德意志帝国内取得了特权地位。然而繁荣与相对免受德国暴政侵害，并没有使他们接受外国统治。当其他附庸民族已经被酷刑彻底吓服时，挪威人仍保存着独立精神。</p>
<p>昔日希特勒信仰最初的狂热，此时早已耗尽。那种疯狂激情已经消失——正是它曾让数百万经过精心灌输的德国青年乐于为祖国赴死，让他们驾驶坦克不只碾过逃亡的难民，甚至碾过本国伤员，并支持一套支配全欧洲的残酷暴政。如今的德国统治少数派只剩下一个高度组织化、机械效率极高、冷酷无情，却头脑迟钝、疲惫而犬儒的官僚集团。德国人民宣称自己从英国人手中接过了令人艳羡的“白人的负担”，实际上却只是一个严厉而毫无灵感的暴政之下驯服的农奴。</p>
<p>后来，帝国围绕那个半神化的“元首”职位继承问题发生严重分裂。（最初那位元首自然早已成为过去的神话人物，帝国各地都矗立着纪念他的巨型雕像。）挪威人突然抓住德国贵族内斗提供的机会，发动一套准备已久的密谋。他们夺取潮汐发电站和军事中心，宣布挪威独立，并号召所有热爱自由的民族起来反抗暴君。挪威自身其实处在极为有利的位置：他们不仅控制帝国最主要的能源来源，还控制商船队与帝国海军的很大部分；庞大的水上飞机部队也大多站在他们一边。起初这场叛乱看似毫无希望，却很快扩散到英国和法国北部，随后瑞士、奥地利和德国南部也爆发起义。决定胜负的，一方面是重新苏醒的自由激情与人道同情，另一方面则是一种新的、效率极高而又惊人轻便的蓄电池，它使船舶乃至飞机都能够使用电力推进。这种新型蓄电池在挪威秘密发明，并在斯匹次卑尔根大规模秘密生产。起义以前，许多船只和飞机就已暗中完成改装。战争爆发后，一支规模巨大的电动飞机舰队凭借远胜旧式汽油飞机的灵活性，很快击垮帝国空军的士气。几周之内，叛军便彻底获胜。</p>
<p>德意志帝国覆灭后，人类又一次获得机会，从野蛮拐向真正的文明；可这个机会又一次被浪费。人们原本期待“自由欧洲联邦”带来持久和平，事实却证明它根本不是什么真正自由的联邦。德国被拆回旧日那些小邦，并解除武装。这本来也未必不合理，问题在于胜利的挪威人因为看见新秩序何等脆弱，坚持保留对本国潮汐发电站以及空中舰队的控制；后者虽然名义上解除武装，却极容易重新改装成轰炸机。他们希望借此在联邦脆弱的幼年期继续控制并引导局面。于是，“解除挪威武装”的口号不可避免地被光明的秘密敌人利用，以谋求支配联邦。经历一段充满猜忌与阴谋的不稳定和平后，斯堪的纳维亚诸民族与欧洲联邦其余国家之间爆发欧洲内战。等到交战各方把彼此拖得精疲力竭，俄国出兵干预；不久之后，俄罗斯帝国的疆域便从白令海峡一直延伸到布拉斯基特群岛。</p>
<p>在我死后体验最早那段混乱阶段里，我始终没能获得关于俄国事件的清晰图景。我只模糊记得光明与黑暗交替出现的时期。有时是真正的社会主义与民主力量占上风，有时则是极权与专制势力占上风。尽管最初慷慨的革命冲动已经受到严重扭曲，但它毕竟留下太多扎实成果，因此由苏维埃构成的国家体系从未真正面临瓦解危险。在第四德意志帝国长期威胁期间，俄罗斯独裁者——此时被称作“首席同志”——对整个人民实行极严厉的军事纪律。德国垮台后，一股好战的共产主义帝国主义浪潮席卷广阔的俄罗斯领土。大批“青年共产主义者”要求用坦克与飞机把“列宁精神”传播到全世界。征服欧洲正是这种情绪第一次大规模爆发。不过，俄国内部同时也有别的力量活动。德国力量被摧毁后，真正的社会主义、自由主义，乃至经过改革的基督教倾向，又一次在欧洲俄罗斯以及西欧各地出现。西方民族此时已经开始厌恶那套把冷酷强权奉为宗教的假信仰。各种基督教派、实验性宗教运动、自由社会主义者与“改革共产主义者”的地下组织，到处旺盛生长。我一度以为，自己必定正在目睹人类历史的转折点，这个物种终于吸取了教训。然而我错了。我看见的，只是光明意志与黑暗意志长期僵持时代中许多夭折的上升波动之一。人们固然极端厌恶战争带来的苦难，但他们的道德能量仍然微弱。对于全人类共同事业、对于这个种族的精神任务，他们的忠诚并没有增强。</p>
<p>正因如此，那个一度仿佛要使俄国重生的运动，最后只形成一股较清醒的感受与行动的暗流。独裁政权的权力仍完整而严酷；更何况，它仍能够鼓舞人口中的多数，尤其是青年，让他们以惊人的精力与献身精神去传播政权依旧声称代表、实际上却已严重歪曲的马克思主义理想。</p>

<h2>i. RISE AND FALL OF A GERMAN REICH</h2>
<p>OF THE DETAILED historical events of this age of fluctuation I cannot recover much. Of the war which is present to me as I write this book I remember almost nothing. A few shreds of recollection suggest that it resulted in a British victory of sorts, but I place no reliance on this surmise. If it is correct, the great opportunity afforded by this victory, the opportunity of a generous peace and a federal order in Europe, must have been missed; for rival imperialisms continued to exist after that war and real peace was not established. Subsequent wars and upheavals come rather more clearly into my mind. For instance, I seem to remember a defeat of the democratic peoples, led at first by the British, but later by the North Americans, against a totalitarian Europe. For a while the struggle was between Britain alone and the whole of Europe, martialled once more by Germany. Not till the remnant of the British forces had been driven into Scotland, and were desperately holding a line roughly equivalent to the Roman Wall, did the American power begin to make itself felt, and then only for a while; for in America, as elsewhere, the old order was failing, its leaders had neither the imagination nor the courage to adjust themselves to the new world-conditions. Consequently, when at last their turn carne they were quite incapable of organizing their haphazard capitalism for war. The American people began to realize that they were the victims of incompetence and treachery, and the population of the Atlantic seaboard demanded a new regime. In this state of affairs resistance became impossible. Britain was abandoned, and North America reverted to a precarious isolationism knowing that the struggle would very soon begin again.</p>
<p>This Euro-American war was certainly not the war which is being waged while I write this book, in spite of obvious similarities. At this time the Germans had recovered from that extravagant hooliganism which had turned the world against them in an earlier period. They had in a manner reverted from Nazism to the more respectable Prussianism. Other facts also show that this was not our present war. Both India and South Africa had left the British Empire and were already well- established independent states. Moreover, weapons were now of a much more lethal kind, and the American coast was frequently and extensively bombarded by fleets of European planes. In this war Scotland had evidently become the economic centre of gravity of Britain. The Lowlands were completely industrialized, and huge tidal electric generators crowded the western sounds. Tidal electricity had become the basis of Britain’s power. But the British, under their effete financial oligarchy, had not developed this new asset efficiently before the German attack began.</p>
<p>After the defeat of the democracies it seemed that the cause of freedom had been lost for ever. The Russians, whose initial revolutionary passion had long since been corrupted by the constant danger of attack and a consequent reversion to nationalism, now sacrificed all their hard-won social achievements for a desperate defence against the attempt of the German ruling class to dominate the planet. China, after her victory over Japan, had split on the rock of class strife. Between the Communist North and the Capitalist South there was no harmony. North America became a swarm of ‘independent’ states which Germany controlled almost as easily as the Latin South. India, freed from British rule, maintained a precarious unity in face of the German danger.</p>
<p>But the Totalitarian world was not to be. The end of the German power came in an unexpected manner, and through a strange mixture of psychological and economic causes. Perhaps the main cause was the decline of German intelligence. Ever since the industrial revolution the average intelligence of the European and American peoples had been slowly decreasing. Contraception had produced not only a decline of population but also a tendency of the more intelligent strains in the population to breed less than the dullards and half-wits. For in the competition for the means of comfort and luxury the more intelligent tended in the long run to rise into the comfortable classes. There they were able to avail themselves of contraceptive methods which the poorer classes could less easily practise. And because they took more forethought than the dullards for their personal comfort and security, they were more reluctant to burden themselves with children. The upshot was that, while the population as a whole tended to decline, the more intelligent strains declined more rapidly than the less intelligent; and the European and American peoples, and later the Asiatics, began to suffer from a serious shortage of able leaders in politics, industry, science, and general culture.</p>
<p>In Germany the process had been intensified by the persecution of free intelligences by the former Hitlerian Third Reich, and by the subsequent Fourth Reich, which had defeated America not by superior intelligence but superior vitality and the resources of an empire which included all Europe and most of Africa.</p>
<p>The Fourth Reich had persecuted and destroyed the free intelligences in all its subject lands, save one, namely Norway, where it had been necessary to allow a large measure of autonomy.</p>
<p>The Norwegians, who many centuries earlier had been the terror of the European coastal peoples, had in recent times earned a reputation for peaceable common sense. Like several others of the former small democracies, they of had attained a higher level of social development than their mightier neighbours. In particular they had fostered intelligence. After their Conquest by the Fourth Reich their remarkable fund of superior minds had stood them in good stead. They had successfully forced their conquerors into allowing them a sort of ‘dominion status’. In this condition they had been able to carry on much of their former social life while fulfilling the functions which the conquerors demanded of them. Two influences, however gradually combined to change their docility into energy and berserk fury. One was the cumulative effect of their experience of German domination. Contact with their foreign masters filled them with contempt and indignation. The other influence was the knowledge that under German exploitation their country had become the world’s greatest generator of tidal power, and that this power was being used for imperial, not human, ends.</p>
<p>The German dictatorship had, indeed, treated the Norwegians in a very special manner. Other conquered peoples had been simply enslaved or actually exterminated. The British, for instance, had been reduced to serfdom under a German landed aristocracy. The Poles and Czechs and most of the French had been persecuted, prevented from mating and procreating, and finally even sterilized, until their stock had been completely destroyed. But the Scandinavian peoples were in a class apart. The Nordic myth had a strong hold on the German people. It was impossible to pretend that the Norwegians were not Nordic, more Nordic than the Germans, who were in fact of very mixed stock. Moreover Norwegian maritime prowess was necessary to the German rulers; and many Norwegian sailors were given responsible positions for the training of Germans and even the control of German ships. Finally, the exploitation of tidal power in the fjords had produced a large class of Norwegian technicians with highly specialized skill. Thus little by little the small Norwegian people attained for itself a privileged positionin the German Empire. Prosperity and relative immunity from German tyranny had not brought acceptance of foreign domination. The Norwegians had preserved their independent spirit while other subject peoples had been utterly cowed by torture.</p>
<p>The initial fervour of the old Hitlerian faith had long since spent itself. Gone was the crazy zeal which had led millions of carefully indoctrinated young Germans to welcome death for the fatherland to drive their tanks not only over the fleeing refugees but over their own wounded, and to support a cruel tyranny throughout Europe. The German ruling minority was by now merely a highly organized, mechanically efficient, ruthless, but rather dull-witted and rather tired and cynical bureaucracy. The German people, who claimed to have taken over from the British the coveted ‘white man’s’ burden, were in fact the docile serfs of a harsh and uninspired tyranny.</p>
<p>There came a time when the Reich was seriously divided over the question of succession to the semi-divine post of Fuhrer. (The original Fuhrer, of course, was by now a mythical figure in the past, and the empire was sprinkled with gigantic monuments to his memory.) Suddenly the Norwegians, seizing the opportunity afforded by dissension in the German aristocracy, set in action a long-prepared system of conspiracy. They seized the tidal generators and military centres, and declared Norway’s independence. They also issued a call to all freedom-loving peoples to rise against their tyrants. The Norwegians themselves were in a very strong position. Not only did they control the Reich’s main source of power, but also a large part of the mercantile marine and Imperial Navy. The huge sea-plane force was also mainly on their side. Though at first the rebellion seemed a forlorn hope, it soon spread to Britain and Northern France. Insurrection then broke out in Switzerland, Austria, and southern Germany. The decisive factors were the revived passion for freedom and for human kindness, and also the new, extremely efficient and marvellously light accumulator, which enabled not only ships but planes to be driven electrically. The new accumulator had been secretly invented in Norway and secretly manufactured in large quantities in Spitsbergen. Even before the insurrection many ships and planes had been secretly fitted with it. After the outbreak of war a great fleet of electric planes, far more agile than the old petrol planes, soon broke the nerve of the imperial force. Within a few weeks the rebels were completely victorious.</p>
<p>With the fall of the German Reich the human race was once more given an opportunity to turn the corner from barbarism to real civilization. Once more the opportunity was lost. The free Federation of Europe, which was expected to bring lasting peace, was in fact no free federation at all. Germany was divided into the old minor states, and these were disarmed. This would have been reasonable enough if the victorious Norwegians, realizing the precariousness of the new order, had not insisted on retaining control of their own tidal generators and their air fleet, which, though disarmed, could very easily be turned into bombers. Thus, they hoped, they would be able to control and guide the Federation during its delicate infancy. Inevitably the demand for ‘the disarmament of Norway’ was used by the secret enemies of the light in their effort to dominate the Federation. After a period of uncertain peace, full of suspicion and intrigue, came the great European Civil War between the Scandinavian peoples and the rest of the European Federation. When the federated peoples had reduced one another to exhaustion, Russia intervened, and presently the Russian Empire stretched from the Behring Straits to the Blasket Islands.</p>
<p>During the first, confused phase of my post-mortal experience I failed to gain any clear vision of events in Russia. I have an impression of alternating periods of light and darkness. Sometimes the truly socialistic and democratic forces dominated, sometimes the totalitarian and despotic. In spite of the grave perversion of the original generous revolutionary impulse, so much of solid worth had been achieved that the Soviet system of states was never in serious danger of disintegration. During the long peril from the German Fourth Reich the Russian dictator, who was now known as the ‘Chief Comrade’, enforced a very strict military discipline on the whole people. When Germany had fallen, a wave of militant communist imperialism swept over the vast Russian territories. Hosts of ‘Young Communists’ demanded that ‘the spirit of Lenin’ should now be spread by tank and aeroplane throughout the world. The conquest of Europe was the first great expression of this mood. But other forces were also at work in Russia. After the destruction of German power, true socialistic, liberal, and even reformed Christian tendencies once more appeared throughout European Russia and in Western Europe. The Western peoples had by now begun to sicken of the sham religion of ruthless power. Christian sects, experimental religious movements, liberal-socialist and ‘reformed communist’ conspiracies were everywhere leading a vigorous underground life. It seemed to me that I must be witnessing the turning-point of human history, that the species had at last learnt its lesson. But in this I was mistaken. What I was observing was but one of the many abortive upward fluctuations in the long age of inconclusive struggle between the will for the light and the will for darkness. For, though men utterly loathed the hardships of war, their moral energy remained slight. Their loyalty to the common human enterprise, to the spiritual task of the race, had not been strengthened.</p>
<p>Thus it was that the movement which had seemed to promise a regeneration of Russia succeeded only in creating an under-current of more lucid feeling and action. The power of the dictatorship remained intact and harsh; and was able, moreover, to inspire the majority, and particularly the young, with superb energy and devotion in the spreading of the Marxian ideals which the regime still claimed to embody, but had in fact sadly perverted.</p>
