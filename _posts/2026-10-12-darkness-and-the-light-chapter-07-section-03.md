<h2>三、武装和平</h2>
<p>人类由此进入了一段漫长的武装和平时期。山地联邦一方面发展自己的防御力量，一方面传播自己的福音，同时努力把本国社会秩序建设成未来世界秩序的范本。与此同时，帝国主义者也在准备下一场战争；他们的目标，是彻底根除西藏及其附属力量，连同它们那些危险思想一起消灭。</p>
<p>然而，和平重新唤醒了两个帝国专制政权之间的竞争。遥远的不列颠群岛突然爆发叛乱，而山地民族又同时进攻驻伊朗的俄国部队；在这种情况下，中国政府却没有从东方攻击西藏、帮助俄国。这是一个严重错误。英国因此成功获得独立，而伊朗、伊拉克和土耳其也加入山地联邦。</p>
<p>与两个帝国相比，联邦的经济资源依然少得可笑。除孤立的英国之外，两个帝国仍控制着世界其余全部地区；可是，联邦的声望和道德权威却不断上升。俄国帝国的领土此时已经频繁发生叛乱，其中最麻烦的是离西藏很近的印度，以及远离莫斯科的美洲。</p>
<p>中国统治者很清楚，如果再不采取行动，整个俄罗斯体系很快就会崩溃；而它解体后的碎片很可能同那个可恨的联邦结合起来。因此，他们决定趁还来得及，先把能够夺到手的东西拿走。印度自然是最明显的起点。中国提出，可以“替俄国政府维持这个动荡次大陆的治安”，并用威胁强化这一提议。俄国别无选择，只能同意。</p>
<p>随后，中国帝国主义者把大量警察、商业代理人和宣传人员灌入印度，很快便获得完全控制。俄国同印度之间的关系最后只剩下一种理论上的、毫无实际力量的宗主权。</p>
<p>至于整个俄罗斯帝国后来怎样一步一步被中国吞并，这里不必再详述。最终，世界只剩下两个政治体系：一边是庞大的帝国系统，另一边则是一个规模很小的自由民族联邦，主要占据世界上一整片山地区域。不列颠最终也没能在更有效率的帝国强权面前保持独立。</p>
<p>在帝国体系中，绝大多数人实际上都是农奴；而在自由体系中，所有人都平等分享整个联邦那种虽朴素却足够的繁荣，同时拥有充分的个人自由。一个是庞大的警察国家，另一个则是自由人共同合作的事业。一个实行严格审查，另一个则拥有完整的表达自由。帝国社会的主要情绪，是冷漠、相互猜疑与神经质的报复心；联邦内部则恰恰相反，即使始终面临外部危险，仍以充满活力的信心和稳定的相互友善为主。</p>
<p>人们本来很容易预料，为了保持警觉和团结，西藏人迟早会牺牲自由、走向军事独裁，并重演外部威胁当年在革命俄国造成的那种退化。然而此时的西藏人已经对自己、也对彼此拥有足够信心，不再觉得有必要限制自由。</p>
<p>他们的纪律，从根本上说是一种彻底的自我纪律。它允许无限度的讨论和批评，但到了最后关头，人们仍会自由而热烈地接受政府作出的决定。至于叛国，在这样的社会里已经几乎不可想象。</p>
<p>当然，也不能把两种制度之间的反差画得过于简单。帝国内部仍有大量美好事物：仍有真正正当的人际关系，仍有真正的文化，也仍有人诚实地寻找通往更好世界的道路。但这一切都受到制度的压制，也被支撑该制度的错误假设污染。</p>
<p>同样，联邦内部也存在大量真正糟糕的东西。构成这些获得自由民族的个人，本身大多仍是旧有恶劣制度塑造出来的产物。过去对他们心灵造成的损害，不可能一下子全部消失。除西藏之外——在那里新秩序此时已经相当稳固——联邦各地普通人的日常生活中，纯粹的自私、卑鄙、迟钝、残酷和愚蠢，恐怕并不比世界其他地方少多少。</p>
<p>有时，黑暗力量也会在联邦某些地区获得相当大的势力，甚至看起来可能重新取得支配地位。例如在土耳其，就曾出现一场要求为这个新加入联邦、而且最富裕的国家争取特殊特权的运动。联邦内部一度出现危险的民族主义复燃。帝国的“第五纵队”当然尽全力利用这一机会削弱敌人，帝国政府甚至暗中暗示：如果土耳其人想实现自己的要求，帝国的黄金和武器都可以提供帮助。</p>
<p>然而，联邦政府凭借克制与策略，把这场危险最终转化成新的力量。土耳其人以压倒性多数再次确认了自己对联邦的忠诚。</p>
<p>帝国与联邦之间真正最深的区别在于：在前者，人类本有的体面与善意，被错误的社会制度和道德传统不断压低；而在后者，这些品质却被新制度和持续占据主导地位的光明意志大幅增强。</p>
<p>在帝国里，一个普通人虽然脆弱，却仍潜藏着成为真正“人”的可能，但这种潜力几乎总会被恶劣环境腐蚀；在联邦里，同样脆弱的个体则会不断得到社会支持，使自己保持在比原本可能达到的更高程度的正直与理智上。</p>
<p>此后几十年，世界一直分裂为帝国与联邦两个阵营。在这段时期内，帝国不止一次准备彻底摧毁联邦；但每当“零时刻”临近，帝国内部的骚乱就会奇怪地加剧到极端程度，以至于在真正关键的时刻，总会有严重叛乱——通常发生在英国、美洲，甚至中国本土——迫使帝国取消进攻。</p>
<p>整个这几十年里，联邦政府都把主要精力放在防御和社会发展上。防御一部分依靠山地地形，但主要还是依靠一支庞大的空军。为了建立这支空军，联邦不得不牺牲大量奢侈品与生活舒适。它的经济资源十分贫乏。西部领土仍能生产少量石油，水力资源则被开发到极限。人们勤奋地从河床淘金，也在山中开采黄金，用它购买迫切需要的外国商品。</p>
<p>除制造军火和飞机之外，整个联邦最主要的生产活动就是农业和畜牧。自由民族的生活方式因此不得不非常朴素，但这种生活足以维持身体健康和完整的精神生活，而且所有人的生活标准大体相同。</p>
<p>在这几十年中，山地政府继续在帝国每一处领土进行宣传，同时向所有能够通过严格心理审查、证明自己确实真诚的政治难民开放边境。经过一个月的细致观察后，这些人会获得联邦公民身份。</p>
<p>很多逃离帝国暴政的人还没走到边境就被抓住了，但来自世界各地的移民仍持续以细流般的规模进入联邦。他们从小亚细亚沿海城市、从高加索山口、从里海、从远东的南山地区、沿川边河谷、翻越喜马拉雅山口，也从波斯湾港口进入。</p>
<p>于是，自由民族一点一点吸收了新的血液、新的技能以及新的文化成分。难民不断涌入确实造成严重粮食问题，但尽管一些目光短浅的批评者不断抗议，联邦政府仍坚持欢迎新来者。通过集约化耕作以及进一步发展无土农业，这个问题最终得到缓解。</p>
</section>

<h2>iii. ARMED PEACE</h2>
<p>THE HUMAN RACE now settled down to a long period of armed peace, in which the Mountain Federation developed its defences, propagated its gospel, and strove to make its own social order a model for the future order of the world. The imperialists meanwhile prepared for the war in which Tibet and its satellites and their dangerous ideas must be extirpated. Peace, however, revived the rivalry of the imperial tyrannies. When a sudden rebellion broke out in the remote British Isles, and Was supported by an attack by the Mountain Peoples against Russian forces in Iran, the Chinese government refrained from helping Russia by attacking Tibet from the east. This was a grave error, for Britain gained its independence, and Iran, Iraq, and Turkey joined the Federation. The economic resources of the Federation were still ridiculously small compared with those of the empires, whose sway covered all the rest of the earth save isolated Britain; but the prestige and moral authority of the Federation were ever increasing. The Russian Empire’s territories were now constantly in revolt. Chief offenders Were India, so near to Tibet, and America, so remote from Moscow. It was clear to the Chinese rulers that the whole Russian system would soon collapse, if nothing was done to save it; and that its fragments would coalesce with the hated Federation. They therefore determined to seize what they could before it was too late. India was the obvious starting-point. They ‘proposed to police the turbulent subcontinent for the Russian government, and they reinforced the offer with threats. Russia had no choice but to agree. The Chinese imperialists then flooded India with police, commercial agents, and propagandists. Rapidly they gained complete power, so that Russia’s relationship became one of theoretical and impotent suzerainty.</p>
<p>Further details need not be given of the process by which the whole Russian Empire was gradually annexed to China. The world now consisted of a mighty imperial system and a small federation of free peoples occupying a tract which was very largely mountain. Britain had failed to maintain itself against the more efficient imperial power.</p>
<p>In the imperial system the great majority of human beings were practically serfs, while in the free system all shared equally in the frugal prosperity of the whole federation, and there was ample individual freedom. The one was a gigantic police state, the other a co-operative venture of free men. In the one there was strict censorship, in the other complete freedom of expression. In the one the dominant mood was apathy, mutual suspicion, and neurotic vindictiveness; in the other buoyant confidence and unfailing mutual friendliness prevailed in spite of the constant external danger. It might have been expected that the need for watchfulness and unity would have forced the Tibetans to sacrifice freedom to military dictatorship, and would set up the kind of deterioration which external danger had long ago caused in revolutionary Russia. But the Tibetans were by now too sure of themselves and of each other to feel the need to restrict freedom. Their discipline was at bottom a thorough self-discipline, which, though it permitted unlimited discussion and criticism, freely and fervently accepted in the last resort the decision of the government. And treason was by now unthinkable.</p>
<p>The contrast between the two systems must not be overdrawn. Within the Empire was much that was good, much right personal relationship, much of true culture, much honest search for the way to a better world. But all this was crippled by the system and poisoned by the false assumptions on which the system was based. On the other hand in the Federation there was much that was thoroughly bad. The individual human beings who made up the freed peoples were themselves mostly pro- ducts of the bad old system. They could not at a stroke wipe out the mental damage that had been done to them. Save in Tibet, where the new order was by now well established, there was probably in men’s daily lives almost as much sheer self-seeking, downright meanness, insensitivity, cruelty, and stupidity as there was in the rest of the world. Sometimes the forces of darkness gained considerable power in some region of the Federation, and might threaten to dominate. In Turkey, for instance, a movement was started to gain special privilege for this wealthiest of the newly federated countries. There was a dangerous recrudescence of nationalism within the Federation. The ‘fifth column’ of the Empire did its best to use this opportunity for weakening its enemies. The imperial government even suggested secretly that imperial gold and armaments might help the Turks to gain their point. But this danger was turned to a new strength by the forbearance and tact of the federal government. By an overwhelming majority the Turks reaffirmed their loyalty.</p>
<p>The great difference between the Empire and the Federation was that, while in the one case human decency was damped down by a false social system and moral tradition, in the other it was immensely strengthened by the new institutions and the steady dominance of the will for the light. In the one case the average frail but potentially humane individual was nearly always corrupted by a debasing environment, while in the other he was constantly supported in a higher range of integrity and intelligence than would otherwise have been possible to him.</p>
<p>For several decades the world remained divided between the Empire and the Federation. More than once in this period the Empire made ready to crush the Federation; but, as zero hour approached, unrest within the Empire itself strangely increased to such a pitch that at the critical moment serious rebellions, generally in Britain or America or China itself, made attack impossible. Throughout these decades the government of the Federation concentrated on defence and social development. For defence it relied partly on its mountains, but mainly on a great air force, built at heavy cost of luxury and comfort. Economic resources were meagre. A. modest supply of oil was still produced in the western territory of the Federation. Water-power was developed to the utmost. Gold was assiduously sifted from the river- beds and mined in the mountains for the purchase of urgently needed foreign goods. Agriculture and pasture were the main occupations throughout the territory, apart from the manufacture of munitions and planes. The manner of life of the Free Peoples had perforce to be very simple, but it was adequate to health and fullness of mentality, and the standard was the same for all.</p>
<p>Throughout these decades the Mountain government continued its propaganda in every part of the Empire, and kept its frontiers open to all political refugees who were able to pass an expert psychological examination for sincerity. After a month of this careful observation they were given citizenship. Many fugitives from imperial tyranny were caught before they reached the ‘frontier, but a steady trickle of immigration from every part of the world crept in through the coastal cities of Asia Minor, the passes of the Caucasus, the Caspian Sea, from far-eastern Nan Shan, along the valleys of Chwanben, over the Himalayan passes, and through the ports on the Persian Gulf. Thus little by little the Federated Peoples were impregnated with new blood, new skills, and new elements of culture. This influx of refugees caused a serious food problem, but in spite of protests from short-sighted critics the Federal Government insisted on welcoming the new-comers. Intensive cultivation and new development of earthless agriculture alleviated the problem.</p>
