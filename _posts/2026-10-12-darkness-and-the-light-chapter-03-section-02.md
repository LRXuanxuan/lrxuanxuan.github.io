<h2>二、北美</h2>
<p>我不打算停下来逐一叙述这个时代所有的战争与社会动乱。即使我愿意，也无法给出清楚的报告。我只记得，一波又一波徒劳的痛苦像地壳中的地震波一样在整个行星四处扩散。之所以说这些痛苦徒劳，是因为希望一次又一次遭到挫败：通往新世界的大门刚被推开一条缝，随即又砰然关上。</p>
<p>印度便是如此。它终于赢得自由之后，在外部危险的压力下，印度教徒与穆斯林暂时放下分歧。有一阵子，人们仿佛看见拯救人类的真理会从这些肤色黝黑的雅利安民族中出现。因为渗透于各种信仰中的古印度智慧，此时摆脱各教派无关紧要的附属成分，显得比以往更清晰。新的印度似乎会一面掌握欧洲科学与欧洲式的决断力，一面教给人类一种欧洲恰恰缺乏的宁静与超脱。然而，不知怎么，这场运动走偏了；印度王公和资本家残余的权力腐蚀了它。富人为了自身利益控制新国家，公职人员贪腐而低效；古老智慧虽被大肆宣传，却逐渐沦为容忍严重社会罪恶的借口。等到俄罗斯帝国军队终于越过喜马拉雅山口涌入印度时，统治者根本无力应付进攻，而印度人民对不过是“换一个主人”大体漠不关心。直到很久以后，印度人才会为人类历史作出他们重大的贡献。</p>
<p>还有别的、同样一度令人充满希望的复兴运动。我的记忆里，北美曾出现一场宏大而前景光明的复兴。逆境已经洗去了美国人的浪漫商业主义。百万富翁——那种把金钱权力人格化的半神——再也不能让普通大众崇拜和争相模仿。百万富翁本身也已不存在。人口开始意识到，私人金钱权力正是旧文明走向歪曲的首要原因。有一阵子，美国人仍拒绝承认自己那套“百分之百美国主义”已经失败；然而某一刻，阻挡这一认识的心理壁垒突然崩塌。短短两三年内，美国人的整个精神气候都改变了。大陆上下，人们重新审视美国文明赖以建立的原则，开始把真正必要的价值同后来附着上去的虚假成分区分开来。美国人珍视的《人权宣言》又添上了同样有力的“人的义务”。对于自由的强调，也由一种新的纪律观加以平衡——纪律必须服务于共同体。与此同时，在逆境的学校里，美国人原先在思想上走极端的习惯也大体被克服：无论是走向顽硬的物质主义，还是走向多愁善感、追逐新奇的宗教，都不再那么有市场。公谊会，也就是贵格会，过去在北美一直是个重要教派，如今终于迎来了它的时代。早在英国殖民的最初时期，他们就发挥过显著作用；他们不仅主张温和而理性地对待原住民，也强调个人勇气、献身精神以及在一切实际事务中的主动性。最好的贵格会信徒向来能把精明务实的商业能力与神秘主义的宁静结合起来。当然，在最坏的时候，这种结合也会导致一种尤其令人厌恶的自欺：工作日里对雇员实行冷酷却自称“家长式”的专制，星期日则躲进宗教静观的梦境，用精神补偿和自我放纵来安慰自己。但时代已经改变，贵格会也经历了复兴与净化。青年贵格会那种不拘教义的神秘主义，加上他们对实际公益事业的投入，恰好为一个已经深切意识到自己需要这种结合的民族提供了显著榜样。</p>
<p>在贵格会影响以及俄罗斯威胁不断增长的压力下，北美四个国家——加拿大、大西洋共和国、密西西比共和国与太平洋共和国——再次统一。北美又一次成为一个大国，虽然已经不是最强大的国家。有一段时间，它甚至仿佛会成为模范社会，通过自身榜样和领导拯救人类。这里似乎终于出现了对“精神”的真正信仰：它并不善于用理论表达自己，也不依赖教条。这里似乎也出现了真正的自由主义——由能够自我约束的自由公民组成；以及真正的共产主义——由彼此尊重的个人组成。关于这个新而幸福社会的传闻，即使在被征服的欧洲，也越过俄罗斯帝国的审查传播开来，鼓舞着无数反对独裁政权的地下人士。新北美与新印度之间保持密切联系，频繁交换思想。贵格会从印度智慧中学到许多，同时也把大量美国式的实践能力带给印度。</p>
<p>可是渐渐地，人们发现美国复兴不知为何缺少生命力。旧日美国那股强硬的冲劲与推进力似乎衰退了。表面上一切都很好，甚至像乌托邦。人口生活在安全而节制的舒适中，阶级差异几乎完全消失，教育有意识地指向培养负责任的公民。欧洲古典文化与基督教文化被重新研究，而且带着新的热情与新的批判眼光；因为人们已经意识到，真正足以对抗新式野蛮主义的解药就在欧洲传统之中。然而尽管社会处处表现出理智与善意，却仍然缺了些什么。美国的榜样只能吸引本来就倾向于接受它的人，对全人类的大多数毫无震撼力。许多观察者承认北美是一个舒适、和善的社会；但他们说，它停滞而平庸，无力给这个动荡的世界提供方向。毫无疑问，这种普遍无力的一部分原因，是北美和欧洲共同遭受的平均智力下降。社会缺乏有能力的领导者和眼光深远的人；普通公民虽然受过良好的公民训练，心智却迟钝，无法以清醒的头脑真正献身于国家的理想。另一方面，新的俄罗斯帝国主义虽然缺陷重重，却把短命的第四德意志帝国那种近乎十字军式、骨子里带神秘主义色彩的狂热，同最初俄国革命至少一部分根本性的正当性结合了起来。面对俄罗斯的活力与魅力，美国的榜样很难吸引人。就连在南美大陆，北美提供的引导最后也证明无效；南方国家一个接一个，或者主动转向俄罗斯寻求指导，或者被强行吞并。</p>
<p>北美大陆自身也开始蔓延沮丧情绪。其原因之一、同时也是结果之一，就是人口以越来越快的速度减少。国家用尽各种办法鼓励生育，却毫无成效。政府发放高额生育补贴，还以荣誉称号奖励多子女父母。避孕虽未被法律禁止，却受到道德谴责。尽管如此，出生率继续下降，人口平均年龄不断上升。劳动力成了无比珍贵的资源。节省劳动的设备被发展到地球此前从未见过的程度。家务劳动几乎被电气装置完全消灭；全国交通主要由自动调节铁路承担。由于人口以中年人为主，他们待在地面比飞在空中更自在。能源并不短缺，西北部深切而曲折的海岸线蕴藏巨大潮汐电力资源。然而即便拥有充足能源和其他自然资源，北美社会还是因为平庸的智力水平以及青年人口日益短缺而逐步陷入紊乱。每个孩子都由“国家生育部”焦虑地照管，从教育到技术训练，一切最好的资源都倾注在他们身上。每个年轻男女都能得到繁荣生活的保证，并获得一份服务共同体的技术工作。然而，中年人口比重越来越高，使整个社会政策越来越保守。嘴上虽然仍然赞美旧日的拓荒精神和永无止境的进步理想，这个社会实际上追求的不过是维持自身稳定与舒适。对于青年而言，这绝不是令人满足的理想。那些没有被长辈权威压服的年轻人，于是猛烈反对共和国的整个社会秩序与意识形态，因此极易受到俄罗斯帝国共产主义的宣传感染。后者此时正借用革命时代那些仍能激动人心的旧口号，为支配全球作最后努力，并向成群充满活力、却缺乏批判精神的青年提供大量冒险与勇武的机会。</p>
<p>印度的陷落令以中年人为主的北美社会极度惊惶。等到苏维埃独裁政权终于挑起与北美的冲突，内部裂痕已经使抵抗变得不可能。中年人政权垮台，青年少数派夺取权力，并欢迎俄罗斯的空中舰队到来。那面锤子与镰刀的旗帜，过去曾经是最鼓舞人心的光明意志象征，如今虽已严重堕落，却还是被悬挂在国会大厦之上。</p>
<p>整个南北美洲就此落入俄罗斯控制，澳大利亚和新西兰也一样。与此同时，在非洲南部和中部，黑人各族经历一连串失败而血腥的起义后，终于推翻白人统治者，并以人类历史上规模最巨大的屠杀报复数百年的压迫。倘若他们具有足够的政治经验，此时本可能建立世界上最强大的国家之一，因为非洲内陆拥有惊人的水力资源。即使在欧洲统治时期，这些资源已经得到相当程度开发，但仍有巨大储量未被利用。可惜，黑人各族长期处于奴役之中，以至于无法有效组织自己和自己的大陆。非洲出现的黑人国家很快彼此冲突。外国压迫一旦消失，共同目标也随之消失；非洲于是陷入连绵不断的小规模战争与内战。然而俄罗斯帝国主义逐步利用黑人之间的不团结，把整个非洲一点点吞并。</p>
</section>

<h2>ii. NORTH AMERICA</h2>
<p>ISHALL NOT pause to recount all the wars and social tumults of this age. I could not, if I would, give a clear report on them. I can remember only that waves of fruitless agony spread hither and thither over the whole planet like seismic waves in the planet’s crust. Fruitless the agony seemed to me because time after time hope was disappointed. The door to a new world was thrust ajar, then slammed.</p>
<p>Thus in India, when freedom had at last been gained, and under the stress of external danger Hindus and Mohammedans had sunk their differences, it seemed for a while that out of these dark Aryan peoples the truth was coming which could save mankind. For the ancient Indian wisdom, which permeated all the faiths, now came more clearly into view, stripped of the irrelevances of particular creeds. The new India, it seemed, while armed with European science and European resolution, would teach mankind a quietude and detachment which Europe lacked. But somehow the movement went awry, corrupted by the surviving power of the Indian princes and capitalists. The wealthy controlled the new state for their own ends. Public servants were venal and inefficient. And the ancient wisdom, though much advertised, became merely an excuse for tolerating gross social evils. When at last the armies of the Russian Empire poured through the Himalayan passes, the rulers of India could not cope with the attack, and the peoples of India were on the whole indifferent to a mere change of masters. Not until much later were the Indians to make their great contribution to human history.</p>
<p>There were other hopeful movements of regeneration. Obscurely I can remember a great and promising renaissance in North America. Adversity had purged Americans of their romantic commercialism. No longer could the millionaire, the demi-god of money power, command admiration and flattering imitation from the humble masses. Millionaires no longer existed. And the population was becoming conscious that personal money power had been the main cause of the perversion of the old civilization. For a while the Americans refused to admit to themselves that their ‘hundred per cent Americanism’ had been a failure; but suddenly the mental barrier against this realization collapsed. Within a couple of years the whole mental climate of the American people was changed. Up and down the continent men began to re-examine the principles on which American civilization had been based, and to sort out the essential values from the false accretions. Their cherished formulation of the Rights of Man was now supplemented by an emphatic statement of man’s duties. Their insistence on freedom was balanced by a new stress on discipline in service of the community. At the same time, in the school of adversity the former tendency to extravagance in ideas, either in the direction of hard-baked materialism or towards sentimental new-fangled religion, was largely overcome. The Society of Friends, who had always been a powerful sect in North America, now came into their own. They had been prominent long ago during the earliest phase of colonization from England, and had stood not only for gentleness and reasonableness towards the natives but also for individual courage, devotion, and initiative in all practical affairs. At their best they had always combined hard-headed business capacity with mystical quietism. At their worst, undoubtedly, this combination resulted in self-deception of a particularly odious kind. A ruthless though ‘paternal’ tyranny over employees was practised on weekdays, and on Sundays compensation and self-indulgence was found in a dream- world of religious quietism. But changed times had now brought about a revival and a purging. The undoctrinal mysticism of the Young Friends and their practical devotion to good works became a notable example to a people who were by now keenly aware of the need for this very combination.</p>
<p>Under the influence of the Friends and the growing danger from Russia, four North American states, Canada, the Atlantic Republic, the Mississippi Republic, and the Pacific Republic, were once more unified. North America became once more a great, though not the greatest, power. For a while, moreover, it looked as though North America would become the model community, destined to save mankind by example and by leadership. Here at last, it seemed, was the true though inarticulate and un-doctrinal faith in the spirit. Here was the true liberalism of self-disciplined free citizens, the true communism of mutually respecting individuals. Rumour of this new happy society began to spread even in conquered Europe in spite of the Russian imperial censorship, and to hearten the many secret opponents of the dictatorship. Between the new North America and the new India there was close contact and interchange of ideas. From the Indian wisdom the Friends learned much, and they gave in return much American practical skill.</p>
<p>But it became clear that the American renaissance somehow lacked vitality. Somehow the old American forcefulness and drive had waned. On the surface all seemed well, and indeed Utopian. The population lived in security and frugal comfort. Class differences had almost wholly vanished. Education was consciously directed towards the creation of responsible citizens. European classical and Christian culture was studied afresh, with a new zeal and a new critical judgment; for it was realized that in the European tradition lay the true antidote to the new-fangled barbarism. Yet in spite of all this manifestation of sanity and good will, something was lacking. The American example appealed only to those who were already well- disposed. The great mass of mankind remained unimpressed. Many observers conceded that North America was a comfortable and amiable society; but it was stagnant, they said, and mediocre. It was incapable of giving a lead to a troubled world. No doubt this general ineffectiveness was partly due to the decline of average intelligence which North America shared with Europe. There Was a lack of able leaders and men of far-reaching vision; and the average citizen, though well trained in citizenship, was mentally sluggish and incapable of clear-headed devotion to the ideals of his state. The new Russian imperialism, on the other hand, in spite of all its faults, combined the crusading and at heart mystical fervour of the short- lived German Fourth Reich with some measure at least of the fundamental rightness the original Russian revolution. In competition with the vigour and glamour of Russia the American example had little power to attract ‘men. Even in the South American continent the lead given by the North Americans proved after all ineffective. One by one the Southern states turned increasingly to Russia for guidance, or were forcibly annexed.</p>
<p>In the Northern Continent itself disheartenment was spreading. One of its causes, and one of its effects, was an increasingly rapid decline of population. Every inducement was made to encourage procreation, but in vain. The state granted high maternity subsidies, and honorific titles were offered to parents of large families. Contraception, though not illegal, was morally condemned. In spite of all this, the birth rate continued to decline, and the average age of the population to increase. Labour became a most precious commodity. Labour-saving devices were developed to a pitch hitherto unknown on the planet. Domestic service was completely eliminated by electrical contraptions. Transport over the whole country was carried out mainly by self- regulating railways. The predominantly middle-aged population felt more at home on the ground than in the air. There was no shortage of power, for the deeply indented north western coast-line afforded vast resources of tidal electricity. But in spite of this wealth of power and other physical resources North American society began to fall into disorder simply through its mediocre intelligence and increasing shortage of young people. Every child was brought up under the anxious care of the National Fertility Department. Every device of education and technical training was lavished upon him, or her. Every young man and every young woman was assured of prosperity and of a career of skilled work in service of the community. But the increasing preponderance of the middle-aged gave an increasingly conservative tilt to the whole social policy. In spite of lip-service to the old pioneering spirit and the old ideal of endless progress, the effective aim of this society was merely to maintain itself in stability and comfort. This was no satisfying ideal for the young. Those young people who were not cowed by the authority of their elders were flung into violent opposition to the whole social order and ideology of the Republic. They were thus very susceptible to the propaganda of Russian imperial communism, which under the old heart-stirring slogans of the Revolution was now making its supreme effort to dominate the world, and was able to offer great opportunities of enterprise and courage to its swarms of vigorous but uncritical young.</p>
<p>The fall of India dismayed the middle-aged North American community. When at last the Soviet dictatorship picked a quarrel with it, internal dissensions made resistance impossible. The regime of the middle-aged collapsed. The youthful minority seized power and welcomed the Russian aerial armada. The Hammer and Sickle, formerly the most heartening emblem of the will for the light, but now sadly debased, was displayed on the Capitol.</p>
<p>The whole double American continent now fell under the control of Russia, and with it Australia and New Zealand. In Southern and Central Africa, meanwhile, the Black populations, after a series of abortive and bloody rebellions, had at last overthrown their white masters, avenging themselves for centuries of oppression by perpetrating the greatest massacre of history. If the Negroes had been politically experienced they might now have become one of the most formidable states in the world, for the inland water power of their continent was immense. Even under European domination this had been to a large extent exploited, but vast resources remained to be tapped. Unfortunately the Black populations had been so long inservitude that they were incapable of organizing themselves and their country efficiently. The Negro states which emerged in Africa were soon at loggerheads with one another. When foreign oppression had been abolished, unity of purpose ceased; and the condition of Africa was one of constant petty wars and civil wars. Little by little however, Russian imperialism, profiting by Negro disunity, annexed the whole of Africa.</p>
