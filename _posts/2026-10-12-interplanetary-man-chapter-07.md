<h3>七、世界共同体</h3>
<p>在前面那段过于粗略的精神讨论之后，现在我可以说明，在我看来，其他行星究竟应该被怎样使用。</p>
<p>如果其中任何一颗行星上住着智慧生命，那么人类显然都应该尽最大努力，与那些非人类智慧建立真正的共同体关系；应当认真设法进入它们的立场，并同它们合作，使双方在经济和精神上都得到丰富。</p>
<p>但既然其他行星事实上不大可能住着智慧生命，我们必须回答的问题就变成：人类应该拿这些处女世界怎么办？答案是：应当用对待自己母星的同一种原则去对待它们。人类不应该只为了增加自己的奢侈享受而使用它们，也不应该仅仅把它们当作获取力量、为力量本身服务的手段。人类应该为了精神而使用它们。应当利用这些世界的资源，使精神能够在人类生活中得到更充分的表达。应当这样利用它们：给每个人尽可能大的机会，发展并表达那种独特的人类能力——成为精神的工具，成为敏感而有智慧地认识客观宇宙的中心，成为热爱一切可爱事物的中心，并为了精神采取创造性行动。人类应努力使自身成为“共同体中的人格”（personality-in-community）的光辉范例：一个由高度多样的个体构成的社会，而这些个体又通过相互洞察、理解、同情，以及共同表达精神而结合起来。对于任何共同体来说，只要所有个体都拥有足够的想象力，能够进入彼此的立场，那么个体差异越大，精神就越可能得到充分表达。</p>
<p>正是在这里，其他行星打开了新的可能。如果人类能在某些世界上建立适应当地环境的新的人类或准人类种族，那么这些种族按照各自适宜的方式生活，应当会发展出地球人目前无法想象的精神表达形式。不同世界之间如果能够交流，而且各个物种都拥有足够的想象力，那么所有世界都将因此在精神上得到丰富。因此，太阳系的目标似乎应当是：成为一个由高度多样的世界组成的行星际共同体。每个世界都由与其相适应的智慧种族居住，都形成自己独特的“人类”，并向共同经验贡献自己独特的宇宙观。通过汇聚这笔经验财富，通过这种“世界共同体”（commonwealth of worlds），应该能够出现全新的心灵与精神发展层次，而这些层次对今天的人类完全不可想象。</p>
<p>可以冒险举一个日常生活中的类比。真正令人满意的婚姻，并不是夫妻双方性格和兴趣完全一致的婚姻，而是双方尽可能不同，同时又各自拥有足够的想象力，能够进入对方的世界并分享对方的兴趣。换句话说，满意的婚姻是多样性中的统一，是一种精神共生。</p>
<p>另一个类比也许更有帮助。今天的人类被两套极不相同的思想与价值体系、两种意识形态深深分裂。大体上，这两种意识形态分别以美国和俄国为中心。在我看来，每一方都包含另一方忽略的非常重要的真理。如果战争能够避免，并且双方在适当时候都能向对方学习，那么最后出现的人类，也许会比没有这场强大对立面之间的文化冲突时，成熟得多，在精神上也丰富得多。顺便说一句，我忍不住希望西欧，尤其是不列颠，能够在统一这两个“半真理”的过程中发挥重要作用。因为欧洲所处的位置，使它能够同时同情双方的半真理。我们过去曾是个人自由的捍卫者；如今严酷环境又迫使我们规划自己的社会，并约束个人主义冲动。也许我们能够给世界上一堂示范课：怎样为了自由而进行规划。此外还要加上一点——恕我对那些精力旺盛的青春期民族，也就是美国人和俄国人，给予应有尊重——欧洲虽然缺点很多，相对而言却已经成年；同时，它还是一个伟大文明传统和精神生活传统的保管者，而这个传统在今天这个大体仍然野蛮的世界里还有重要功能。美国和俄国，当然，还有印度和中国，无疑都将在未来扮演重大的角色；但在今天，要恢复分裂人类的统一、拯救我们的物种免于自我毁灭，所需要的是欧洲的气质——是的，尤其是这个岛屿民族的气质。想到我们此时此刻、就在这个岛屿、就在这座伟大城市中的行动，也许会决定那个“世界共同体”的梦想究竟成为现实，还是永远不再可能，这实在是一件庄严的事情。</p>
</section>
<section data-story-lang="en" lang="en" hidden>
<h3><span id="a1089"></span>VII. A Commonwealth of Worlds</h3>
<p><span class="t28">I</span><span class="t31">N</span><span class="t30"> </span><span class="t31">THE</span><span class="t30"> </span><span class="t31">LIGHT</span><span class="t30"> </span>of this all too superficial discussion of the spirit, I can now say what, in my view, is the right use of the planets.</p>
<p>If any of them is inhabited by intelligent beings, then clearly man should do his utmost to adopt a relationship of genuine community with those non-human intelligences, seeking earnestly to enter into their point of view, and to co-operate with them for mutual enrichment, both economic and spiritual. But since it is unlikely that any other planet is in fact inhabited by intelligent beings, then the question that we must answer is, what should man do with those virgin worlds? And the answer is that he should deal with them precisely as with his native world. He should use them neither for the sole purpose of increasing his luxury, nor simply as a means to power for mere power’s sake. He should use them for the spirit. He should avail himself of their resources in such ways as to advance the expression of the spirit in the life of mankind. He should use them so as to afford to every human being the greatest possible opportunity for developing and expressing his distinctively human capacity as an instrument of the spirit, as a centre of sensitive and intelligent awareness of the objective universe, as a centre of love of all lovely things, and of creative action for the spirit. He should strive to make of the human race a glorious example of personality-in-community, a society of very diverse individuals united in mutual insight, understanding and sympathy, and in co-operative expression of the spirit. And for the full expression of the spirit in any community, the greater the diversity of individuals the better, provided that they all have sufficient imagination to enter into each other’s points of view.</p>
<p>It is in this connection that the planets open up new possibilities. If man can establish in some of those other worlds new and specially adapted human or quasi-human races, then those races, living in their appropriate ways, should develop new expressions of the spirit at present inconceivable to terrestrial man. And through the intercourse of these diverse worlds, provided that each species has sufficient imaginative power, all should be spiritually enriched. Thus the goal for the solar system would seem to be that it should become an interplanetary community of very diverse worlds each inhabited by its appropriate race of intelligent beings, its characteristic “humanity,” and each contributing to the common experience its characteristic view of the universe. Through the pooling of this wealth of experience, through this “commonwealth of worlds,” new levels of mental and spiritual development should become possible, levels at present quite inconceivable to man.</p>
<p>A homely analogy may be hazarded. The really satisfactory marriage is not one in which husband and wife have identical character and interests, but one in which each is as different as possible from the other, though each has enough imagination to enter into the other and share the other’s interests. In fact, the satisfactory marriage is a unity in diversity and a spiritual symbiosis.</p>
<p>Another analogy may help. Mankind to-day is deeply divided between two profoundly different systems of thought and value, two ideologies. Roughly, these ideologies centre upon America and Russia respectively. Each, I believe, contains very important truth which the other ignores. If war is avoided, and if in due season each side can learn from the other, the result may be a far more adult and spiritually enriched humanity than could ever have occurred without this cultural clash of mighty opposites. Incidentally, I cannot help hoping that Western Europe, and particularly Britain, may play an important part in the unifying of the two half-truths. For Europe is in a position to sympathize with both the half-truths. Formerly champions of individual liberty, we are now being forced by dire circumstances to plan our own society and discipline our individualistic impulses. Perhaps we shall be able to give the world an object lesson in planning for liberty. Add to this the fact that after all, with due respect to those vigorous adolescents, the Americans and the Russians, Europe, with all its faults, is relatively adult; and moreover is the custodian of a great tradition of civilization and spiritual life which has still an important function in this largely barbarian world. America and Russia, yes, and India and China, have doubtless momentous parts to play in the future; but to-day it is the European temper, yes, and particularly the temper of this island people, that is needed to restore unity to a divided mankind, and save our species from destroying itself. It is a solemn thought that perhaps our actions here and now, in this island, in this great city, may determine whether that dream of a commonwealth of worlds is to be made real or made for ever impossible.</p>
