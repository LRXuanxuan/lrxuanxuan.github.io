<h2>一、美洲问题</h2>
<p>人类的麻烦远没有结束，而且永远也不会结束。不过，随着新的世界秩序建立起来，这个物种进入了自身历史的一个新阶段。光明力量与黑暗力量之间的均势原本已经略微偏向光明，而一种大为改善的社会结构又使天平进一步向光明倾斜。对于创建新世界的那一代人中的许多人来说，他们不仅觉得一个新时代已经开始——这一点确实如此——还觉得从此再也不会有严重的麻烦。在这一点上，他们错了。许多尚未准备好接受新秩序的人，不顾自身意愿也被纳入其中。他们内心仍眷恋旧有价值。他们仍希望世界保持混乱，好让自己能够继续从事这样或那样的劫掠；他们最在意的仍是个人支配或部族荣耀。因此，在新世界中，他们开始制造麻烦。他们试图削弱联邦权威，动摇人民对新秩序的信心；他们夸大它的失败，贬低它的成功，挑动各民族之间以及各社会阶层之间的分歧。</p>
<p>在新秩序能够稳固到任何大集团都不再敢以武力反抗它以前，有两场重大冲突必须解决。一场是东西半球之间的冲突，另一场则是领导者与被领导者之间的冲突。</p>
<p>在半球冲突中，澳大利亚和新西兰必须算入美洲半球，因为很早以前它们就已深受美国影响。在自由民族同帝国作战的时期，美国人所受波及相对较小。北美自从那座疲惫的乌托邦被俄罗斯帝国吞并后，已经发生了巨大变化。在随后那种并不十分高效的暴政下，他们发现了一个新的目标：争取自身解放。早先那些曾欢迎俄国人的青年男女，其子女长大之后，重新发现了伟大美国传统中的德性。第一共和国的英雄——华盛顿、杰斐逊、林肯——又一次被人暗中研读和赞颂。一股新的、却很有活力的个人主义暗流开始流遍北美。国家，即使是昔日美国那种乌托邦式国家，也再次显得不过是一种令人不快却又必要的东西；而俄罗斯式国家则是一场纯粹的灾祸。人们重新赞美个人进取心与创造力、自由以及个人正直。</p>
<p>在俄罗斯权力衰落期间，这股个人主义潮流催生出一种潜藏的个人资本主义，甚至可以说是一种‘罗宾汉式资本主义’。美国最聪明的一批人，在这一点上仿效中世纪世界的犹太人，想方设法从征服者手中攫取财富，再把其中很大一部分转给受压迫的本国人民。后来，中国人实行了更有效率的统治，这套美国地下资本主义被有计划地摧毁了，但它留下了一种强烈的个人主义精神。中国垮台以后，美国人恢复了一种多少带有仁慈色彩、也较为节制的资本主义。随后，在新一代财富贵族推动下，美国掀起了巨大的物质重建浪潮。新的资本主义同旧资本主义有显著不同。它更像是旧资本主义曾经宣称自己是、实际上却从未成为的那种制度。毫无疑问，新资本主义较高的道德水准，正是普通人心中光明意志略有增强的一种表现。</p>
<p>帝国暴政从未像蹂躏世界其他地方那样，以同样彻底的残酷程度压到美洲、澳大利亚和新西兰人民身上。因此，这些民族对自己那种负责任的个人主义精神颇有信心，也就不容易认识到，把个人、社会阶级和国家的私人事业置于共同世界权威的控制之下，究竟有多么迫切。</p>
<p>争端首先围绕美国潮汐能源的处置爆发。世界联邦政府宣布，今后一切主要生产资源都必须专由世界政府控制，因为只有世界政府才能把它们有效组织起来，以完成一项巨大任务：把所有民族的生活水准提高到足以使人的心理得到充分发展的程度。美国资本家回答说，这套宏大的潮汐发电系统是靠他们自己的进取精神建成的，而他们又眼看着它长期遭到刚刚倒台的世界帝国政府的榨取和滥用，所以他们打算继续自己控制它。当然，他们也同意，这套系统必须严格服务于全人类的利益；他们并不想让它只造福美国，更无意借此加强本国资本家阶级。‘但是，’他们说，‘正因为我们在本国鼓励私人企业，才成为世界上最伟大的发明者和组织者，所以我们要求——不，我们负有责任——管理自己独一无二的发电体系，并按照我们认为最有利于世界全面经济发展的方式分配它的能源。还有谁能胜任？西藏革命领袖不能。无论他们的功绩多么辉煌，他们在经济组织方面的经验都过于有限。印度人不能，因为他们既不是组织家，也不是工程师。中国人也不能，因为眼下他们仍过分浸泡在最近那段帝国主义传统里。只有美国人必须负责组织领域，而把教育与精神领导这项伟大任务交给西藏人。’反对者则急切指出，任何民族、任何阶级都不应被指定为某一领域的天然领导者。凡具备领导才能的个人，自会在适合自己的领域上升到负责职位。特权与既得利益决不能再度出现在地球上。况且，美国的社会制度虽然曾经瞒着异族暴君有效地建立了美国繁荣，却完全不适合新的世界秩序；在新秩序中，整个世界必须接受充分协调的总体规划。</p>
<p>美国资本家拒绝让步。虽然他们没有武装，却对自己的力量十分自信，因为他们确信正义站在自己一边。美国国家政府宣布退出世界联邦。面对这一直接挑战，世界政府——其中也包括美国委员——用尽可能友善的措辞呼吁美国人重新考虑这一意义重大的举动，并提醒他们美国历史上那场古老的‘分离战争’。他们又不情愿却坚定地补充说，如有必要，将使用武力阻止美国脱离这个新的、更伟大的联邦。人类已经宣告自身统一，不会再容忍地方性的主权国家。作为回应，美国资本家切断了向欧洲输送剩余电力的巨型电缆。世界政府随即命令驻美世界警察占领所有发电站，并确保电缆得到修复。</p>
<p>美国人当然和其他所有民族一样，早已同意废除各国武装。他们有自己的非武装警察；与此同时，由各民族人员组成的武装世界警察部队，驻扎在两块大陆的各个要地。接管发电站的行动没有遭到抵抗；但美国政府组织了一场总罢工作为抗议，各城市爆发大规模示威。在大陆若干地区，骚乱者袭击了世界政府办事处，而本地警察没有干预。于是世界警察接管了南北美洲，并连同澳大利亚、新西兰一起置于直接控制之下。美洲半球的民主政府停止运作。骚乱广泛蔓延。不过，美国、澳大利亚和新西兰政府认识到单纯骚乱毫无意义，于是组织了一场规模巨大的公民不服从和不合作运动。</p>
<p>在整个危机期间，世界政府表现出极大的克制。许多人被捕，但囚犯发现自己几乎像贵宾一样受到待遇。许多人甚至根本没有被继续拘禁，而是在假释条件下离开美洲半球，并由世界政府出资，在另行通知之前周游世界其他地区。世界政府希望，这样他们就能亲眼看到新制度如何运作，并受到它的感染。有关方面还为他们提供特别便利，让他们采访主管工业组织的高级官员。</p>
<p>与此同时，一场巨大的言辞战争响彻全球。美国人被允许享有完全的表达自由，双方的无线电宣传如洪水般倾泻而出。维持美洲秩序变得越来越困难，袭击外国人的事件层出不穷，赤裸裸的民族主义激情一天比一天高涨。</p>
<p>最后，当时那位祖鲁人出身的世界总统决定作出一个巨大姿态，以结束争端。他提出同两名美国籍世界政府成员一起周游美洲，并与美国资本主义的一切领袖进行深入会谈；在离开这个半球之前，他将提出新的方案。美国方面接受了。很难说这一行三人中究竟谁需要更大的勇气：是那位其种族在美国人眼中仍不受欢迎的总统，还是那两名被视为‘叛徒’的美国人。他们没有护卫，也没有武装，在这个半球旅行了四个月，随后召集所有美国领袖开会。总统提醒听众西藏人那场史诗般的斗争，以及人类联邦的建立。</p>
<p>接着，他慷慨赞扬了美国的成就，也赞扬了叛乱者——他并不回避这个词——如今真诚捍卫的那些理想。总统承认，自己也从此次旅行中学到了很多东西，现在愿提出一项方案。他承认，在世界目前这种过渡状态中，在经济财富迅速而令人目眩地增长之际，允许工业中的私人企业拥有相当大的活动余地是有充分理由的。他也承认，大多数美国资本家的动机是慷慨的社会性动机，而且美国人民总体上支持他们。另一方面，世界政府不能容忍任何公然蔑视其权威的企图，否则，这个历经艰辛建立、总体上十分有益的新秩序很快就会崩溃。不过，权威已经毫不犹豫地得到了维护，世界政府现在有资格表现得宽宏一些。因此，他在本届政府完全同意的情况下提出一项临时安排：允许美洲在联邦内部享有经济自治。世界政府保留持续检查美国工业的权力，也绝不允许侵犯联邦宪法序言所规定的工人权利。某些工业领域完全不得交给资本主义企业，例如军备工业和主要传播媒介。这些领域以及教育都必须由美国国家实行公有化，同时最终受世界政府控制。世界政府也保留否决任何它认为不符合世界利益的产业的权力，并可以命令美国工业生产世界所需的某种特定产品；这类生产可由世界政府补贴。因此，美国资本家必须把自己看成世界政府之下的公务人员：他们通过公开市场获得报酬，但如果违反协议，就可能被免职并没收财产。当然，美国人民随时保有废除这整套地方资本主义制度的权利。</p>
<p>最终建立起来的，就是这种‘社会主义内部的资本主义’妥协。若不是双方都确信对方在根本上抱有善意，这种本来极不稳固的安排绝不可能成功解决冲突。世界政府从第一次重大危机中走出时，其权威反而有所增强。总体而言，这一妥协运转良好。</p>
<p>然而在南美，它只维持了十年。当地资本家阶级中较坏的一派掌握了权力，开始秘密违反协议。南美人民逐渐意识到自己正在遭到剥削——虽然不像过去那样明目张胆，却至少足以令人恼火。社会主义运动迅速壮大。世界政府预见到结局，并未采取行动，因为它宁愿由当地力量完成向社会主义地方经济的转变。南美资本主义的老板们向北美同行求援，却没有得到响应。于是南美没有发生严重冲突便转向了社会主义。</p>
<p>几年后，澳大利亚和新西兰也照此办理。又过了不到二十年，北美人自己在经历一番激烈争论后，也决定全面加入世界经济体系。</p>

<h2>i. DIFFICULTIES OVER AMERICA</h2>
<p>THE TROUBLES OF mankind were by no means over. Nor will they ever be. But with the founding of the new world-order the species entered on a new phase of its career, in which the balance of the forces of the light and the forces of darkness, already slightly favourable to the light, was tipped still farther by a much improved social structure. To many of the generation which founded the new world it seemed not only that a new age had started, which was true, but that henceforth there could be no serious troubles. In this they were mistaken. Masses of human beings who were not ready for the new order were included in it against their wills. In their hearts they still clung to the old values. They still desired a disorderly world so that they could continue to practise brigandage of one kind or another. They still cared mainly for personal dominance or for tribal glory. In the new world, therefore, they set out to make trouble. They tried to undermine the federal authority and the people’s confidence in the new order. They exaggerated its failures, disparaged its successes, fomented the differences between the peoples and between social classes.</p>
<p>Two great conflicts had to be solved before the new order could be so firmly established that no large group within it would ever dare to take arms against it. The one was a conflict between the eastern and western hemispheres, the other between the leaders and the led.</p>
<p>In the conflict of hemispheres, Australia and New Zealand must be counted in the American hemisphere, as they had long ago come under the American influence. During the struggle between the free peoples. and the empires the Americans had been relatively untouched. The North Americans had greatly changed since their tired Utopia had been annexed by the Russian Empire. Under the not very efficient tyranny which followed they discovered a new aim, namely to free themselves. A new generation of young people, sons and daughters of those earlier young who had welcomed the Russians, began to rediscover the virtue of the great American tradition. The heroes of the first American republic, Washington, Jefferson, Lincoln, were once more, though secretly, studied and praised. A new but vigorous and underground current of individualism began to flow through North America. Once more the state, even in the Utopian form that had once existed in America, began to seem merely an unpleasant necessity. The Russian state was an unmitigated curse. Men lauded once more the virtues of individual enterprise and ingenuity, liberty and personal integrity.</p>
<p>During the decline of the Russian power the movement of individualism generated a sort of submerged individual capitalism, a Robin Hood capitalism, one might say; for the outstanding American intelligences, copying in this respect the Jews of the medieval world, found means of wresting wealth from their conquerors and transferring much of it to their own oppressed people. Under the subsequent and more efficient Chinese rule this system of illicit capitalism in America was methodically destroyed, but it left a spirit of passionate individualism. With the fall of China the Americans reverted to a more or less benevolent and restrained capitalism. There followed a great wave of material reconstruction under the influence of the new aristocrats of wealth. The new capitalism was strikingly different from the old. It was much more like what the old capitalism had claimed to be but never was. No doubt the higher standard of the new capitalism was a symptom of the slightly increased power of the will for the light in the minds of ordinary people.</p>
<p>Imperial tyranny had never impinged on the peoples of America, Australia, and New Zealand with quite the same searching brutality as on the rest of the world. And so, confident in their own spirit of responsible individualism, they did not easily recognize the urgency of bringing the private enterprise of individuals, social classes and nations under the control of a common world authority.</p>
<p>Trouble arose over the disposal of American tidal power. The World Federal Government declared that all the great resources of production must henceforth be controlled exclusively by the World Government, which alone could organize them effectively for the immense task of raising the standard of life of all peoples to the level needed for full psychological development. The American capitalists replied that, having constructed their great tidal system by their own enterprise, having watched it for so long being exploited and misused by the late imperial government of the world, they intended to retain control of it themselves. They agreed, of course, that the system ought to be used strictly for the benefit of the human race as a whole. They had no intention of using it to benefit America exclusively, still less to strengthen their own capitalist class. ‘But since we,’ they said, ‘by fostering private enterprise in our country, have become the world’s greatest inventors and organizers, we claim the right, nay the duty, of managing our own unique generating system and disposing of its power as seems fit to us for the full economic development of the world. Who else could do it? Not the Tibetan revolutionary leaders. Splendid as their record is, their experience of economic organization is far too restricted. Not the Indians, for they are neither organizers nor engineers. Not the Chinese, for they are for the present too soaked in the tradition of their recent imperialism. It is the Americans alone who must take charge in the field of organization, leaving to the Tibetans the great task of educational and spiritual leadership.’ In reply it was urgently pointed out that no one people and no one class should be assigned leadership in any sphere. Those individuals who were capable of leadership would rise to positions of responsibility in whatever fields were suited to them. Privilege and vested interest must never more be allowed to appear on the earth. Moreover the American social system, though it had usefully built up American prosperity behind the backs of the alien tyrants, was quite unsuited to the new world-order, in which there must be fully co- ordinated planning of the world as a whole.</p>
<p>The American capitalists refused to give way. Though unarmed they were confident in their strength, because they were confident in the rightness of their cause. The American national government announced its withdrawal from the World Federation. To this direct challenge the World Government, including its American members, appealed to the Americans in the most friendly terms to reconsider this momentous step, and reminded them of the ancient American ‘War of Secession’. They added, reluctantly but firmly, that, if necessary, force would be used to prevent the secession from the new and greater Federation. The human race had declared its unity and would no longer tolerate local sovereign powers. In answer the American capitalists cut the great cables by which their surplus current was transmitted to Europe. The World Government ordered the world police in America to occupy all the generating stations and see that the cables were repaired.</p>
<p>The Americans, of course, like all other peoples, had agreed to the abolition of national armaments. They had their own unarmed police; and a contingent of the armed World Police, drawn from all peoples, was stationed at key points throughout the two continents. The seizing of the generators was carried out without opposition; but the American Government organized a general strike in protest, and there were great demonstrations in all the cities. In several parts of the continent rioters attacked the offices of the World Government. The native police did not intervene. Thereupon the World Police took control of the whole of the two American continents, along with Australia and New Zealand. Democratic government in the American hemisphere ceased. Rioting became widespread. But the American, Australian, and New Zealand governments, recognizing the futility of mere rioting, organized a vast campaign of civil disobedience and non-co-operation.</p>
<p>Throughout these troubles the World Government showed great forbearance. There were many arrests, but the prisoners found themselves treated almost as honoured guests. Many of them were not even retained in captivity at all but put on parole on condition that they left the American hemisphere and spent their time, until further notice, in touring the rest of the world, at the expense of the World Government. Thus, it was hoped, they would see the system at work and be impressed by it. Special facilities were given them for interviewing high officials in charge of industrial organization.</p>
<p>Meanwhile a great war of words was resounding throughout the world. The Americans were allowed complete freedom of expression. Floods of radio propaganda issued from both sides. It became increasingly difficult to keep order in the Americas. There were many attacks on foreigners. Sheer nationalistic passion grew from day to day.</p>
<p>At last the President of the World, at this time a Zulu, decided to make a great gesture to end the dispute. He offered to tour the Americas, along with two American members of the World Government, and to meet all the leaders of American capitalism for intimate discussion. Before leaving the hemisphere he would make fresh proposals. The offer was accepted. It is hard to say which member of the party needed the greater courage, the President, whose race was still distasteful to the Americans, or the two American ‘traitors’. Unescorted and unarmed, they travelled in the hemisphere for four months, then called all the American leaders to a conference. The President reminded his hearers of the epic struggle of the Tibetans, and the founding of the Federation of Mankind.</p>
<p>He then paid a generous tribute to the achievement of America and the ideals for which the rebels (he did not shrink from the word) were now (he recognized) making a sincere stand. He himself had learnt much from his tour, and he now had a proposal to make. He recognized that in the world’s present transitional state, a state of rapid and bewildering economic enrichment, there was much to be said for allowing a good deal of scope to private enterprise in industry. He recognized also that the motives of most of the American capitalists were generous social motives, and that the American peoples on the whole supported them. On the other hand the World Government could not tolerate any attempt to flout its authority; otherwise the whole new order, so painfully created and on the whole so beneficial, would soon break down. Authority, however, had been unhesitatingly asserted. The World Government could now afford to be generous. He therefore proposed, with his Government’s full assent, a temporary arrangement allowing the Americas economic autonomy within the Federation. The World Government reserved the power of constant inspection of American industry and would not permit any infringement of the rights of the workers, as laid down in the preamble to the constitution of the Federation. Certain kinds of industry were excluded from capitalist enterprise entirely, such as armaments and the great means of expression. These, and education, were to be nationalized under the American state, subject to final control by the World Government. It also reserved a power of veto on any industry which it regarded as undesirable from the point of view of the world, and it might order American industry to produce some particular kind of goods needed by the world. Such work might be subsidized by the World Government. The American capitalists, then, must regard themselves as civil servants under the World Government, liable to dismissal and confiscation of their property if they broke the agreement, though paid for their services through the open market. The American peoples, of course, would regain the right to abolish the whole system of local capitalism at any time.</p>
<p>Such was the compromise of ‘capitalism within socialism’ that was finally established. The conflict could never have been successfully solved by such a precarious arrangement had not both sides been convinced of the fundamental goodwill of the other. The World Government came out of its first great crisis with increased authority. On the whole the compromise worked.</p>
<p>In South America, however, it lasted only for a decade. There the worse elements of the capitalist class gained power and indulged in secret violations of the agreements. The peoples of South America came to realize that they were being exploited, not flagrantly, as in former times, but at least annoyingly. The movement for socialism rapidly gained ground. The World Government, foreseeing the end, refrained from action, preferring that the change to a socialistic local economy should be brought about by local effort. The bosses of South American capitalism appealed to their colleagues in the northern continent, but in vain. Without trouble the South Americans went over to socialism.</p>
<p>A few years later Australia and New Zealand followed suit. And within a couple of decades the North Americans themselves, not without heated discussion, decided to enter fully into the world economic system.</p>
