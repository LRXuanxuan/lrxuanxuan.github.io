<h2>三、恶魔式世界帝国</h2>
<p>第二俄罗斯帝国就这样终结了。这个帝国本来是人类第一次伟大却命运不济的尝试——试图为了多数人的福祉、而不是少数人的权力来组织社会——所生下的邪恶后代。俄罗斯原有的一些省份匆忙求和，另一些则宣布同时脱离两个帝国独立，却很快又被镇压。只有美洲继续抵抗了两年，但最终还是被征服，并因为这种顽抗受到一种极其特殊的惩罚：所有儿童都被运往世界各地充作奴隶。</p>
<p>随着美洲陷落，人类有史以来第一次成功建立了整个行星的政治统一。帝国中国政府此时采用了“世界天朝政府”的称号，并命令地球上每座城镇、每一个家庭都举行庆祝。各地都拼命制作勉强像样的古代中国龙旗；这种旗帜在第二帝国时期重新启用，从此便成为令人恐惧的世界政府象征。世界各处，甚至在鲜血染红的俄罗斯平原上，人们也焦急地悬挂这种旗帜，或者某种粗劣的仿制品。官方宣称，绿色的中国龙终于吞下了那个长期挑衅般悬在金色天空中的红色圆球。红球如今不再被解释为日本的太阳，而被解释为俄罗斯帝国主义的红色世界。人们则私下低声补上一句：若是运气好，这条龙没准很快就会因为消化不良而死。</p>
<p>世界统一终于实现了！但这是怎样一种统一！整个世界除了统治阶级及其走狗之外，几乎找不到任何相当规模的人群能够安然生活。各地农民都沦为那个全球帝国大地主的奴隶；各地农民都辛苦生产全世界的粮食；各地农民又都在饥饿中遭受严厉管束。矿工和工厂工人同样如此。世界政府没有发动一场宏大的、全球性的社会重建运动，让工人和士兵继续有工作可做，反而解散了一半军队，让其余军队闲置。它完全蔑视工人，因为深信自己有能力强迫他们服从。庞大的技术人员阶层当初之所以被说服支持战争，是因为他们相信世界统一之后，自己终于可以建设全人类的繁荣；可现在他们发现，自己要么被用来加强寡头统治，要么被用来生产寡头的奢侈品；否则就被解雇，然后由国家勉强供养，在贫困与绝望中维持一种半死不活的生活。</p>
<p>个人主义资本主义虽然早已消失，这种遍及全球、已经腐朽的国家资本主义却在许多方面遭受同样的病症。世界政府手中明明掌握着社会规划的权力，却缺乏真正进行规划的意志。统治者唯一关心的只是维护自身地位。原本由中国几大统治家族独占的巨大经济权力，如今被承包给不负责任的国家官员；这些人于是把自己变成所控制产业的独裁者。由于彼此行动几乎没有协调，而且他们反正主要都只想肥自己的腰包，结果自然是一片混乱。失业不断增加，各种相伴而来的灾祸也随之增长。绝望的人口越来越难管理，于是惩罚性屠杀极为频繁。</p>
<p>终于，一项新发明暂时缓解了困境；这也是这个正在衰退的物种还能完成的极少数真正创新之一。一名生物化学家发明出一种方法，可以让人类进入暂停生命活动的状态；据他说，人在其中沉睡多年之后还能轻易被唤醒，仍然“新鲜而年轻”。世界政府相信失业只是暂时现象，未来终有一天会重新需要大量劳动力，于是开始在每个国家修建一整套冷藏仓库，把暂时“多余”的人类存放其中，等时代改变以后再取出来。失业者及其家属被强制送入这些仓库。挣扎的人们被锁链固定在巨大罐体内部一层层搁架上，肩挨着肩平躺；随后，罐中先依次充入几种气体，最后再灌满保存液。几乎每个国家都有数以百万计的男人、女人和儿童，就这样被储存起来供未来使用。工人的生活本来已经枯燥而痛苦到近乎无法忍受，可他们仍想尽一切办法避免被送进冷藏库。光明意志在他们身上表现为一种盲目却顽强的愿望：无论活得多么卑贱，也要继续过主动的生活。然而也有少数人欢迎这个机会——可以逃离现实，却不必永远消灭。他们相信，等到下一阶段重新醒来，自己或许会拥有更好的自我实现机会。对其中多数人来说，接受暂停生命在根本上其实是黑暗意志的表现，只不过被理性化成一种说法，用来安抚仍在余烬般燃烧的光明意志。因为，一个光明意志强烈而清楚的人，会发现自己的心已经无法同出生时代和地方的光明力量斗争分离。无论他多么渴望在一个更幸福的世界中得到更充分的自我实现机会，他都明白，对自己而言，离开这个自己的心智扎根其中的世界，也就根本不可能真正实现自己。只有光明意志较弱的人，才会很快说服自己：真正的机会在别处。于是，随着身体与心理条件持续恶化，人类种族的精神越来越被侵蚀，接受所谓“深眠”的人也越来越多。</p>
<p>这个时期光明意志衰退的重要原因之一，是知识分子的态度。学者、音乐家、画家、电影艺术家，尤其是作家，都公然背叛了自己的职责。在这些群体中，大致可以看到四种人。许多人是政府付钱豢养的仆役，表面上从事独立创作，实际上负责宣传。他们主要做的，是替现行政权粉饰外观，并赞美“合成信仰”的基本原则，尤其是顺从与服从的美德，以及残酷所带来的狂喜。数量更多的，是那些独立却毫无用处的“知识分子鸵鸟”。他们闭眼不看自己时代的恐怖，却通过编织自我膨胀与色情享乐的幻想赢得崇拜和权力；他们用描写其他时代、其他世界的诱人传奇，把人们的注意力从当代罪恶上引开，或者用关于死后生命的高调而空洞的术语让人沉醉。此外，还有大批进步知识分子。他们非常清楚当代社会已经病入膏肓，也以一种梦游般、不太当真的态度阐述自己脆弱的乌托邦；那些设想中往往确实包含大量常识，甚至不乏智慧。但他们的宣传缺少那种只有真正坚定的信念才能产生、足以把人推动到绝望行动中的狂热力量。而他们自己则继续舒适地依靠现有制度生活，住在公寓和郊区住宅里。他们模模糊糊知道，自己本该为了革命放弃一切；可是以他们这样的性格，他们做不到。第四类，则是极少数真诚却无能为力的反叛者；他们试图成为伟大先知，在徒劳而疯狂的努力中白白丢掉性命。</p>
<p>此时，对人类命运最关键的，是技术人员这个阶层的态度：大量受过高度训练的工程师、电气专家、航空专家、农业专家，以及工业中的科学工作者。如果他们能够清楚认识整个人类种族所处的困境，也许本来能够拯救它。然而，他们都是“专家”，而专家从小接受的传统教育就是：专家不应干预政治。当然，在压力真正巨大时，他们也会插手；但由于此前一直刻意同政治保持距离，他们发表的政治意见往往幼稚得可笑，实际政治行动则常常酿成灾难。确实有少数人认真研究过社会，并理解当下疾病的根源。这些人不断努力启发同僚，希望把他们联合起来，对事件发展施加一次巨大的、有组织的控制。毫无疑问，如果光明意志在这个庞大阶层中足够强烈——而这个阶层掌握着全球社会物质生活中无数的杠杆、开关与按钮——他们完全可以在几天之内推翻世界寡头政权，然后着手建立一种健全秩序。然而，号召技术人员行动只得到一种半心半意的回应。多数人耸耸肩，继续自己的工作。少数人胆怯地采取行动，很快就被统治者抓住并加以酷刑。运动失败了。</p>
<p>我觉得非常奇怪：一个几乎汇集了全世界最优秀智力、也包含了相当多善意的阶层，为什么竟然无法推翻一群既迟钝又愚蠢的暴君。原因似乎有两方面。第一，统治者手中已经掌握一套庞大而高度机械化的压迫系统。任何人只要做出一点令政权不快的事情，立刻就会自动被清除出行动行列。总会有某个同事去举报他，剩下的事由警察完成。必须记住，此时整个人口都被神经质的嫉妒与恐惧折磨。让另一个人遭受痛苦，本身就能带来一种疯狂的满足。告密者当然得到丰厚奖励，但真正驱动他们的，是迫害别人的快乐。第二，宣传的机械化已经发展到前所未有的程度。心理学这个最年轻的科学，此时已经对人类身上原始与病态的部分了解得极为深入，却仍完全没有真正理解人格中那些最具人性的高度。政府心理学家设计出一套精巧的暗示技术：不断使用象征刺激，反复勾起被压抑的动机。从婴儿期开始施加这种方法，就可以确保一个神经质人口所有未被自觉认识的欲望——他们不愿承认的恐惧、仇恨、精力、残酷、淫欲、自私与群体激情——无论被刺激还是被安抚，都依赖现存社会秩序；而在意识层面，这些冲动最终都会转化成对寡头统治充满嫉妒和报复性的忠诚。本来应当运用自己的技能净化并照亮人类心灵的一群科学家，就这样帮助整个世界陷入更深的黑暗与痛苦。流行的教育原则又进一步增强了宣传力量。那种能够无畏、无偏见地批判事物的自由智力，被嘲笑、谴责并小心压制；只有被束缚的智力——也就是在既成文化的话语宇宙内部活动的智力——得到鼓励。但每个学生都会被明确告知，智力与其说是值得为自身而珍视的东西，不如说是一种不得不忍受的必要之恶。真正内在善好的，是正统，是同传统保持一致。为了加强人们对正统的激情，学校被规定班级人数越多越好，而主要教学方法则是组织集体齐声吟诵传统真理。如果光明意志没有衰弱到这种程度，这种做法很可能反而会使一部分学生产生反感，从而转向自由智力；可在人类种族这个晚期阶段，这种反叛已经极少发生。</p>
<p>政府对臣民的控制，又因为一项新发明而大大加强。若这项发明出现在一个更健康的社会里，它原本很可能成为提高社会福祉的巨大恩惠。它来自生理学与电气工程的进步。人类大脑的机制此时已经被相当充分地理解；通过一种极其高明的外科技术，可以在大脑皮层与颅骨之间植入由无数微小光电细胞组成的网格，从而极精确地记录皮层神经纤维不断变化的活动模式。无线电技术的发展又使这种记录能够轻易传输到极远处，并自动解码，让遥远政府办公室中的观察员准确“读出”被观察者的思想与冲动。实现这些发明所投入的巨大知识与技术，本来完全可能给人类带来无法估量的利益；可是，由于技术人员的背叛与统治者的权力欲，它们却被组合成一件恶魔般的暴政工具。</p>
<p>随后，政府通过法律：凡被怀疑藏有危险思想的人，都必须让自己的大脑接受持续观察。这意味着接受手术，把光电网植入颅骨下方，并用直接拧进颅骨的螺钉把必需的微型蓄电装置固定在头顶。如果有人试图破坏设备，或者让蓄电装置的电量下降到某个限度以下，这个不幸的人就会自动遭受最剧烈的疼痛；若疼痛持续超过大约一小时，最终会造成永久性精神错乱。除了这种思想传输装置，乳突骨中还会植入一个微型无线电话接收器。于是，臣民不但在生命中的每一刻都可能被某个远处官员检查思想和感情，政府还可以从早到晚向他施加命令、威胁，或者循环播放留声机式宣传。</p>
<p>最初，这种技术只用于受到怀疑的人；但慢慢地，除寡头本人及其最受宠的仆从之外，它扩展到了社会所有阶层。各个主要中心都设立了巨大的办公室，大批检查员日夜工作，对全世界二十亿人的心智进行抽样读取。每一个普通男人、女人和青少年都知道，自己随时可能正在接受检查。任何时刻，都可能突然有一个声音打断他的思考，对他的思想进行某种宣传式评论，或者发出粗暴警告，甚至直接宣布处罚。在他即将入睡时，耳中又可能突然灌入某种音乐和咒语，专门用来把他的心智塑造成政府认可的情绪状态。那些从小就在这种处理下长大的人，会愉快地接受它。年纪最小的人有时甚至迫不及待想得到这种装置，因为他们愚蠢地把它视为“已经长大”的证明。在官方持续审视之下，青少年的心灵变得几乎完全“正确”。哪怕最温和的危险思想，对他们来说也根本不可想象。至于那些成年以后才接受这种处理的人，则长期承受精神痛苦，其中许多人最终自杀。</p>
<p>控制这套庞大监视体系的人，政策其实非常简单：确保所有人的心智都保持正统。随着时间推移，负责审讯与检查的人本身，也只从已经由这套体系塑造出来的人中挑选。这些人的思想正确得惊人，以至于自己的全部心智活动公开暴露，也不会因此感到任何痛苦。</p>
<p>这套体系最奇怪的一点在于：控制它的人自己也成了它的奴隶。他们并没有运用权力解放自己，反而用它来支撑统治阶层。在中华世界帝国的早期阶段，这个阶层——更准确说，是后来演变成世袭等级之前那个还非世袭的统治阶层——依靠比别人更高明的狡猾和决断力维持地位。但到了后期，当狡猾与决断已经被愚蠢和自我放纵取代时，统治阶层的地位反而靠既成社会制度那种机械化、自行运转的功能自动维持。统治者拥有巨大特权和广泛的任意权力。工人替他们堆积奢侈品。随着统治者反复无常的品味改变，时尚也不断变化；整批劳动人口可能突然被驱使到劳累致死，或者直接扔进冷藏仓库。统治者说“做这个”或“做那个”，全世界便照办。然而，他们的权力完全建立在一个事实之上：技术人员已经被催眠般束缚在为他们服务的状态中。造成这种催眠的并不是统治者自己还有多么狡猾、坚定，而是传统文化已经积累出的巨大惯性。于是统治阶层一点一点变成一种奇特存在：既完全无能，又绝对安全。正如那些蓄奴蚂蚁完全依赖忠顺奴蚁的照料；奴蚁掌握一切技能，却没有足够智慧想到反叛。</p>
<p>社会控制系统最终达到近乎完美，是由于发明才能又取得一次胜利。经过大量艰苦实验，人们终于设计出一种方法，可以利用无线电刺激或压制个人的冲动与欲望。这样一来，远方政府办公室里的官员便能强迫某个人产生一种不可抵抗的渴望，去执行预先指定的行动。那个人就像处于催眠状态，却又完全清楚自己正在做多么骇人的事：他可能发现自己被迫背叛朋友、杀死妻子、折磨自己的孩子或自己，工作直到死亡，或者去打一场明知必败的战斗。</p>
<p>渐渐地，全世界所有被统治人口都装上了这种意志控制设备。政府此时实际上已经近乎全能。</p>
<p>而这项新发明最奇怪的地方再次出现了：操纵它的人，自己同样处于它的控制之下。因为操作员本人也装着控制设备。每个部门的操作员受上级控制，上级又受更高一级控制；再往上，则由当地最高委员会控制，而最高委员会由各部门负责人组成。地方最高委员会又受到省级或州级委员会控制；州级委员会则接受世界帝国委员会控制。至于这个最高机构的成员，他们的控制过程已经完全自动化。自动机器会确保：任何刚刚萌生、同正统欲望体系不一致的欲望，都立刻自动被抹除；与此同时，某些构成正统基础的欲望则会被自动维持。</p>
<p>必须指出，这套精巧体系并不是统治者本人设计出来的，而是技术专家——生理学家、心理学家和电气工程师——创造的。他们之所以这样做，一部分出于盲目的职业热情；另一部分，则因为他们自己偶尔也会受到某些非正统冲动困扰，于是觉得有必要建立这种体系，来加固自身的正统性。</p>
<p>至于统治者本人，这些神圣的人、这些神圣的动物，则不接受控制。他们可以按照自己的本性自由思考与行动，而这一本性此时已经退化成愚蠢、自私和恶意混成的一团。他们的愚蠢是野兽式的愚蠢。他们虽然自由，却毫无真正能力。这些人来自已经退化的血统，又从小被训练去过一种荒唐奢侈、毁灭性的自我放纵生活。只要他们的行为符合正统模式，就会受到保护与崇拜；若其中有人表现出一点独立个性，就会立刻被剥夺阶级身份，并接受手术，装上无线电控制设备。不过这种情况极其罕见。几乎所有统治者都满足于享用全国最肥美的资源，在大众奉承中舒适生活。他们每天忙于无数仪式与庆典，因为人们相信没有这些活动国家就会崩溃，而统治阶层的代表人物又总在其中扮演中心角色。少数模模糊糊感觉到自己生活空虚的人，会试图在体育或航空领域追求名声；但随着一代代人的能力继续退化，他们不得不寻找要求更低的自我展示方式。其中最主要的一种就是施加酷刑。被统治人口虽然从小被训练去相信残酷具有神秘美德，也能够在一定程度上欣赏对陌生人施加酷刑的场面，却仍时常重新产生反胃感，甚至同情心。统治者却不会。由于自己生命的无用与卑劣，他们在无意识中早已中毒，心中充满对大众、技术人员、同阶级者乃至自己的仇恨。因此，他们根本不需要任何无线电控制，也能平静地实施最令人作呕的酷刑，甚至从中获得毫不虚假的享受。如果某个统治者必须亲手把祭品受害者的眼睛、内脏或生殖器撕出来，他也不会有丝毫犹豫。对那些既着迷又恶心的旁观者来说，这种冷酷反而显得是一种贵族美德。普通人后来普遍接受意志无线电控制时，甚至常常欢迎这种人工装置加强自己的无情。相反，若一个犯错的统治阶层成员被剥夺阶级身份并送去受刑，他表现出来的坚忍通常反而低于平均水平。大众会对他的狼狈兴奋嚎叫，却从来没有想到：这些贵族即使在被剥夺身份以前，其实也并不比自己高贵。因为官方解释是，剥夺阶级身份的仪式本身已经让罪犯失去了他天生拥有的美德。</p>
<p>这个时期的世界文化之所以崇拜残酷，其中一个原因，是人们普遍尊崇“无意识”。早期心理学中“意识动机”与“无意识动机”的区分，原本曾发挥极其有益的作用；如今却被推向荒谬。人们声称，无意识就是神圣意志在我们体内运作。因此，一切来自无意识的行动源头都被视为神圣。可对于一个长期经过不健康塑造、其所谓“无意识”已经变成一团变态欲望的种族而言，这也就等于把变态本身神化。</p>
<p>这种堕落文化还有另一个奇怪方面：在把“无意识”神化的同时，人们也把“理性”神化——尽管他们又极其严厉地谴责自由运用智力。但这种神圣理性与普通人类推理完全不是一回事。它被说成宇宙隐藏的理性秩序，永远无法被人类触及。官方宣称，从神的视角看，一切都有其理由；一切都必然由神圣理性推出。在人类领域，自由运用智力试图探究这种神圣理性，本身就是亵渎。人类自身那一点“神圣理性火花”真正该发挥作用的地方，不是自由思考，而是虔诚、毫无疑问地研读受启经典中的形而上学论证。</p>
<p>“理性崇拜”的一个分支，是以一种荒诞方式使用数学。但同样，人们崇拜的并不是数学智力的自由发挥；事实上，这种自由发挥同样遭到强烈谴责。技术人员当然仍会为了实际目的进行大量复杂而有效的数学运算，可这些全都是早已确立、由更聪明的一代传下来的固定方法。任何数学创新都被视为邪恶。</p>
<p>不仅如此，数学符号本身也逐渐获得神秘力量。随着智力继续退化，那些世代相传的运算仍同时用于工业研究和宗教仪式，但执行者对它们的理解越来越浅。在最后阶段，数学理解已经彻底消失。这些运算仍被称为“理性的”，但人们声称，它们的理性只有神圣理性才能看见。证明就在于，整个物理自然都“服从”数学定律。可是，人类理性绝无可能洞察高等数学过程背后的隐秘必然性；任何试图做到这一点的行为，都属于亵渎。</p>
</section>

<h2>iii. DIABOLIC WORLD EMPIRE</h2>
<p>THUS ENDED THE Second Russian Empire, the evil offspring of man’s first great though ill-starred attempt to organize society for the well-being of the many rather than for the power of the few. Some of the former Russian provinces hastily made peace, others declared their independence of both empires, only to be speedily crushed. America alone resisted for two years, but was finally overcome and treated to a very special punishment for its contumacy. The whole child population was transported to various parts of the world as slaves.</p>
<p>With the fall of America the human race had succeeded for the first time in establishing the political unity of the whole planet. The imperial Chinese government now assumed the title ‘The Celestial Government of the World’, and ordered celebrations in every town and every household of the planet. Everywhere desperate efforts were made to produce tolerable specimens of the ancient Chinese dragon flag, which had been revived by the second empire and was henceforth to be the dreaded emblem of the world-government. Everywhere, even on the blood-stained Russian plains, this emblem, or some crude approximation to it, was now anxiously flaunted. It was affirmed that at last the green Chinese dragon had devoured the red orb that had for so long hung tantalizingly before him in the golden sky. The red orb was no longer interpreted as the sun of Japan but as the red world of Russian imperialism. It was added in a whisper that, with luck the dragon might soon die of indigestion.</p>
<p>World-unity had been attained! But what a unity! Nowhere throughout the world was there any considerable group who were at peace with the world, save the governing class and its jackals. Everywhere the peasants were enslaved to the universal imperial landlord. Everywhere they toiled to produce the world’s food. Everywhere they starved and were harshly regimented. Miners and factory hands were in the same condition. The world-government, instead of organizing a great and universal movement of social reconstruction, thereby keeping the workers and the soldiers in employment, dismissed half its armies and kept the rest in idleness. The workers it treated with utter contempt, confident in its power to coerce them. The great class of technicians who had been persuaded to support the war in the hope that under world-unity they would be given the chance to build universal prosperity, found themselves used either for strengthening the oligarchy or for producing its luxuries; or else dismissed and maintained by the state in a sort of half-life of penury and despond.</p>
<p>Although individualistic capitalism had long since vanished, the universal decadent state-capitalism was in many ways subject to the same disorders. Though the power for social planning was in the hands of the world-government, the will was lacking. The rulers were concerned only to maintain their position. Vast economic powers, at first the perquisites of the great ruling Chinese families, were now farmed out to irresponsible state-servants, who turned themselves into dictators of the industries under their control. And since there was little co-ordination of their actions, and, anyhow, they were mainly concerned to feather their own nests, chaos followed. Unemployment increased, and brought with it its attendant evils. Desperate populations became difficult to handle. Punitive massacres were very frequent.</p>
<p>At last a new invention, one of the very few which the declining species managed to achieve, brought temporary aid. A biochemist produced a method of putting human beings into a state of suspended animation from which, he said, they could be easily wakened, ‘fresh and young’, after a sleep of many years. The world-government, believing that unemployment was a passing phase, and that later on there would be a great need of labour, set about building in every country a system of cold-storage warehouses where unwanted human beings could be deposited until the times changed. The unemployed and their families were forcibly stored in these warehouses. The struggling creatures were chained down, lying shoulder to shoulder on tiers of shelves inside huge tanks, which were then filled first with a succession of gases and finally with a preserving liquid. Millions of men, women, and children in almost every country were thus stored for future use. Though the lives of the workers were almost intolerably arid and distressful, they did all in their power to avoid being sent to the cold-storage houses. The will for the light expressed itself in them as a blind will for active life, however abject. But a few welcomed this opportunity of escape, without irrevocable extinction; believing that in their next phase of active life they would have better opportunities of expressing themselves. In most of these, the acquiescence in suspended animation was at bottom an expression of the will for darkness, though rationalized to satisfy the still smouldering will for light. For the individual in whom the will for the light is strong and clear finds his heart inextricably bound up with the struggle of the forces of light in his native place and time. Much as he may long for the opportunity of fuller self- expression in a happier world, he knows that for him self-expression is impossible save in the world in which his mind is rooted. The individual in whom the will for the light is weak soon persuades himself that his opportunity lies elsewhere. And so, as the spirit of the race was progressively undermined through ever deteriorating physical and psychological conditions, acquiescence in ‘the deep sleep’ became more and more widespread.</p>
<p>One of the main factors in the waning of the will for the light in this period was the attitude of the intellectuals. The academics, musicians, painters, cinema-artists, and, above all, the writers flagrantly betrayed their trust. In all these groups there were persons of four types. Many were paid servants of the government, engaged on propaganda through work which was ostensibly independent. These were concerned chiefly to put a good complexion on the regime, and to praise the fundamental principles of the synthetic faith, in particular the virtues of acquiescence and obedience, and the ecstasy of cruelty. Still more numerous were the independent but futile intellectual ostriches who shut their eyes to the horror of their time and won adulation and power by spinning fantasies of self- aggrandizement and sexual delight, distracting men’s attention from contemporary evils with seductive romances of other ages and other worlds, or with exalted and meaningless jargon about a life after death. There were also large numbers of progressive intellectuals. These saw clearly enough that contemporary society was mortally sick, and in a dream-like, unearnest way they expounded their tenuous Utopias, in which there was often much common sense and even wisdom; but they preached without that fury of conviction which alone can rouse men to desperate action. And they themselves lived comfortably upon the existing system, in their flats and suburban houses. Vaguely they knew that they ought to give up all for the revolution; but being what they were, they could not. The fourth type were the very few sincere and impotent rebels, who flung away their lives in vain and crazy attempts to be great prophets.</p>
<p>Crucial to the fate of the human race at this time was the attitude of the class of technicians, the host of highly trained engineers, electricians, aeronautical experts, agricultural experts, and scientific workers in industry. These, if they could have formed a clear idea of the plight of the race, might have saved it. But they were experts who had been carefully trained in the tradition that the expert should not meddle in politics. In times of great stress, of course, they did meddle; but, because they had consistently held themselves aloof, their pronouncements were childish, and their attempts at political action disastrous. A few had, indeed, taken the trouble to study society, and had come to understand its present ills. These fought constantly to enlighten their fellows and unite them in a great effort to control the course of events. Undoubtedly, if the will for the light had been strong in this great class, which controlled throughout theworld all the innumerable levers and switches and press- buttons of the material life of society, it could have overthrown the world-oligarchy in a few days, and set about organizing a sane order. But the appeal to the technicians met with a half-hearted response. Most of them shrugged their shoulders and went on with their work. A few took timid action and were promptly seized and put to torture by the rulers. The movement failed.</p>
<p>It seemed to me very strange that a class which included nearly all the best intelligence of the world and very much of the world’s good will should be incapable of ousting a set of tyrants who were both insensitive and stupid. The explanation, seemingly, was twofold. First, the rulers found themselves in possession of a vast and highly mechanized system of oppression. If anyone did anything obnoxious to the régime, immediately and automatically he was put out of action. Some colleague would certainly inform against him, and the police would do the rest. For the whole population, it must be remembered, was now tormented by neurotic jealousy and fear. The infliction of pain on a fellow mortal could afford a crazy satisfaction. Informers were, of course, well rewarded, but it was the joy of persecution that inspired them. Secondly, the mechanization of propaganda had been developed to an extent hitherto unknown. Psychology, the youngest of the sciences, had by now attained a thorough knowledge of the primitive and the morbid in man without reaching to any real understanding of the distinctively human reaches of human personality. Government psychologists had worked out a subtle technique of suggestion by reiterated symbolic appeals to suppressed motives. This method, applied from infancy onwards, had ensured that all the unwitting cravings of a neurotic population, all their unacknowledged fear, hate, energy, cruelty, lechery, selfishness, and mob-passion, should depend both for stimulation and assuagement on the existing social order, and should issue consciously in a jealous and vengeful loyalty to the oligarchy. Thus did a group of scientists who should have used their skill for the purgation and elucidation of men’s minds help to deepen the general darkness and misery. The power of propaganda was greatly increased by the prevailing educational principles. The free intelligence, which criticizes fearlessly and without prejudice, was ridiculed, condemned, and carefully suppressed. Bound intelligence, acting within the universe of discourse of the established culture, was encouraged; but it was mane clear to every pupil that intelligence was rather a necessary evil than a thing to prize for its own sake. What was intrinsically good was orthodoxy, unison with the tradition. To strengthen the passion for orthodoxy it was ordained that school classes should be as large as possible, and that the main method of teaching should be by organizing mass chanting of the traditional truths. Had the will for the light been less feeble, this procedure might well have induced in some pupils a revulsion in favour of free intelligence; but in this latter day of the human race, such rebellion was very rare.</p>
<p>The government’s control over its subjects was greatly increased by a new invention which would have been a eaurse of increased social well- being had it occurred in a more wholesome society. This was the product of advances in physiology and electrical engineering. The mechanism of the human brain was by now fairly well known; and by means of a vast mesh of minute photoelectric cells, inserted by a brilliant surgical technique between the cerebral cortex and the skull, it was possible to record very accurately the ever-changing pattern of activity in the cortical nerve-fibres. Advances in the technique of radio made it easy to transmit this record over great distances, and to decode it automatically in such a way that the thoughts and impulses of the observed person could be accurately ‘read’ by observers in far-away government offices. The immense knowledge and skill which went to these inventions might have caused untold benefits to mankind; but through the treason of the technologists and the power-lust of the rulers they were combined to form a diabolical instrument of tyranny.</p>
<p>A law was passed by which everyone suspected of harbouring dangerous thoughts was condemned to have his brain made available for constant observation. This involved an operation for the insertion of the photoelectric mesh under his skull and the attachment of the necessary miniature accumulators to his crown by screws driven into the skull itself. If any attempt was made to tamper with the instrument, or if the accumulator was allowed to run down beyond a certain point, the unfortunate individual was automatically subjected to the most excruciating pain, which, if prolonged for more than an hour so so, culminated in permanent insanity. In addition to this transmission- instrument there was a minute radio telephone receiver driven into the mastoid bone. Thus not only were the subject’s thoughts and feelings open to inspection at every moment of his life by some remote official but also instructions, threats, or repetitive gramophone propaganda could be inflicted on him morning, noon, and night.</p>
<p>At first this technique was applied only to those under suspicion, but little by little it was extended to all classes of society, save the oligarchs themselves and their most favoured servants. Immense offices were set up in all the main centres, where hosts of inspectors were constantly at work taking sample readings of the world’s two thousand million minds. Every ordinary man, woman, and adolescent knew that at any moment he might be under inspection. At any moment a voice might interrupt his thoughts with some propaganda commentary on them, or with a rough warning or the imposition of a penalty. While he was going to sleep he might be invaded by music and incantations calculated to mould his mind into the temper approved by the government. Those who were brought up from childhood to be accustomed to this treatment accepted it cheerfully. The very young were sometimes even impatient to receive what they foolishly regarded as this certificate of maturity. Under the constant influence of official scrutiny the minds of adolescents became almost perfectly correct. Dangerous thoughts, even of the mildest type, were for them unthinkable. Those who received the treatment as grown men or women suffered prolonged mental agony, and many committed suicide.</p>
<p>The policy of those who controlled this vast system of espionage was simply to ensure that all minds should be orthodox. As time went on, the inquisitors themselves came to be chosen solely from the ranks of those who were products of the system itself. So amazingly correct were these minds that they suffered nothing from the publicity of all their mental processes.</p>
<p>The strangest aspect of the system was this. Those who controlled it were themselves enslaved to it; they used their power not to emancipate themselves but to support the ruling caste. In the earlier phase of the Chinese world-empire the caste, or rather the non- hereditary class from which the caste later developed, had maintained its position by superior cunning and resolution; but in its later phase, when cunning and resolution had given place to stupidity and self-indulgence, the position of the ruling caste was maintained automatically by the mechanical functioning of the established social system. The rulers had immense privileges and great arbitrary powers. For them the workers piled up luxuries. In accordance with the vagaries of their fickle taste, fashions changed, whole working populations were suddenly worked to death or flung aside into the cold-storage warehouses. When the rulers said ‘do this’ or ‘do that’, the world obeyed. But their power lay wholly in the fact that the technicians were hypnotized in their service, hypnotized, not through the cunning and resolution of the rulers themselves, but through the vast momentum of traditional culture. Thus little by little the ruling caste became at once helpless and absolutely secure. In the same manner the slave-owning ants depend wholly on the ministrations of devoted slaves who have all the skill but not the wit to rebel.</p>
<p>The perfection of the system of social control was reached by means of a further triumph of inventive genius. After much laborious experiment a method was devised by which the impulses and desires of the individual could be either stimulated or suppressed by radio. Thus it was possible for the officials in a distant government office to force upon a man an irresistible craving to carry out a prescribed course of action. Like one under hypnotic influence, but with full consciousness of the enormity of his action, he might find himself compelled to betray his friend, to murder his wife, to torture his child or himself, to work himself to death, to fight against impossible odds.</p>
<p>Little by little the whole subject population of the world was fitted with the instruments of volitional control. The government was now practically omnipotent.</p>
<p>Once more, the strangest aspect of the new invention was that those who controlled it were themselves under its control. For the operators themselves were fitted with the instruments. Operators in each department were controlled by their superiors, and these by their superiors. These again were controlled by the supreme council of the locality, which was composed of all heads of departments. The supreme council of the locality was in turn controlled by the council of the province or state; and the state councils by the World Imperial Council. Members of this body were automatically controlled. Automatic machinery ensured that any incipient desire inconsistent with the orthodox system of desires should automatically be obliterated, while certain desires fundamental to orthodoxy were automatically maintained.</p>
<p>This ingenious system, it must be noted, had not been devised by the rulers themselves but by the technologists, by physiologists, psychologists, and electrical engineers. They had done it partly out of blind professional enthusiasm, partly because they felt the need of such a system to fortify their orthodoxy against the unorthodox impulses which occasionally distressed them.</p>
<p>As for the rulers themselves, these sacred beings, these sacred animals, were not controlled. They were free to think and act according to their nature, which by now had degenerated into a mess of stupidity, selfishness, and malice. Their stupidity was the stupidity of beasts. Though they were free, they were powerless. Of degenerate stock, they were conditioned by upbringing to a life of fantastic luxury and desolating self-indulgence. So long as they behaved according to the orthodox pattern, they were preserved and reverenced. If any showed some sign of individuality he was at once de-classed and operated upon for radio control. But this was very rare. Nearly all were content to live at ease on the fat of the land and the adulation of the masses. They were kept busy with the innumerable ceremonies and pageants without which, it was thought, the state would collapse, and in which the representative members of the ruling caste always played the central part. Those who obscurely felt the barrenness of their lives sought notoriety in the fields of sport or aeronautics. But, as the generations passed and their capacity deteriorated, they were forced to seek less exacting forms of self-display. Of these, one of the chief was the infliction of torture. The subject population, though conditioned to believe in the mystical virtue of cruelty, and though capable up to a point of relishing the spectacle of torture inflicted on strangers, were prone to lapse into squeamishness or even compassion. Not so the rulers. Unconsciously poisoned by their own futility and baseness, they were obsessed by hatred of the masses, the technicians, their own peers, and themselves. Without any radio control, therefore, they could inflict the most disgusting tortures with equanimity, and even unfeigned relish. When one of them had to perform the office of tearing out the eyes or bowels or genitals of the sacrificial victim, he did so without a qualm. To the fascinated and nauseated spectators this callousness appeared as aristocratic virtue. When humble people came to be subject to radio control of volition they often welcomed the artificial reinforcement to their ruthlessness. On the other hand when an erring member of the ruling caste had to be de-classed and put to torture, he invariably showed less than the average fortitude. It never occurred to the public, while they howled with glee at his discomfiture, that the aristocrats, even before de-classing, were after all no better than themselves; for the ceremony of de-classing was supposed to have deprived the culprit of his native virtue.</p>
<p>One of the causes of this admiration of cruelty in the world-culture of this period was the widespread respect for ‘the unconscious’. The distinction between the conscious and unconscious motives, which had played such a beneficial part in an earlier psychology, had by now led to absurdities. The unconscious was now said to be the divine will working in us. The unconscious sources of action were therefore sacred. In a race in which, through unwholesome conditioning, the ‘unconscious’ was a tissue of perverted cravings, this meant that the perverse was deified.</p>
<p>Another curious aspect of this degenerate culture was that, along with ‘the unconscious’, reason was deified; and this in spite of the bitter condemnation of the exercise of free intelligence. But sacred reason was nothing whatever like ordinary human reasoning. It was the occult rationality of the universe, forever inaccessible to man. Everything, it was said, had in the divine view its reason. Everything followed necessarily from the divine reason. In the human sphere free intelligence was an impious attempt to probe the divine reason. The true scope for man’s own divine spark of reason was not in the free exercise of intelligence but in the pious and unquestioning study of the metaphysical arguments of the inspired scriptures.</p>
<p>One branch of the cult of reason was a fantastic use of mathematics. But again, what was admired was not the free exercise of mathematical intelligence. This, indeed, was heartily condemned. A number of complex and valid mathematical operations were, of course, performed by the technicians for practical purposes; but they were all well- established operations, handed down from a more intelligent generation. Mathematical innovation was deemed wicked.</p>
<p>Further, the actual symbols of mathematics were gradually acquiring mystical virtue. As intelligence deteriorated, the time-honoured operations continued to be used both in industrial research and in religious ritual, but they were performed with ever-dwindling insight. In the final phase mathematical understanding had vanished altogether. The operations were still called rational, but their rationality was said to’ be patent only to the divine reason. This was proved by the fact that the whole of physical nature ‘obeyed’ mathematical laws. Human reason, however, could not possibly detect the occult necessity of the higher mathematical processes. Any attempt to do so was sacrilegious.</p>
