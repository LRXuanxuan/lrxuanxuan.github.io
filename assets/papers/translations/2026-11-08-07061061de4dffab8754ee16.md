**Original:**

Abstract—This paper investigates channel estimation for affine
frequency division multiplexing (AFDM) systems over channels
with fractional delay and fractional Doppler (FDFD) shifts. An
exact FDFD channel model is first established, and the corre-
sponding input–output relationship is also derived. To address
the FDFD-induced delay–Doppler coupling, a first-order linear
approximation grid-refinement sparse Bayesian learning (FLA-
GR-SBL) method is developed. To further remedy the conver-
gence and stability issues caused by the limited accuracy of the
first-order approximation, we propose a two-dimensional grid-
approximation SBL (2D-GA-SBL) algorithm, which iteratively
refines the delay–Doppler grid and thereby characterizes the
channel more accurately. Simulation results demonstrate that
the proposed algorithms significantly outperform existing on-grid
and off-grid SBL benchmarks in terms of both normalized mean
square error (NMSE) and bit error rate (BER).

**中文:**

## 摘要

本文研究在存在分数时延与分数多普勒（FDFD）频移的信道下，仿射频分复用（AFDM）系统的信道估计问题。首先建立一个精确的 FDFD 信道模型，并推导相应的输入–输出关系。为应对 FDFD 引起的时延–多普勒耦合，提出一种一阶线性近似网格精化稀疏贝叶斯学习（FLA-GR-SBL）方法。为进一步缓解由一阶近似精度有限所导致的收敛性与稳定性问题，提出一种二维网格近似 SBL（2D-GA-SBL）算法，该算法迭代地精化时延–多普勒网格，从而更准确地刻画信道。仿真结果表明，所提算法在归一化均方误差（NMSE）与误比特率（BER）两方面均显著优于现有的在网格与离网格 SBL 基准方法。

A
FFINE frequency division multiplexing (AFDM) has
recently emerged as a promising waveform for next-
generation high-mobility scenarios, such as vehicular and low-
Earth-orbit (LEO) satellite networks [1]–[3], owing to its
strong robustness against Doppler spread and its enhanced di-
versity gain over doubly dispersive channels [4], [5]. Accurate
channel estimation is fundamental to realizing the promised
error-rate performance of AFDM, especially in highly dynamic
environments. Existing works on AFDM channel estimation
generally assume integer normalized delay and Doppler shifts
for each path [6]–[9]. In practice, however, both parameters
are typically fractional. Such fractional delay and fractional
Doppler (FDFD) components spread the channel energy across
the discrete affine Fourier transform (DAFT) domain, destroy-
ing the ideal channel sparsity and severely degrading most ex-
isting estimators, including linear and diagonal-reconstruction-
based schemes [7], [8].

## V. 结论

---

<span id="S006-05"></span>
**Source:** p.6 S006-05

**Original:**

This paper investigated channel estimation for AFDM sys-
tems over doubly dispersive channels with fractional delay and
fractional Doppler shifts. We first formulated the correspond-
ing DAFT-domain input–output relationship and characterized
the resulting coupled delay–Doppler spreading. Based on this
model, the FLA-GR-SBL algorithm was developed to jointly
estimate the channel gains and the off-grid delay and Doppler
components using a first-order approximation. To reduce the
modeling error associated with fixed expansion points, the 2D-
GA-SBL algorithm was further developed to iteratively relo-
cate the grid points in both the delay and Doppler dimensions.
Simulation results under the considered channel and system
settings showed that the proposed algorithms achieve lower
NMSE and BER than the conventional on-grid and off-grid
SBL benchmarks, with 2D-GA-SBL providing more stable
performance across the tested grid resolutions. Future work
will investigate robust guard-interval design, colored residual-
interference modeling, unknown path-number estimation, and
low-complexity implementations for more practical vehicular
channel models.

**中文:**

本文研究了存在分数时延与分数多普勒频移的双色散信道下 AFDM 系统的信道估计。首先建立了相应的 DAFT 域输入–输出关系，并刻画了由此产生的耦合时延–多普勒扩展。基于该模型，提出了 FLA-GR-SBL 算法，利用一阶近似联合估计信道增益以及离网格的时延与多普勒分量。为减小固定展开点带来的建模误差，进一步提出 2D-GA-SBL 算法，在时延与多普勒两个维度上迭代重定位网格点。在所考虑的信道与系统设置下的仿真结果表明，所提算法相比传统的在网格与离网格 SBL 基准取得了更低的 NMSE 与 BER，且 2D-GA-SBL 在所测试的网格分辨率下性能更稳定。未来的工作将研究鲁棒的保护间隔设计、有色残余干扰建模、未知路径数估计，以及面向更实际的车载信道模型的低复杂度实现。
