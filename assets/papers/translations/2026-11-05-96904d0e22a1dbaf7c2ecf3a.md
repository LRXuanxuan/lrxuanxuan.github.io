**Original:**

Radio maps provide essential wireless propagation knowledge for network optimization and au-
tonomous platforms. Acquiring dense measurements, however, is prohibitively costly, requiring ac-
tive sampling to prioritize informative measurements. Existing active sampling methods typically
rely on variances from prescribed spatial models or learned pointwise uncertainty, which may not
fully capture the spatial correlation of different measurements. This paper proposes GraphBARC, a
graph-structured active radio map construction framework that integrates learned spatial modeling
with analytical Bayesian inference. A graph network learns correlated node and edge factors using
only acquired measurements, inducing a joint full-map belief that supports analytical observation
updates without retraining. Rather than immediate one-step acquisition, it adopts a nonmyopic
sensing strategy that evaluates joint map-wide Bayes risk reduction over feasible multi-step routes,
accounting for information redundancy among planned measurements. We reuse candidate responses
and derive residual-based bounds directly on route-value differences, retaining shared response-error
structure to avoid uniformly high-accuracy evaluation. An error-aware continuation bound screens
unpromising prefixes, after which only responses needed for unresolved comparisons are refined. We
further establish a computable decision-gap bound and show that certified exclusions remain valid dur-
ing refinement. Experiments show that GraphBARC reduces mean root-mean-square error (RMSE)
by over 16% relative to the evaluated baselines and core planning time by 56% compared with the
fixed-accuracy, unpruned implementation.

**中文:**

无线电地图为网络优化和自主平台提供重要的无线传播知识。然而，获取密集测量的成本高得难以承受，因此需要通过主动采样优先获取信息量大的测量。现有主动采样方法通常依赖预先指定空间模型的方差或学习得到的逐点不确定性，可能无法充分刻画不同测量之间的空间相关性。本文提出 GraphBARC，这是一种图结构的主动无线电地图构建框架，将学习式空间建模与解析贝叶斯推断相结合。图网络仅利用已采集的测量学习相关的节点因子和边因子，从而诱导出全地图联合信念，并支持无需重新训练的解析观测更新。该方法不采用即时的一步式采集，而是采用非短视感知策略，评估可行多步路径带来的联合全图贝叶斯风险降低，并考虑计划测量之间的信息冗余。我们在不同路径间复用候选响应，并直接针对路径价值差异推导基于残差的界限，保留共享的响应误差结构，从而避免一律进行高精度评估。随后，误差感知的延续界限筛除无望的路径前缀，只对未解决比较所需的响应进行细化。我们进一步建立可计算的决策间隙界限，并证明在细化过程中，已认证的排除仍然有效。实验表明，相较于所评估的基线，GraphBARC 将平均均方根误差（RMSE）降低了 16% 以上；与固定精度、未剪枝的实现相比，其核心规划时间减少了 56%。

In summary, existing approaches face two key limitations. First, model-based methods support analytical
updates but rely on prescribed covariance structures, whereas data-driven methods learn richer spatial struc-
tures, yet often provide only deterministic predictions or marginal uncertainty. Second, effective sensing
requires evaluating the joint map-wide benefit of multiple measurements under mobility constraints, rather
than solely one-step scores, as high local uncertainty does not necessarily imply a large reduction in overall
uncertainty. Some studies (Lu et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026) have extended beyond one-step acquisition,
but they require selecting a destination before planning the route, which may overlook the joint value of
the endpoint and intermediate measurements. Joint route optimization also entails combinatorial search and
repeated posterior computations, whose numerical errors may still affect route ranking.

总的来说，现有方法面临两项关键局限。第一，基于模型的方法支持解析更新，但依赖预设协方差结构；数据驱动方法能够学习更丰富的空间结构，却往往只提供确定性预测或边缘不确定性。第二，有效感知需要在移动约束下评估多次测量对整幅地图的联合效益，而不应仅依据单步分数，因为局部不确定性高并不必然意味着整体不确定性会大幅降低。一些研究（Lu et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026）已将采集扩展到单步以外，但需要先选定目的地再规划路径，因此可能忽略终点和中间测量的联合价值。联合路径优化还涉及组合搜索和重复后验计算，其中的数值误差仍可能影响路径排序。

To address these limitations, we develop GraphBARC, a graph-induced Bayesian framework that learns a
full-map belief with explicit cross-location dependencies using nonmyopic active sensing. Newly acquired
measurements are assimilated analytically, with their information propagated across correlated locations
through the learned graph dependencies. Rather than relying on one-step gains, GraphBARC evaluates joint
map-wide Bayes risk reduction over feasible multi-step routes. To reduce computational cost, shared posterior
responses are reused across routes, while residual-certified bounds on route-value differences enable safe prefix
pruning and selective numerical refinement of unresolved comparisons. New observations immediately update

5 Network Implementation and Training

Realizing the framework in Sections III and IV requires the learned representation, training objective, and
online execution to remain consistent. The network must generate valid Gaussian factors from sparse obser-

5.3 Implementation

GraphBARC represents the current belief by its natural parameters and computes only the inverse actions
required for reconstruction, training, and planning. It computes the meanµt and candidate responsesC t,j
throughΛ tµt =η t andΛ tCt,j =E T
j, respectively. Both queries use matrix-free preconditioned conjugate
gradient (PCG) iterations with a symmetric positive-definite preconditioner. They share the same numerical
solver, differing only in their right-hand sides.

Within each planning call, the belief, task weights, noise model, and candidate domain remain fixed. Shared
responses and Gram blocks support the certified prefix screening and selective numerical refinement. The
planner returns a feasible route and its attained decision gapgt, distinguishing satisfaction of the prescribed
tolerance,g t ≤ε dec, from termination at a numerical work limit. The selected route is executed as a batch,
while measurements are assimilated sequentially.

The network is initialized using the sparse observationsD0 and adapted periodically using the accumulated
datasetD t. Between graph refreshes, its factors remain fixed and only newly acquired measurements are
assimilated. At a scheduled refresh, the regenerated belief replaces the current information system. The final
map is obtained by solving the mean system after the last update. The complete procedure is summarized
in Algorithm 1.
