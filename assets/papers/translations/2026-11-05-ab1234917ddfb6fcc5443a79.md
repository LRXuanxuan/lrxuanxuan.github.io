**Original:**

Flow matching has emerged as a powerful alternative to diffusion models for
generative modeling, but standard conditional flow matching is fundamentally mis-
aligned with partially observed time series: starting from an uninformed Gaussian
source forces the model to transport even already-observed entries from noise to the
target, wasting capacity on trivially known regions. We proposeMissPath-FM, a
partial-observation-aware flow matching framework that redesigns the source prior
and probability path for incomplete time series. MissPath-FM employsstructured
source priors(interpolation priors and Gaussian process posterior priors) to initial-
ize the flow close to the observed signal, paired with amissingness-aware smooth
paththat allocates transport asymmetrically between observed and missing regions.
We evaluate systematically across four datasets, four missingness mechanisms
(MCAR, block, MNAR, irregular), two missing ratios, and three sequence lengths
(T∈ {24,48,96} ), totaling 48 settings against eight baselines. MissPath-FM
wins 29 of 32 settings at T=48 and 45 of 48 overall (93.8%). Our ablations re-
veal a regime-dependent design hierarchy in which no single component suffices:
under random missingness the source prior yields a large initial gain, but under
structured block missingness it canfail entirelyand the learned flow becomes
indispensable; the missingness-aware path contributes a further 12%–26% across
all regimes. Mechanistically, structured priors shorten observed-region transport by
up to 15×, concentrating the velocity field on genuinely uncertain entries. Project

Flow matching 已成为扩散模型之外一种有力的生成建模方案，但标准条件 Flow Matching 与部分观测时间序列的任务结构存在根本错配：从未经信息化的高斯源分布出发，会迫使模型将已观测条目也从噪声传输至目标，因而在本来已知的区域浪费建模能力。我们提出 MissPath-FM，这是一种面向部分观测的 Flow Matching 框架，通过重新设计源先验和概率路径来处理不完整时间序列。MissPath-FM 使用结构化源先验（插值先验和高斯过程后验先验）将流初始化在靠近观测信号的位置，并配合一种缺失感知的平滑路径，使观测区域和缺失区域的传输分配具有非对称性。

We argue that the central bottleneck in applying flow matching to incomplete time series lies not
in the velocity network architecture, but in thegeometry of transport under partial observability.
Two questions naturally arise: (1) what source distribution should the transport originate from
when part of the sequence is already known? and (2) how should the probability path allocate
transport effort between observed and missing regions? Addressing these questions yields a simple
design principle: the source prior should respect the observed signal, and the transport path should
concentrate generative effort on the uncertain entries.

**中文:**

我们认为，将 Flow Matching 用于不完整时间序列时，核心瓶颈不在速度网络架构，而在部分可观测条件下的传输几何结构。由此自然产生两个问题：（1）当序列的一部分已知时，传输应从何种源分布出发？（2）概率路径应如何在观测区域和缺失区域之间分配传输工作量？回答这些问题可得到一项简单设计原则：源先验应尊重已观测信号，而传输路径应将生成工作集中在不确定条目上。

3 Method

We present MissPath-FM, a generative framework for incomplete time series that reshapes the flow
matching transport around partial observability (Figure 1). The method has three core components, all
used in every reported result: (i) an observation-aware source prior (Section 3.2), (ii) a missingness-
aware probability path (Section 3.3), and (iii) a context-conditioned velocity network (Section 3.4).

5.4 Transport Geometry Analysis

Figure 4 and Table 6 directly quantify the transport reallocation on Synthetic Block 50%. Under the
Gaussian prior, all entries travel long distances from noise to target; under the interpolation prior,
observed entries stay near targets (∼15× shorter transport). The miss-to-obs ratio increases from
1.1× to 19.2×, confirming that the velocity field concentrates on genuinely uncertain entries. The
GP prior additionally shortens missing-region transport by 39%, explaining its advantage under block
and MNAR settings. Figure 5 visualizes this progression at the entry level: from uniform velocity
under the Gaussian prior (a), to concentrated missing-entry velocity under interpolation (b), to further
reduced velocity under the GP prior (c). The same effect is visible in the final samples: measured
beforethe deterministic restoration step, observed-entry MAE is 0.023–0.025 for MissPath-FM
against 0.40–0.77 for a Gaussian-source flow (Appendix K.2), so observed values are held in place by
the transport itself rather than by the final projection. This perspective explains why many alternatives
fail: once the prior removes the dominant inefficiency, regularizers, cost-aware paths, and larger
backbones have little residual room to contribute (Appendix L, H).

**中文:**

### 5.4 传输几何分析

图 4 和表 6 直接量化了 Synthetic、Block 50% 设置下传输分配的重新调整。在高斯先验下，所有条目从噪声到目标都要经过较长距离的传输；而在插值先验下，观测条目保持在目标附近（传输距离约缩短 15 倍）。缺失条目与观测条目的传输长度比从 1.1× 增至 19.2×，证实速度场集中于真正不确定的条目。GP 先验还将缺失区域的传输距离缩短了 39%，这解释了它在 block 和 MNAR 设置下的优势。图 5 在条目层面展示了这一变化：速度场从高斯先验下分布于所有条目 (a)，转为插值先验下集中于缺失条目 (b)，再到 GP 先验下进一步减小 (c)。

最终样本中也能观察到同样的效应：在确定性恢复步骤之前测量时，MissPath-FM 的观测条目 MAE 为 0.023–0.025，而高斯源分布的 flow 为 0.40–0.77（附录 K.2）；这表明观测值由传输过程本身保持在原位，而非依赖最终投影。这个视角解释了许多替代方案为何效果有限：先验消除了主要效率低下之后，正则化器、代价感知路径和更大的骨干网络几乎没有进一步发挥的空间（附录 L、H）。

## 6 结论

---

<span id="S010-04"></span>
**Source:** p.10 S010-04

**Original:**

We presentedMissPath-FM, a flow matching framework that treats partial observability as a transport
design problem rather than an input-side conditioning mechanism. By combining structured source
priors, a missingness-aware probability path, and context-conditioned velocity estimation, MissPath-
FM reshapes the generative transport to concentrate on genuinely uncertain entries. MissPath-FM
wins 45 of 48 evaluation settings (93.8%) across four datasets, four missingness mechanisms, and
three sequence lengths against eight baselines.

**中文:**

我们提出 MissPath-FM，这是一种将部分可观测性视作传输设计问题、而非输入侧条件机制的 Flow Matching 框架。通过结合结构化源先验、缺失感知概率路径和上下文条件速度估计，MissPath-FM 重塑生成传输，使其集中于真正不确定的条目。面对八种基线，MissPath-FM 在四个数据集、四种缺失机制和三种序列长度构成的 48 个评估设置中赢得 45 个（93.8%）。

<span id="S010-05"></span>
**Source:** p.10 S010-05

Limitations and future work.Our largest tested setting is D=36 and T=192 ; behaviour at larger
scale is not established. The advantage shrinks when observations are very sparse or unreliable:
against SAITS, MissPath-FM falls behind on two of four rate-sweep settings at90% missingness, and
under observation noise beyond about 0.2 (ETTm1) to 0.3 (Electricity) z-scored units (Appendix K).
The GP prior is per-variable and ignores cross-variable dependencies; a multi-output GP could exploit
them, but its cost grows cubically in D, already the bottleneck at D=36. Downstream decision tasks
and continuous-time irregular settings remain open. More broadly, we hope this work encourages
treating partial observability as atransport design problemfor generative time-series modeling.
10

**中文:**

**局限与未来工作。** 本文测试的最大规模为 \(D=36\)、\(T=192\)，尚未确立更大规模下的表现。当观测极为稀疏或不可靠时，方法优势会缩小：在缺失率扫描的四个设置中，90% 缺失率时 MissPath-FM 有两个设置落后于 SAITS；当观测噪声超过约 0.2（ETTm1）至 0.3（Electricity）个 z-score 单位时，也会出现性能问题（附录 K）。GP 先验按变量独立建模，忽略变量间依赖；多输出 GP 可能利用这些依赖，但其计算量随 \(D\) 立方增长，而 \(D=36\) 时这已是瓶颈。下游决策任务和连续时间不规则场景仍有待研究。更广泛而言，我们希望本工作促使研究者将部分可观测性视为生成式时间序列建模中的传输设计问题。

**中文:**

### K.5 含噪观测

---

<span id="S022-07"></span>
**Source:** p.22 S022-07

**Original:**

The MA path locks in observed entries early, which could over-anchor the flow if observations are
unreliable. We add Gaussian noise of standard deviation σnoise to the observed test entries (targets
stay clean; MAE is computed on missing positions) and compare three separately trained models
with observed-entry speed sobs ∈ {2.0,1.5,1.0} ; sobs=1.0 is the standard path. Aggressive locking
(sobs=2.0) is best at every noise level on both datasets, and the order 2.0<1.5<1.0 never reverses
(Table 18). The gap between sobs=2.0 and 1.0 stays roughly constant (0.008–0.013 on ETTm1,
0.017–0.020 on Electricity), so the MA path does not become a liability under noise; its relative
benefit shrinks as observations degrade (on Electricity,21% at σnoise=0 and 9% at 0.5). MissPath-
FM leads SAITS up to σnoise ≈0.2 on ETTm1 and 0.3 on Electricity; beyond that SAITS is more
robust. Raising σobs at test time to loosen the prior does not help (ETTm1 at σnoise=0.2: 0.1256,
0.1308, 0.1395 for σobs = 0.1,0.2,0.3 ; Electricity shows the same trend), because it departs from
the training-time value.
22

**中文:**

MA 路径会较早锁定观测条目；如果观测不可靠，这可能导致流过度锚定。我们向测试阶段的观测条目加入标准差为 \(\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}\) 的高斯噪声（目标值保持干净；MAE 仅在缺失位置计算），并比较三个单独训练、观测条目速度分别为 \(s_{\mathrm{obs}}\in\{2.0,1.5,1.0\}\) 的模型；\(s_{\mathrm{obs}}=1.0\) 对应标准路径。在两个数据集的每种噪声水平下，激进锁定（\(s_{\mathrm{obs}}=2.0\)）均最佳，排序 \(2.0<1.5<1.0\) 从未反转（表 18）。\(s_{\mathrm{obs}}=2.0\) 与 1.0 的差值大致保持稳定（ETTm1 为 0.008–0.013，Electricity 为 0.017–0.020），因此噪声并未使 MA 路径成为负担；只是当观测质量下降时，其相对收益会缩小（Electricity 上从 \(\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}=0\) 时的 21% 降至 0.5 时的 9%）。当 \(\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}\) 不超过 ETTm1 的约 0.2、Electricity 的约 0.3 时，MissPath-FM 领先 SAITS；再高时 SAITS 更稳健。测试时提高 \(\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}\) 以放松先验并无帮助（ETTm1 在 \(\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}=0.2\) 时，\(\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}=0.1,0.2,0.3\) 对应 0.1256、0.1308、0.1395；Electricity 趋势相同），因为这偏离了训练时使用的数值。
