**Original:**

Abstract—A key challenge of downlink (DL) channel estimation in frequency division duplex (FDD) massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems is the pilot overhead, which grows with the numbers of antennas and subcarriers. However, a prior on path delays and directions shared by the uplink (UL) and DL channels can reduce this overhead. A model trained only on UL channels measured at a base station (BS) can predict the path structure (delays, directions and power gains) from the location of a user equipment (UE) and thereby assist DL channel estimation. In this paper, we propose Gaussian-Informed Structure Transfer (GIST), in which each Gaussian primitive models one propagation path, whose delay, directions and power gain are predicted from the UE location relative to the primitive center . The complex gains of the predicted paths are estimated from DL pilots by linear minimum mean square error estimation with this prior . Experiments on a ray-traced and a measured dataset show that GIST outperforms all baselines, reaching a lower DL estimation error with fewer pilot subcarriers. Further experiments demonstrate its robustness to UE location errors, its low storage and computational requirements.

**中文:**

## 摘要

在频分双工（FDD）大规模多输入多输出（MIMO）系统中，下行（DL）信道估计的一个关键挑战是导频开销，它随天线数与子载波数增长。然而，上、下行信道共享的路径时延与方向先验可以降低这一开销。仅在基站（BS）测得的 UL 信道上训练的模型，可以从用户设备（UE）位置预测路径结构（时延、方向与功率增益），从而辅助 DL 信道估计。本文提出 Gaussian-Informed Structure Transfer（GIST）：每个高斯基元（Gaussian primitive）建模一条传播路径，其路径时延、方向与功率增益由 UE 相对于基元中心的位置预测得到；被预测路径的复增益则在该先验下由 DL 导频经线性最小均方误差（LMMSE）估计得到。在射线追踪数据集与实测数据集上的实验表明，GIST 优于所有基线，以更少的导频子载波达到更低的 DL 估计误差。进一步实验验证了其对 UE 位置误差的鲁棒性，以及其低存储与低计算需求。

In frequency division duplex (FDD) systems, the uplink
(UL) and downlink (DL) occupy different carrier frequencies,
separated by more than the coherence bandwidth. Since the
phase of each propagation path depends on the carrier fre-
quency, instantaneous reciprocity is lost and the base station
(BS) cannot directly reuse its UL channel estimate as the DL
channel. Especially in FDD massive multiple-input multiple-
output (MIMO) systems, the pilot overhead is substantial,
as conventional estimators such as least squares require DL
pilots scaling with the number of BS antennas [1], [2].

**中文:**

在频分双工（FDD）系统中，上行（UL）与下行（DL）占用不同的载频，两者间隔大于相干带宽。由于每条传播路径的相位取决于载频，瞬时互易性不再成立，基站（BS）无法直接把 UL 信道估计当作 DL 信道复用。尤其在 FDD 大规模多输入多输出（MIMO）系统中，导频开销十分可观：最小二乘等传统估计器要求 DL 导频数量随 BS 天线数同比例增长 [1], [2]。

## V. 结论

---

<span id="S006-05"></span>
**Source:** p.6 S006-05

**Original:**

We proposed GIST for FDD DL channel estimation with a
compact, location-dependent prior learned only from UL chan-
nels at training locations. Results on ray-traced and measured
channels demonstrate accurate estimation with few DL pilot
subcarriers, outperforming the compared baselines. Although
GIST takes the UE location as input, it remains highly robust
to location errors. Its low storage requirements and estimation
latency could make GIST a promising candidate for resource-
constrained devices and frequent model distribution. This work
considers UE locations on a horizontal plane, a fixed UE array
orientation and exact training locations. Future work could
extend GIST to varying UE heights by 3D primitives at the
sameO(G)complexity and to varying UE orientations by ro-
tating global-frame directions into the UE array. Collaborative
updates using UL observations collected across devices could
also adapt the learned prior to environmental changes.

**中文:**

我们提出了 GIST，用于 FDD 下行信道估计：其先验紧凑、依赖位置，且仅从训练位置处的 UL 信道学习。射线追踪信道与实测信道上的结果表明，GIST 能以很少的 DL 导频子载波取得精确估计，并优于所比较的基线。尽管 GIST 以 UE 位置为输入，它对位置误差仍高度鲁棒。其低存储需求与低估计时延使 GIST 有望用于资源受限设备与频繁的模型分发。本文假设 UE 位于水平面、UE 阵列朝向固定且训练位置精确。未来工作可用三维基元把 GIST 扩展到 UE 高度变化的情形，并保持相同的 $O(G)$ 复杂度；也可将全局坐标系方向旋转到 UE 阵列，从而扩展到 UE 朝向变化的情形。利用跨设备采集的 UL 观测进行协同更新，还可使学习到的先验适应环境变化。
