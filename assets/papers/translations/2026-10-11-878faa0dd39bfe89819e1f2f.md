**Original:** Abstract—Near-field (NF) line-of-sight (LoS) MIMO systems enable efficient channel state information (CSI) acquisition and precoding by exploiting known antenna geometries at both the base station (BS) and user equipment (UE). This paper introduces a compact parameterization of the NF LoS MIMO channel using two angles of departure (AoDs) and a BS–UE relative rotation angle. The inclusion of the second AoD removes the need for fine-grained distance grids imposed by conventional NF channel parametrization. To address the user-specific uplink pilot overhead in multiuser NF CSI acquisition, we propose a scheme that uses a fixed, UE-independent set of downlink pilots transmitted from a carefully selected subset of BS antennas. In dominant LoS conditions, as few as four pilots suffice, with Cramér–Rao bound (CRB) analysis confirming that increased antenna spacing improves estimation accuracy. Each UE estimates and quantizes angular parameters related to the NF LoS MIMO channel and feeds them back to the BS for geometry-based CSI reconstruction, eliminating the need for full channel feedback. To enhance robustness against noise, quantization errors, and additional non-line-of-sight (NLoS) components, we introduce a two-stage precoding method. The initial precoding is computed from estimated LoS CSI and refined through bidirectional over-the-air (OTA) training. Furthermore, a two-step stream allocation strategy reduces pilot and computational overhead. Simulations demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves high data rates with significantly fewer OTA iterations, approaching the performance of perfect CSI.

**中文:** 摘要——近场（near-field，NF）视距（line-of-sight，LoS）MIMO 系统可利用基站（base station，BS）和用户设备（user equipment，UE）双方已知的天线几何结构，高效获取信道状态信息（channel state information，CSI）并进行预编码。本文提出一种紧凑的 NF LoS MIMO 信道参数化方式，使用两个出发角（angle of departure，AoD）和一个 BS–UE 相对旋转角。引入第二个 AoD 后，传统 NF 信道参数化所需的细粒度距离网格不再必要。针对多用户 NF CSI 获取中的用户专属上行导频开销，本文提出一种方案：从精心选取的一部分 BS 天线发送固定且与 UE 无关的下行导频集合。在 LoS 占主导的条件下，最少只需四个导频；Cramér–Rao 界（CRB）分析确认，增大天线间距可提高估计精度。每个 UE 估计并量化与 NF LoS MIMO 信道相关的角度参数，再反馈给 BS 以进行几何辅助 CSI 重构，从而无需反馈完整信道。为增强方法对噪声、量化误差和额外非视距（non-line-of-sight，NLoS）分量的鲁棒性，本文提出两阶段预编码方法：首先根据估计的 LoS CSI 计算初始预编码，再通过双向空口（over-the-air，OTA）训练进行细化。此外，两步流分配策略可降低导频和计算开销。仿真表明，该方法以显著更少的 OTA 迭代实现较高数据速率，性能接近完美 CSI。

**Original:** Emerging 6G applications, such as the metaverse, digital twins, ultra-high-definition media, and extended reality (XR), demand beyond-5G data rates, operation over ultra-wide frequency ranges, and the ability to sustain massive user connectivity [3], [4]. One key enabler is the deployment of extremely large-scale antenna arrays operating at higher frequencies, including FR3 (7–24 GHz), millimeter wave (mmWave), and sub-terahertz (THz) bands [5], [6]. This suggests that 6G networks will increasingly operate in the near-field (NF) instead of the far-field (FF) [6], [7]. This shift opens up higher-resolution beamforming, enhanced spectral efficiency, optimized connectivity for users and Internet-of-Things (IoT) devices, improved positioning and sensing accuracy, and additional rank gains through spatially spreading antennas (e.g., in indoor setups) [3], [8], [9].

**中文:** 新兴的 6G 应用（如元宇宙、数字孪生、超高清媒体和扩展现实 XR）需要超越 5G 的数据速率、超宽频率范围运行能力，以及支持海量用户连接的能力[3], [4]。一个关键推动因素是在更高频段部署超大规模天线阵列，包括 FR3（7–24 GHz）、毫米波（mmWave）和亚太赫兹（THz）频段[5], [6]。这意味着 6G 网络将越来越多地工作在近场（NF）而非远场（FF）[6], [7]。这一转变带来了更高分辨率的波束成形、更高的频谱效率、面向用户和物联网（IoT）设备的优化连接、更高的定位与感知精度，以及通过空间展开天线（例如室内配置）获得的额外秩增益[3], [8], [9]。

**Original:** In the NF region, electromagnetic propagation is characterized by spherical rather than planar wavefronts, in contrast to conventional FF models. This spherical nature introduces both opportunities and challenges [10]. It enables highly focused beams that can target specific spatial points, improving interference management [6], [7] and enhancing spectral efficiency in multiuser scenarios [11], [12], whereas traditional FF beam steering is restricted to angular directions [8]. NF LoS propagation can also increase the spatial multiplexing gain of MIMO systems [8]. However, models and techniques designed for FF conditions are generally inadequate, motivating new approaches to fully exploit NF communication [6], [13].

**中文:** 在 NF 区域，与传统 FF 模型不同，电磁传播表现为球面波前而非平面波前。这种球面特性既带来机遇，也带来挑战[10]。它能够形成高度聚焦的波束并指向特定空间位置，从而改善干扰管理[6], [7]并提升多用户场景下的频谱效率[11], [12]；相比之下，传统 FF 波束赋形仅能沿角度方向进行[8]。NF LoS 传播还可提高 MIMO 系统的空间复用增益[8]。然而，为 FF 条件设计的模型和技术通常并不适用，因此需要新的方法充分发挥 NF 通信的潜力[6], [13]。

**Original:** Accurate channel state information (CSI) acquisition at the base station (BS) is essential for effective beam focusing [14]. Unlike FF channels, which rely primarily on the angle of arrival/departure (AoA/AoD) and exhibit angular-domain sparsity, NF channels also depend on the distance between transmit (TX) and receive (RX) antennas [15]. This dual dependency necessitates revising sparse representation models and estimation algorithms [5]. To capture both angle and distance information, a polar-domain representation was introduced in [15], enabling compressive sensing (CS)-based channel estimation with low pilot overhead. To further reduce complexity, [5] proposes a distance-parameterized angular-domain sparse model, where the dictionary size depends only on angular resolution. Channel estimation, including both LoS and non-line-of-sight (NLoS) components, is performed via joint dictionary learning and sparse recovery, treating the user distance as an unknown dictionary parameter. For wideband NF channels, [16] introduces a CS-based algorithm that estimates AoA and distance while addressing the beam-split effect by exploiting its bilinear structure. In [5], [15], [16], both LoS and NLoS channels are reconstructed using CS-based methods.

**中文:** 在基站（BS）处准确获取信道状态信息（CSI）对于有效聚焦波束至关重要[14]。远场（FF）信道主要依赖到达角/出发角（AoA/AoD），并呈现角域稀疏性；近场（NF）信道还取决于发射（TX）与接收（RX）天线之间的距离[15]。这种双重依赖要求修订稀疏表示模型和估计算法[5]。为同时刻画角度和距离信息，文献[15]引入了极域表示，从而能够以较低导频开销进行基于压缩感知（CS）的信道估计。为进一步降低复杂度，文献[5]提出距离参数化的角域稀疏模型，其字典规模仅取决于角度分辨率。包括 LoS 和非视距（NLoS）分量在内的信道估计，通过联合字典学习与稀疏恢复完成，并将用户距离视为未知的字典参数。针对宽带 NF 信道，文献[16]利用双线性结构提出一种基于 CS 的算法，在估计 AoA 和距离的同时处理波束分裂效应。文献[5], [15], [16]均使用基于 CS 的方法重构 LoS 和 NLoS 信道。

**Original:** Unlike FF LoS channels, which typically have a rank-one structure, NF channels can exhibit higher ranks, enabling spatial multiplexing [17]–[19]. The NF LoS MIMO channel can be efficiently reconstructed using a few geometric parameters, such as the TX–RX antenna-pair-specific reference distance, AoA (AoD), and relative transceiver rotation [17], [20]. This property allows for accurate channel estimation with minimal pilot overhead [14], [17]–[21].

**中文:** 与通常具有秩一结构的 FF LoS 信道不同，NF 信道可能具有更高的秩，从而支持空间复用[17]–[19]。NF LoS MIMO 信道可通过少量几何参数高效重构，例如针对每一 TX–RX 天线对的参考距离、AoA（AoD）和收发端相对旋转角[17], [20]。这一性质使得仅需极少导频开销即可准确估计信道[14], [17]–[21]。

**Original:** In [17]–[19], the NF LoS channel is decomposed into parallel subchannels, where an FF approximation is applied to each separately. Using geometric relationships among subchannel-specific FF AoAs, a likelihood function is computed for estimation of the LoS parameters using a message passing algorithm. A more accurate method is presented in [20], where the MIMO LoS channel is parameterized by three geometric parameters and recovered via a maximum-likelihood (ML) estimator, followed by a gradient-based refinement.

**中文:** 在文献[17]–[19]中，NF LoS 信道被分解为多个并行子信道，并分别对每个子信道采用 FF 近似。利用各子信道特有的 FF AoA 之间的几何关系，通过消息传递算法计算似然函数，以估计 LoS 参数。文献[20]提出了一种更精确的方法：使用三个几何参数对 MIMO LoS 信道进行参数化，并通过最大似然（ML）估计器恢复信道，随后再通过基于梯度的方法进行细化。

**Original:** Each MIMO NLoS component is modeled using two NF array response vectors, enabling a sparse polar-domain representation and estimation via sparse recovery methods. As noted in [20], since the LoS MIMO channel cannot be decoupled into two NF array response vectors, it cannot be represented in the polar domain using a transformation matrix. [14] models LoS, reflection, and NLoS paths of an NF MISO channel using three geometric parameters for a uniform planar array (UPA) at the BS, estimating them via a damped Newtonized OMP algorithm. In [21], a sequential approach decouples angle and distance estimation, first applying a 2-dimensional discrete Fourier transform for angle estimation, followed by a low-complexity closed-form distance estimation method.

**中文:** 每个 MIMO NLoS 分量由两个 NF 阵列响应向量建模，从而可构造稀疏极域表示，并通过稀疏恢复方法进行估计。正如文献[20]所指出的，由于 LoS MIMO 信道无法解耦为两个 NF 阵列响应向量，因此不能通过变换矩阵在极域中表示。文献[14]针对 BS 处均匀平面阵列（UPA）的 NF MISO 信道，使用三个几何参数建模 LoS、反射和 NLoS 路径，并通过阻尼 Newtonized OMP 算法估计这些参数。文献[21]采用顺序方法将角度与距离估计解耦：先使用二维离散傅里叶变换估计角度，再通过低复杂度闭式方法估计距离。

**Original:** We propose a CSI acquisition framework that leverages the parametric representation of the NF LoS channel, requiring only a few downlink pilots and limited geometry information exchange between nodes. Instead of acquiring the full LoS MIMO channel, only three angular parameters are estimated: relative TX–RX rotation and two AoDs from a carefully selected pair of reference antennas. The second AoD is introduced to address distance sampling challenges in existing works. Users estimate these parameters based on known antenna placements and a few pilots from a subset of BS antennas. The quantized estimates are then fed back to the BS, enabling full LoS MIMO channel reconstruction with minimal overhead. However, estimation noise, quantization errors, and unaccounted NLoS components can degrade the reconstructed channel and impact the precoding/combining design. If strong NLoS components are overlooked, relying solely on LoS estimates may be ineffective. To mitigate this, we incorporate bidirectional over-the-air (OTA) beamformer training, eliminating the need for explicit NLoS estimation. Unlike traditional OTA training, which starts with random precoding/combining [27]–[30], our approach exploits the acquired multiuser LoS CSI at the BS for efficient initialization, significantly accelerating convergence. The main contributions of our work are summarized as follows:

<span id="S188"></span>

**Original:** This paper proposed a geometry-aided NF LoS CSI acquisition scheme at the BS, followed by a two-stage transmission design. The NF LoS MIMO channel is characterized by three angular parameters, improving on conventional reference-distance-based NF parameterizations. A limited number of downlink pilots enable each UE to estimate LoS angles. Simulations and CRLB analysis show accurate multi-UE LoS channel reconstruction with only 4–6 pilot sequences, far fewer than the M BS antennas (4–6≪M). In Stage 1, the BS obtains LoS CSI from angular triplets estimated and fed back by UEs, then performs coarse precoding from estimated multi-UE LoS MIMO CSI. Stage 2 refines it through OTA iterations between BS and UEs. The two-stage design converges to full-CSI performance within a few OTA iterations and significantly outperforms conventional OTA initialized with random precoding.

**中文:** 本文提出了 BS 侧几何辅助的近场 LoS CSI 获取方案，并在此基础上设计两阶段传输流程。近场 LoS MIMO 信道由三个角度参数表征，改进了传统依赖参考距离的近场信道参数化方式。通过少量下行导频，每个 UE 即可估计 LoS 角度。仿真和 CRLB 分析表明，仅用 4–6 个导频序列即可准确重构多 UE LoS 信道，远少于 BS 天线数 M（4–6≪M）。阶段1中，BS 根据 UE 估计并反馈的角度三元组获取 LoS CSI，再基于估计的多 UE LoS MIMO CSI 进行粗预编码。阶段2通过 BS 与 UE 之间的 OTA 迭代细化预编码。该两阶段设计仅需数次 OTA 迭代即可达到完美 CSI 性能，并显著优于随机预编码初始化的传统 OTA 方法。
