**Original:**

Abstract—Channel estimation for stacked intelligent metasurfaces (SIMs) has relied on the cascade model, which ignores
mutual coupling and inter-layer reflection. Under a coupling-consistent multiport model, we show that the pilots are linear
in the unknown channel segments and derive least-squares and
linear minimum-mean-square-error estimators, an identifiability condition and an exact error decomposition for the reactance training schedule, and a gradient-based schedule design.
Cascade-based estimation floors above0dB normalized error at
practical layer spacings and collapses the sum rate below that
of the bare feed, whereas the proposed estimators retain about
80%of the perfect-channel-knowledge sum rate.

**中文:**

## 摘要

堆叠智能超表面（stacked intelligent metasurfaces, SIM）的信道估计一直依赖级联模型，而该模型忽略了互耦与层间反射。在互耦一致的多端口模型下，我们证明导频关于未知信道分段是线性的，并推导了最小二乘（LS）与线性最小均方误差（LMMSE）估计器、可辨识性条件、关于电抗训练调度的精确误差分解，以及基于梯度的调度设计。在实际层间距下，基于级联的估计其归一化误差下限高于 0 dB，并使和速率跌至裸馈源（bare feed）以下；而所提估计器可保持约 80% 的理想信道已知（perfect-channel-knowledge）和速率。

> 摘要中粗体强调的 “above 0 dB”“80%” 在抽取文本中被压平，译文按原意保留。

<span id="S001-08"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-08

**Original:**

Nearly all of these schemes adopt thecascade model:
each layer is a diagonal mask of unit-modulus phase shifts,
and consecutive layers are connected by a scalar, unilateral
Rayleigh–Sommerfeld (RS) propagator [1]. A parallel, circuit-theoretic literature has shown that this abstraction is not the
physics of a compact stack. Physically consistent scattering- and impedance-parameter models of SIMs [8], [9] reveal intra-layer mutual coupling, inter-layer reflections, and a load-dependent input impedance seen by the feed. The model of
[10] captures all of these in one closed-form impedance kernel
sampled at the three-dimensional (3-D) port separations, shows
that the cascade is the single-pass limit of one matrix inversion
under assumptions that fail in compact stacks, and finds
that a precoder designed on the cascade can perform worse
than open-circuiting the stack. The only coupling-aware SIM estimator is available in [11], which configures a receive SIM
as a single analog projector onto a subspace derived from prior
location knowledge, for near-field channel and localization
estimation. Training over multiple load states, identifiability
without prior knowledge, and the error committed by cascade-based estimators remain open [2], and are addressed here.

**中文:**

几乎所有此类方案都采用级联模型：每层是单位模相移的对角掩模，相邻层之间由标量、单向的 Rayleigh–Sommerfeld（RS）传播子连接 [1]。与之并行的一类电路理论文献表明，这种抽象并非紧凑堆叠的物理本质。对 SIM 的物理一致散射参数与阻抗参数模型 [8], [9] 揭示了层内互耦、层间反射，以及馈源所看到的、与负载相关的输入阻抗。[10] 的模型用一个在三维（3-D）端口间距上采样的闭式阻抗核刻画了上述全部效应，证明级联只是某些在紧凑堆叠中失效的假设下对一次矩阵求逆的单次通过极限，并发现按级联设计的预编码器性能可能还不如把堆叠开路。目前唯一考虑互耦的 SIM 估计器见 [11]：它把接收 SIM 配置为单个模拟投影器，投影到由先验位置信息导出的子空间上，用于近场信道与定位估计。多负载状态下的训练、无先验知识时的可辨识性，以及基于级联的估计器所引入的误差，仍是开放问题 [2]，也是本文要处理的问题。

Contributions:(i) we formulate uplink SIM channel estimation under a coupling-consistent multiport model and show
that, although the loaded stack responds nonlinearly to its
reactances, the received pilots arelinearin the unknown
feed-side and stack-side channel segments, with a known,

sufficient identifiability condition on the schedule, the exact
mean square error (MSE) and its feed/stack decomposition,
the exact prediction error at untrained load states, the high-SNR floor of the LMMSE estimator below the pilot counting
bound, and a gradient-based schedule design; (iii) we derive
the effective-channel normalized MSE (NMSE) of every linear
estimator in closed form, including the bias floor of cascade-based estimation and of model errors in general; and (iv) we
show that this bias is minor below0.1λof layer spacing, where
the stack is nearly inert, but reaches about100%NMSE or

## VI. 结论

---

<span id="S005-10"></span>
**Source:** p.5 S005-10

**Original:**

This letter formulated channel estimation for SIMs under
a mutual-coupling-consistent multiport model, in which the
pilots are linear in the unknown channel segments. The LS and
LMMSE estimators come with an identifiability condition, an
exact error decomposition and prediction error at untrained
load states, the LMMSE floor below the counting bound,
and a closed-form gradient that optimizes the schedule over
realizable reactances. Cascade-based estimation incurs a bias
floor, predicted in closed form, of about100%NMSE or
more fromλ/6toλ/2and collapses the sum rate of load
optimization below that of the bare feed, whereas the proposed
estimators retain about80%of the perfect-CSI rate, paying off
once the coherence block exceeds a few hundred symbols, and
tolerate position errors up to0.005λ,2–3-bit reactances, and
multipath. Since the linear model (6), the estimators (8) and
(13), the rank condition (9), and Proposition 2 require only that
Zss+Mbe invertible and symmetric, they hold for any lossless
reciprocal beyond-diagonal load networkM[13]; redesigning
(7) and (15)–(16) for such networks, and wideband kernels,
are left for future work.

**中文:**

本文在互耦一致的多端口模型下建立了 SIM 的信道估计，其中导频关于未知信道分段是线性的。LS 与 LMMSE 估计器附带了可辨识性条件、精确的误差分解、未训练负载状态下的预测误差、低于计数界的 LMMSE 下限，以及在可实现电抗上优化调度的闭式梯度。基于级联的估计会产生一个偏差下限（以闭式预测）：从 $\lambda/6$ 到 $\lambda/2$ 约为 100% NMSE 甚至更高，并使负载优化的和速率跌至裸馈源以下；而所提估计器可保持约 80% 的理想 CSI 速率，在相干块超过几百个符号后开始获益，并能容忍最大 $0.005\lambda$ 的位置误差、2–3 bit 电抗与多径。由于线性模型 (6)、估计器 (8) 与 (13)、秩条件 (9) 以及命题 2 只要求 $\mathbf Z_{ss}+\mathbf M$ 可逆且对称，它们对任何无损、互易的 beyond-diagonal 负载网络 $\mathbf M$ [13] 都成立；针对此类网络以及宽带核重新设计 (7) 与 (15)–(16)，留待未来工作。
