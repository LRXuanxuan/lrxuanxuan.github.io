`Multistep CM` 负责在采样步数与生成质量之间建立连续折中；`CPS` 负责在生成先验的噪声空间中高效探索多样后验；`MACS` 负责把观测残差直接写入一致性采样过程；`UrbanMIMOMap` 提供面向 MIMO 的复数 CSI 数据基础；`QuaMoE-DRF` 则把动态环境、多模态可靠性和 beam/rate 决策连接起来。

## 1. Multistep Consistency Models

**论文信息**：Jonathan Heek, Emiel Hoogeboom, Tim Salimans；arXiv:2403.06807v3。

### 1.1 Abstract 摘要

**原文**

Diffusion models are relatively easy to train but require many steps to generate samples. Consistency models are far more difficult to train, but generate samples in a single step. In this paper we propose Multistep Consistency Models: A unification between Consistency Models and TRACT that can interpolate between a consistency model and a diffusion model: a trade-off between sampling speed and sampling quality. Specifically, a 1-step consistency model is a conventional consistency model whereas an infinity-step consistency model is a diffusion model.

Multistep Consistency Models work well in practice. By increasing the sample budget from a single step to 2–8 steps, we can train models more easily that generate higher-quality samples, while retaining much of the sampling speed benefits. Notable results are 1.4 FID on ImageNet-64 in 8 sampling steps and 2.1 FID on ImageNet-128 in 8 sampling steps with consistency distillation, using simple losses without adversarial training. We also show that our method scales to a text-to-image diffusion model, generating samples close to the quality of the original model.

**中文翻译**

扩散模型相对容易训练，但生成样本时需要执行许多步。Consistency Model（一致性模型，CM）训练难度高得多，却能够在一步内生成样本。本文提出 Multistep Consistency Models（多步一致性模型），将 Consistency Models 与 TRACT 统一起来，使模型可以在一致性模型和扩散模型之间连续插值，从而实现采样速度与生成质量之间的折中。具体而言，1 步一致性模型就是传统的一致性模型，而步数趋于无穷的一致性模型则等价于扩散模型。

多步一致性模型在实践中表现良好。将采样预算从单步增加到 2–8 步后，模型更容易训练，并能生成质量更高的样本，同时保留大部分一致性模型的速度优势。在不使用对抗训练、仅采用简单损失的条件下，基于一致性蒸馏的方法在 ImageNet-64 上 8 步采样达到 1.4 FID，在 ImageNet-128 上达到 2.1 FID。作者还证明，该方法可以扩展到文本到图像扩散模型，生成接近原始模型质量的样本。

### 1.2 Introduction 引言

<a id="MCM-S001"></a>
**Source: p.1 MCM-S001**

**Original:** Diffusion models have rapidly become one of the dominant generative models for image, video and audio generation. The biggest downside to diffusion models is their relatively expensive sampling procedure: whereas training uses a single function evaluation per datapoint, it requires many, sometimes hundreds, of evaluations to generate a sample.

**中文：** 扩散模型已经迅速成为图像、视频和音频生成领域的主流生成模型之一。扩散模型最大的缺点是采样过程成本较高：训练时每个数据点通常只需一次函数评估，而生成一个样本却需要进行很多次、甚至数百次函数评估。

<a id="MCM-S002"></a>
**Source: p.1 MCM-S002**

**Original:** Recently, Consistency Models have reduced sampling time significantly, but at the expense of image quality. Consistency models come in two variants: Consistency Training (CT) and Consistency Distillation (CD), and both have considerably improved performance compared to earlier works. TRACT focuses solely on distillation with an approach similar to consistency distillation, and shows that dividing the diffusion trajectory in stages can improve performance. Despite their successes, neither of these works attain performance close to a standard diffusion baseline.

**中文：** 近年来，Consistency Models 显著降低了采样时间，但代价是图像质量有所下降。一致性模型有两种形式：Consistency Training（CT，一致性训练）和 Consistency Distillation（CD，一致性蒸馏）；与更早的方法相比，两者都取得了明显更好的性能。TRACT 只关注蒸馏，并采用了类似一致性蒸馏的方法；它表明，将扩散轨迹划分为多个阶段可以提升性能。尽管这些方法取得了成功，但它们的性能仍未接近标准扩散模型基线。

<a id="MCM-S003"></a>
**Source: p.1 MCM-S003**

**Original:** Here, we propose a unification of Consistency Models and TRACT that closes the performance gap between standard diffusion performance and low-step variants. We relax the single-step constraint from consistency models and allow as many as 4, 8 or 16 function evaluations for certain settings. We generalize TRACT to consistency training and adapt step schedule annealing and synchronized dropout from consistency modelling. We also show that as steps increase, Multistep CT becomes a diffusion model.

**中文：** 本文提出 Consistency Models 与 TRACT 的统一框架，以缩小标准扩散模型和低步数模型之间的性能差距。作者放宽了一致性模型的单步约束，在某些设置下允许使用 4、8 甚至 16 次函数评估；同时将 TRACT 推广到一致性训练，并引入一致性建模中的步长调度退火和同步 dropout。作者还证明，随着步数增加，多步一致性训练会逐渐变成扩散模型。

<a id="MCM-S004"></a>
**Source: p.1 MCM-S004**

**Original:** We introduce a unifying training algorithm to train what we call Multistep Consistency Models, which splits the diffusion process from data to noise into predefined segments. For each segment a separate consistency model is trained, while sharing the same parameters. For both CT and CD, this turns out to be easier to model and leads to significantly improved performance with fewer steps. Surprisingly, we can perfectly match baseline diffusion model performance with only eight steps, on both ImageNet-64 and ImageNet-128.

**中文：** 作者提出了一个统一训练算法，用于训练所谓的 Multistep Consistency Models。该算法把从数据到噪声的扩散过程划分为预先定义的多个区间；每个区间对应一个独立的一致性映射，但这些映射共享网络参数。对于 CT 和 CD，这种做法更容易建模，并且能在较少步数下显著提升性能。值得注意的是，在 ImageNet-64 和 ImageNet-128 上，仅用 8 步采样就可以达到与扩散模型基线相当的性能。

<a id="MCM-S005"></a>
**Source: p.2 MCM-S005**

**Original:** Another important contribution is a deterministic sampler for diffusion models that can obtain competitive performance on more complicated datasets such as ImageNet-128 in terms of FID score. We name this sampler Adjusted DDIM (aDDIM), which essentially inflates the noise prediction to correct for the integration error that produces blurrier samples. In terms of numbers, we achieve performance rivaling standard diffusion approaches with as little as 8 and sometimes 4 sampling steps. With only 4 sampling steps, multistep consistency models obtain 1.6 FID on ImageNet-64 and 2.3 FID on ImageNet-128.

**中文：** 本文的另一项重要贡献是一个确定性扩散采样器，它能够在 ImageNet-128 等更复杂数据集上取得具有竞争力的 FID。作者将该采样器命名为 Adjusted DDIM（aDDIM），其核心是放大噪声预测，以修正数值积分误差导致的样本变模糊问题。实验上，模型只需 8 步、某些情况下甚至 4 步采样，就能达到接近标准扩散方法的性能。仅使用 4 步采样时，多步一致性模型在 ImageNet-64 和 ImageNet-128 上分别取得 1.6 和 2.3 的 FID。

### 1.3 主要内容概括

这篇论文的核心不是提出一个新的无线应用，而是重新设计 CM 的“步数—质量”接口：单步模式追求极低时延，多步模式逐渐恢复扩散模型的质量。关键技术是把噪声到数据的轨迹分段，每段学习局部一致性映射，但共享网络参数；同时用 aDDIM 修正少步确定性采样中的积分误差。

### 1.4 你可以学习与应用的知识

- **训练目标层面**：对无线 CM，不必只比较 1 NFE。可以把 (K=1,2,4,8) 作为可调节预算，报告 `NMSE–NFE–latency–downstream rate` 曲线。
- **条件建模层面**：将复数信道拆为实部/虚部或幅相通道，把位置、环境、导频条件注入每个局部轨迹段；这比只做一个全轨迹的一步映射更容易稳定。
- **工程层面**：aDDIM 的思想提示你检查低步数采样中的范数偏差和积分误差，而不能把采样步数减少后性能变化全部归因于网络容量。
- **局限**：原文主要验证图像和文本到图像生成，没有证明在复数 MIMO、导频一致性或时变无线环境中同样成立；迁移时需要独立做物理一致性与下游通信指标验证。

## 2. Consistency Posterior Sampling for Diverse Image Synthesis

**论文信息**：Vishal Purohit, Matthew Repasky, Jianfeng Lu, Qiang Qiu, Yao Xie, Xiuyuan Cheng；CVPR 2025；arXiv:2410.02078。

### 2.1 Abstract 摘要

**原文**

Posterior sampling in high-dimensional spaces using generative models holds significant promise for applications including inverse problems and guided generation tasks. Generating diverse posterior samples remains expensive, as existing methods require restarting the entire generative process for each new sample. In this work, we propose a posterior sampling approach that simulates Langevin dynamics in the noise space of a pre-trained generative model. By exploiting the mapping between the noise and data spaces provided by distilled flows or consistency models, our method enables seamless exploration of the posterior without re-running the full sampling chain, drastically reducing computational overhead.

Theoretically, we prove a guarantee for the proposed noise-space Langevin dynamics to approximate the posterior, assuming that the generative model sufficiently approximates the prior distribution. Experiments on image restoration with noisy linear and nonlinear forward operators on LSUN-Bedroom and ImageNet demonstrate high-fidelity samples with enhanced semantic diversity, even with limited function evaluations, and superior efficiency compared with diffusion-based posterior sampling methods.

**中文翻译**

利用生成模型在高维空间中进行后验采样，对于逆问题和引导式生成等任务具有重要潜力。然而，生成多样的后验样本仍然代价高昂，因为现有方法通常需要针对每一个新样本重新启动完整的生成过程。本文提出一种后验采样方法：在预训练生成模型的噪声空间中模拟 Langevin dynamics（朗之万动力学）。该方法利用蒸馏流模型或一致性模型提供的噪声空间到数据空间的映射，从而无需重新运行完整采样链，就能连续探索后验分布，大幅降低计算开销。

在理论上，作者证明：当生成模型能够充分逼近真实先验分布时，噪声空间朗之万动力学能够以一定误差逼近目标后验。作者在 LSUN-Bedroom 和 ImageNet 上的带噪线性与非线性图像恢复任务中验证了方法，结果表明，即使函数评估次数有限，该方法也能生成高保真且语义更多样的样本，并且比基于扩散的后验采样更高效。

### 2.2 Introduction 引言

<a id="CPS-S001"></a>
**Source: p.1 CPS-S001**

**Original:** Generative models that approximate complex data priors have been widely used for guided generation. While early approaches relied on GANs, diffusion models have since outperformed them and become state-of-the-art for conditional generation. Posterior sampling methods have gained traction for inverse problems, where the goal is to sample from (p(x\mid y)\propto p(y\mid x)p(x)). Although these posteriors are often intractable, generative models enable efficient approximations. Earlier diffusion-based solutions required task-specific training, while recent works use pre-trained diffusion priors in a training-free manner, with extensions to nonlinear tasks.

**中文：** 能够逼近复杂数据先验的生成模型已经广泛用于引导式生成。早期方法主要依赖 GAN，但扩散模型后来取得了更好的性能，并成为条件生成的主流方法。后验采样在逆问题中受到越来越多关注，其目标是从 (p(x\mid y)\propto p(y\mid x)p(x)) 中采样。尽管这类后验通常难以直接求解，生成模型可以对其进行高效近似。较早的扩散方法需要针对任务训练模型，而近期方法开始以无训练方式使用预训练扩散先验，并扩展到非线性任务。

<a id="CPS-S002"></a>
**Source: p.1 CPS-S002**

**Original:** Inverse problem solvers are typically grouped into point-estimate or multiple-estimate approaches. Most recent methods focus on the former and face challenges in generating diverse samples efficiently. For example, DPS requires full denoising for each sample, increasing computational cost. While energy-based models offer progressive sampling via MCMC and recent works explore HMC in generative noise spaces, such techniques are largely unexplored for inverse problems. We bridge this gap by introducing a measurement-guided posterior sampler in noise space.

**中文：** 逆问题求解器通常分为点估计方法和多估计方法。近期多数方法聚焦于点估计，因此在高效生成多样样本方面存在困难。例如，DPS 为每个样本都需要执行完整去噪过程，导致计算成本随样本数量增加。虽然能量模型可以通过 MCMC 实现渐进式采样，近期也有工作探索在生成模型的噪声空间中使用 HMC，但这类技术在逆问题中仍研究不足。本文通过在噪声空间中引入测量引导的后验采样器，弥补这一空缺。

<a id="CPS-S003"></a>
**Source: p.2 CPS-S003**

**Original:** We propose a posterior sampling method that performs exploration directly in the noise space of a pre-trained generative model. By leveraging measurements from the inverse problem to initialize the noise space, our approach enables targeted and efficient exploration. We employ Langevin dynamics in noise space, taking advantage of the deterministic, one-to-one mapping between noise and data provided by models like consistency models. This eliminates the need to approximate the measurement likelihood and allows us to derive a theoretical bound on the approximation error of our posterior samples. Sampling in noise space enables progressive accumulation of diverse reconstructions without repeated full denoising runs.

**中文：** 本文提出一种直接在预训练生成模型噪声空间中进行探索的后验采样方法。该方法利用逆问题的测量结果初始化噪声空间，使探索具有针对性且更高效；随后在噪声空间执行 Langevin dynamics，并利用一致性模型等方法所提供的噪声到数据的确定性一一映射。这样既不需要显式近似测量似然，也可以推导后验样本近似误差的理论界。由于采样在噪声空间中逐步进行，因此能够累积多样的重建结果，而无需反复执行完整去噪链。

<a id="CPS-S004"></a>
**Source: p.2 CPS-S004**

**Original:** The key contributions are: a posterior sampling method defined by Langevin dynamics in the noise space of a pre-trained generative model, enabling accumulation of posterior samples; and a theoretical guarantee on the posterior sampling approximation error, bounded by the approximation error of the prior by the pre-trained generative model.

**中文：** 本文的主要贡献包括：第一，提出在预训练生成模型噪声空间中定义的 Langevin 后验采样方法，使后验样本可以持续累积；第二，给出后验采样近似误差的理论保证，并将该误差与预训练生成模型对真实先验的逼近误差联系起来。

### 2.3 主要内容概括

CPS 的关键视角是：如果 CM/蒸馏流已经提供了一个确定性映射 (x_0=\Phi(x_1))，就可以不再反复重启生成链，而是在低维或同维噪声变量 (x_1) 上做 Langevin 探索。测量信息用于 warm start，之后连续产生多个符合观测的后验样本，从而同时保留数据一致性和不确定性。

### 2.4 你可以学习与应用的知识

- **从单点重建转向后验集合**：对 (p(H\mid Y,r,e,M)) 生成多个信道样本，可评估 beam-selection entropy、outage risk、rate 分布和 posterior coverage，而不只看一个 NMSE。
- **噪声空间后验校正**：可令 (H=\Phi(z;r,e,M))，在 (z) 上使用导频损失
  \[
  \mathcal L_Y(H)=\frac{\|Y-\mathcal A(H)\|_F^2}{2\sigma_n^2}
  \]
  驱动 Langevin 更新。
- **计算效率**：warm start 后连续生成后验样本，适合比较多个候选信道或多个波束决策的风险。
- **关键风险**：噪声空间映射需足够平滑且近似一一对应；原文证明依赖生成先验质量，迁移到复数 MIMO 时仍需检查 Jacobian、噪声标定和观测算子稳定性。

## 3. MACS: Measurement-Aware Consistency Sampling for Inverse Problems

**论文信息**：Amirreza Tanevardi 等；arXiv:2510.02208v3（2026-07-04 更新）。

### 3.1 Abstract 摘要

**原文**

Diffusion models have emerged as powerful generative priors for solving inverse imaging problems. However, their practical deployment is hindered by the substantial computational cost of slow, multi-step sampling. Although Consistency Models address this limitation by enabling high-quality generation in only one or a few steps, their direct application to inverse problems has remained largely unexplored. This paper introduces a modified consistency sampling framework specifically designed for inverse problems. The proposed approach regulates the sampler’s stochasticity through a measurement-consistency mechanism that leverages the degradation operator, thereby enforcing fidelity to the observed data while preserving the computational efficiency of consistency-based generation.

Comprehensive experiments on Fashion-MNIST and LSUN Bedroom demonstrate improvements across perceptual and pixel-level metrics, including FID, KID, PSNR and SSIM, compared with baseline consistency and diffusion-based sampling methods. The proposed method achieves competitive or superior reconstruction quality with only a small number of sampling steps.

**中文翻译**

扩散模型已经成为解决逆成像问题的强大生成先验。然而，其实际部署受到慢速多步采样所带来的高计算成本限制。Consistency Models 能够在一步或少数几步内实现高质量生成，从而缓解这一问题，但它们在逆问题中的直接应用仍然研究不足。本文提出一个专门面向逆问题的改进一致性采样框架。该方法利用退化算子，通过测量一致性机制调节采样器的随机性，在保持一致性生成计算效率的同时，强制重建结果与观测数据相符。

在 Fashion-MNIST 和 LSUN Bedroom 上的综合实验表明，与一致性模型和扩散模型采样基线相比，MACS 在 FID、KID、PSNR 和 SSIM 等感知与像素级指标上均有改进。该方法只需少量采样步数，就能达到具有竞争力甚至更优的重建质量。

### 3.2 Introduction 引言

<a id="MACS-S001"></a>
**Source: p.1 MACS-S001**

**Original:** Inverse problems represent a fundamental class of challenges in computational imaging and signal processing, with critical applications in medical imaging, remote sensing, seismic analysis, video reconstruction, and audio signal processing. The core objective is to reconstruct the original signal (x) from degraded measurements (y) acquired through a degradation operator (A), which is typically ill-conditioned or non-invertible. This inherent ill-posedness necessitates the incorporation of strong prior knowledge to obtain meaningful reconstructions.

**中文：** 逆问题是计算成像和信号处理中的一类基础问题，广泛应用于医学成像、遥感、地震分析、视频重建和音频信号处理。其核心目标是：给定通过退化算子 (A) 获得的退化观测 (y)，重建原始信号 (x)。由于 (A) 通常病态或不可逆，该问题天然具有不适定性，因此必须引入较强的先验知识，才能得到有意义的重建结果。

<a id="MACS-S002"></a>
**Source: p.1 MACS-S002**

**Original:** Traditional inverse problem methods rely on filtering, optimization and classical machine learning techniques. Recently, diffusion models have become powerful priors for inverse problems and have achieved state-of-the-art performance in super-resolution, inpainting, deblurring and colorization. However, their reliance on hundreds to thousands of neural function evaluations remains a major limitation for practical deployment.

**中文：** 传统逆问题方法依赖滤波、优化和经典机器学习技术。近年来，扩散模型成为逆问题的强大先验，并在超分辨率、修复、去模糊和着色等任务上取得了先进性能。然而，扩散模型通常需要数百到数千次神经函数评估，这仍是其实际部署的主要限制。

<a id="MACS-S003"></a>
**Source: p.1 MACS-S003**

**Original:** Consistency Models address this limitation by enabling high-quality generation in only one or a few sampling steps. However, unlike diffusion models, CMs lack an inherent mechanism for enforcing measurement consistency during sampling, making efficient CM-based inverse solvers an open research problem.

**中文：** Consistency Models 通过只需一步或少数几步采样即可实现高质量生成，缓解了上述限制。但与扩散模型不同，CM 没有一个内生的机制在采样过程中强制满足测量一致性，因此，如何构建高效的 CM 逆问题求解器仍是一个开放问题。

<a id="MACS-S004"></a>
**Source: p.1 MACS-S004**

## 5. QuaMoE-DRF: Proactive Beam and Rate Adaptation via Multimodal Dynamic Radio Map Forecasting in ISAC Networks

**论文信息**：Zhihan Zeng, Kaihe Wang, Zhongpei Zhang, Chongwen Huang；arXiv:2607.00974v1。

### 5.1 Abstract 摘要

**原文**

Its core representation is a future beam-SINR field. The full multi-BS beam-SINR field is sufficient for finite-codebook threshold-rate BS, beam, MCS, goodput, and outage decisions.

It jointly predicts communication-oriented map channels and proactive BS, beam, and MCS decisions. On a dynamic multi-BS and multi-UE urban benchmark, the paper reports 402.5 Mbps effective rate, 0.0417 outage probability, and 0.1836 map RMSE; the current validation uses labels from a compact blockage/path-loss simulator, with ray tracing used only for calibration and sanity checking.

- **评价闭环**：地图 RMSE 只是中间指标，应同时报告 effective rate、outage、beam robustness、MCS accuracy 和 modality degradation 下的性能。
- **理论启发**：MCS 阈值附近、波束边界和遮挡切换处的误差最危险，因此训练和测试应按通信裕量分层，而不只按平均误差。
- **局限**：论文当前标签主要来自遮挡/路径损耗模拟器，射线追踪只做校准；尚不能据此断言真实多模态感知、真实 MIMO CSI 或跨城市场景一定保持相同收益。

## 六、五篇论文对你当前研究的统一启示

### 1. 方法主线

可以把你的目标写成：

\[
p(H\mid r,e,M)\xrightarrow{\text{CM/Flow prior}}p(H\mid Y,r,e,M)
\xrightarrow{\text{measurement-aware posterior correction}}
\{\text{beam, rate, outage}\},
\]

其中 (r) 表示位置或连续条件，(e) 表示环境/多径先验，(M) 表示系统配置或缺失模态，(Y=\mathcal A(H)+N) 表示导频或其他观测。

### 2. 建议的最小可行实验

1. 用 UrbanMIMOMap 或 OpenPathNet 类数据构造复数信道先验，至少保留 (H)、AoA/AoD、ToA、路径增益和位置。
2. 采用 CM/Flow 主干，比较 1、2、4、8 NFE，测试多步一致性训练是否比单步训练更稳定。
3. 用 MACS 风格残差控制采样随机性，用 PnP-CM 风格数据步处理导频一致性。
4. 用 CPS 风格噪声空间 Langevin 生成多个后验信道样本，评价 posterior coverage、beam entropy 和 outage risk。
5. 用 QuaMoE-DRF 风格的 `mask + confidence + age` 融合位置、环境图、导频、历史 CSI 和角度模态。
6. 指标同时覆盖 `NMSE`、`pilot residual`、`EVM`、`achievable rate`、`beam accuracy`、`outage`、NFE 和推理时延。

### 3. 优先级判断

对你的研究，建议优先级为：

1. **MACS + PnP-CM**：直接学习如何把观测残差写入低 NFE 先验采样；这是最接近导频/LS-CSI 后验修正的技术入口。
2. **CPS**：补上多样后验与不确定性量化，避免信道生成只输出单点结果。
3. **UrbanMIMOMap**：建立复数 MIMO、阵列相关和预编码感知的数据/指标底座。
4. **Multistep CM**：解决 CM 训练难度与采样质量折中，为不同实时性预算提供统一模型。
5. **QuaMoE-DRF**：作为动态环境、多模态缺失和下游 beam/rate 决策的结构化参考。

这组论文中，真正适合作为核心方法锚点的是连续条件生成、观测一致性后验校正和多样后验采样；QuaMoE-DRF 更适合作为应用与系统闭环参考，UrbanMIMOMap 更适合作为数据与仿真基础。它们的实验结果主要来自图像、射线追踪或模拟环境，迁移到真实复数 MIMO、复杂硬件失真和跨城市场景时，仍需实验验证。
