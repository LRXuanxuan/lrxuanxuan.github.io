**Original:**

Abstract—Radio maps characterize the spatial distribution of
wireless conditions and are fundamental to network planning
and optimization. However, existing learning-based methods re-
construct isolated radio-map snapshots and do not model how
network actions drive the evolution of the radio environment.
In this paper, we present RadioDreamer, a two-stage world
model that unifies action-conditioned radio-map forecasting and
imagination-based network optimization within a latent space.
To learn causal environmental transitions from sequential inter-
actions, we introduce a structured spatio-temporal transformer
backbone coupled with dynamic-region supervision, enabling
accurate modeling of action-sensitive areas. Second, rather than
relying on conventional physical trial-and-error, RadioDreamer
establishes a general planning paradigm that generates counter-
factual latent trajectories to evaluate and optimize long-horizon
network parameter configurations. Experiments show that Radio-
Dreamer improves both multi-step rollout forecasting and closed-
loop network optimization. It reduces full-map forecasting error
by 25.2% on average and achieves approximately 80% accuracy
in predicting the evolution direction of radio signals. More im-
portantly, for long-horizon network configuration, imagination-
based planning surpasses model-free policies optimized directly
on radio-map observations by 8.8% in accumulated return.

## 摘要

无线电地图刻画了无线条件的空间分布，是网络规划与优化的基础。然而，现有基于学习的方法重建的是彼此孤立的无线电地图快照，并未建模网络动作如何驱动无线电环境的演化。本文提出 RadioDreamer——一个两阶段世界模型，它在潜空间中统一了动作条件化的无线电地图预测与基于想象的网络优化。为从序列交互中学习因果性的环境转移，我们引入结构化时空 Transformer 主干，并配合动态区域监督，从而能够准确建模对动作敏感的区域。其次，RadioDreamer 不依赖传统的物理试错，而是建立一个通用规划范式：生成反事实潜轨迹，用以评估并优化长时程网络参数配置。实验表明，RadioDreamer 同时改善了多步推演预测与闭环网络优化：它把全图预测误差平均降低 25.2%，并在预测无线电信号演化方向上达到约 80% 的准确率。更重要的是，对于长时程网络配置，基于想象的规划在累积回报上比直接在无线电地图观测上优化的无模型策略高出 8.8%。

## I. 引言

<span id="S001-06"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-06

**Original:**

The radio propagation environment determines how wireless
signals propagate through physical spaces and is essential for
reliable mobile network connectivity. Radio map (RM), by
representing the spatial distribution of radio conditions, has be-
come a key enabler for network planning, beam configuration,
and radio resource management [1]–[3]. Constructing them ac-
curately with low cost has received considerable attention, and
learning-based methods have become the prevailing approach.
For example, RadioUNet [4] and PMNet [5] adopt encoder–
decoder networks to regress path-loss distributions from build-
ing layouts and transmitter information, and RadioDiff [6]
utilizes conditional generation to recover fine-grained radio
maps. Despite their different architectures, these methods
typically model the relationship among a static urban scene, a
fixed set of network parameters, and the corresponding radio
state, learning statistical mappings from a given scene and
network configuration to a radio-map snapshot.

**中文:**

无线电传播环境决定了无线信号如何在物理空间中传播，是可靠移动网络连通性的基础。无线电地图（radio map, RM）通过表示无线电条件的空间分布，已成为网络规划、波束配置与无线资源管理的关键使能技术 [1]–[3]。以低成本准确构建 RM 已受到广泛关注，基于学习的方法已成为主流途径。例如，RadioUNet [4] 与 PMNet [5] 采用编码器–解码器网络，从建筑布局与发射机信息回归路径损耗分布；RadioDiff [6] 利用条件生成恢复细粒度无线电地图。尽管架构不同，这些方法通常建模的是「静态城市场景、一组固定网络参数与相应无线电状态」之间的关系，即学习从给定场景与网络配置到无线电地图快照的统计映射。

<span id="S001-08"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S001-08

**Original:**

The fundamental limitation of this static paradigm is that it
cannot capture the causal relationship between network actions
and radio-environment changes. The radio environment is
jointly determined by the propagation scene and network con-
figurations: adjusting transmit power or antenna tilt reshapes
signal strength, coverage boundaries, and interference condi-
tions [7]. By treating different configurations as independent
samples, static models learn only statistical correlations and
cannot identify changes directly induced by network actions.
Meanwhile, moving vehicles/pedestrians continuously alter
blockage, reflection, and propagation paths, creating dynamics
across spatial regions [8], [9]. Such evolution cannot be ade-
quately represented by isolated snapshots, limiting multi-step
rollout accuracy. More importantly, tasks such as antenna tun-
ing, coverage optimization, and energy-saving control require
counterfactual rollouts of candidate actions and comparisons
of their long-term returns [10]–[12]. Without the ability to
predict radio-environment evolution, a model may produce
inaccurate long-horizon estimates and suboptimal policies.

**中文:**

这种静态范式的根本局限在于，它无法刻画网络动作与无线电环境变化之间的因果关系。无线电环境由传播场景与网络配置共同决定：调整发射功率或天线倾角会重塑信号强度、覆盖边界与干扰条件 [7]。静态模型把不同配置当作独立样本，因而只学到统计相关性，无法识别由网络动作直接引起的变化。与此同时，移动的车辆/行人不断改变遮挡、反射与传播路径，在各空间区域上产生动态变化 [8], [9]。这类演化无法由孤立快照充分表示，从而限制了多步推演的精度。更重要的是，天线调优、覆盖优化与节能控制等任务需要候选动作的反事实推演，并比较其长期回报 [10]–[12]。若不能预测无线电环境的演化，模型就可能给出不准确的长时程估计与次优策略。

## II. 相关工作

<span id="S002-06"></span>
**Source:** p.2 S002-06

**Original:**

Radio-map prediction has been extensively studied. Early
approaches relied on repeated measurements or propagation
models, but their data-collection and computational costs
limit continuous forecasting [1], [19]. Recent learning-based
methods have directly mapped environmental conditions to
radio maps. RadioUNet [4] and PMNet [5] predict path-loss
distributions from building layouts and transmitter informa-
tion, while RadioDiff [6] formulates radio-map construction
as conditional generation. Subsequent studies further incorpo-
rate electromagnetic knowledge for multipath modeling [20],
and foundation models exploit large-scale self-supervised pre-
training to learn radio-environment representations [21]–[23].

**中文:**

无线电地图预测已被广泛研究。早期方法依赖重复测量或传播模型，但其数据采集与计算成本限制了连续预测 [1], [19]。近期基于学习的方法直接把环境条件映射为无线电地图。RadioUNet [4] 与 PMNet [5] 依据建筑布局与发射机信息预测路径损耗分布，而 RadioDiff [6] 把无线电地图构建表述为条件生成。后续研究进一步引入电磁知识以建模多径 [20]，基础模型则利用大规模自监督预训练学习无线电环境表示 [21]–[23]。

World models learn compact environment representations
and action-conditioned transition dynamics from sequential
observations, enabling future states to be imagined under
alternative actions. Dreamer trains control policies from latent

世界模型从序列观测中学习紧凑的环境表示与动作条件化转移动力学，使未来状态能够在不同动作下被想象。Dreamer 从潜空间想象轨迹中训练控制策略 [24]，V-JEPA 2 支撑动作条件化的机器人规划 [25]。在自动驾驶中，GAIA-1 [26]、Drive-WM [27] 与 DrivingWorld [28] 生成可控的未来场景以用于轨迹规划。这些工作展示了世界模型统一预测、反事实推演与动作规划的能力。

## VI. 结论

---

<span id="S009-09"></span>
**Source:** p.9 S009-09

**Original:**

This paper presented RadioDreamer, a world model for
learning the action-conditioned dynamics between radio envi-
ronments and BS configurations in the latent space. By com-
bining structured attention mechanisms with region-adaptive
supervision, RadioDreamer learns an informative latent repre-
sentation of radio-environment evolution and supports multi-
step imagination for network optimization. Experiments across
three urban scenes show that RadioDreamer reduces radio-map
forecasting error by 25.2% on average over the strongest base-
lines, demonstrating the effectiveness of world models for dy-
namic radio-map forecasting. RadioDreamer further supports
network optimization through latent imagination, achieving an
8.8% higher episode return than planning over decoded radio
maps and validating the advantage of optimizing long-term
radio-state dependencies directly in the latent space. This work
represents an initial attempt to introduce world models into
wireless communications. Future work will incorporate real-
world measurements, unified training across multiple urban
scenes, and more comprehensive network planning and opti-
mization tasks.

**中文:**

本文提出了 RadioDreamer——一个在潜空间中学习无线电环境与 BS 配置之间动作条件化动力学的世界模型。通过把结构化注意力机制与区域自适应监督相结合，RadioDreamer 学到无线电环境演化的信息量丰富的潜表示，并支撑用于网络优化的多步想象。三个城市场景的实验表明，RadioDreamer 相对最强基线把无线电地图预测误差平均降低 25.2%，证明了世界模型在动态无线电地图预测中的有效性。RadioDreamer 还通过潜空间想象支撑网络优化，取得比在解码无线电地图上规划高 8.8% 的回合回报，验证了直接在潜空间中优化长期无线电状态依赖的优势。本工作是把世界模型引入无线通信的一次初步尝试。未来工作将纳入真实世界测量、跨多个城市场景的统一训练，以及更全面的网络规划与优化任务。
