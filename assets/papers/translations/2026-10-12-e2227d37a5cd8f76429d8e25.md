<span id="S004"></span>

**Original:** Diffusion and flow-matching models can produce high-quality posterior samples for inverse problems, but typically require tens to thousands of network evaluations per draw. MeanFlow enables one-step generation, yet applying it to inverse problems leaves no intermediate steps at which to enforce measurement consistency. We introduce SNaP, a one-step MeanFlow posterior sampler for linear inverse problems with Gaussian noise. Its central innovation is a measurement-adapted source: a Gaussian distribution whose mean and anisotropic covariance are determined by the measurement operator, observation, and noise level. The source anchors well-measured directions while preserving variation where the measurements are weak or uninformative. We show that the exact conditional flow transports this source to the true posterior. Across natural-image restoration and multi-coil MRI, SNaP produces diverse, high-quality samples with one network evaluation per draw, 30 to 2250× faster than iterative samplers.

**中文:** 扩散模型和流匹配模型能够为逆问题生成高质量的后验样本，但每生成一个样本通常需要进行数十至数千次网络评估。MeanFlow 支持一步生成；然而，将其用于逆问题时，不再有可施加测量一致性的中间步骤。本文提出 SNaP：一种面向含高斯噪声线性逆问题的一步 MeanFlow 后验采样器。其核心创新是构造一种适配测量的源分布：一个高斯分布，其均值和各向异性协方差由测量算子、观测值和噪声水平共同决定。该源分布在测量充分的方向上提供锚定，同时在测量较弱或缺乏信息的方向上保留变化。我们证明，精确的条件流会将该源分布输运到真实后验。在自然图像复原和多线圈 MRI 实验中，SNaP 每个样本只需一次网络评估，就能产生多样且高质量的样本；与迭代采样器相比，速度提升 30 至 2250 倍。

**Original:** Many imaging tasks require recovering an unknown image x_1 from noisy linear measurements y = A x_1 + n, where A models the imaging system and n ~ N(0, σ_n^2 I) denotes additive Gaussian noise. This formulation encompasses magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), computed tomography (CT), microscopy, astronomical imaging, and remote sensing [1, 2, 3]. Because A is often ill-conditioned or rank-deficient, the measurements do not uniquely determine x_1. Therefore, we need prior information on images for a reliable recovery. Classical methods impose handcrafted regularizers such as total variation [4]. More recently, deep generative models, including diffusion models [5, 6] and flow-matching models [7, 8, 9], have learned rich image distributions that can serve as priors for inverse problems [10].

**中文:** 许多成像任务都需要根据含噪线性测量恢复未知图像 $x_1$：$y=Ax_1+n$，其中 $A$ 表示成像系统，$n\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_n^2 I)$ 表示加性高斯噪声。该建模形式涵盖磁共振成像（MRI）、计算机断层成像（CT）、显微成像、天文成像和遥感 [1, 2, 3]。由于 $A$ 常常是病态或秩亏的，测量值无法唯一确定 $x_1$。因此，要可靠地恢复图像，需要引入图像先验信息。经典方法采用总变分等人工设计的正则项 [4]。近年来，扩散模型 [5, 6] 和流匹配模型 [7, 8, 9] 等深度生成模型学习了丰富的图像分布，可将其作为逆问题的先验 [10]。

<span id="S007"></span>
**Source:** p.1 S007

**Original:** Most generative inverse solvers use an unconditional generative model as an image prior and impose measurement consistency repeatedly during an iterative sampling trajectory. One family modifies the generative dynamics using data-fidelity gradients or pseudoinverse corrections [11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16]. Another alternates generative updates with explicit data-consistency steps, following the plug-and-play paradigm [17, 18, 19, 20, 21]. Although these corrections improve agreement with the measurement y, they require many sequential network evaluations and therefore make posterior sampling slow. Depending on the method, generating a single sample may require tens to thousands of network evaluations (NFEs). In our CelebA Gaussian-deblurring experiment, for example, DPS [11] requires 1000 NFEs and 27 seconds to produce one (128 × 128) reconstruction (Figure 1).

**中文:** 大多数生成式逆问题求解器将无条件生成模型用作图像先验，并在迭代采样轨迹中反复施加测量一致性。一类方法利用数据保真度梯度或伪逆校正来修改生成动力学 [11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16]；另一类方法遵循即插即用（plug-and-play）范式，在生成更新之间交替执行显式的数据一致性步骤 [17, 18, 19, 20, 21]。这些校正虽然能提高结果与测量值 $y$ 的一致性，但需要顺序执行大量网络评估，因而使后验采样变慢。根据具体方法，生成单个样本可能需要数十至数千次网络评估（NFE）。例如，在 CelebA 高斯模糊实验中，DPS [11] 生成一幅 $(128\times128)$ 重建图像需要 1000 次 NFE 和 27 秒（图 1）。

**Original:** Inverse Problems. We consider a linear measurement operator A ∈ R^{m×n} with additive white Gaussian noise, y = A x_1 + n, n ∼ N(0, σ_n^2 I). (1) The goal is to infer the unknown image x_1 ∈ R^n from the measurement y ∈ R^m; in posterior sampling, this means drawing samples from p(x_1 | y). We write the singular value decomposition of A as A = UΣV^⊤ and let ρ := rank(A). We order the singular values so that s_1 ≥ ··· ≥ s_ρ > 0 and set s_i = 0 for ρ < i ≤ n. The corresponding right singular vectors are the columns of V. We denote the pseudoinverse by A^† and the orthogonal projection onto null(A) by P := I − A^†A.

**中文:** 逆问题。考虑带加性白高斯噪声的线性测量算子 $A\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$：$y=Ax_1+n,\quad n\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_n^2 I).\tag{1}$ 目标是根据测量值 $y\in\mathbb{R}^m$ 推断未知图像 $x_1\in\mathbb{R}^n$；在后验采样中，这意味着从 $p(x_1\mid y)$ 抽取样本。将 $A$ 的奇异值分解记为 $A=U\Sigma V^\top$，并令 $\rho:=\operatorname{rank}(A)$。奇异值按 $s_1\ge\cdots\ge s_\rho>0$ 排序，并对 $\rho<i\le n$ 令 $s_i=0$。相应的右奇异向量构成矩阵 $V$ 的各列。记 $A^\dagger$ 为伪逆，$P:=I-A^\dagger A$ 为投影到 $\operatorname{null}(A)$ 上的正交投影。

**Original:** Flow Matching. Flow matching [7, 8, 9] learns a velocity field that transports a source distribution p_0 to a data distribution p_1. We index time so that t = 0 corresponds to the source and t = 1 to the data. The flow ψ_t is defined by the ODE d/dt ψ_t(x) = v_t(ψ_t(x)) for t ∈ (0, 1), and it maps samples from p_0 to samples from p_1. For the exact marginal velocity field, the terminal map satisfies (ψ_1)# p_0 = p_1. This marginal velocity is generally not available in closed form, so flow matching instead regresses a sample-conditional velocity. Given a coupling of endpoints (x_0, x_1) with x_0 ∼ p_0 and x_1 ∼ p_1, the straight-line path z_t = (1 − t)x_0 + t x_1, v = x_1 − x_0, (2) has constant conditional velocity v. A network v_θ is trained by minimizing E||v_θ(z_t, t) − (x_1 − x_0)||_2^2. The expected parameter gradient of this conditional regression objective coincides with that of the marginal flow-matching objective [7]. The source is commonly chosen as N(0, I) because it

**Original:** Mean Flows. Sampling a flow-matching model requires integrating the velocity v, which takes many network evaluations. MeanFlow [25, 23] avoids this integration by modeling the average velocity u over an interval [r, t], defined as u(z_r, r, t) := 1/(t−r) ∫_r^t v(z_s, s) ds. (3)

**中文:** 均值流。对流匹配模型进行采样需要对速度 $v$ 积分，这需要多次网络评估。MeanFlow [25, 23] 通过对区间 $[r,t]$ 上的平均速度 $u$ 建模来避免该积分，其定义为 $u(z_r,r,t):=\frac{1}{t-r}\int_r^t v(z_s,s)\,ds.\tag{3}$

<span id="S017"></span>
**Source:** p.3 S017

**Original:** Once u is learned, the entire flow reduces to a single evaluation, z_1 = z_0 + u(z_0, 0, 1). Learning u directly from equation 3 is intractable, since it requires the integral of v. Instead, rewriting equation 3 as (t−r)u = ∫_r^t v ds and differentiating with respect to r, with t fixed, gives the MeanFlow identity u(z_r, r, t) = v(z_r, r) + (t−r) d/dr u(z_r, r, t). (4) This differential identity provides the training relation used by MeanFlow. It also connects MeanFlow to consistency and shortcut models, which learn finite-time flow maps through related consistency relations [22, 23, 24].

**中文:** 学习得到 $u$ 后，整个流只需一次评估：$z_1=z_0+u(z_0,0,1)$。由于直接依据式 (3) 学习 $u$ 需要计算 $v$ 的积分，因而不可行。作为替代，将式 (3) 改写为 $(t-r)u=\int_r^t v\,ds$，并在固定 $t$ 时对 $r$ 求导，可得 MeanFlow 恒等式 $u(z_r,r,t)=v(z_r,r)+(t-r)\frac{d}{dr}u(z_r,r,t).\tag{4}$ 该微分恒等式给出了 MeanFlow 所使用的训练关系，也将 MeanFlow 与一致性模型和捷径模型联系起来；后两者通过相关的一致性关系学习有限时间的流映射 [22, 23, 24]。

**Original:** Measurement-dependent sources. Standard MeanFlow formulations use a fixed, measurement-independent source. Applying MeanFlow to inverse problems therefore requires incorporating the measurement model into the one-step transport. <span id="S021"></span>

**Original:** SNaP builds the geometry of the forward model into a one-step posterior transport. Its measurement-dependent source is designed to keep the path to the posterior short while preserving the randomness needed for sampling. It concentrates draws in well-measured directions and retains variation in weakly measured and null-space directions. A conditional MeanFlow then learns to transport these draws toward p(x_1 | y), producing an approximate posterior sample from one source draw and one network evaluation. We now derive the source, characterize the exact transport, and describe how the model is trained and sampled.

**Original:** We introduced SNaP, a one-step posterior sampler for linear inverse problems with Gaussian noise. Instead of correcting toward the measurement at every step, SNaP builds it into the transport. The transport starts from a distribution centered at the linear estimate that minimizes the expected transport distance, with a spread that matches the remaining uncertainty. We proved that the exact one-step map carries this distribution to the target posterior. On natural images and multi-coil MRI, SNaP draws a posterior sample in a single network evaluation, 30 to 2250× faster than iterative samplers.

**中文:** 本文提出 SNaP，一种面向含高斯噪声线性逆问题的一步后验采样器。SNaP 不在每一步都向测量值校正，而是将测量信息纳入输运过程。输运从一个分布出发：其中心取为使期望输运距离最小的线性估计，离散程度则与剩余不确定性相匹配。我们证明，精确的一步映射会将该分布输运到目标后验。在自然图像和多线圈 MRI 实验中，SNaP 仅需一次网络评估即可抽取一个后验样本，速度比迭代采样器快 30 至 2250 倍。

**Original:** We check whether the spread of a sampler’s draws has the right size for a sampler of p(x | y). Let p_s(x | y) be the distribution of the draws and μ_s(y) its mean, and let (x_1, y) be a ground-truth image and its measurement. Define B := E_{x_1,y}[||μ_s(y) − x_1||_2^2], V := E_y[tr Cov_s(x | y)]. If p_s = p, then μ_s(y) = E[x | y] and B = E_y E_{x_1|y}[||E[x | y] − x_1||_2^2] = E_y[tr Cov(x | y)] = V.
